# University thesis format verification checklist

Status 2026-09-24: **official template and institution-specific guide not yet identified**. Issue #13 (B6). The local proposal is a proposal form from a computer engineering and sciences faculty, not an authenticated final-thesis template. It does not establish the institution's thesis margins, section order, citation style or approval-page wording. Do not create `thesis/template.docx` by guessing these requirements or relabeling the proposal form.

## Source-of-truth acquisition

- [ ] Identify the university, faculty/graduate-office authority, degree/program and required thesis language from an authenticated source or the user's confirmed institutional details.
- [ ] Obtain the official current thesis Word/DOTX template or formatting guide and record its source URL/file, retrieval date, version or effective date, and SHA-256. If a login is required, ask for the minimum supported access; never request a password or cookie in chat.
- [ ] Check whether the official source is a thesis template rather than a proposal form, sample thesis or third-party conversion.
- [ ] Resolve conflicts between the approved proposal titles/questions and institutional front-matter wording through the authorized decision process; preserve the original proposal.

## Template and document checks after acquisition

- [ ] Preserve official styles, page size, margins, section breaks, header/footer and page-number scheme. Record any required deviation rather than silently substituting a generic style.
- [ ] Confirm mandatory title/approval/declaration pages, Persian/English abstract and keyword order, contents/lists, chapter sequence, appendices and required forms.
- [ ] Confirm heading levels, body typography, line spacing, paragraph indentation, captions, table/figure numbering and cross-reference conventions.
- [ ] Confirm equation notation/numbering, bilingual text direction, Latin terms, abbreviations and bibliography/citation style.
- [ ] Test that a **copy** of the actual official template opens and renders, with representative bilingual headings, one figure, one table, one citation and one equation. Inspect every rendered page; retain a test receipt and do not treat a successful render as academic approval.
- [ ] Check accessibility and metadata before final handoff; do not expose student IDs, signatures or private proposal content in the public repository.
