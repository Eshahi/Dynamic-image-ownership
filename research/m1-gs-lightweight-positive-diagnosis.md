# B-LW1 positive result: diagnosis and bounded next decision

Version **`m1-gs-positive-diagnosis-v1`**, 2026-10-04, design escalation **xhigh**. This is an exploratory diagnosis after seeing development outcomes. The additional tests below are prospective; no GPU experiment or model execution was performed for this document. Only code, installed pipeline source, metadata, retained image-file hashes and recorded bit/vote arrays were inspected. The four synthetic prompt clusters remain separate from photographic development data and held-out data.

**Decision: retain the positive B-LW1 result as a plausible carrier observation, but require one independent, inexpensive re-encoding/control probe before treating it as a reliable implementation finding. Do not dismiss this family or immediately change its carrier.** High message accuracy is compatible with moderate noise-sign transport amplified by repetition. The result is neither a verified implementation of the proposal's dual-key binding nor a discovery that initial noise can correlate with final latents.

The native source is MAIN `.thesis-build/dev-runs/20261004-0000-gs-native`, commit `6a46faad51d91b285d00bbb6b2a4563ef681c11b`. It reports 112/112 completed conditions in 18.844 s. The existing [paired report](m1-gs-paired-results.md) gives every native/readout match vector and retained quality/runtime limits.

All eight readout output-file receipts were recomputed and matched. All 112 condition PNGs matched both their native and readout image hashes. For both keys on every condition, an independent array calculation checked `bits == (votes > 32)`, Hamming matches against the declared payload, tie counts and the fixed `matches >= 180` decision: **224/224 checks agreed**. This validates bookkeeping; it cannot independently establish that the recorded votes came from the images.

It receives no reference payload, source noise, prompt, native inverse output or attack label. Lines 258–261 derive the reference only after extraction, for evaluation. The reconstructed-noise self-check is a separate function, not its image input.
- Installed diffusers 0.35.1 `pipelines/stable_diffusion/pipeline_stable_diffusion.py`, SHA-256 `7466201901e68667164344a9fb58596d9d253e524c9addad38494ee6bc1f7e08`, lines 992–1085: supplied latents pass through timestep setup, the U-Net/scheduler loop and final latent decoding. Across all readout conditions, recorded fp16 scaling changed **zero signs**; the largest scaled zero count was two coordinates out of 16,384. Positive scaling cannot explain the message correlation. The nonce depends on generation seed, which also identifies the payload, but separate hash domains and the actual extraction code do not derive payload bits from that seed. Public four-seed lookup would be a possible dishonest alternative implementation; the independent probe below deliberately removes that route.

## The mechanism is quantitatively plausible

Let `v_j` be a recorded dewhitened-one count among the 64 copies of payload bit `b_j`. Then the number of raw coordinates agreeing with the embedded sign mask is `g_j = v_j` for `b_j=1`, and `64-v_j` for `b_j=0`. Thus `sum(g_j)/16384` is the raw sign agreement, derived without seeing a new latent. This measures agreement with the marked initial-noise signs, not with its Gaussian magnitudes.

| Cluster | C0 clean raw sign agreement | C1 clean raw sign agreement | C1 clean decoded matches /256 | C1 .4 seed0 raw sign agreement | C1 .4 seed0 decoded matches /256 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| prompt-0 | .504333 | .600830 | 237 | .554199 | 206 |
| prompt-1 | .499756 | .702148 | 256 | .612488 | 251 |
| prompt-2 | .489014 | .660278 | 254 | .590942 | 235 |
| prompt-3 | .501831 | .693481 | 256 | .584534 | 226 |

Mean raw C1 agreement is **.664185 clean**, **.658844 after VAE**, and **.585541/.585464/.588440 at .4 seeds 0/1/2**. Corresponding mean decoded accuracies are .979492, .980469, and .896484/.892578/.890625. This is substantial coordinate noise with reliable repetition decoding, not 98% preservation of 16,384 noise signs. On these same three preselected channels, comparing recovered C1 messages with each of the other three reference payloads gives 36 cross-reference scores, all below 180, maximum **142/256**. These queries retain the correct original nonce and therefore test evaluator/reference separation, not wrong-nonce independence.

For intuition only, independent copies with coordinate correctness `p` and equally frequent message bits give

`A64(p) = [Pr{Binomial(64,p) >= 33} + Pr{Binomial(64,p) >= 32}]/2`.

The asymmetric tie term is required because ties decode zero. This ideal calculation gives .787822 at `p=.55` and .946309 at `p=.60`. Actual errors can be spatially dependent and heterogeneous; substituting the mean p is not a fitted prediction, capacity estimate or confidence interval. The observed per-bit vote-count standard deviations around 4 are compatible with a noisy aggregate but do not prove independence.

[NoisePrints v1, §3.3, §4 and Appendices E/G](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.13793v1) already studies direct correlation between a generated image's VAE latent and its initial noise, without inversion. Its seed-enrollment detector and independent-seed null differ from our repeated GS message readout. Its low-variance failure example also cautions against extrapolating four ordinary prompts. That primary result makes transport plausible; it neither validates these bytes nor supplies an adaptive-forgery guarantee. The parent's primary-source card records the broader method and limits.

More directly, [OSI v1, Appendix D.1 and Figure 7](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.09494v1#A4.SS1) defines Step-0 as the VAE encoder output and reports over 98% clean message-bit accuracy there when the spatial repetition factor is eight. It distinguishes message accuracy from coordinate-level latent sign accuracy. Sections 3.2–4 introduce a separately trained one-step sign predictor, but the Appendix's zero-inversion observation is already close prior art for B-LW1. We inspected that primary passage: our measured mean .979492 is consistent with a known phenomenon, and is not a novelty claim or a reproduction of their precise model/data configuration.

## Prospective independent probe B-LW1-P1

Implement a separate script/config; leave both original runs and both original scripts unchanged. Commit first and use a new run directory. Cap this probe at **120 seconds, 10 GiB GPU allocation and 100 MiB artifacts**, pinned fp16 SD1.5 VAE, no new generation, no text encoder, no U-Net, no diffusion inversion. Metadata preparation and array controls may run on CPU; one VAE encode per original image is the only model operation. All failures and mismatches remain in its 24-condition inventory.

1. **Fixed image set.** Four cases × C0/C1 × `{clean, vae, regen-0.4-seed0}` = **24 saved PNGs**. Use their exact original file hashes. Opaque IDs sorted by the PNG SHA-256 determine processing order. A sealed readout input contains only opaque ID, image path/hash and four nonce candidates plus correct/wrong public key bytes. No case seed, payload, native score, old readout, arm label or original-noise array is available to the model/readout phase. Readout emits raw sign tensor bytes/hash, per-nonce vote arrays and bits before a separate evaluator opens the ID-to-reference mapping. Arbitrary filenames are not themselves scientific blinding; the restricted data flow is the test.
2. **Independent arithmetic.** Do not call either existing `decode`/`decode_votes` or either `evaluate`. SHAKE whitening is independently implemented from the documented byte concatenation. For bit `(c,a,b)`, explicitly visit `(c, a+8*r, b+8*s)` for `r,s=0..7`; XOR each sign with its corresponding whitening coordinate, count, and compare strictly `>32`. CPU synthetic known-message, complement, tie and one-coordinate fixtures must pass first. Code independence here is modest, not an independent research-team review.
3. **Exact replication.** Correct-nonce, unmodified-image signs decoded with the original key must reproduce all retained vote arrays, bits and match counts for the 24 images. Compare all 256 votes, not only binary decisions. Also reproduce wrong-key votes. A mismatch blocks the replication gate; investigate numerical/runtime differences before attributing it to leakage or silently allowing a tolerance. Record preprocessing/module/asset hashes and fp16 scaling diagnostics.
4. **Wrong nonce and coordinate controls.** Reuse each encoded latent without a second VAE pass. Query all four original nonces; the three foreign nonces produce 72 negative queries against their corresponding foreign reference messages. Separately roll raw latent signs by one column (`axis=3`, wrap) before dewhitening, then decode with the own nonce/key, producing 24 alignment controls. Do not roll by eight: repetition periodicity would make that a weak control. Test wrong key on the unchanged tensor as above. Predicted outcome is loss of message agreement for foreign nonces and alignment controls; a positive triggers investigation, not a refitted cutoff. These correlated finite controls do not estimate population FPR.
5. **Algebraic causality check.** Negate every retained sign before XOR, treating exact zero via the original `>0` predicate on the negated floating tensor. For nonzero coordinates, correct-key dewhitened-one votes should complement to `64-v`. Surface every exception attributable to zero values. Output message complements apart from ties must follow algebraically; comparison references must not alter any raw output bits. Re-evaluate emitted bits against the own reference and its complement in the evaluator. This catches overwritten/borrowed predictions, not every possible malicious implementation.
6. **Transport measurement.** In the evaluator only, reconstruct the initial fp16 marked noise from its documented seed and verify its existing self-check tensor hash. Compare raw retained image-latent signs with that sign mask, and compute cosine with its actual magnitudes. Repeat against the paired C0 initial Gaussian noise and three foreign initial noises. The C0-own-noise cosine can be positive even though its GS message agreement is near chance: ordinary generation can transport noise without an embedded code. Report all values with source clusters; add no acceptance threshold for these explanatory correlations.

P1 passes its implementation gate only if all 24 original/wrong-key vote arrays replicate exactly, all synthetic/algebra checks pass, and all 72 foreign-nonce plus 24 shifted-sign queries stay below 180. These are conservative engineering gates fixed now. Report each failed cell; do not summarize a partially completed probe as passed. If only an exact fp16 arithmetic comparison fails, retain it and diagnose a fresh fp32 repeat in a new version; do not change the scientific readout silently.

The readout never loads the reconstructed initial noise. Hash-valid source PNGs, the fresh data boundary and raw-sign retention are the main new evidence; the original 112 repeated conditions alone cannot supply it. If P1 fails a boundary check, repair the reader, rerun the frozen 112-condition assessment into a new directory, and preserve the original positive as an invalidated implementation result. If P1 passes, one later small crossed-message generation control can test causal flexibility: fixed prompt/magnitude seed with original versus complemented payload, both extracted against both messages. That fresh generation is not required to keep inspecting existing evidence, and is not silently included in P1.

## Conditional next candidate: registered content binding B2-R

If P1 passes, the next inexpensive content-binding candidate should **keep the initial-noise carrier and add an explicit post-generation enrollment record**. This is an amendment with extra side information, not the proposal's blind public-OwnerID detector or image-DCT-only extractor. Family A remains the closer blind dual-key attempt. Registering final generated content avoids a real circularity: deriving `Ks/Ki` from a first generated image, changing its initial-noise message, and claiming the second image has the same features has not been justified. Cryptographic hashes make small feature changes catastrophic; merely increasing message bits does not solve that loop.

The bounded B2-R screen reuses all four existing C1 clean outputs and their 14 channels; no new carrier enrollment, seed selection or repetition count is needed. An enrollment record contains schema/model/preprocessor hashes, public OwnerID, carrier nonce/key identifier and original 256-bit reference message, the **full normalized 512-dimensional CLIP feature** of the saved C1 image, its existing **32-bit DCT perceptual hash from the 8×8 hash transform**, and its exact RGB8 digest. Freeze each record before evaluating its transformed images. Define domain-separated, length-prefixed digests `Ks=H(version,OwnerID,E_bytes)` and `Ki=H(version,OwnerID,E_bytes,H32,message,nonce)` as record commitments. They are stored in the record, **not retroactively claimed to have been embedded in the noise**. The carried message links a suspect to the record.

For honest fixed enrollment, the detector takes suspect RGB8, the public owner and its finite record roster. One VAE pass yields signs; each roster nonce produces a message score. CLIP and DCT are recomputed from the suspect. This uses reference features and a message but no source RGB, prompt or diffusion inversion. For the first exploratory screen, freeze `message_matches >=180`, full-feature cosine **>=.90**, and hash Hamming distance **<=4/32**. The latter two are prospective operating choices, not optimized thresholds or established semantic equivalence. Store continuous cosine/distance and all record candidates; no winning projection seed, q32 substitution or per-attack tuning is permitted.

Per eligible record, the operational states are: all three predicates = `both_match`; carrier+semantic but failed instance hash = `semantic_only`; carrier present but failed semantics = `content_mismatch`; no eligible carrier = `unverified`. If multiple distinct records qualify, return an ambiguity flag rather than assigning a unique original/owner. These states describe content consistency, not causal regeneration/copy-paste. Multiple matches after VAE are permissible observations, not evidence the attack did not happen.

The four-record screen produces 112 image conditions × four record hypotheses = **448 scored queries**, with own-record clean/T3 positives, foreign-record and paired C0 negatives kept distinct. A score cache may reuse the one VAE/CLIP feature per image. It is a first content-association test, **not yet T4/T5 evidence**: four unrelated prompts do not provide controlled same-semantic distinct-instance pairs. T4 patch transfer, same-semantic T5 generation pairs and adaptive public re-embedding require their own frozen manifests before a B2-R package could contribute a complete M1 threat assessment. Passing these 448 queries only justifies that next bounded screen.

If record authenticity is required, sign the complete canonical record with a development signing key and pin its verification key to the development OwnerID roster. This authenticates an enrollment statement and blocks unauthorized record edits under a stated secret-key assumption; it does not establish legal identity or ownership. A bare public digest does not authenticate anything. Do not count forged-record rejection as empirical carrier FPR, and do not treat a signer creating a fresh competing record as impossible. Signature implementation is a separate amendment with exact algorithm/key handling, not an undocumented assumption of the first public-fixture screen.

Keeping full CLIP reference features outside the carrier preserves their geometry without pretending that a 256-bit channel carries a 512-float vector. The storage/enrollment cost must be reported. If the required deployment forbids enrollment records, B2-R is only a comparator; a blind dual-key initial-noise design remains open. Do not hide that limitation by calling reference information evaluator-only once it influences the final decision.

## Capacity, FPR and claim boundary

There are 16,384 sign coordinates and 256 message bits repeated 64 times. These are not 16,384 independent message bits, and high presence at a 180/256 agreement cutoff is not exact 256-bit recovery. Clean B-LW1 exact recovery is 2/4 and VAE exact recovery 1/4; strongest regeneration has none. An ECC layer or a smaller independently decoded payload would consume capacity and needs separate testing. Message presence against a known record can succeed despite those errors; exact cryptographic tags cannot simply be parsed from the noisy bits.

The prior conditional independent-chip null and its tie bias remain arithmetic references only. Correlated spatial signs, fixed public streams, repeated images and a roster search invalidate treating the 112 conditions as independent FPR trials. Public content-adaptive injection is outside a random-key/nonce null. Four successful prompt clusters have a one-sided 95% binomial lower bound of only about .473 under independent sampling; they cannot establish the .80 lower-bound objective or universal regeneration survival.

B-LW1-P1 passing would support **initial-noise message transport to an inversion-free terminal-VAE readout for this pinned synthetic configuration**. It would not establish photographic source preservation, strict DCT-only extraction, semantic/instance binding, authenticated ownership, all-strength/cross-model regeneration, or arbitrary bit capacity. It would also mean the initial-noise family has a promising unexhausted route: native inversion alone is no longer the only tried readout. No M1 family-exhaustion statement should ignore it.
