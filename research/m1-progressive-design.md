## Mechanism and exact scaling

Generate matched C0 and C1 with identical prompt and initial Gaussian noise seed1000..1003, 512x512, DDIM50steps, sampler eta0 and CFG7.5. Output is exclusively VAE decoding of the final denoised latent; no source-pixel residual, pixel mark, or compositing occurs.

Latent shape is `[1,4,64,64]`, in SD scaled latent units. Apply an orthonormal two-dimensional DCT to predicted clean channel0. Candidate coefficient band is inclusive `16 <= u+v <= 48`; choose128 distinct positions by ascending SHA256 of public `m1-progressive-public-v1:u:v`, split into16 consecutive groups of8 repetitions. Payload `1011010001101001` is public and predeclared. Target is alpha times sign; all unselected coefficients are unconstrained. Alpha in `[0.1,0.3,0.5]` is a target coefficient magnitude, not a bounded image perturbation norm.

For orthonormal DCT matrix B and plane x, F=B x B^T. Loss is `L=sum(M*(F-S)^2)/128`, and exact analytic gradient is `g=2*B^T*(M*(F-S))*B/128`. The paper's equation5 prints a squared norm while its prose calls this MSE. This candidate chooses normalization by the number of selected coefficients, not all4096 coefficients, and makes that departure explicit. Changing loss scaling without changing eta changes the experiment.

After computing ordinary CFG noise epsilon, estimate `x0=(zt-sqrt(1-a_t)*epsilon)/sqrt(a_t)`, correct channel0 by `x0_guided=x0-eta*g`, and recompute noise exactly as `(zt-sqrt(a_t)*x0_guided)/sqrt(1-a_t)`. Feed that noise to the explicit DDIM scheduler. Guidance eta is25 or100, distinct from sampler eta0. The one-step coefficient contraction has factor `1-2*eta/128`; eta100 can overshoot the target in a single step. The predeclared sweep evaluates this rather than tuning it retrospectively. Arithmetic for DCT and guidance is fp32; UNet/VAE are pinned fp16 inference.

The paper uses `t > T*r_start`, but its use of a start ratio leaves diffusion-time versus loop-index interpretation easy to confuse. Candidate interprets descending denoising time as the early high-noise half: guide indices0..24 of50, then stop guidance. Exact scheduler timesteps and guided indices are retained per generated image. Candidate guidance is applied **after CFG**, whereas paper describes a conditional prediction guidance followed by CFG. This is a material explicit implementation departure. No benefit is presumed.

## Native extractor and diagnostics

Reopen saved RGB8, preprocess with the pipeline image processor, encode VAE posterior mode, multiply by resolved VAE scaling factor, and DCT channel0. Signs at each eight-copy group use strict majority; four-four ties decode0. Native bit accuracy uses the fixed correct payload. A second hypothesis uses its bitwise complement as wrong payload. A direct image-domain diagnostic resizes saved RGB to64x64 Bicubic, computes BT.601 luminance normalized to[-1,1], and reads identical DCT positions. This diagnostic has no claim of matching the latent channel.

Threshold0.875 means at least14/16 correct bits and is **descriptive only**. In addition to the complementary wrong payload, predeclare64 wrong sixteen-bit payload hypotheses with domain-separated SHAKE256 inputs `m1-progressive-wrong-payload-v1:query:counter`; redraw only accidental equality with the true payload, independently of any image/score. Each extracted word reports all64 bit accuracies and count/64 exceeding the same frozen threshold. These are fixed pseudorandom queries, not64 independent negative images: all queries share an extracted word, and images/variants/attacks reuse hypotheses, so their outcomes are correlated. Four generated images and dependent repetitions do not validate population FPR or a cryptographic false-positive bound. Neither majority signs nor a public carrier authenticate a key, bind content, resolve semantic collision, or prevent public reembedding.
