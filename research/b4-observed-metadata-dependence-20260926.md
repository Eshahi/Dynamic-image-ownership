Follow A4 `sample-size.md`, A5 native input contract, research contract and scope guard. No final split, independent-N claim or science execution here.

`scripts/b4_metadata_dependence.py` consumes the exact pinned metadata bytes (2M rows) and joins all 14,000 acquired candidate image IDs to all fourteen hash-bound native-image inventories, including excluded rows. The same B3 prompt normalization is applied to **every** candidate prompt, not only eligible ones. Prompt, raw-byte and prompt/seed edges are transitive. Prompt-seed edges are subordinate to full prompt grouping; unrelated images do not become duplicate content merely because a random seed number matches. Private raw prompts and upstream producer hashes are not output. Known producer hashes receive a domain-separated private grouping fingerprint; the complete private graph stays ignored. Source metadata cannot establish legal identity or all real-world dependence.

The official [authors' schema](https://github.com/poloclub/diffusiondb/blob/main/README.md#metadata-schema) and pinned Parquet schema describe deleted accounts as `deleted_account`; the pinned actual frame has 134 literal `deleted_acount` values. Two initial strict parser attempts rejected those values before producing an output. The parser now accepts exactly the documented and observed sentinel spellings as **unknown identity**, not an actual producer. It does not accept arbitrary malformed users. Null/deleted rows are conservatively quarantined together when producer isolation is enabled; this is an uncertainty safeguard, not a claim that they share one true user.
