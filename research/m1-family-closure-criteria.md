## What may be closed

The user permits M1's negative alternative when at least three substantially different design families have been tried and a numerical report explains their shortfalls and measured limits. This is **not** a requirement to prove every possible latent watermark impossible. The appropriate conclusion is: “Within the declared models, data, extractors, quality limits and finite development program, these implemented families did not meet the stated conjunction.”

Use four separate statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Implemented and evaluated | A valid scientific run exists; this alone does not settle follow-up work. |
| Configuration stopped | The named fixed operator failed its prospective gate; a different operator in that family may remain promising. |
| Bounded family closed for this M1 package | The evidence and finite follow-up criteria below are met; remaining alternatives are explicitly outside the evaluated envelope. |
| Accepted or impossible | Neither follows from bounded closure. Acceptance needs the stated evidence; universal impossibility is not claimed. |

A family cannot be closed by a launch error, a deadline before any valid endpoint, a missing model, an incorrect implementation, or an unanswered positive mechanism result. Preserve these events, repair technical defects, and distinguish operational failure from measured scientific failure. A failed necessary condition can justify stopping a configuration before later attacks; those attacks remain **not evaluated**, never failures, successes or zero false positives.

The bounded envelope is the pinned SD1.5/VAE/CLIP stack; existing reserved development photographs at canonical512 resolution or explicitly separate generated prompt/seed blocks; local USD0 computation; no learned decoder training or new model family; fixed public-owner or explicitly enrolled comparator interfaces; and each version's frozen optimizer, attack schedule, quality reference and budgets. Pure decoding, source bypass, decoder dtype and any image-domain delivery are recorded separately. This scope is a development amendment, not fulfillment of the protected multi-domain/native-resolution study.
