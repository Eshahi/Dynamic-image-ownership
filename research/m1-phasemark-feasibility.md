# PhaseMark feasibility decision for M1

Decision version `m1-phasemark-feasibility-v1`, 2026-10-03; design escalation at **xhigh**. This is a development decision, made before any local PhaseMark carrier experiment. It authorizes a bounded implementation recommendation within the existing autonomous handoff; it records no human verdict, execution result or change to the protected proposal. The parent owns scheduling, implementation assignment and run provenance.

**Decision: test a small PhaseMark APM/IPS adaptation after the current GPU queue.** A direct phase carrier with VAE encoding at detection is a credible, inexpensive alternative to family A's optimized image-DCT objective and family C's progressive latent-DCT guidance. It does not require denoising optimization. Label it **family D, terminal-latent phase carrier**, with an explicitly amended extractor. A/B/C remain distinct candidates, but an unrun cheap alternative with this mechanism should not be called exhausted. D is not an initial-noise baseline and does not replace the planned Gaussian Shading comparison.

## Inspected evidence and implementation caveats

The [primary paper, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.13128v1), studies 128-bit phase marks on SD2.1-generated images. Its reported APM verification under two compression VAEs and diffusion attack is promising; those compression VAEs are not our SD1.5 decode/encode control. PCQ's reported PSNR 34.156 and the unmarked reconstruction value 36.4193 concern generated images, not the current photographic cohort. These numbers do not establish our quality target or a decoder optimum. The [paper card](literature/m1-primary-extension/phasemark-card.md) retains the exact claims and qualifications.

Use official code revision [`dfe42ad0449459e26fe1579957c6d05ccdee9b92`](https://github.com/thomas11809/PhaseMark/tree/dfe42ad0449459e26fe1579957c6d05ccdee9b92), not an invented mask. The retained [download receipt](literature/m1-primary-extension/phasemark-code/download-records.json) pins each source hash. Its [CC BY-NC 4.0 notice](https://github.com/thomas11809/PhaseMark/blob/dfe42ad0449459e26fe1579957c6d05ccdee9b92/LICENSE) permits a research adaptation with attribution and change disclosure. Keep the attribution and license with adapted functions. Do not import the upstream utility module wholesale: it initializes additional models and CUDA resources outside our asset controls.

Two inspected code issues must be corrected transparently in the adaptation:

1. Local `utils.py:505–513` subtracts and squares RGB uint8 arrays before taking MSE; this can wrap and understate error. `fast_try.py:231` uses that function. Our existing float PSNR, RGB SSIM and AlexNet LPIPS must measure saved/reopened RGB8 output. This is a defect in the inspected implementation, **not evidence that the paper tables were computed using that defect**.
2. `utils.py:658–682` calibrates `P(X > i)` but returns `i/L`; `fast_try.py:243–246` accepts bit accuracy `>= i/L`. Use the integer rule derived below. Preserve the upstream result only as an explicitly labeled reproduction diagnostic if needed; never call its inconsistent nominal cutoff our calibrated FPR.

Exact source identities: local `utils.py` SHA-256 `de23bef357e3524dde2cead837c0d08d112610ed652374e230c3d0621395178d`; `fast_try.py` SHA-256 `8867b19b732f85d919fc2f5f67f5555661157f550faba1bd3049619fa6b767fe`.

## Frozen carrier definition for the bounded pilot

The source recipe in [fast_try.py](https://github.com/thomas11809/PhaseMark/blob/dfe42ad0449459e26fe1579957c6d05ccdee9b92/fast_try.py) resolves the coordinate ambiguity: encode the source using the posterior mean, multiply by VAE scaling, then Fourier-transform the **central spatial latent crop** `[10:54,10:54]`. This is not cropping the center of a full-latent FFT. Use all four channels, 32 bits per channel, ordinary `fft2`/`ifft2`, and restore the modified crop into its source latent. Decode after undoing the scale. Detection repeats posterior-mean encoding, scaling, spatial cropping and FFT. Preserve this sequence in the adaptation. Use the pinned local SD1.5 VAE and canonical 512x512 RGB preprocessing; both differences from the published setup must be named. Pilot output uses the project's rounded RGB8 conversion, also an explicit adaptation.

Use the [upstream utility layout](https://github.com/thomas11809/PhaseMark/blob/dfe42ad0449459e26fe1579957c6d05ccdee9b92/utils.py) with `shape=(22,22), num_bits=32, block_size=2, r_min=10, r_max=18, axis_offset=1`. The returned rectangles index the unshifted 44x44 FFT. Preserve its radius ordering and greedy nonoverlap rule, including transposed pairs and final truncation; serialize the exact returned rectangles. APM writes magnitude-preserving phases `+pi/2` or `-pi/2`. IPS keeps row-major entries 1 and 3 as anchors, aligning entries 2 and 4 with their anchors or adding pi. Apply the upstream shifted Hermitian restoration before inverse FFT, preserving its authoritative half-plane. Use its native APM phase-sum and IPS cosine-difference decisions, ties to bit zero. PCQ's code supplies the previously unclear constellations: multiples of pi/2 versus those values plus pi/4. Do not add PCQ, SPS, mask searches or one-channel variants to this first pilot.

These are **128 message bits**, not 512 independent symbols: each 2x2 block carries one bit, and conjugate restoration adds no payload. IPS may tolerate a common phase rotation of a pair; frequency-dependent phase shifts from spatial translation do not generally cancel. APM and IPS are two modulation arms of one family, not two additional design families.

The distortion mechanism is explicit. For a coefficient `a exp(i theta)` changed to `a exp(i(theta+delta))`, squared complex displacement is `2 a^2 (1-cos(delta))`. Parseval converts the summed spectral displacement into crop-space latent energy with the chosen FFT normalization. A conjugate partner contributes corresponding energy too. Preserving magnitudes does not imply small latent displacement, and the decoder Jacobian can amplify it. Consequently this design cannot be assumed to solve the existing-photo reconstruction problem. Conversely, a poor finite source reconstruction endpoint is not proof that this carrier cannot work on generated images or a better fitted latent.

## Decision rule, controls and side information

Use a public 128-bit pilot string from the first 16 bytes of SHAKE256 over UTF-8 `M1-PhaseMark-pilot-v1|` followed by the owner string; byte bits are MSB first, bit one maps to +1. Fix owner `qim-pilot-owner-alpha` and wrong-owner controls beta/gamma/delta with the same prefix. This is a new balanced-looking deterministic test message, not the paper's repeated ASCII payload, a secret, or cryptographic authentication. Record every bit and the code revision. No outcome-driven message selection is permitted.

For a *hypothetical independent fair-bit null*, choose the smallest integer `k` with `sum(j=k..128, C(128,j))/2^128 <= alpha`, and accept when the observed number of matching bits is **at least k**. Exact enumeration gives:

| Declared search | Nominal per-comparison tail | Required matches | Exact fair-bit tail |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| One owner, one arm | .01 | 78/128 | .0083353671 |
| Four-owner roster, one arm | .0025 | 81/128 | .0016870925 |
| Four owners, either of two arms | .00125 | 82/128 | .0009312343 |

Freeze **82/128** for pilot go/no-go across the two arms and four-owner roster; retain scores at every threshold as descriptive columns, without selecting a favorable decision rule. At 77/128 the fair-bit tail is .0133675864, illustrating the upstream off-by-one issue. These are analytic reference bounds, **not established empirical FPRs**. Decoded blocks/channels may be dependent or biased, the fixed public strings are not fresh random trials, and adaptive forgery violates this null. Twelve controls cannot validate a 1% or cryptographic false-positive claim.

Detector side information is the public owner message, phase variant, complete mask and transform conventions, preprocessing, VAE architecture and exact weights. It gets the suspect image only; it does not get the original source, source latent, original phase, optimization target, paired clean image or a diffusion inversion. It returns extracted bits, soft block scores, match counts, wrong-owner counts and `pilot-present`/`pilot-absent`. **This pilot does not implement the proposal's three states.** It must not label absence as regeneration or presence as authenticity.

## Cheap falsification sequence and bounded execution recommendation

The unit is an image; report every image/arm and keep the fixed pilot denominator. Use only reserved development IDs **1675 and 4795**, in that order, with the source hashes from the reconstruction manifest. Do not inspect held-out images, change the cohort or select images by phase performance. Failed/missing outcomes remain in the inventory. This is exploratory screening, not a power calculation or population-level test.

Before GPU execution, write CPU tests for exact layout stability, nonoverlap, conjugate pairs, real inverse output, untouched latent exterior and 128/128 direct latent decoding for both variants on fixed nondegenerate synthetic latents. Test all-zero/all-one and three deterministic pseudorandom messages; test phase-wrap boundaries and zero-magnitude scores explicitly. Assert the integer-tail boundary and a float-PSNR example where uint8 arithmetic fails. Synthetic tests diagnose implementation fidelity, not image robustness. Keep upstream transforms and local metric corrections separately inspectable.

Then use one posterior-mean encode per source, two phase writes, and one pure decode per arm. Save matched C0 `D(E(source))` and C1, evaluate source-relative quality plus incremental C1-versus-C0 distortion, and extract after saved RGB8 conversion. Apply exactly one additional pinned SD1.5 VAE roundtrip to both C0 and C1 and extract again. Report raw 128-bit accuracies, corrected presence decisions, wrong-owner and C0 decisions, and all quality metrics. The source reconstruction and phase damage must stay distinguishable. No image-domain residual hybrid is part of this pilot.

The hypothesis survives this screen for an arm only if **both images** retain correct-owner presence after clean saving and the additional VAE cycle, with no wrong-owner match on either C1 and no roster match on either matched C0, before or after that cycle. Source quality is a separate required gate: PSNR >35 dB, SSIM >.9 and LPIPS <.1 on both clean C1 images. Passing the carrier gate while failing quality motivates a labeled generated-image or better-reconstruction diagnosis, not a claim that the photo method passed. Failure of both arms kills this exact posterior-mean/SD1.5 recipe for escalation, not the entire phase family. Revisit only for a recorded reason such as verified implementation discrepancy, changed source domain or improved reconstruction evidence.

If an arm survives, freeze it before expanding to all 12 sources and the existing paired T3 strength/seed conditions, T4 transfer operator and T5 pairs. Keep the other arm's failures. Do not assume the author's diffusion settings equal our strengths. Without a content-binding extension, T4/T5 can only characterize pilot transfer/false attribution; they cannot validate the proposed semantic-state detector.

Execution is local, USD 0, batch one, float32, no UNet, no downloads and no training. Require a unique retained output directory, pinned assets, source hashes, code/config commit, seed 0, all controls and elapsed/peak-memory receipts. Reserve at most 10 GiB GPU allocation, 16 GiB system RAM, 250 MiB new artifacts and 15 minutes wall time for the two-image pilot; these are conservative caps, not measured runtime claims. Run only when the parent releases the GPU. No experiment or executable PhaseMark implementation is produced by this decision file.

## Content binding remains the next design problem

PhaseMark offers a carrier, not the dual semantic/instance signature. With 128 raw bits, a 32-bit semantic sketch and 32-bit instance sketch leave 64 bits for pilot, owner separation and redundancy; there is no room to assume each component also receives the existing 320-chip redundancy. A 128-bit semantic sketch plus a 32-bit instance sketch already exceeds the raw capacity before error correction. Full CLIP's favorable AUC does not answer this channel budget. Four channel copies of a 32-bit payload improve possible reliability while reducing distinct information to 32 bits. Correlated errors can defeat the apparent repetition gain.

A public hash of `(semantic sketch, owner)` and `(semantic sketch, instance hash, owner)` is forgeable by anyone who can re-embed; it is not a keyed PRF or a digital signature. Hashing also destroys the metric needed for noisy semantic comparison unless the detector can recover or search an explicit finite code. A signature needs secret signing material and a robust way to canonicalize noisy content. Neither is supplied by the current public-owner proposal. An enrolled full-vector registry could preserve semantic geometry but would add registration and retrieval side information, requiring a separate documented amendment.

Therefore do not redesign semantic coding before the clean/VAE channel evidence. If D works, the next xhigh decision must allocate its measured reliable bit budget, define semantic and fragile instance decoding, state any enrollment helper data, and freeze how the resulting evidence maps to the qualified three states with abstention. A robust public pilot by itself identifies neither regeneration history nor copy-paste causality. M1 may count D as tried only after retained local execution; this document is not an additional failed family or evidence of global exhaustion.
