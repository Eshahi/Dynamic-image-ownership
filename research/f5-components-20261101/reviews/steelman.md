## Three genuinely missed options

### STEELMAN-O01 — Joint four-symbol decoding of q and h from the existing second carrier

SOURCE: The instance table already has four ten-chip patterns per segment: `Wi[j][q_bit][h_bit]` (`scripts/revised_watermark_v5.py:352–360,369–380`). The current F5 reader chooses `q_bound` from the semantic route or suspect q and then offers only the two h alternatives to `_key_test` (`scripts/f5_latent_codec.py:300–304`). Thus the physical instance pattern depends on q, but the present reader does not independently decode q from that carrier.

DERIVED/JUDGMENT — mechanism: Score all four existing symbols per segment and produce an independently decoded pair `(q_i,h_i)` from the second carrier, with full joint-word ambiguity and content consistency reported. An unrestricted maximum remains computationally separable: 32 local four-way choices rather than binary choices. The statistical search, however, is over up to `4^32 = 2^64` joint words, not `2^32`; a restricted acceptance set needs its own proof. Cheap arithmetic does not make that multiplicity free. No particular score cutoff, binding radius, correction formula or fusion policy is proposed here.

JUDGMENT — why it is new: K03-O3 rewrites redundant semantic information across carriers and may sacrifice h allocation; this option first asks what q information the existing four-symbol table already transports, without rewriting. K08-O1 and K09-O2 union existing routes. Fixed B still inherits the existing q-conditioned binary instance decoder. K10-O3 checks contradictions in available outputs; it does not recover the omitted q symbol likelihoods. These are distinct observations, not names for the same option (`OPTIONS.md`, register; component D sections).

JUDGMENT — why current kills do not cover it: O/O_U's N3 event depends on recomputed binding and the existing conditional table. B's later failure shows that decoded h consistency is insufficient, not that independently extracted q contains no useful contradictory information. The existing physical second-tier found counts 52/60 and 30/60 motivate measurement but are neither joint-q/h recovery counts nor a performance ceiling (`components/K08-fragile-tier.md:87,108–110`). Joint decoding could fail completely once the extra alternatives, codeword proximity and h instability are considered.

JUDGMENT — cheapest decisive research test and kill: First audit all four symbol distances and the exact joint-score algebra without claiming efficacy. Before examining a new fresh evaluation cohort, specify the allowable joint content set, complete null budget and a diagnostic requiring recovered q and h to be compared with their separately declared intended targets. Use existing final-writer metadata only where those targets are actually known; never substitute clean-source h for a post-writer target. Kill the research priority if joint q information supplies no improvement over the q-conditioned baseline at the same complete error budget. Any subsequent deployable endpoint also fails on no foreign T3 gain, any retained SD1.5 .1/.2 C loss, or any new quality-eligible T4/T5 acceptance. Those criteria need a new preregistration; they are not an executable protocol in this review.

JUDGMENT — cost, scope and limits: No writer distortion or new model is inherently required for the initial reader experiment. Full four-symbol scores/projections are needed; existing B summaries alone cannot reconstruct them. Arithmetic roughly doubles the symbol comparisons within the instance decoder, not total reader latency; pixel/feature extraction remains the likely cost and is unmeasured for this task. Side information remains the declared profile, owner, VAE and CLIP for a blind deployed reader; original q/h are diagnostic-only references. No descriptor replacement, external baseline or E3 change is proposed. Same-owner residuals can transport both q and h, and public adaptive attackers can exploit the public table: no authentication or general transplant guarantee follows. **Priority: highest of these missed options, deferred; no B rescue.**

### STEELMAN-O02 — Test whether semantic-code concentration originates in a removable common feature direction

SOURCE: G2-C reports EVALUATE q mean distance 10.857471 and 143/435 pairs within nine bits, versus h mean 16.022989 and 3/435 within nine (`results/g2-c/summary.csv`; `GATE2-SYNTHESIS.md`, G2-C). These are dependent cohort observations, not an entropy estimate. The current q uses signs of fixed Rademacher projections of an uncentered unit feature vector (`scripts/f5_latent_codec.py:115–128`).

DERIVED/JUDGMENT — mechanism: Diagnose whether a common feature component or a small correlated subspace contributes to q concentration. If so, a future candidate could apply a fixed, regularized centering/projection transform to the existing seven-view CLIP feature before q construction. A minimal candidate is a fixed center; high-dimensional full whitening is not justified by thirty design sources. The transform and compatibility statistic must be specified jointly before new marks. Balanced individual bits would not establish independent bits, high min-entropy or adaptive collision resistance.

JUDGMENT — why it is new: K01-O2 changes spatial pooling; K01-O3 trains an instance-aware representation. K02-O1 calibrates the current q's compatibility without changing its bits; K02-O3 changes the random row distribution to Gaussian. None explicitly diagnoses and removes a common component before the existing projection. Changing row distribution alone preserves angular proximity in expectation and need not address concentrated source geometry (`components/K01-semantic-descriptor.md:72–85`; `components/K02-content-code.md:77–87`).

JUDGMENT — why current kills do not cover it: The 1/207 fixed-read binding headroom and TK2's small approximation-calibration improvement concern unchanged codes and current transport. They cannot determine whether a rewritten q has a better stability/specificity tradeoff. Conversely, G2-C does not establish that centering helps: the common component may be the stable content information, and its removal could magnify regeneration drift. Existing q concentration is motivation for diagnosis, not evidence of an improvement.

This unmarked-feature screen cannot promote a watermark. A surviving transform requires new consistently written marks, fixed complete thresholds, equal final quality and fresh T3/T4/T5 evaluation; no gain, low-strength regression or new eligible negative kills that candidate.

JUDGMENT — cost, scope and limits: Descriptor extraction may be reused only if the required features actually exist; no runtime is estimated here. A center adds linear arithmetic and public calibration parameters; low-rank transforms add compute and fitting-data/version side information. It changes q, so old marks cannot serve as its watermark experiment; writing and visibility checks are real costs. It keeps h and the CLIP encoder unchanged and is proposed solely as semantic-code geometry research. If reinterpreted as a new reference-free instance descriptor or as replacing h, label it **REOPENS-CLOSED-LINE** and require a judge decision before implementation. No reopening is assumed. ### STEELMAN-O03 — Host-covariance normalization within a tier, after the existing chip projection

SOURCE: The latent carrier uses radial frequency weights and chip-energy normalization (`scripts/f5_latent_codec.py:58–85`); the reader then normalizes all 320 chip projections by one Euclidean norm and scores words against them (`scripts/revised_watermark_v5.py:1178–1199`). The constant named `WHITENING` controls a radial power, not an estimated cross-chip background covariance. K04-O3 rebalances physical bands, K07-O3 allocates writer strength, and K09-O3 combines calibrated evidence across tiers; none is this within-tier reader transformation.

DERIVED/JUDGMENT — mechanism: Use a predeclared bounded diagonal or low-rank/shrinkage transformation of the 320-dimensional observation to downweight reproducible host variability before matched scoring. Fit it from appropriate unmarked calibration data, without using evaluation marked outcomes, and freeze it. The simplest baseline is the current norm. This asks whether the existing correlation statistic wastes surviving signal on high-variance observation directions; it neither adds signal nor changes the writer's frequency support.

DERIVED/JUDGMENT — validity prerequisite: Under an honest-null random-pattern model, a transformed normalized observation can still be conditioned upon only if its construction is independent of the tested table-pattern randomness. The carrier layout and calibration dependence must be audited domain by domain. Learning weights from marked examples generated with the same table can break that premise. An arbitrary covariance score cannot simply inherit the old threshold, error-rate correction or content-bound acceptance-set proof. Null validity is a prerequisite, not a quantity estimated to 1e-6 from a small development cohort.

JUDGMENT — why current kills do not cover it: Exact r3 and B alter search/binding while retaining the observation statistic. T3's scalar carrier-response summaries and T4's final-margin association do not test whether structured background variability can be suppressed by a different observation weighting. Earlier narrow-band H1 likewise changed a physical writer configuration. None measures this reader-only intervention. At the same time, normalization can amplify noise or transferable signal, so safety is not implied by a larger marked/unmarked score gap.

JUDGMENT — cheapest decisive research test and kill: First determine whether reproducible, safely estimable chip heterogeneity exists on a fresh calibration/report split; with few sources, reject an unconstrained 320-dimensional covariance fit. A later fixed reader comparison needs a complete null proof and frozen binding interpretation before output, plus fresh T3 and operational T4/T5 panels. Kill before performance testing if table independence or normalization cannot be established; otherwise kill on no foreign benefit at the complete budget, any retained low-strength regression, or any new eligible T4/T5 acceptance. Do not refit weights on failed evaluation cases.

JUDGMENT — cost, scope and limits: Reader-only weighting creates no new visual artifact and needs no new encoder, secret, source-image lookup or physical watermark. It adds public calibration coefficients and their training-data/version provenance; diagonal scoring is linear, dense scoring quadratic in 320, with extraction and total latency unmeasured. Additional raw chip vectors, absent from aggregate endpoint tables, are required. T3 improvement and safe recoverable counts are UNKNOWN. T4 residual transfers contain genuine pattern energy and public adaptive attacks violate an honest-null independence premise regardless of calibration. No closed descriptor or E3 line is reopened. **Priority: conditional on a cheap identifiability screen; deferred.**
