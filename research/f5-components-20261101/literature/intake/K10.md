### J02 — Ozkaya2025Dual (Q1J, V02)

**Citation:** Huseyin Ozkaya, Veysel Aslantas. “A Novel Self-Embedded Dual Watermarking Method for Image Tamper Detection and Recovery.” *Arabian Journal for Science and Engineering* 50, 19367–19389, 2025. DOI [10.1007/s13369-025-10047-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13369-025-10047-7). Published 2025-04-27.

**SOURCE — inspected:** PDF §§3–4, pp.4–8,10–12,18–19; Eqs.(5)–(9), Tables1–2, collage Fig.12. Each 2×2 DWT block carries five recovery and three authentication bits. ### J05 — Wang2025EMerging (Q1J, V05)

**Citation:** Ruodu Wang. “The only admissible way of merging arbitrary e-values.” *Biometrika* 112(2), asaf020, 2025. DOI [10.1093/biomet/asaf020](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/asaf020); [publisher](https://academic.oup.com/biomet/article/112/2/asaf020/8086785). Published 2025-03-17.

**SOURCE — inspected:** author pp.2–4, proof structure, §5 pp.8–9; publisher Theorem1. For arbitrary dependent e-values, admissible increasing merging functions are fixed weighted averages of the inputs and constant1, with nonnegative weights summing to1. §5 shows additional joint-distribution assumptions can allow other mergers. This is a theorem paper; watermark experiments, image-quality metrics and T3/T4/T5 datasets are **not applicable**, not omitted successful tests.

**DERIVED:** A fixed convex mixture preserves expectation≤1 when every branch is valid under the **same** null. **JUDGMENT:** Directly relevant to a union/fusion decision, with no promise of increased power. A presence-null guarantee does not become a T4/T5 ownership-null guarantee. Required side information is the null model/calibration and any dependence assumptions; computational cost of arithmetic fusion is small, calibration cost unknown. Confidence: high theorem applicability conditional on assumptions, no validated F5 inputs yet.

## Adversarial synthesis for K10

**SOURCE — frozen structure:** [K10 dossier](../../components/K10-binding-decision.md) A/B and [Gate 1 ruling](../../gates/GATE-1-RULING.md). C=semantic read AND content_match; Q=semantic read AND exact original q; J=Q AND content_match. J does not require instance H. F=`both_match` can include recomputation. Recomputed-only presence gets distance0 and automatic content match, contributing S but not C/J. Unchecked status ranks with match. Instance-table choice normally uses q_now; a found nonmatching semantic branch changes that choice, and instance status inherits the worse semantic status. These are coupled decisions, not independent votes.

**DERIVED — absent semantic branch is the decisive case.** An instance-only read may be useful detection evidence, but its compatibility with a table chosen using the current image is not automatically an independent semantic-content check. A recomputed candidate satisfying its own construction is not new binding evidence. Before any new acceptance endpoint, enumerate decoded/recomputed route, candidate origin, checked/unchecked status, q/h comparisons, conflicts and selection order. Preserve the frozen C/J alongside it. “Semantic absent” is neither proof of mismatch nor permission to map untested binding to authentic.

**SOURCE — Gate 1 N1 source-majority counts /60:** SDXL .1/.2/.4 second-tier detections52/30/5, second-tier content matches32/12/3; C=0/0/0 and the reported two-tier content union32/12/3. K3 .2 C5 and content-union16. These are **exploratory existing-rule outputs**. N2's512-vs1024 resolution confound prevents attributing the contrast solely to independently trained encoders. Zero either-tier detections across1254 C0 and1254 wrong-owner calls is not a demonstrated1e-6 error rate, and it does not cover adaptive T4/T5.

**DERIVED — the union must have a literal predicate and null.** One candidate definition is U=(eligible semantic presence AND an actual semantic binding check) OR (eligible instance presence AND an actual instance binding check), with conflict/unchecked handling fixed before evaluation. It is a new endpoint; it is not C renamed. Apply the per-call OR before the declared source aggregation. OR of source-majority endpoints need not equal source-majority of per-call OR.

For elementary acceptance events E_r with valid marginal null bounds alpha_r, `P0(any E_r) <= sum(alpha_r)` regardless of dependence. This is the elementary union bound, not a result measured on F5. Enumerate every adaptively selectable route/candidate/owner; do not charge a search twice if its existing bound already covers the same maximum. Conversely, do not omit a new candidate search. A fixed e-mixture is an alternative only after J05's marginal-validity conditions hold. Neither device supplies a content-binding null from a presence-only null.

**JUDGMENT — three different claims require three different negatives.** (1) Mark presence: C0 and wrong-owner response. (2) Content consistency: genuinely different, near-sibling and modified content at the declared tolerance. (3) Authorized attribution: valid-looking transferred/replayed marks under the declared attacker/key access. T01–T03 separate detection and tamper evidence; J01–J03 show that nominal “authentication bits” can have much weaker content specificity than their count suggests. X01 shows why even cryptographic validity does not remove the target-image comparison. None supplies measured joint F5 T3/T4/T5 safety for a second-tier union.

**SOURCE — decision-only ceiling:** K10's restricted oracle has stage2-union C gains +1 atSD1.5.2, +8 at.4, +1 atSDXL.1, +0 atSDXL.2/.4, +2 atK3.2; J gains +2 only atSD1.5.4 in those cells. Other studies retain their separate denominators. **DERIVED:** The N1 union32/12/3 is potentially useful under a new detection endpoint but cannot be cited as recovered semantic C/J. Any joint strict-quality/T4/T5-safe improvement ceiling remains UNKNOWN.
