## Inspected paper cards

### K04-J01 — MarkPlugger

**Identity.** Guokai Zhang, Lanjun Wang, Yuting Su, An-An Liu. “MarkPlugger: Generalizable Watermark Framework for Latent Diffusion Models Without Retraining.” 2025, IEEE Transactions on Multimedia; Q1J. DOI 10.1109/TMM.2025.3565960. Citation key zhang2025markplugger. Local artifact J01 above; [inspected author HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.05607v2).

**Inspection locators.** §§IV-A/B and V-A–I; local PDF p.4 equations 6–7, p.6 Table I, p.8 Table III and Figure 8/injection-failure discussion. Metadata/final-version limitation is in the venue table.

[SOURCE: §IV, Eqs.6–7; §V, Table I; Figure 8] A trained watermark encoder adds a one-channel signal to the final denoised latent; a learned pixel decoder reads it, with the diffusion model/VAE frozen. SD1.5/COCO means include PSNR 36.97 dB, SSIM .9446 and LPIPS .0424. The SDXL means are 36.83, .9402 and .0423. Figure 8 captions include PSNR 34.97 and 34.53 examples. Testing covers several generator deployments and six ordinary corruptions. It does not show a marked SD1.5 image regenerated through an independently trained foreign latent encoder. No formal human visibility verdict or T4/T5 experiment was located. Reader side information is trained decoder weights; writing requires latent access, learned encoder and the generator pipeline. Normalized correlation is not the F5 bound ownership decision.

[DERIVED: Table I means versus Figure 8 examples and BRIEF quality gate] A mean above 35 dB does not certify every-image strict quality; the illustrated values are below that PSNR cutoff. Deployment on SDXL does not establish resistance to an SDXL attacker.

[JUDGMENT—PROPOSED] **Supporting:** allocation to selected latent channels can yield favorable average fidelity without retraining the generator. **Conflicting/limiting:** generation-time access, trained readout and missing T3/T4/T5 make it weak evidence for the F5 post-hoc carrier. Transfer only the hypothesis that channel allocation matters. The strongest failure here is the lack of independent-encoder regeneration evidence, compounded by the stricter F5 quality requirement. No direct replacement of the DCT annulus is justified.

### K04-J02 — Latent Watermark

**Identity.** Zheling Meng, Bo Peng, Jing Dong. “Latent Watermark: Inject and Detect Watermarks in Latent Diffusion Space.” 2025, IEEE Transactions on Multimedia; Q1J. DOI 10.1109/TMM.2025.3535300. Citation key meng2025latentwatermark. Local artifact J02 above; [inspected author HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.00230v2). The 2024 author version supplies the claims below, not a claim that every number is unchanged in the final journal text.

**Inspection locators.** §§3.2–3.3, equations 2–4, §§4.1.3–4.1.6 and 4.3.2; local PDF pp.7–8 evaluation setup/Table 1, p.9 Table 2, p.11 Table 3, p.12 Table 4 and limitations.

[SOURCE: methods; Tables 1–4] A learned coupler embeds at the final latent, followed by VAE-based reading and learned decoupling. Channel 0 is selected. COCO/64-bit SD2.1 regeneration gives bit accuracy 99.19% and TPR 100% at a theoretically selected 1% FPR. Table 3 reports reconstructive bit accuracy 98.19% for channel 0; spreading over all four channels loses 2.00 percentage points. Table 1 gives COCO/64-bit SSIM .95; it does not establish F5's joint PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS gate. The threshold derivation assumes independent unbiased bits; Table 4's FPR units are ambiguous: values around .0080 and theoretical .0084 appear under a percent caption. Reader side information includes the VAE and learned extractor; the experiment uses fixed messages. No T4/T5 or formal human visibility test was located. SD1.4-to-SD2.1 regeneration is tested, but independently trained autoencoders are not established.

[DERIVED: Table 3] More occupied channels did not automatically improve this trained method. The FPR table must not be converted into “.008% demonstrated F5 false positives”; an ambiguous unit and a method-specific bit-null assumption cannot validate another detector.

[JUDGMENT—PROPOSED] **Supporting:** channel selection and reconstructive attacks are materially relevant. **Conflicting:** the all-channel ablation opposes a naive diversity-is-always-better story. The strongest mismatch is learned generation-time embedding/readout with no demonstrated foreign independently trained encoder. Treat it as a reason to test controlled carrier allocation, not as evidence that F5 should occupy all four channels or that its public null is certified.

### K04-T01 — GaussMarker

**Identity.** Kecen Li, Zhicong Huang, Xinwen Hou, Cheng Hong. “GaussMarker: Robust Dual-Domain Watermark for Diffusion Models.” 2025, ICML; TOP. Proceedings li25ae, PMLR 267:34688–34701. Citation key li2025gaussmarker. Local artifact T01 above; [inspected author HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.11444v1). Reserved assignment: **K04 + K08**, maximum two components.

**Inspection locators.** §§3.1–3.2.3, 4.1–4.4, appendices B.2/B.4/C/D.2/D.4–D.6; PDF p.4 fused scoring, p.6 Tables 1–2/setup, p.7 Tables 3–4, p.13 D.2–D.3, p.14 Tables 8–9/radius ablation.

[SOURCE: method; Tables 2–4; D.5] Spatial sign-coded bits and a low-frequency Fourier ring share initial Gaussian noise. An inversion/restoration pipeline and a learned MLP combine scores. Its fusion training uses 100 positive and 100 negative examples. Under ordinary distortions, Table 4's mean TPR at 1% FPR changes from .791 spatial and .770 frequency to .946 dual-domain without restoration, and .997 full. Under diffusion regeneration, Table 3 instead reports .667/.853/.744 for SD2.1/SD2.0/SD1.4. The regenerator is a pretrained ImageNet pixel diffusion model; this is not an independently trained latent-encoder test. Experiments use 512-square generation. Larger frequency radii disturb spatial sign structure. Quality uses generation FID/CLIP; Table 2 mean FID is 24.85 versus 24.89 unmarked. No formal human, T4 or T5 test was located. Reading requires keys/patterns, generator inversion, learned restoration and fusion.

[DERIVED: Tables 3–4] The ordinary-distortion complementarity result and the diffusion result are different endpoints; .997 must not be presented as its diffusion-regeneration TPR. A pixel diffusion attacker is evidence about regeneration generally but cannot isolate latent encoder transfer. There is no matched 512/1024 family-resolution factorial.

[JUDGMENT—PROPOSED] **Supporting:** a direct precedent for combining complementary carriers and controlling their mutual interference. **Conflicting:** substantial residual diffusion failures and radius interference prevent a free-additional-carrier argument. The strongest mismatch is generation-time/inversion-heavy detection instead of F5's post-hoc writer and lightweight reader. Prioritize a joint K4/K5/K8 hypothesis, with K9 calibrating the entire learned or deterministic fusion; do not import the MLP threshold or assume independent errors.

### K04-T02 — Fourier Integrity

**Identity.** Sung Ju Lee, Nam Ik Cho. “Semantic Watermarking Reinvented: Enhancing Robustness and Generation Quality with Fourier Integrity.” 2025, ICCV; TOP, pp.18759–18769. Stable CVF URL in the venue table; no DOI verified. Citation key lee2025fourierintegrity. Local artifact T02 above; [inspected author HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07647v1).

**Inspection locators.** §§3.2, 4.1–4.3, 5.1; local PDF p.3 symmetry argument, p.5 Table 1/settings, p.6 Table 2/attacker description; supplementary §8.4–8.6, PDF pp.14–15 Tables 8–10. PDF page numbers here include the supplement even where printed numbering restarts.

[SOURCE: §3.2; Tables 1/9/10; §5.1] Hermitian symmetry preserves Fourier watermark information when converting to real spatial noise. HSTR combines a symmetric ring with a centered region; HSQR carries QR-structured information. Table 1 COCO diffusion verification TPR is .997 for HSTR and 1.000 for HSQR. Supplement Table 10 increases attack noise from 60 to 180: HSTR verification falls .997 to .980 but identification falls .971 to .781; HSQR identification falls .999 to .997. Tests use SD2.1-base, 512-square generation and a Zhao-style diffusion attack; the attack's independently trained foreign latent encoder is not established. No resolution factorial or formal human/T4/T5 assessment was located. Side information includes keys/reference watermark, generator and inversion. FID/CLIP measure generated-image quality. Table 9's diffusion PSNR 31.224, SSIM .795 and LPIPS .109 are attack-output quality, not clean watermark fidelity.

[DERIVED: Fourier method versus K04 implementation] F5's orthonormal real DCT does not discard the imaginary part of an inverse DFT. Therefore this paper's specific repair is not a direct patch to F5. Identification, detection and content-bound ownership are also distinct endpoints.

[JUDGMENT—PROPOSED] **Supporting:** representation integrity and domain coupling can dominate nominal carrier shape. **Conflicting:** strong detection can coexist with weaker identification under stronger regeneration. The strongest reason the technique fails as a direct K04 improvement is that its identified numerical defect is absent from F5's real DCT path. Inspect representation conventions and channel allocation; do not switch to Fourier carriers solely on these results.

### K04-T03 — ZoDiac

**Identity.** Lijun Zhang, Xiao Liu, Antoni Viros Martin, Cindy Xiong Bearfield, Yuriy Brun, Hui Guan. “Attack-Resilient Image Watermarking Using Stable Diffusion.” 2024, NeurIPS 37; TOP. DOI 10.52202/079017-1215. Citation key zhang2024zodiac. Local artifact T03 above, an author-hosted final conference PDF. Assigned K04; possible second component K05 remains subject to root reconciliation.

**Inspection locators.** Full method and experiment sections, local PDF pp.3–9: p.4 Algorithm 1, p.5 §3.2/equations 3–6, p.6 §3.2.3/equations 7–8 and §3.3 detection statistic, p.7 Table 1/setup/attacks, p.8 evaluation, p.9 Tables 2–3.

[SOURCE: §§3.2–3.3; Tables 1–3] ZoDiac marks existing images: inversion initializes a latent, a Fourier ring occupies the last channel, optimization passes through frozen diffusion, and adaptive RGB blending targets SSIM at least .92. Reading uses inversion and a keyed reference with a noncentral-chi-square statistic. COCO means in Table 1 are PSNR 29.41, SSIM .92 and LPIPS .09; Zhao-style regeneration detection is .988 at threshold .9. Table 3 pairs that threshold with FPR .062; threshold .99 gives FPR .004 and detection .938. Table 2's zero/one/50 denoising-step variants give regeneration detection .712/.970/.988. The 60-step regeneration evaluation does not establish the independently trained foreign latent-encoder condition. Changing the watermarking model to SDXL is a different comparison. No formal human/T4/T5 assessment was located. Side information is the inversion model plus watermark key/mask; no original image is needed for detection, while writing uses it.

[DERIVED: Table 1 and BRIEF quality gate; Table 3] Mean PSNR 29.41 is well below the strict 35 dB requirement. The .988 detection and .004 FPR belong to different thresholds and cannot be reported together. A fitted/noncentral statistic does not prove F5's public null assumptions.

[JUDGMENT—PROPOSED] **Supporting:** the closest post-hoc carrier-optimization precedent among these five. **Conflicting:** its displayed quality/robustness and FPR/TPR tradeoffs expose the exact constraints a proposed carrier change must respect. The strongest failure here is the quality gap, followed by the heavier reader and missing T4/T5 binding evidence. This is literature evidence only; it does not authorize re-opening a closed external-baseline experiment line.
