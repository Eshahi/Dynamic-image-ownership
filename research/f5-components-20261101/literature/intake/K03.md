Let `b` be the one payload bit, and `z` the modulo-two sum of the ten known bits. Then the physically transmitted parity is

`p(b,z) = (b XOR z, b, b, b)`.

## 7. Capacity and ECC accounting against 32 bits / 320 chips

**DERIVED, idealized codebook calculation.** F5's nominal semantic payload density is 32/320=0.1 bit per chip. It is a labeling rate, not measured channel capacity. If the two length-ten patterns are independently uniform, their Hamming distance `D_j` is Binomial(10,1/2), with mean 5 and collision probability 1/1024. Under independent segments, the chance that at least one of 32 pairs collides is `1-(1023/1024)^32`, approximately 3.08%. This is a code-generation probability, **not an observed defect rate for retained owner keys**. Distinct q strings can become indistinguishable if a segment collides; otherwise messages differing in one bit expose the weakest segment. Thus a nominal 2^32 labels is not automatically 2^32 distinguishable, equally robust codewords.

**DERIVED.** Antipodal length-ten pairs have distance ten, but that does not imply twice F5's usable robustness: projection normalization, covariance, image-writing costs, bit dependencies and the damage distribution intervene. A balanced pattern (five signs of each polarity) would also remove segment means, but the resulting writer feasibility remains unmeasured.

**DERIVED.** A proposed outer polar `(N=64,K=40)` with 32 payload bits plus CRC8, mapped to five chips per coded bit, fits the same 320-chip budget. Its outer information rate is 40/64; useful payload remains 32/320. At fixed chip amplitude in an ideal independent-coordinate model, each transmitted coded bit has half the energy of a ten-chip symbol, roughly 3 dB less. ECC must overcome that loss as well as correlated errors; coding is not free redundancy. CRC is not authentication. Neither the polar paper's N=256 experiments nor PRC's 16,384 latent coordinates validate this N=64 proposal. PRC uses 51.2 times as many coordinates as F5, and its randomization/detection overhead must also be paid.

**DERIVED / scope boundary.** Tolerating an r-bit difference from the observed content fingerprint is a composite hypothesis, not ordinary successful recovery of an enrolled message. Its Hamming-ball multiplicity is `M(r)=sum_{i=0}^r C(32,i)`; for example M(3)=5,489. An exact r>0 statistic must search the **full segmented projection**, including pattern-dependent normalization, with its multiplicity and content-dependent null calibrated. Sorting positive segment increments is exact only for an actually additive objective; a normalized score can violate that condition. Public q(x), owner comparisons and reuse of image features prohibit an unqualified 2^-32 false-positive claim. Root's projection analysis, not a heuristic shortcut here, governs any exact implementation.

**JUDGMENT.** These distinctions favor investigating code geometry and calibrated soft evidence before claiming an ECC breakthrough. Conditional diagnostic gains at SD1.5 .4 and K3 .2 identify possible room within those retained analyses. They neither predict a new code's gain nor bound a new writer's attainable result. No inspected paper establishes that changing coding alone restores semantic C under foreign-family regeneration.
