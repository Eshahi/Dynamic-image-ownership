## K12-J02 — Safe testing

**Citation/class.** Peter Grünwald, Rianne de Heide, and Wouter M. Koolen. “Safe testing.” *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series B: Statistical Methodology* 86(5):1091–1128, 2024; online 7 March 2024. DOI [10.1093/jrsssb/qkae011](https://doi.org/10.1093/jrsssb/qkae011). [Final publisher record](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssb/article/86/5/1091/7623686); [author version](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.07801). Proposed **Q1J**, V-Q1; PDF J02 above. Second assignment after K09, independently re-inspected.

**Inspected.** Author-PDF pp1–29 (definitions, propositions, construction, optional continuation and power comparisons); Appendix A.1 pp38–39 and B.1/B.3/B.4 pp44–47. Remaining detailed growth/minimax proofs are not invoked. Final publication metadata was separately verified; this is not a claim to have byte-compared the final journal PDF.

**SOURCE — claim/mechanism.** A nonnegative e-variable satisfies E_P[E] ≤1 under every P in the null. Proposition 1 p3 gives rejection at E≥1/α by Markov's inequality. Definition 1 p5 and Proposition 2/Corollary 1 pp6–7 require conditional validity relative to the permitted past information; sequential products then form a test supermartingale and support optional stopping/continuation. The paper expressly warns on p8 against multiplying evidence after reusing old data as if it were a fresh study. Composite-null Bayes factors are not automatically safe; the choice of null and construction matter.

**SOURCE — numbers and boundaries.** §6 p27 compares a particular Gaussian location model at 80% power: approximate fixed-sample-size factors of 2.2 at α=.05 and 1.7 at α=.01. Figure 4 p28 compares stopping behavior and fixed maxima. These are model-specific statistical efficiency comparisons, not F5 sample requirements or effect sizes. §5 pp21–26 distinguishes optional stopping from optional continuation and the permitted filtration; a special t-test construction cannot be transferred while ignoring what information drives continuation. Appendix B.1's treatment of external information does not turn repeated measurements of one image into new independent evidence.

**Threat/quality/independent encoders.** No watermark attacks, image dataset, T3/T4/T5 performance, independent-encoder regeneration, strict image-quality test or human evaluation: all **MISSING / not the paper's subject**. Statistical null distributions and access to past results are the relevant assumptions; neither adaptive attackers nor arbitrary image dependence are covered by invoking the word “safe.”

**JUDGMENT — concrete K12 transfer and failure.** Repeatedly choosing a new tier, seed, threshold or candidate after seeing the same development sources does not create independent replication. Fix the current batch comparison, label exploratory continuation, and reserve any future e-process proposal for a defensible conditional construction on appropriate data units. Correlated seed/owner/tier calls from the same image cannot simply be multiplied. An e-value alone cannot establish 10⁻⁶ false acceptance under an adversarial null, repair post-hoc selection, or improve F5 TPR. Current K12 should document the unit and continuation rule rather than retrofit a sophisticated statistic to already inspected outcomes.

## K12-J03 — Confidence on the focal

**Citation/class.** Ying Jin and Zhimei Ren. “Confidence on the focal: conformal prediction with selection-conditional coverage.” *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series B: Statistical Methodology* 87(4):1239–1259, 2025; online 15 April 2025. DOI [10.1093/jrsssb/qkaf016](https://doi.org/10.1093/jrsssb/qkaf016). [Final publisher record](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssb/article/87/4/1239/8113856); [author version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03868). Proposed **Q1J**, V-Q1; PDF J03 above. This is the final K09 J05 and second component assignment. The earlier Biometrika lead is **not** this paper and is not counted.

**Inspected.** Author-PDF pp1–18 (all main methods and experiments); Appendix C.1 p24, C.3/C.4 pp27–29, C.7 p32, D.1–D.4 pp32–36, E.4/E.5 pp47–48. Duplicated plot labels in PDF extraction were removed only for reading; no graph values were digitized. Remaining specialized algorithm proofs are not invoked.

**SOURCE — claim/mechanism.** Marginal conformal coverage can fail on a subset chosen by a data-dependent selection procedure. JOMI constructs a reference set by swapping a calibration unit and the focal test unit and retaining swaps that preserve the relevant selection event. Theorem 1 p7 and Eqs7–10 require calibration permutation invariance of the selection rule and exchangeability of calibration observations and the focal test observation conditional on the other test covariates. Training is separate and the nonconformity score is fixed. Coverage is at least 1−α under these conditions; randomized treatment can attain exact coverage, while the nonrandomized bound depends on reference-set size. This is not a theorem for arbitrary adaptive adversarial images or an arbitrarily refitted detector.

**SOURCE — numbers and distinctions.** §5 p13 uses 41,127 HIV compounds, 20% for training and the remainder split between calibration/test, with ten training seeds and 100 splits each. The cost-constrained synthetic experiment §5.3 p15 uses 2,500 calibration and 2,500 test points, 500 runs; MIMIC §6 uses n=3,000 and m=2,000, 500 runs. Figures demonstrate selection-dependent under/overcoverage; exact curve values are not claimed. Proposition 2 needs additional selection conditions for a false-coverage-rate statement; the counterexample in Proposition 3/Appendix D p33 yields FCR 2α/(1+α)>α despite a weaker selected-coverage property. Conditional coverage and FCR are distinct endpoints, including how empty selections are handled.

**Threat/quality/independent encoders.** No watermark-performance experiment, T3/T4/T5 test, independently trained image encoder, strict image-quality assessment or human invisibility evidence: **MISSING / not the subject**. Appendix E.4's split-calibration alternative works for the specified restricted selection structure; it does not validate every post-hoc split. E.5 notes the cost of small reference sets. Tail validity for arbitrary attacker-selected scale/owner/image combinations is not supplied.

**JUDGMENT — concrete K12 transfer and failure.** If K12 selects a detector route, surviving tier, owner candidate or operating point from the evaluation outcomes, a marginal statement for each unselected score does not automatically survive that choice. Freeze the full chooser before calibration, calibrate/evaluate the resulting decision as a procedure, and report selected subsets without presenting them as the original population. JOMI is an instructive condition-check, not an off-the-shelf proof that the F5 joint detector is calibrated. Exchangeability can fail across families, resolutions or adversarial negatives; a source split does not by itself establish it. No numerical F5 improvement is predicted.

## Adversarial synthesis

1. **Family versus resolution is not identified by a model-name comparison.** SOURCE: the component dossier couples SD1.5 with 512 and SDXL/K3 with 1024 followed by a 512 detector resize; scheduler/noise conventions also differ. Black-Box Forgery shows why even different model names can share VAEs. WAVES and the competition supply varied attacks, but neither supplies the needed crossed F5 experiment. DERIVED: the retained family gap cannot be assigned wholly to encoder independence or wholly to resizing from these papers. JUDGMENT: Gate-1 Q-RES is the first diagnostic; the adversary may select the least favorable supported scale.

2. **Equal nominal strength is not equal intervention or equal distortion.** SOURCE: regeneration pipelines differ in timestep/noise schedules and repeated-pass budgets. WAVES explicitly exposes quality-survival tradeoffs; the competition admits unmatched initial watermark quality. DERIVED: matching a string such as strength=.2 or comparing averaged leaderboard scores is insufficient for a causal family comparison. Record noise/timestep mapping and display per-source quality eligibility alongside survival. Do not obtain an attractive average by silently discarding quality failures or missing outputs.

3. **A union of channels is a new decision procedure.** SOURCE: the competition jointly calibrates superposition thresholds and does not universally improve on the stronger single mark. Focal selection theory requires conditions for selected inference; Safe testing requires conditional validity for accumulated evidence. DERIVED: combining robust/secondary/content indicators or searching routes/owners/scales changes the null event being tested. JUDGMENT: keep frozen C/J distinct from exploratory composites, calibrate the complete rule, and expose all negative populations. A carrier-presence gain is not automatically an ownership-binding gain.

4. **Per-call negative events and per-source positive majorities answer different questions.** SOURCE: the dossier retains source-majority positives but individual C0/wrong-owner calls and unresolved negatives. Its historical T4 donor-residual checks and T5 descriptor comparisons do not equal an end-to-end public-reference forgery evaluation. Black-Box Forgery separates successful detection/attribution from truthful content provenance. JUDGMENT: retain this asymmetry deliberately, label the number of unique sources and correlated calls, and never turn missing/safety-blocked negative calls into observed zeros. Existing zero counts do not establish a population 10⁻⁶ error rate.

5. **Statistical sophistication cannot replace the right negative distribution.** SOURCE: the two JRSSB results assume specific null/conditioning/exchangeability structures. DERIVED: neither licenses multiplying all seed/owner calls from one image, calibrating after choosing a favorable resolution, or transferring a clean iid bit tail to public-reference forgery.
