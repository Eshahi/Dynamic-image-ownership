## Paper cards

### K05-J1 — DiffMark

SOURCE — A pixel-space conditional diffusion writer and learned reader are trained through a frozen VQGAN reconstruction surrogate. Inference optionally backpropagates watermark recovery through SimSwap. This is a reconstruction channel, not an encoder norm objective. CelebA-HQ trains on 24,183 images; 2,824 test images and 2,000 LFW images are evaluated at 128/256 resolution. Tables 1/3 report 256-resolution PSNR 41.9572, SSIM .9769, LPIPS .0116; VQGAN BER .02% but SimSwap BER 5.96% and FSRT 4.05%. Table7 adds SimSwap guidance: average BER 2.77%→1.60%, PSNR 41.96→41.03, SSIM .977→.968. Table8 reports 128-resolution writing .1320→.7502 s with guidance on RTX3090Ti. §4.1.2 says beta 10→1, whereas §4.4.1/Table5 says 1→.1; that inconsistency remains unresolved.

JUDGMENT — Useful mechanism evidence for a learned surrogate plus explicit harder-channel feedback. Independent Deepfake models are tested, but independently trained diffusion attacker encoders and family×resolution T3 coverage are MISSING. Its frozen VQGAN is the training attack surrogate, not a robustly trained reader VAE. Learned decoder weights and payload are detector side information; original image is writer input only. BER and mean image quality do not establish F5 C/J, per-image strict quality or calibrated owner/null tails. T4/T5 are MISSING: persistence through face substitution can be the wrong binding behavior. Transfer only the channel-model hypothesis; do not import the face writer or its timing.

### K05-J2 — Latent Watermark

SOURCE — The inspected v2 inserts messages through a learned coupler into channel 0 of a generated clean latent and reads through the frozen SD1.4 VAE encoder plus learned decoupler/MLP. Three-stage training uses 50,000 LAION images, no noise layer, and freezes the diffusion model. §4.1.5 tests SD2.1 regeneration and BMSHJ/Cheng codecs. Table2, 48 bits: SD2.1 bit accuracy 99.09%, TPR 100%; reconstruction-average bit accuracy 98.10%, TPR 99.99% at the stated .01 theoretical FPR operating point. Table1 gives SSIM .97, not PSNR >35 evidence. Table5: 56-bit injection/extraction 67.8/60.1 ms, 512 resolution. Table4 labels FPR “%” while §4.1.6 uses probability thresholds; do not treat its .0066 entry as an audited .0066% guarantee.

JUDGMENT — A learned latent reader can exploit information beyond F5's fixed chip readout, but both carrier and decoder are changed; this does not isolate encoder replacement. SD1.4→SD2.1 names do not prove independently trained VAEs; encoder-lineage controls, SDXL/K3/FLUX T3 coverage, strict joint quality and T4/T5 negatives are MISSING. Detector needs the VAE, learned decoupler/decoder and candidate message, not an original-image registry. Training a new reader also changes null score distributions. The final-journal-version mismatch limits the card; it is not a second publication or a result newly verified in TMM.

### K05-T1 — ZoDiac

SOURCE — Zero-bit ZoDiac inverts an arbitrary image into diffusion noise, implants a Fourier ring, optimizes reconstruction through the frozen generator, and blends with the source to SSIM .92. Reader inversion plus a noncentral-chi-square statistic needs model weights and watermark settings. Table2/Appendix B2: 50/1/0 denoising steps give PSNR 29.41/28.67/29.09 and Zhao23 regeneration WDR .988/.970/.712; zero steps means autoencoder only. Evaluation includes 500 images per COCO/DiffusionDB/WikiArt. Table3 reports FPR .062/.030/.004 at thresholds .90/.95/.99; Zhao23 WDR .988/.974/.938. Appendix C2/Table19 reports original WDR .106 under fully pipeline-aware overwrite. Appendix D: one-step writing 45.41 s, 50-step 255.94 s on RTX8000.

JUDGMENT — Strongest counted evidence that a generator-aware path can beat an autoencoder-only path, even with one denoising step. It does not show that one-step denoising in F5's writer is sufficient: injection coordinates, statistic and distortion are different, PSNR misses F5's threshold, and empirical FPR is much too large. SD1.4/SDXL writer-backbone variations are not an independently crossed attacker-encoder test. T4/T5 content-binding protection is MISSING, with overwrite fragility directly adverse. Keep the denoise-versus-codec ablation idea; reject inheriting its security assumptions or success rate.

### K05-T2 — ROBIN

SOURCE — ROBIN implants a frequency ring at an intermediate generation step, alternates watermark-strength and hiding-prompt optimization, and inverts only to the injection step for reading. Training uses 50 generated images and 1,000 optimization rounds; evaluation uses 1,000 marked/1,000 clean. §3.4 reports that using the optimized hiding prompt during inversion harms recovery. Appendix C2/Table7 reconstruction AUC is .998 for BMSHJ, .999 for Cheng and .997 for a diffusion model. Table4 Stable Diffusion quality is PSNR 24.03, SSIM .768; Table9 pixel-alignment/latent-alignment average AUC .983/.972. Table6 reader time .531 s at 512 on RTX3090, excluding optimization.

JUDGMENT — Writer representations that hide a mark well need not be the representations or conditions that recover it best. This directly motivates separating F5's writer surrogate from reader coordinates. However, the reconstruction attack checkpoint/independent-encoder provenance and operating TPR at F5's false-positive tail are MISSING. AUC is not C/J. It is an in-generation method and fails F5's strict quality level by wide margins. Reader requires watermark, model and partial inversion, not original prompt/image. T4/T5 coverage is MISSING. Its ordinary-attack averages are diagnostic context, not a reason to broaden thesis threat scope.

### K05-T3 — VINE

SOURCE — VINE evaluates quality on 10,000 images and each regeneration branch on 1,000, averaged over difficulty. It learns an SDXL-Turbo writer and ConvNeXt-B 100-bit reader with blur surrogates; VINE-R additionally fine-tunes through Instruct-Pix2Pix using a straight-through estimator. Table1 at 512: VINE-B PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS 40.51/.9954/.0029; stochastic/deterministic regeneration TPR 91.03/99.25%. VINE-R: 37.34/.9934/.0063 and 99.66/99.98%, at .1% FPR. Table2 simple-UNet ablation A→B adds blur: stochastic TPR 54.61→86.85%, PSNR 38.21→35.85. Config H without pretraining gives 35.18 PSNR, versus G's 37.34, with similar regeneration TPR. Appendix D resizes the watermark residual from native training resolution to 512; fixed blur kernels become effectively milder at larger scale. Appendix F trains on eight A100s, then fine-tunes through 25 Pix2Pix steps.

JUDGMENT — Best counted lead for cheap differentiable channel surrogates at apparently compatible mean quality. It does not isolate an encoder replacement: all writer parameters are unfrozen after stage one and the decoder is learned. “Watermark encoder” means the full image-to-watermarked-image network, not an independently trained attacker VAE. §4.1 names VP/DPM schedules but a controlled attacker-encoder lineage matrix is MISSING; model diversity in editing does not supply it. T4/T5 safety is MISSING, especially survival through content-changing edits. Means are not per-image strict gates, and .1% FPR is not 1e-6. Reader weights/payload replace F5's native readout. Use the blur/scale hypothesis, not VINE's whole trained pipeline as an authorized replacement.

## Adversarial synthesis for the judge

JUDGMENT — The attractive interpretation “F5 failed on foreign encoders, therefore train a robust encoder” has three unsupported steps. First, saved Phase A already shows foreign encoder amplification: scalar gain is not the lost property. Second, the same-family/foreign-family comparison also changes resolution and resizing. Third, the final margin target was never reached, and missing traces cannot identify whether carrier writing, later fragile/binding stages, or normalization destroyed it. Literature cannot make those confounds disappear.

| Claim to challenge | Source evidence | Bounded conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| A robustly trained VAE is enough to survive an independent regenerator. | VINE trains a complete writer/reader; DiffMark trains through a frozen VQGAN; Latent Watermark freezes the VAE and trains couplers. | None is a controlled robust-reader-VAE versus independent-attacker-VAE demonstration at F5 gates. Terminology alone is not mechanism evidence. |
| Codec preservation is a sufficient T3 proxy. | ZoDiac 0→1 denoise WDR .712→.970, while DiffMark's codec BER is far below several downstream Deepfake BERs; F5 loses signal around noise. | A codec-only proxy can miss the damaging stage. Joint channel behavior is worth testing; the magnitude of benefit for F5 is UNKNOWN. |
| More latent amplitude guarantees robustness. | ROBIN hiding and extraction conditions disagree; F5 foreign gain coexists with detection failure. | Directional signal after noise and the reader's decision statistic matter. Maximizing encoder norm alone can amplify irrelevant directions. |
| Multi-model paper tables establish independent-family transfer. | Different writer backbones, different editing models, or SD1.4/2.1 labels lack a crossed lineage/scale design. | Record model roles, checkpoint lineage, noise schedule and scale separately. Cross-model names are insufficient. |
| High TPR proves a safe ownership system. | All counted methods omit F5's full T4/T5 content-binding negative panel; reported FPRs/BER/AUC differ. | Never translate these rates into C/J improvements, negative-gate passes or content ownership. |
| The 60 SDXL misses are recoverable by K05. | Saved current detections impose only arithmetic headroom. | Physical recovery ceiling and strict-quality/T4/T5-safe ceiling stay UNKNOWN. Headroom cannot be added to K04/K08. |

DERIVED — If the writer maximizes a projection of E_reader(x+delta) while the true reader sees E_reader(R_attacker(x+delta)), its gradient models the identity channel. Good conditioning of E_reader alone does not imply preserved projections through R_attacker. An encoder ensemble whose feature spaces have different channels/scales is also not a valid average without an explicit observation-space objective. The least disruptive comparison keeps the reader fixed and changes only the surrogate channel before that reader.

JUDGMENT — A useful surrogate must predict the relevant residual direction, final bit margins and failure ordering, not merely frequency plots, latent norm or average quality. Proxy gain without real-channel C/J or with inflated C0/wrong-owner responses should be rejected. A full generation-path method may remain scientifically interesting while being unsuitable for the fixed arbitrary-image F5 task.

## Three concrete option leads — proposals only

1. **Writer-only blur and scale channel before the existing reader.** Inspired by VINE, use a small fixed family of low-pass/resampling maps as hypotheses about T3 attenuation, with F5's reader, carrier and decision rule held fixed. Couple only after Q-RES and Q-STAGE clarify native scale and margin destruction. Match total strict image quality; record raw and final per-bit margins and robust-only versus combined stages. Failure criterion: proxy margins improve without held-out-from-surrogate development T3 C/J gain, or strict quality/negative gates worsen. Cheap inference arithmetic does not mean writer optimization is authorized here. It may preserve the wrong low-frequency signal or worsen T4 copy-paste survivability.

2. **Writer channel ensemble that includes codec, noise and a short denoising path.** Inspired by the ZoDiac ablation and DiffMark's two levels of surrogate feedback, compare channel outputs in the fixed SD1.5 reader coordinates, rather than averaging unrelated SDXL/SD1.5 latents. Separate codec-only, noisy codec and short denoise so cost and causal contribution remain interpretable; leave at least one independent attacker family outside surrogate construction. The necessary family×resolution and matched-quality controls are prerequisites, not already satisfied. Failure criterion: benefits vanish at matched total quality or are confined to a seen channel. Cost is potentially high and gradient bias remains; no F6 edit, model download or trial is proposed for this research session.

3. **Reader change as a distinct, later method branch.** A learned latent decoupler or robustness-trained reader could be evaluated only after demonstrating fixed-reader writer-channel insufficiency. Latent Watermark motivates the representation, VINE motivates joint learning, and ROBIN warns that writer/reader roles diverge. This changes the detector, its side information and null distribution; it needs new owner/null calibration and T4/T5 evaluation, not a reused threshold. First compare frozen coordinate replacement against learned readout so a decoder benefit is not called encoder robustness. Recovery ceiling, training budget and all strict-gate performance are UNKNOWN. Highest redesign cost; not the first recommended intervention.

JUDGMENT — Priority proposal is Q-RES/Q-STAGE and the first two writer-only hypotheses under the joint K04/K05/K08 diagnosis. No paper justifies committing to option 3, selecting a new threshold, or reopening closed E3/FreqMark variants. A replacement reader has no measured gain to rank above channel adequacy.
