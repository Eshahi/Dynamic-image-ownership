# Interactions that constrain an integrated F5 change

JUDGMENT: Component gains are not additive. The same lost source can appear in several conditional ceilings, and changing a payload, writer or threshold changes the assumptions behind those ceilings. Sources are [component B-E dossiers](components/), [WP4 ledger](results/LEDGER.md) and the linked inspected literature. These are proposed interpretations, not Claude rulings.

| Coupled changes | What compounds or conflicts | Consequence for a fair comparison |
|---|---|---|
| K04 carrier + K05 surrogate + K12 working resolution | F5 robust and second alignment reverse order between native SD1.5/SDXL, but family and scale move together | Q-RES before claiming encoder-specific failure or designing a narrow frequency fix; attack quality distributions remain visible |
| K08 second write + K06 optimizer + K07 distortion | A later operation can rotate or cancel the earlier residual; squared norms contain signed cross terms | Save stage residuals and margins; measure actual shares. Nominal47/52/58dB settings are not an additive budget or spendable percentage |

A disappearing h obligation cannot be called a robust ownership improvement |
| K09 lower multiplicity threshold + K10 binding | Exact constrained detection searches fewer words, but a score inside a suspect-centered ball need not establish the original q | F5 wrong-center T5 exact30/30 and T4 events block promotion, even when C0/wrong-owner zeros remain |
| K08 union + K09 error allocation + K10 unchecked semantic state | Bonferroni controls a stated stochastic null, not same-owner transplanted evidence; missing semantic evidence is not a contradiction | One saved F5 O_U T4 accept survives budgeting. Reject the simple union and contradiction-only rescue; retain separate tier diagnostics |

Fresh nonces alone do not prevent marked-reference transplant |
| K12 sample/split + every learned selector | Stage2/PhaseA share sources; source/seed pairs and T4/T5 pairs are dependent; historical cohort has already informed design | Even/odd reporting is exploratory, not untouched confirmation. No EVALUATE threshold/radius/model selection; later method studies need fresh disjoint development |

DERIVED/JUDGMENT: A useful distinction is preservation of signal, decoding of a word, binding to the suspect, and the claimed identity decision. T3 measures an aligned response, T2 maximizes a score, T1 routes evidence, and T4 relates final margin to later survival. None alone proves all four stages or lawful ownership. The observed O_U counterexample is why a two-carrier architecture remains interesting while the current combined acceptance rule is rejected.

JUDGMENT: Strongest simpler alternative is unchanged F5 with explicit tier/status reporting, followed by Q-RES and Q-STAGE. This does not spend extra quality, move the threshold, introduce a secret/registry, or reopen the descriptor/E3/FreqMark lines. A more complex integrated writer has to beat this retained reference at equal final quality, with near negatives and human visibility still enforced.
