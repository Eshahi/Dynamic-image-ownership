This still does not execute the full ball maximizer. Radius zero is exact recomputed-only presence; radius >0 remains a lower bound and differs from soft-angle binding. Zero negative lower-bound hits do not establish zero full-ball false accepts or T4/T5 safety. Sources: synthesis Finding 2; accounting CSV rows 24/33; code audit null section.

3. **MEASURED/DERIVED — Finding 3, reject universal fragile extinction and amplitude-to-score prediction.** Nominal 47/52/58 dB budgets are not measured orthogonal distortion shares. Normalized correlation does not scale linearly with a common amplitude multiplier even before considering foreign-channel nonlinearity. Every final clean margin median is below target 4 (overall median 1.036); 56/60 have negative chips. These are final post-fragile values and do not identify optimizer failure or reclaimable budget. Sources: synthesis Finding 3; fragile CSV rows 26/44; writer summary.

4. **SOURCE/DERIVED — Finding 4, preserve morphology as a lead, reject capacity/performance extrapolation.** The gains are pooled differential projection slopes, not retained energy or bit SNR; equal-RGB “luma” differs from perceptual luminance. The capacity implementation uses clipped host gain, despite prose describing mark gain. Existing marks differ in distortion and structure. Real lattice T3 is unrun and visible defects remain. This does not prioritize lattice over F6 or prove a gain from K4/K7. Sources: synthesis Finding 4; distortion audit section 5; inventory E08/E17.
