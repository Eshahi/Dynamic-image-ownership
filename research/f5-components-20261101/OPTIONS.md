# Option register conventions

JUDGMENT: Component sections D are proposals for Claude, not approvals. Each Kxx-O1/O2/O3 is a distinct mechanism or decision policy. The test mapping in sections E and results/LEDGER.md records all feasible saved-data checks; unavailable interventions are named, never scored as failures merely because they were not run.

JUDGMENT: Forecast ranges are planning judgments, not confidence intervals, measured improvements or sums of WP1 ceilings. Unless stated otherwise, a range is the change in successful source counts per 60 attempts in the named cell. A physical rewrite can be worse than the proposed planning range. UNKNOWN physical ceilings remain UNKNOWN. A zero range for an audit/protocol change means the existing frozen detector outputs do not change, not that the information has no value. Reader diagnostics retain C/J verbatim and label their own endpoint separately.

JUDGMENT: Every writer option requires the same later visibility check: fixed flat-region masks from the unmarked source, local residual RMS and maxima, enlarged residual/marked patches at native display scale, all-image PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS, and blinded human assessment. Human verdicts stay MISSING until supplied; no image is dropped for being flat or hard. A mask must be fixed without seeing regeneration or detection outcomes. The current session does not run a writer or generate these counterfactual images.

JUDGMENT: Any option replacing the instance descriptor, retrying FreqMark, modifying E3 or adding an external baseline is labelled REOPENS-CLOSED-LINE and remains inactive. Merely reading such literature does not reopen it. Side information, keys and computation are explicit per option; a secret-key or registry variant is a different threat model.

Logical counterexamples to an exact model or claimed guarantee are valid negative tests; they do not require synthetic runs that merely restate the proof.
