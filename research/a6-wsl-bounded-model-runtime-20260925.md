1. `check_a6_local_model_load.py --load-model` exited **0**. The local fp16 safetensors assembled into `StableDiffusionImg2ImgPipeline` with a `PNDMScheduler`, non-null safety checker and feature extractor, and moved to CUDA. It returned `model_loaded_no_inference`, 17 verified files, VRAM total `12,820,480,000`, free before `11,561,598,976`, free after `8,740,929,536`, peak Torch allocation `2,765,889,536` bytes, and internal elapsed `11.297` seconds. This is a one-time load footprint, not a gradient/activation budget. Stderr emitted a `torch_dtype` deprecation and a Transformers warning: “model of type clip_text_model to instantiate a model of type clip.” A read-only source inspection found that the pinned `text_encoder/config.json` declares `CLIPTextModel` and `model_type=clip_text_model`, while installed Transformers 4.57.6 exposes `CLIPTextModel.config_class=CLIPConfig` with `model_type=clip`; the mismatch explains the warning path but **does not prove** equivalent loaded text-model behavior. Tensor/config parity must be checked before trusting text conditioning.
