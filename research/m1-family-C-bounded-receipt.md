## Technical diagnosis and control chain

The cheap original-step diagnosis verified the analytic orthonormal-DCT gradient, descent sign, scheduler transport and fp16 perturbation survival on declared CPU fixtures. Author-code inspection supplied a specific phase discrepancy; the follow-up changed the guidance window while retaining carrier/readout settings. Late guidance then produced exact sixteen-bit words at terminal, float-cycle, clean and VAE stages on all eight outputs, eliminating failure to establish the latent target as a universal explanation. It simultaneously revealed an all-image PSNR failure.

The final-five equality and minimum-change comparison tested the next identified cost mechanism. EQ5 imposed all 128 coefficient targets and retained exact words at excessive distortion on seven outputs. Q5 constrained only the nearest five votes per group, kept all eight quality-admissible, but lost votes during decoding/re-encoding. Its terminal words remained exact while extra-VAE matches fell to 13 and 11 for seeds 1002 and 1003 original. Per-step corrected-clean objective success was never treated as proof of delivery.

The single .3→.5 margin amendment was justified prospectively by coefficient-space cost comparisons, measured quality headroom and retained vote erosion. It was not stopped merely because earlier operators failed: Q5M5 passed its clean/VAE gate and received all 24 mandatory conditional T3 attacks. Relative to exact same-run Q5 replays, measured pixel MSE increased 1.720–2.459 times; the tightest PSNR headroom fell to 0.302524 dB. Thus it answers the positive follow-up: improved VAE margin alone did not satisfy the prescribed diffusion channel.

Replay controls make the successive comparisons attributable to the declared amendments: the phase run exactly replays old C0/early PNGs; Q5/EQ5 exactly replay C0/late original PNGs; Q5M5 exactly replays all 24 old C0/Q5 clean/VAE PNGs.
