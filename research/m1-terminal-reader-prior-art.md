# Terminal continuous-reader prior-art check

Exploratory, 2026-10-04. This bounds claims for [the prospective A-C design](m1-terminal-continuous-design.md); it is not a novelty clearance or method acceptance. Primary method and experiment sections were inspected, rather than relying on abstracts. New version-specific cards are [retained here](literature/m1-terminal-reader/cards.md); earlier archived cards remain authoritative for their specified versions.

Terminal-latent optimization and inversion-free extraction already have direct predecessors. The distinctive *candidate to test* is the conjunction of source-preserving residual composition, a fixed continuous owner-conditioned VAE correlation reader, independently recomputed CLIP/pHash templates, and the declared decision rule. This bounded inspection found no exact match to that conjunction. Absence from this set cannot support a first/novel claim.

## Candidate-specific inference and unresolved evidence

The following is our inference, not a claim made by these papers:

- Optimizing the actual encode-after-decode reader can repair a surrogate mismatch. It cannot guarantee survival under diffusion regeneration; RGB8 rounding, fp16 production encoding and the additional VAE cycle must each retain separate results. A success against a fixed training template is not a success against the suspect's recomputed template.
- The public owner map and public content features expose the same differentiable objective to an adversary. Successful embedding may demonstrate feasible public forgery. Correlation consistency cannot establish causal authorship, a signature, or legal ownership. Secret-key authentication would require a separate documented amendment and threat analysis.
- Continuous CLIP semantics are many-to-one; pHash is discontinuous and collision-prone. Joint margins do not resolve adaptive semantic-collision or copy-paste attacks. Test T4 recipient/donor rules and T5 predeclared pairs with fixed thresholds, including source-template versus recomputed-template diagnostics.
- Source-bypass composition is a hybrid existing-photo adaptation. Generated-counterfactual quality, VAE reconstruction quality and source-photo quality require distinct denominators. FreqMark's fidelity regime and learned methods' generated-image evaluations cannot be transferred to the protected35dB target.
- Fixed-owner random-image false-positive arguments do not establish adaptive public-template FPR. Report paired negatives and wrong owners at the frozen threshold; reserve population bounds for the approved confirmatory cohort and declared selection rule.

The immediate comparison priority is FreqMark's fixed-feature optimization, followed by LW/SERUM if learning is introduced. Remaining screening leads include AEON and *Spread Spectrum Image Watermarking Through Latent Diffusion Model*: full-text access failed in this check, so neither contributes a mechanism/result claim. No model assets were fetched or executed. This is a bounded neighbourhood check, not an exhaustive patent/security survey.
