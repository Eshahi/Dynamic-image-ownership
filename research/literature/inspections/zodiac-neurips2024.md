## Direct source observations

- Algorithm 1, p. 4, starts from an **existing image**, initializes a latent by DDIM inversion, injects a Fourier-ring watermark, optimizes the latent through a frozen diffusion model against reconstruction losses, and mixes back toward the source image to meet an SSIM condition. Thus existing-image latent optimization itself predates this thesis candidate.
- Fig. 2 and §3.3, pp. 4–6, verify by **DDIM inversion → latent Fourier transform → statistical test**, not by a blind image-domain 8×8 DCT coefficient comparison. The verifier's latent model cost is part of a fair full-pipeline timing comparison.
- §4.1, p. 6, uses 500 sampled images each from MS-COCO, DiffusionDB and WikiArt. Thus testing real and generated domains together is not, by itself, a first-of-kind contribution. The thesis's 1,000 COCO/5,000 DiffusionDB/900 DIV2K reference counts are separate commitments, not paper-replication counts.
- Table 3, p. 8, reports a detection operating-point tradeoff: at the paper's `p*=0.90`, FPR 0.062; at `p*=0.99`, FPR 0.004 with lower reported post-attack WDR. These are author-reported results under its own protocol, not comparable to a thesis 1% validation-frozen objective or evidence that the thesis method succeeds/fails.

## Agent inference and limitations

ZoDiac is close prior work for existing-image diffusion-latent embedding and the real/generated route; do **not** claim those two ingredients independently as novel. The candidate thesis difference is the joint public OwnerID-bound semantic and pHash instance signatures with a *blind image-DCT* verifier. This is a structural difference, not proof of originality or effectiveness; SemBind and SLICE add overlapping semantic binding in other diffusion schemes and must also constrain the wording. The paper does not inspect an identical proposal configuration in this targeted reading; absence is not proof that no such method exists.
