# Terminal reader neighbours: inspected primary cards

Inspected 2026-10-04 through primary arXiv HTML. These are version-specific paper reports, not local reproductions or claims of latest-version coverage. No assets or weights were downloaded. Each derived card is below 200 words. Bibliography keys are in `research/references.bib`.

## FreqMark — Guo2024FreqMarkv1

[Primary v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.20824v1), 2024-10-28. Inspected §§4.1–4.5, 5.1–5.4, Tables1–3, AppendixA.1. FreqMark optimizes a per-image perturbation in the FFT of a VAE-encoded image, decoding through fixed networks. Hinge margins align fixed DINOv2 feature directions with message bits; PSNR/LPIPS losses and latent/pixel noise improve fidelity/robustness. Extraction uses the image encoder and direction signs, without diffusion inversion or original pixels. The experiment uses SD2.1 VAE, DINOv2-small, 48 bits, 400 Adam steps; AppendixA.1 reports 5–6 minutes for two512px images on A10040GB. Table1: ImageNet PSNR31.27/SSIM.857/diffusion bit accuracy.969; DiffusionDB31.20/.854/.945. §5.1 specifies60 denoising steps, not our img2img strengths. Table3 accuracy falls to.637 at200 steps. This directly precedes terminal-latent optimization against fixed neural readout. It does not inspect our public OwnerID, recomputed CLIP×pHash binding, source-bypass quality cap or three-state rule. Paper-quality means do not meet a35dB target. License shown: arXiv perpetual non-exclusive; model licenses are separate.

## Latent Watermark — Meng2024LatentWatermarkv3

[Primary v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.00230v3), 2024-09-26. Inspected §§3.1–3.3, 4.1–4.2, Tables1–2. LW couples an encoded message into channel0 of the terminal latent before image decoding. Detection encodes the suspect with the VAE, then applies a trained decoupler and message decoder; no diffusion inversion is used. Four learned modules include two U-Nets and two four-layer fully connected networks; “no inversion” therefore does not imply negligible extractor cost. Three-stage training uses50,000 LAION-Aesthetics images; generation evaluation uses SD1.4 and5,000 captions per COCO/Flickr30k set. Table2 reports48-bit accuracy99.09% under SD2.1 reconstruction,97.27/97.93% under Cheng/BMSHJ VAEs. §4.1.5 delegates reconstructive parameters to an earlier attack source; no equality to our .05–.4 channel grid is established. Table1 SSIM compares marked/unmarked generations, not preservation of input photographs. Learned terminal injection and VAE-based message readout are direct prior art. Public self-recomputed semantic/perceptual content binding and the proposed decision states are not established by these experiments. License shown: arXiv perpetual non-exclusive.

## SERUM — inspected additional neighbour, no local reproduction

[Primary v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13396v1), 2026-03-11. Inspected §3, §§4.1–4.3, Tables1–2. SERUM mixes a fixed normalized Gaussian watermark with initial noise and trains a classifier on VAE-encoded generated images, including dynamically sampled augmentations. Thus initial-noise injection with direct terminal-latent detection is prior art, but detection requires learned per-pattern classifiers. Multi-user scores combine classifiers rather than content-derived keys. Table2 reports TPR99.88% for VAE and88.50% for Regen-30 at1%FPR. Thresholds are calculated separately per perturbation; these numbers are not evidence for a single frozen threshold on existing photographs. Training uses40,000 prompts; testing uses disjoint5,000 prompts. This preprint supplies a relevant learned-reader comparison, not the exact proposed continuous content-binding construction.
