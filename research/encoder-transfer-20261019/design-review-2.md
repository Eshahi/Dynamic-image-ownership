## 3. Substantive design and amendment review

I read `design-research.md`, `protocol.md`, `frozen-config.json`, `budget-and-proposals.md`, all eight syntheses, consequential evidence records/settings/prediction/evaluation/threat tables, and the code/test diff since the historical freeze. I also inspected the relevant surrounding analysis, runtime, source, asset and freeze contracts. This is a scoped design review, not a fresh audit of every historical experiment or every line of an upstream dependency.

**The scientific core is preserved.** The design uses 60 deterministically selected development IDs and hash metadata, disjoint smoke IDs 1790 and 1822, F5 r2, v5 r3, WAM32 and approved VINE-R100, without a registry. No held-out pixels were required for selection or this review. R0 is SD1.5 at 512; R1 is SDXL at 1024 with the stated Lanczos ingress/egress. DDIM20, empty prompt, CFG1, eta0, strengths .1/.2/.4 and seeds 12/13/14 remain fixed. D1 uses the deterministic VAE mode; X1/X2 separate resize effects; the .1+.1 rinse saves the intermediate 512 image and uses second seeds 112–114. It is not equivalent to strength .2. Gated FLUX remains excluded.

**The endpoint remains literal.** C is `semantic.read && semantic.content_match`; it does not require `semantic.found`. Source majority is two of three. At n=60, TRANSFERS requires C at least 45 and complete zero F5 C0/wrong-owner A on all active R1 outputs; fewer than 30 is FAILS; the remainder is PARTIAL. A is the union of semantic and instance found states. External margin is three against the best planned external source-majority presence count. Missing positives fail at fixed n; missing negatives leave the gate unresolved. The protocol retains the distinction between the literal route calculation and scientific comparisons when a comparator is unavailable; unavailable model execution cannot establish inferiority. Neither p-values nor descriptive diagnostics replace the route rule.

**The new carrier report is a justified pre-outcome descriptive amendment.** It reads existing persisted raw fields, checks booleans, evaluates read before found, and separates missing calls/raw data, no-found, found-without-read, read/content mismatch and C success. Read-without-found and exact-Q coverage remain separately visible. The report adds no model call, threshold, optimization, gate or outcome-conditioned analysis. This resolves a possible misdescription of semantic content survival without changing the frozen predicate.

**Native-path distinctions are explicit enough to interpret the experiment.** SDXL uses official checkpoint values with full-fp32 VAE arithmetic, while upstream img2img first rounds the image to prompt/half dtype before upcasting. D1's direct-fp32 input therefore differs slightly; it is a diagnostic, not an exact decomposition of all R1 loss. VINE's released SD-Turbo path differs from the paper's SDXL-Turbo description and includes the native tiny DDPM variance draw from global RNG; seed 42 covers that draw. No claim of RNG-free native inference is made. The original implementation pins, native thresholds and these distinctions must survive final re-freeze.

**Detection and reporting avoid the main inference traps.** WAM's common-null threshold is 27/32 and VINE's 69/100; their fair independent-bit tails are approximately 5.6537101e-5 and 9.1571612e-5. These are reference-null calculations, not calibrated population FPRs. WAM's released native mask rule is reported separately. Public deterministic per-source message bits are not an ownership proof or cryptographic secret. F5's q/H/chip dimensions are not interchangeable payload-capacity measures.

There are exactly three primary paired sign comparisons with one Holm family; unavailable comparisons have internal p=1 for family accounting and display unavailable. Secondary cells have no added tests. Clopper–Pearson intervals are exploratory under a hypothetical independent-source model; deterministic development selection does not turn them into population coverage guarantees. Seeds are nested within sources rather than treated as independent images.

Source-median ROC inserts negative infinity for each missing positive seed before the median, while any missing negative makes that negative unit unresolved. A cutoff accepts `score >= cutoff`; strict empirical FPR targets, complete tie blocks, maximum TPR/fewest FP/highest cutoff and a reject-all point are explicit. The corrected inequality prose agrees with code. Selected ROC operating points are descriptive selections, not the frozen binary endpoint or externally calibrated low-FPR guarantees. Quality matching requires complete finite three-seed quality vectors, uses predeclared bins/calipers and deterministic label-blind matching, and labels sparse overlap; it cannot make unmatched portions of the distortion distributions comparable.

**Mechanism claims stay appropriately bounded.** Training the SDXL autoencoder independently of SD1.5 supports a specific encoder-transfer test. It does not prove disjoint training data, independent Jacobians, universality across regenerators, or a causal isolation of the encoder from all other pipeline changes. Encoder response magnitude does not lower-bound end-to-end reader survival. Gaussian regeneration bounds require their stated channel assumptions. The proposal to smooth an encoder is not a certified defense merely because randomized smoothing exists. The design documents these limits and records falsifiable predictions rather than converting them into expected results.

**Budget and alternatives remain reviewable proposals.** The full grid has 7,500 units including C0, plus 250 smoke units. Budget is the smaller of 15 hours and USD15 divided by hourly price, with 1,800 seconds for retrieval; the timing-only smoke projection and frozen reductions preserve R1 .2/.4 and the R0 anchor. Core timing and GPU memory are estimates, not measurements. The model footprint is distinguished from VRAM, and official asset inventory is distinguished from the total download allowance.

P1–P8 remain excluded/unapproved, with P9 PixelSeal added as a bounded option and P10 Klein considered but rejected. TrustMark's 61 payload bits, 35 parity bits and four header bits are distinguished, as are its native 256 encoder/245 decoder dimensions. Kandinsky's full stack, AE-only cost and bypassed MoVQ quantization are distinguished. Paired smoothed/unsmoothed mode control costs more than a compound comparison; per-suspect captioning does not use clean-image oracle prompts. Pixel-space regenerators lack a qualified common img2img-strength experiment here, and the learned-encoder proposal exceeds the cheap-core objective. None of these estimated costs authorizes extra compute or a baseline addition.

## 6. Nonblocking limitations and release actions

1. The study can answer a bounded development transfer question for this SDXL pipeline and cohort. It cannot establish general robustness, a production FPR, cryptographic ownership, disjoint training data, or a latent-method claim for the image-domain F5 comparator.
2. Native compatibility, memory, actual throughput, remote stopping and retrieval are unmeasured. The frozen timing-only smoke and budget rules are required operational constraints, not evidence already obtained.
3. Empirical negatives remain source-dependent and finite. Even zero observed negatives does not validate a 1e-4 population claim. Reusing sources across seeds/channels cannot enlarge the independent negative sample arbitrarily.
4. Matched-quality analysis can be sparse and selection-dependent. Published payload/quality/FPR conventions differ across baselines; nominal bit-tail comparability does not equal detector calibration or ownership capability.
5. Literature coverage remains bounded by query ranking, indexing, accessibility, English terminology and undisclosed training histories. Known inaccessible sources and late leads must stay visible. A found but inaccessible primary text is a documented gap, not a fabricated V2 read.
6. `frozen-config.json` correctly remains `pre-outcome-stage1b-under-review`; its old freeze time/review reference is historical. After all blockers close, the parent must update the release metadata, record this same review's final verdict, commit and push the re-freeze, and make the stage-1 user handoff. Those truthful pending headers are release tasks, not additional scientific blockers. This review does not assert that the runtime itself enforces a status-based execution lock.
