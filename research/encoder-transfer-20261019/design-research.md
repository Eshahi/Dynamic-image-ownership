## Mechanism predictions and interpretation

The six detailed T4 predictions are adopted: clean self-encoder alignment does not guarantee independent-encoder alignment; D1 can preserve or erase the signal before denoising; stronger regeneration may reduce survival but monotonicity is not assumed; resampling itself can contribute; survival and content binding/exact-code recovery can diverge; an encoder-aware regenerator can attack a weakness absent from this oblivious panel. Every prediction is falsifiable descriptively through the fixed grid/controls; no prediction supplies a new decision threshold.

F5 writes final RGB pixels using gradients through the SD1.5 encoder, and reads with that same encoder. SDXL is the attacker's encoder. Let the writer perturbation be delta and attack encoder be E_a. Zhao's smoothing-style distinguishability bound depends on the perturbation after E_a and the injected noise. Increasing sensitivity under E_w says nothing sufficient about the norm or direction under E_a, and a loose upper bound is not a lower bound on detector performance. A successful SDXL cell supports survival under this exact channel; failure supports a bounded encoder-transfer limitation. Neither result proves all independent encoders behave similarly.

The reader uses no enrolled reference/registry in this experiment. F5/v5 use owner/profile/CLIP and F5 additionally SD1.5 VAE. External common detection uses the known expected message. F5's q32/H32 are structured content codes, not 64 arbitrary payload bits; 320 chips per tier are redundancy, not capacity. T4 copy-paste and T5 collision results are not rerun or strengthened here.

## Limits that remain after implementation

The consulted COCO development cohort is deterministic, with unknown residual creator/content dependence and possible model-training overlap. Pixel/hash checks protect declared held-out separation but cannot prove semantic independence or absence from pretrained training sets. Human visual judgments remain missing. Only one independently trained attack VAE is in the core; full-pipeline changes also alter denoiser, latent scale and conditioning. D1 uses posterior mode whereas img2img samples the posterior. Native baseline payload, task and side information differ. Common binomial cutoffs are random-message calculations, not image-FPR guarantees; 60 source groups cannot certify 1e-4 FPR. Approximate quality matching may have no overlap. WAM's published native calibration does not automatically transfer to the MIT checkpoint. VINE research-license restrictions govern release. Runtime compatibility, throughput and VRAM remain unmeasured until the authorized stage-2 smoke. Budget truncation is retained as missing data, never a smaller denominator.

This research considers all families and threats named by the brief and records adjacent recent work. It does not claim an exhaustive census of unpublished, private, gated or newly released methods. Unverified items stay proposals or coverage gaps. The independent reviewer assessed these remaining gaps and accepted freezing this bounded study; see design-review.md. They remain limitations, not claims resolved by the review.
