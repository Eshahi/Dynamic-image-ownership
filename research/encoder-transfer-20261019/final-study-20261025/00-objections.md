# Fifteen objections to the current ROUTE C evidence

Written before journal-level reanalysis, 2026-10-25. Ranked by damage to the proposed scientific claim, not by ease of repair. Existing aggregate results are known from `../results.md`, `../results-2b.md` and MAIN `STATE.md`; these objections are not blinded. TIFS is a rigor target, not an acceptance or quartile assertion.

| Rank / severity | Hostile reviewer's objection | Current evidence and limit | Required response / package |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 / blocking for mechanism | You changed the encoder, decoder, diffusion model, resolution, precision and scheduler together. Why call this an encoder effect? | R0/R1/R3 are compound interventions. D1/D3 retain 55/60 F5 source-majority C, while full R1 .2 retains 0/60 and R3 5/60 (`../results-2b.md`, Full Kandinsky 3; `../results.md`, diagnostics). | WP2 derives a full encoder–denoiser–decoder–readout chain. WP5 isolates stage retention with precision-matched controls. Claim only family dependence until interventions identify more. |
| 2 / blocking for novelty | Cross-model regeneration and encoder-aware attacks already exist. What is new beyond one weak watermark? | Existing evidence concerns an unpublished image-domain F5 comparator. A new model name or a failed method is not by itself a contribution. | WP1 inspects closest attacks, transfer theory and watermark papers; dated query coverage and explicit claim boundaries in positioning. WP5 must test a quantitative prediction, not re-demonstrate known removal. |
| 3 / blocking for confirmation | All results came from a repeatedly consulted development cohort; your explanation and final hypotheses were selected after seeing them. | Sixty development sources, two stages; original label was fixed but mechanism/contrast interpretation is post-hoc. | WP2 timestamps the analysis plan honestly; WP5 freezes development-to-confirmation outputs and uses a fresh source pool with no pixel access now. No retrospective preregistration language. |
| 4 / blocking for transfer law | A high latent norm is not readable signal, and a family indicator can memorize six cells. Your proposed law may be unfalsifiable. | No per-encoder residual gain, directional alignment or denoiser transport has been measured. Scores are nonlinear, key-conditioned statistics. | WP2 specifies observable quantities, small nested models and failure cases; WP5 tests genuinely held-out source predictions and separately held-out-family predictions. No fitted family intercept presented as mechanistic generalization. |
| 5 / blocking for causal severity claim | Equal strength is not equal noise; lower LPIPS is not perceptual equivalence. Matching almost fails. | T7 R0/R1 overlap is F5 m=2; 58/58 complete F5 sources have milder R1 .1 than R0 .2/.4 on three metrics, but this is descriptive (`../results-2b.md`, Strength curve). | WP2 derives actual scheduler alpha/noise. WP5 preregisters within-source dominance and quality-constrained attacks without claiming causal matching or human equivalence. |
| 6 / blocking for a robustness/security claim | The adversary is weak. A fixed smoothing attack at an already-zero primary endpoint proves little. | F5 C is zero under both R1-mode and R1-smooth; A falls 32 to 16 but A is not ownership verification (`../results-2b.md`, P5). | WP3 specifies detector-free, arm-generic informed attacks, quality targets and straw-man risks. WP5 develops settings on development only and retains all outcomes. |
| 7 / major | F5 and VINE do not have matched payload, readout, quality or FPR. Your apparent ranking is unfair. | F5 carries content-derived q32/H32; VINE has 100 random bits and known-message readout. Strict clean quality differs; 31/60 VINE versus 60/60 F5 in MAIN state. | WP1/WP5 preserve T7 native operating points, separate presence from content binding and report embedding/attack/total quality. No equal-quality superiority claim. |
| 8 / major | Zero negative calls does not establish low image false-positive probability, especially with repeated seeds. | F5 R1 negatives are 0/540 C0 and 0/540 wrong owner, but each set contains 60 source clusters. VINE has an observed false detection at R1 .1 (`../results-2b.md`, Negatives). | WP2 audits source-any-event endpoints; WP5 uses source-level exact bounds and zero-event gates, gives the n needed for any advertised low-FPR bound and limits the target population. |
| 9 / major | You have one writer and two purportedly independent encoders. What population does this represent? | SDXL has explicit from-scratch support; Kandinsky MoVQ lineage is qualified. Family labels alone do not prove independent initialization or training. | WP1 audits primary lineage evidence. WP4 recommends a genuinely informative third family with honest unknowns; WP5 labels absence/failure rather than silently substituting. |
| 10 / major | The binary endpoint hides why it fails: carrier loss, read failure, wrong content, or a fragile auxiliary tier? | F5 semantic and instance tiers behave oppositely across R0/R1; C is a compound predicate. | WP2 audits score/threshold margins and read-first state transitions, links code predicates, and separates semantic/instance tiers. WP5 records raw values without retuning thresholds. |
| 11 / major | Correlated seeds, multiple cells, selected plots and missing outputs can inflate the evidence. | Three seeds/source; 23 R0 safety blocks in the union; original T7 rules count missing positive attempts as failures. | WP2 retains final journal state per id, incomplete cases and denominators. WP5 fixes source-level testing, multiplicity, all-cell reporting and missing-negative handling. |
| 12 / major | Unclear implementation fidelity can manufacture a family effect. | Native scheduler and quantization differ. R1-mode versus sampled R1 also changes precision and noise draws; smooth versus mode is the cleaner contrast. | WP2 reads adapters without running. WP5 specifies identity, precision, posterior and common-random-number controls; no out-of-distribution encoder swap sold as a valid native pipeline. |
| 13 / major | The final protocol is not executable at the proposed budget, or requires inaccessible assets. | Prior 4.02 h stage-2b run is not a 200-source mechanism-plus-confirmation budget. Extra derivatives and new-family loading add cost. | WP4 checks access/licences/VRAM; WP5 derives itemized counts and time from saved timings, separates estimates from quotes, and fixes reduction/stop rules before outputs. |
| 14 / major | Your theorem assumes Gaussian isotropy and locally linear behavior where clipping, quantization and denoising are nonlinear. | F5 optimization/decoding and regeneration are not a scalar additive-noise channel. No measured Jacobian evidence exists. | WP2 supplies conditional derivations, null alternatives and explicit nonidentifiability. WP5 finite-difference/matched-control measurements test approximations; failed predictions remain publishable negatives. |
| 15 / blocking for broad thesis/security framing | Semantic detection is neither legal ownership nor unforgeability; the study tests only T3 and says nothing new about T4/T5 or lightweight latent insertion. | F5 is an image-domain optimized writer with pinned CLIP and SD1.5 encoder side information. Registry study is separate. | All packages retain scope. WP6 checks language; WP7 lists unsupported claims and leaves publication/institutional decisions with the user. |

## Exit criterion for this session

A persuasive final design must reduce objections 1–6 to explicit falsifiable server measurements or bounded claims, preserve objections 7–15 as enforceable reporting rules, and receive an independent hostile review. Reading more papers cannot itself prove the mechanism. A clean negative final study remains an admissible outcome.
