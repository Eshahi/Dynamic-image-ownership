The rank-ratio publisher text was inaccessible; Crossref verifies identity, not equality with the inspected author manuscript. Shallow Diffuse's substantive inspection uses v5 HTML, not asserted byte identity with conference text. Its mutable-main code observations are not a pinned reproduction. Early Semantic Commitment has section-level indexed access only. Other access receipts and exact locators remain in the linked cards; no reported result was independently reproduced here.

**Current: bounded empirical account.** Retained development evidence shows F5 compound C survives foreign AE round trips on 55/60 sources yet vanishes under full SDXL regeneration. Complete pipelines differ, and the existing journals lack the derivatives needed to test the proposed law. These results support a case-specific limitation, not encoder-only causality or a predictive mechanism claim. See [mechanism](../04-mechanism.md).

**Future: possible predictive contribution.** Under the unchanged [final protocol](../07-final-experiment.md), the privileged `p_hat = p_C0 + kappa*v` forecast must demonstrate useful incremental prediction over frozen simpler comparators for the unchanged compound reader, on new image sources and a family excluded from fitting/calibration. It uses original/marked inputs, C0 trajectories and target derivatives; it is not a lightweight detector. Required Kandinsky is fit-excluded but historically observed. H4 alone does not establish a nontrivial high/low contrast; the protocol's H1/H2 reporting gate still applies. Failure retains the bounded empirical outcome. Success would support a conditional predictive account within tested pipelines, still neither encoder-only causality nor proof of novelty. No “first Jacobian,” “first post-generation,” or “first cross-model regeneration” claim is defensible here.
