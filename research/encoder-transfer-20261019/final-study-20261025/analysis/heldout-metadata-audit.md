**79 text/metadata files** from the current worktree and authoritative MAIN checkout were read. Every exact absolute path, byte size and SHA256 appears in `exclusion-files.json`. This includes development-source reservations, download/source records, encoder-transfer source-list, fresh/registry/round3 development selections, source receipts, held-out DIV2K/COCO/shot indexes, confirmation manifests/external index, historical split/holdout ledgers, source/exposure records and prior exclusion inventories. MAIN is `W:/Prrojects/image ownership/THESIS_GUIDE_OFFLINE_v5`; state was read once there. No old held-out pixels were opened. The worktree has no `.thesis-build` or `data` tree; MAIN carries retained exclusion and confirmation records. The missing main-checkout copies of some newer tracked ledgers are covered by the worktree copies explicitly listed in the receipt.

`exclusion-identifiers.json` records 21,351 hash-like tokens and the extracted URL/title tokens. Hashes are lowercased; literal 40-character hex/SHA1, 64-character hex/SHA256 and 31-character base36-like tokens are retained conservatively. A token's presence is an exclusion aid, not a claim that every token is an image hash. URLs are HTML/percent decoded, lowercased, stripped of terminal slash, semicolon or Markdown backtick, and compared literally. Candidate Commons image URLs additionally remove the API-added `utm_source` query; the original returned URL remains in each manifest row. File titles are HTML/percent decoded, underscores converted to spaces, whitespace trimmed and lowercased. These conservative normalizations may conflate distinct case-sensitive strings; they cannot improve evidence of independence.

Canonical Commons title/description URL/original-file URL and supplied SHA1 are compared with prior recorded tokens. Exposed upstream source URLs in Credit/Source, plus recognized image-specific Flickr/staticflickr/COCO/Commons links in ImageDescription, are recorded and compared. Generic creator/help/Wikipedia/Wikidata/licence links are not treated as source-image identities. Numeric identifiers are namespaced: a Commons page integer is never equated to a COCO or DIV2K integer. **No exact audited metadata identifier overlap was found.** Free-text source mentions without a recoverable, namespaced identifier are not independently resolved.

This equality audit cannot detect mirrors, alternate URLs, retitled or differently encoded copies, near duplicates, shared scenes, repeated real creators with different attribution strings or content already seen elsewhere. Existing inventories predominantly supply SHA256; Commons supplies SHA1. No cross-algorithm equality inference is made. **Before any authorized future scientific use, acquisition must compare exact raw and canonical hashes with all recorded project exclusions, without opening old held-out pixels.** No descriptor or visual-similarity screening was performed or authorized. POTD is a curated population and creator strings are metadata clusters, not proof of independent human authorship or representative natural-image sampling.
