| Decision | Selected rule and reason | Claim consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Full channel rather than encoder norm alone | Predict chips using exact input-encoder displacement and C0-path downstream tangent; retain background and signed direction. Foreign codec-only retention already challenges norm-only sufficiency. | No independent-encoder causal theorem; the model is unmeasured until the server phase. |
| Two learned law quantities | One global unconstrained kappa and one global native-score q90 radius, no family-specific fit. | The radius is not a probability; a deterministic C forecast is scored as such. |
| Primary endpoint | Preserve C = read AND content_match, with the unchanged C0 descriptor proxy in the prediction. | Useful margin prediction cannot replace a failed compound-C forecast. |
| Quality comparator | Six permissible pre-output quality features, unrestricted slopes, fixed penalized logistic objective. Supersedes the provisional one-feature/monotone suggestions. | Limits a stronger specified comparator, not every possible quality model or an equal-cost algorithm. |
| Validation weights | Equal total weight per family, then equal weight per strength within family. | Multiple SD-family floor cells cannot numerically drown the K3/FLUX challenge; every cell still has its own .10 rate-error requirement. |
| Inference | Thirteen Holm slots; source-level binomial tails and an empirical-Bernstein paired-loss certificate. | Conditional model-based FWER under the explicit source independence/exchangeability assumption, not an unconditional finite-frame claim. |
| Unknown marked outcomes | Feasible-majority sets and conservative removal/risk bounds. | Safety blocks and technical failures are not proof of removal. |
| Sporadic missing forecasts | A/Q/S set bounds penalize the law and favor the comparator; seal availability before outcomes. | A robust-completion certificate is distinct from delivery of all numerical forecasts. Systematically unsupported paths remain unevaluated. |
| Training availability | Fixed original weights, explicit 90%-per-cell and source-representation coverage floors; no error-based exclusions. | Qualified incomplete fits may be selection-biased; fresh prediction evaluates the frozen procedure, not an unbiased physical coefficient. |
| Missing calibration maxima | Keep all 30; missing maximum is +infinity; q90 remains rank 28. | Finite censored radius is conservative; infinite radius is uninformative, not a successful interval model. |
| Attack selection | Quality-only, arm-generic, one shared setting, no original or detector feedback. | Failure/ineligibility remains reported; no oracle-selected attack disguised as informed. |
| Additional family | Conditional FLUX; K3 is required fit-excluded core with historically known outcomes. | FLUX is access/resource/quality dependent; no unknown-family discovery claim for K3. |
| Fresh pool | Fixed metadata-only Commons POTD draw, 200+40, identity audit and later exact-hash gate. | Curated source population, imperfect creator grouping and no foundation-model training-membership guarantee. |
| Resources | Known timings separated from unmeasured JVP/backward work; server-only bounded pilot. | 100-hour core proposal is an allocation ceiling, not proof of feasibility or execution approval. |
