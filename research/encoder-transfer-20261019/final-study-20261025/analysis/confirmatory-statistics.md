# WP5 statistical design: 200 independent source units, explicit uncertainty

Prospective design refinement, 2026-10-25. This is a specification for the later approved server study, not execution approval, a new result, or the WP6 independent review. The authoritative MAIN STATE was read once. No retained journals, pixels, models, metrics or experiment code were run by this contributor. The accompanying [stdlib calculation](stats-design.py) and [numerical output](stats-design.json) calculate binomial powers and analytical bounds only.

**Prospective WP5 amendment, before any new Phase-A/B measurements:** root adopted [training-missingness-design.md](training-missingness-design.md), including its coverage-qualified available-data fits, infinity-coded incomplete calibration maxima, and sporadic forecast-abstention certificate. These rules replace this file's earlier absolute complete-training-grid and missing-forecast=>p4=1 vetoes. They do not change n=200, the .10/20% targets, the 13 Holm slots, the family weights or historical results. Structural unsupported paths and invalid fits/freezes remain hard prerequisites. A bound for every completion is a conservative risk guarantee, not a claim that missing numerical predictions were delivered.

## 1. Freeze these choices before Phase A and Phase B

Use **n=200 fresh independent source units** in Phase B, with no outcome-dependent extension. One unit is one independent acquisition group, with one image selected per group by the frozen manifest rule. Three seeds 12/13/14 are repeated measurements of that source. Do not turn 200 sources into 600 independent trials, treat cells as independent, or substitute a bootstrap over seed rows. Grouping and the sampling population must be specified by the manifest design; file names alone do not establish independence.

The binomial and fixed-mixture betting guarantees below are conditional on the fitted, frozen Phase-A predictors and on independent identically sampled source units from the declared target population. They allow arbitrary dependence among a source's cells, seeds, arms and controls. They do not account for uncertainty over alternative Phase-A refits, a population of model families, or correlated acquisition groups. A fixed finite public collection sampled without replacement is not literally iid conditional on that collection. If the intended estimand is strictly that finite collection, replace the binary tails by exact hypergeometric tails using its frozen eligible size and use an appropriate without-replacement bound for the loss contrast; do this before Phase B. Do not advertise the iid calculations as unconditional design-based exactness for an unspecified sampling frame. A superpopulation interpretation requires its explicit exchangeability/independence assumption.

The primary F5 prediction grid is seven cells: R0 .1/.2/.4, R1 .1/.2/.4, and native Kandinsky 3 .2. Kandinsky is required for the seven-cell H4 challenge. A prospectively declared optional FLUX cell makes eight only if its identity, schedule, availability and inclusion are frozen **before Phase A fitting/calibration**. No post-outcome optional-cell choice is permitted. If a required challenge cannot run, H4 is not fully evaluated; its family slot is retained with p=1. The other hypotheses remain reportable.

H4 weights are equal by family and within family: seven-cell design has 1/9 on each R0/R1 cell and 1/3 on Kandinsky; eight-cell design has 1/12 on each R0/R1 cell and 1/4 each on Kandinsky/FLUX. The calibration-in-the-large requirement still applies to **every cell**, irrespective of its weight. Clean and mechanistic controls, attack arms and post-output oracle models do not silently enter these weights.

The law and quality baseline fit only on the designated 30 Phase-A fit sources, with six R0/R1 cells per source. The separate 30 sources calibrate the margin radius. Kandinsky and optional FLUX are excluded from both fitting and radius calibration. Their historical aggregate outcomes are already known; they are withheld from fitting, not unknown-to-the-researchers model families.

## 2. Endpoints and missingness

For F5/v5 use the existing semantic `C = read AND content_match`; do not substitute any-carrier A, semantic found, exact payload recovery, or an instance endpoint. For WAM/VINE-R use the frozen common known-message-presence endpoint. In particular VINE-R remains **matches >=69/100**, including equality. Presence is not content binding or ownership verification.

For a three-seed cell, `Y_ic=1[at least 2 of 3 scheduled calls are positive]`. For the conventional all-attempt endpoint, an unavailable/blocked/failed call contributes zero. A deterministic B attack/control has one scheduled output and its single result is its source endpoint. Keep attempt status, successful output, detector status, quality availability and detection logically separate. A missing output is never evidence of completed removal.

For inference that asserts **loss**, additionally define `Yplus_ic=1[observed positive calls + unresolved calls >=2]`. For a single-output cell, an unresolved outcome gives Yplus=1. This pessimistically allows every unresolved result to have been positive. H1 uses this possible-majority count, while displaying both conventional Y and Yplus. Thus a crashed pipeline cannot establish semantic loss. Failures can still depress the explicitly labelled operational all-attempt endpoint.

For H3 all six quality triples across its two cells must exist and be finite; otherwise its joint indicator is zero. Complete quality does not imply complete detector calls: require R1's possible-majority Yplus=0 for its loss event. H5 requires completed non-detections and quality on the **same outputs**. Sporadic missing H4 forecasts use the explicit A/Q sets in section 5; do not substitute zero forecasts, remove a source, change the denominator or claim full delivery. Missing marked H4 outcomes retain the predeclared all-attempt Y for descriptive scoring wherever forecasts identify the score, but supported H4 uses the worst feasible forecast-and-endpoint-completion certificate. Unresolved predictions/outcomes therefore need not automatically make H4 unevaluated; they cannot be credited as correctly forecast negatives. Undefined full-grid point scores remain NA. Preserve execution-failure counts and successful-output sensitivities. Correctly predicting failed execution is not evidence for readable transport.

Complete every planned attempt to the declared retry/stop rule; do not choose retries by detector outcome. At n<200 unresolved provenance, unexpected duplicate groups, unavailable forecasts or an incomplete required grid must be disclosed. There is no automatic smaller-n success rule or unplanned additional sampling.

## 3. One confirmatory family with 13 fixed slots

Family-wise alpha is .05. Slots are H1, H2, H3, H4, H6 and **eight separate H5 attack-by-arm claims** (A/B × F5/v5/WAM/VINE-R). A quality-ineligible, unavailable or unexecuted attack retains its four p-values at 1. This prevents dropping unsuccessful candidates from the multiplicity denominator. The complete 13-slot list is frozen before outcomes.

For `X~Binomial(n,p0)`, define `Lower(k;p0)=Pr(X<=k)` and `Upper(k;p0)=Pr(X>=k)`, inclusive at observed k. Use full precision. For a conjunction hypothesis requiring every component, take the **maximum** of component p-values. This is an intersection-union test (IUT): under its union null, at least one component null is true, so rejecting every component at level alpha has false-rejection probability at most alpha, regardless of dependence. No within-conjunction Bonferroni is necessary. No individual component is promoted to a separate confirmatory discovery unless its slot's conjunction passes.

Apply Holm: sort the 13 p-values ascending, with a frozen lexical hypothesis-ID tie rule. At rank j=1,...,13 compare with `.05/(14-j)`; reject sequentially until the first failure, then retain all later hypotheses. Report adjusted values `min(1,max_{l<=j}(14-l)*p_(l))`. This controls strong FWER under arbitrary dependence, because the earliest rejected true null is bounded by a Bonferroni test on the remaining true nulls. A local p<=.05/13=.003846153846 guarantees rejection regardless of the other slots, so the power calculations below use that conservative threshold.

| Slot | Scientific alternative | Null and p-value |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | F5 source-majority C retention is <.10 at **both** R1 .1 and R1 .2, even allowing unresolved outcomes to be positive | Null: at least one retention rate >=.10. `p1=max(Lower(kplus_R1.1;.10),Lower(kplus_R1.2;.10))`. |
| H2 | F5 R0 .2 source-majority C retention >.80 | Null p<=.80; `p2=Upper(k_R0.2;.80)`. |
| H3 | The joint dominance-and-loss event below occurs on >.80 of source units | Null p_joint<=.80; `p3=Upper(k_joint;.80)`. |
| H4 | Every cell's absolute population prediction-rate bias is <=.10 and aggregate law Brier risk is at least 20% lower than baseline | A union null is tested conservatively by the mismatch-certificate and paired-loss IUT in section 5. Point criteria are separately retained exactly. |
| H5-A-arm / H5-B-arm | More than .50 of sources have demonstrated joint quality-valid removal, and more than .80 meet the output-quality region | Null: p_removal<=.50 **or** p_quality<=.80. `p5=max(Upper(k_removal;.50),Upper(k_quality;.80))`. |
| H6 | VINE-R known-message presence >.90 in **each** of R0 .2, R1 .1 and R1 .2 | Null: at least one rate <=.90; `p6=max_c Upper(k_c;.90)`. |

H3's .80 and H5's .50 removal thresholds are explicit prospective design choices, not thresholds inherited from an old result. They operationalize a pervasive sourcewise reversal and majority-source attack efficacy. Keep them unchanged once frozen. H6 is a positive-control retention claim; its specificity gate is a separate status. Failed VINE specificity does not silently invalidate a supported F5 result.

For H3 define source medians over all three scheduled seed qualities against the **same final F5 marked input** u. Set J_i=1 only when: R1 .1 has **Yplus=0**; R0 .2 has Y=1; both quality triples are complete; median LPIPS_R1 < median LPIPS_R0; median PSNR_R1 > median PSNR_R0; and median SSIM_R1 > median SSIM_R0. This ensures R1's majority is negative even if every unresolved detector result were positive; a missing call cannot establish loss. Strict ties do not count. Do not select seeds, use different quality references, condition the denominator on available quality, or switch to one metric after seeing results. This tests a joint observed reversal, not a causal encoder effect or an equal-distortion comparison. The old caliper analysis remains a separate labelled secondary analysis.

For H5, let P_i denote the arm's **clean positive** endpoint. For A define per seed `V_is=1[completed output AND finite quality AND LPIPS<=.15 AND PSNR>=25 AND SSIM>=.75]` and `E_is=V_is*1[completed valid detector non-detection]`. Then `Q_i=1[sum_s V_is>=2]` and `R_i=P_i*1[sum_s E_is>=2]`. B uses its one V and E instead. All 200 scheduled sources remain in both denominators; unavailable clean positivity gives R=0. A source with quality on one set of seeds and non-detection on another cannot manufacture two joint successes. Missing detection does not equal non-detection. Native WAM abstention is handled exactly as the frozen valid endpoint defines it, with separate state reporting.

H5 allows a confirmatory claim for one arm even when another resists. State that an attack works across all four arms only when all four of its adjusted slots pass. Neither a pooled four-arm fraction nor the best seed is a substitute. A numerical quality rate >=.80 can be reported as observed eligibility; statistical support for population quality >.80 requires the second component. Relative efficacy versus A-first-pass or B-fidelity/B-codec remains descriptive unless another multiplicity allocation is prospectively added; the current H5 is absolute joint efficacy, not a claim of causal improvement over its ablations.

## 4. Prospective quality comparator and information release

The authoritative [quality-baseline-contract.md](quality-baseline-contract.md) supersedes the provisional one-/three-feature suggestions. Use six permissible features: medians over all three C0 seeds of LPIPS(C0 target output,x), PSNR(C0 target output,x), SSIM(C0 target output,x), plus the three clean embedding qualities LPIPS(u,x), PSNR(u,x), SSIM(u,x), where x is the original source and u its final marked INPUT. The last three are repeated across cells for that source. A row yields a numerical comparator prediction only when all its required features are finite and complete. The quality of the **marked target output is forbidden as a forecast feature**. Original/marked inputs and C0 outputs are available to both models; only the law additionally uses mechanistic derivatives.

Fit on the fixed 30 source IDs and six R0/R1 cells with the prospectively qualified availability masks below. Preserve every planned identity and unavailable reason; finite observations cannot be omitted for their residual, leverage, quality, class or effect on the fit. A row with any missing/nonfinite required C0 seed quality or input quality is unavailable; do not use a two-seed quality median. Require at least **five of six available rows per source and 27 of 30 per cell**. Each available row retains original objective weight 1/6; missing weight is not redistributed. Standardize from these available rows and weights only. Weighted means/variances divide by the available total weight as ordinary moments, which does not restore missing loss weight. Freeze means/scales; a zero-variance feature has standardized value and coefficient fixed to zero. No calibration/Phase-B moments, PSNR cap, feature imputation or source-cell loss-weight renormalization is permitted.

Fit one global logistic prediction directly to the terminal-status **all-attempt source-majority Y**, with an unpenalized intercept and six unconstrained coefficients. Minimize the **sum over available rows** of source-weighted Bernoulli negative log likelihood plus `.5*sum_{j=1}^6 beta_j^2`. This is lambda=1 under that exact summed objective, not a mean loss or a sum rescaled to restore 30 source-units. Missing rows make the unchanged penalty somewhat stronger relative to observed loss; disclose this rather than retune. Require both target classes in the available rows, finite converged coefficients, and the coverage floor. Use the contract's deterministic Newton/backtracking solver, gradient infinity-norm tolerance <=1e-8 and at most 200 iterations. Preserve failed fits; do not substitute an intercept cap, selected regularizer or sensitivity comparator. Use no family, nominal-strength, schedule, source-ID, filename or timestamp features. Count seven coefficients and all preprocessing constants separately from the law's two learned quantities.

Fit the law's single global kappa on the same 30 fit sources, using complete finite 320-chip paired vectors only, original trajectory weight 1/18, and the observed least-squares ratio `sum_available w*<v,p1-b> / sum_available w*||v||^2`. Require at least **two available vectors in every source-cell triplet and 81 of 90 in every cell**, with a finite strictly positive denominator and finite kappa. Report unavailable weight; never fill missing chips with zeros or fit selected chip subsets. Law and comparator masks are distinct and both must be published; do not discard usable observations just to equalize masks. Qualified incomplete fits remain selected-data fits, not estimates corrected for missingness or unbiased complete-grid transport coefficients.

Fit coefficients freeze before calibration residual inspection. For the 30 separate calibration sources, retain each complete source's maximum absolute native decoded-margin residual over its 18 planned trajectories; assign an incomplete/nonfinite source maximum **positive infinity**. This is an unmeasured-score sentinel, not a measured infinite residual. q90 is still the 28th order statistic of all 30 scores, not a reduced-denominator quantile. At most 27 complete sources means q90 is infinite; 28/29/30 complete sources give the largest/second-largest/third-largest finite maximum. Serialize infinity by an explicit extended-real status and null numeric field. q90 remains a **margin radius only**, not a probability, standard deviation or primary H4 point gate. Infinity makes the interval-width diagnostic uninformative and cannot be advertised as useful uncertainty prediction. It does not by itself invalidate qualified point-predictor fits or the .10/20% H4 test. No fresh-population conformal coverage guarantee is claimed for these reused development calibration sources.

Record `FIT_COMPLETE`, `FIT_QUALIFIED_INCOMPLETE` or `FIT_UNAVAILABLE` separately for the scalar and comparator, with exact masks, coverage and objective weights. An unavailable required fit gives `UNEVALUATED_TRAINING_UNAVAILABLE`, p4=1; a radius cannot repair it. Fixed coverage floors are design qualifications, not proofs of missing-at-random selection or favorable future power. They cannot be relaxed after observations.

Before any Phase-B marked target outcomes are generated/released, freeze all planned prediction identities, per-seed chips/reader bits that actually exist, C0 semantic proxies, comparator probabilities that actually exist, and the resulting A/Q availability sets from section 5. Tangents are evaluated on C0. No target marked pixels, gradients, descriptors, quality, chips or outcome can enter its own forecast or determine whether a missing forecast is called structural versus sporadic.

Constant training-prevalence, three-feature C0-only unconstrained logistic and three-feature monotone C0-only logistic are labelled sensitivities under the same fixed split/penalty; post-output quality-oracle fits are explanatory diagnostics. No comparator switch can rescue H4. A better law result than this particular frozen quality predictor does not rule out every distortion-only function or establish equal computational cost. Scoring uses the family-balanced weights fixed in section 1, confirmed during root integration; fitting weights remain 1/6 across the six training cells.

## 5. H4 preserves .10 / 20% and uses finite-sample valid inference

Where each required source-majority forecast a_ic and probability q_ic is identified, the conventional all-attempt `D_ic=a_ic-Y_ic` is ternary -1/0/+1. Preserve its exact descriptive point criteria: `abs(mean_i D_ic)<=.10` **in every cell**, and `B_L<=.8*B_Q`, with `B_Q>0`, using the fixed weights. `B_L=mean_i sum_c w_c*(a_ic-Y_ic)^2`; deterministic Brier loss equals misclassification. `B_Q=mean_i sum_c w_c*(q_ic-Y_ic)^2`. If a required a or q is not identified, its full-grid point bias/loss and any aggregate depending on it are **NA**, with set bounds shown instead. Do not turn missing values into zero, use an available-case average as the primary score or report a point pass that cannot be calculated. Two agreeing available seed predictions can identify a majority a even when the third seed prediction was not delivered; that mathematical identification does not make the measurement grid complete. Equality passes an identified numerical criterion but supplies no statistically supported superiority/equivalence by itself.

The supported H4 claim is explicit: the same .10/20% criteria must survive **every feasible completion of sporadically unavailable law forecasts, comparator probabilities and target detections**, followed by the conservative population inference below. This includes the operational all-attempt target endpoint (the all-zero outcome completion), but does not invent missing forecasts for that endpoint. It is a robustness guarantee about a partial forecasting procedure, not proof of complete-grid forecast delivery. It does not assert that a safety-blocked image or its derivative exists, identify an actual counterfactual result, or authorize bypassing safety. Where a complete physical result is not well-defined, describe robustness to numerical completion of the declared endpoints instead. No assumption that missingness is random or ignorable is needed; the observed source vectors, including availability, must still satisfy section 1's sampling assumptions.

For a source-cell with p completed valid positives and u unresolved scheduled calls, let `lo_ic=1[p>=2]` and `hi_ic=1[p+u>=2]`. The feasible majority set S_ic contains all binary values between lo and hi: {0}, {1}, or {0,1}. A deterministic cell uses its single scheduled call and threshold one. A completed valid negative contributes neither p nor u; a missing/invalid detection or unavailable output contributes u, even if other metrics exist. Examples: `(0,0,?)` gives {0}; `(1,1,?)` gives {1}; `(1,0,?)` and `(?,?,?)` give {0,1}. Never treat the last two as resolved zeros in the supported analysis.

Define **A_ic** by the identical majority-set calculation applied to the frozen per-seed law forecast bits: {0} or {1} when identified, otherwise {0,1}. A missing seed forecast ranges over both binary values. Thus `(1,1,?)` identifies A={1} and `(0,0,?)` identifies A={0}, while `(1,0,?)` leaves A={0,1}. Do not claim missing chips, tangents, descriptors or margins were measured. Define **Q_ic={q_ic}** if all frozen comparator features and its prediction are available; otherwise **Q_ic=[0,1]**. No available-seed median, probability imputation or post-outcome forecast recovery is used. These A/Q sets and their reason codes freeze before target-outcome release; S then comes from the immutable outcome ledger.

Compute the following observed bounds, without filling in a single dataset of invented outcomes:

- Cell bias interval: `b_lo,c=mean_i(min A_ic-max S_ic)`, `b_hi,c=mean_i(max A_ic-min S_ic)`. Every feasible completed-sample bias lies between these endpoints. Its worst absolute value is `max(abs(b_lo,c),abs(b_hi,c))`.
- Worst mismatch: `M_upper,ic=max_{a in A_ic,y in S_ic} 1[a!=y]`. An ambiguous law or target majority counts as one potential mismatch, even if an available-case or all-attempt choice would agree.
- Lower loss contribution: `t_lower,i=sum_c w_c*min_{a in A_ic,q in Q_ic,y in S_ic}[.8*(q-y)^2-(a-y)^2]`, and `T_lower=mean_i t_lower,i`.
- Minimum baseline loss: `B_Q,min=mean_i sum_c w_c*min_{q in Q_ic,y in S_ic}(q-y)^2`.

The robust point criteria are `max(abs(b_lo,c),abs(b_hi,c))<=.10` for every cell, `T_lower>=0`, and `B_Q,min>0`. The last condition prevents relative-improvement language if a feasible completion makes the baseline perfect. These minima can use different completions; they are conservative bounds, not one imputed dataset. A Cartesian product may include combinations excluded by unmodelled feature/cell structure, only making the certificate more conservative. For Q=[0,1], the minimizing q for any binary y is q=y, giving the absent comparator **zero loss**; this grants it perfect prediction for the bound and never credits its failure as a law advantage. For A={0,1}, the law can be wrong for either y, contributing loss one. For singleton q and a with ambiguous S, the minimum is at y=1-a. The loss range remains [-1,.8]. With all forecast/outcome sets singleton, these definitions reduce to the original values.

### 5a. Exact conservative certificate for every cell's rate error

Let `r_upper,c=E M_upper,ic`. For every feasible forecast/outcome completion (a*_ic,Y*_ic), `abs(E[a*_ic-Y*_ic]) <= E 1[a*_ic!=Y*_ic] <= r_upper,c`. Consequently an exact one-sided Clopper-Pearson upper bound on r_upper,c below .10 is a sufficient certificate of the promised rate tolerance under every feasible completion. For `k_upper,c=sum_i M_upper,ic`, use `p_cal,c=Lower(k_upper,c;.10)`. If the unacceptable-bias null is true for any feasible forecast/endpoint completion, r_upper,c>=.10; the p-value is therefore valid and conservative for that null. It needs no seed independence, missing-at-random assumption or model for the ternary discrepancy's nuisance probabilities. The CP sampling guarantee still needs independent identically distributed source-level M_upper indicators.

This is **not** an exact efficient equivalence test for paired ternary bias and it is not a new 90% agreement point endpoint. It deliberately gives a sufficient, potentially conservative certificate. For example, 20 positive and 20 negative discrepancies among 200 have zero empirical bias yet fail this certificate. Such a result may meet the promised numerical criterion and remain inferentially inconclusive; it must not be called a falsification of the .10 bias target merely because the certificate fails. No approximate paired-normal or post-hoc multinomial alternative replaces the frozen primary test.

At the first Holm threshold, **at most 9 worst-case mismatches/200 per cell** are sufficient: the exact tail is .00352857 and the corresponding one-sided CP upper limit is .09932885. Ten gives p=.00807125 and cannot pass that conservative threshold. Thus ten non-singleton law or target majorities in a cell already exhaust this conservative first-threshold certificate even with no observed definite error. A missing third law seed with two agreeing available bits does not do so by itself. Missing comparator q affects the loss bound, not this binary mismatch calculation. A later Holm rank may permit more; use the exact actual p-value, never a rounded critical-count shortcut.

### 5b. Finite-sample paired Brier test

For each independent source use **t_lower,i**, not the loss computed by filling unresolved forecasts/outcomes with zero. This observable lies in [-1,.8]. For every feasible (a*,q*,Y*) completion, its source loss contrast satisfies `t_lower,i<=t_i(a*,q*,Y*)` pointwise. Therefore `E(t_lower)>0` implies more than 20% population Brier improvement, and positive baseline risk, for every completion, including all-attempt target coding. If any completion violates the risk target, `E(t(a*,q*,Y*))<=0` implies `E(t_lower)<=0`, so the test remains conservative. No probability distribution for missing forecasts or outcomes is invented. This is a bound for every completion of a partial procedure, not the measured risk of a fully delivered forecast grid.

**Prospective WP6 amendment:** the sole primary mean test is the fixed uniform betting mixture derived in [betting-mean-design.md](betting-mean-design.md). Freeze `Lambda={1/16,1/8,1/4,1/2,3/4,1}` and weight each bet exactly 1/6. With `Z_i=t_lower,i`, calculate `K_lambda=product_i(1+lambda*Z_i)`, `E_mix=sum_lambda K_lambda/6` and `p_bet=min(1,1/E_mix)`. Set p=1 if E=0. Under the composite null E[Z]<=0, the factors are nonnegative and independence gives E[K_lambda]<=1. Therefore E[E_mix]<=1, and Markov's inequality proves superuniform p_bet. This is a fixed-n=200, source-level test; cells/seeds are not independent factors. Do not maximize over bets, choose weights from outcomes, stop adaptively or select the smaller of betting and Bernstein p-values. The proof is conditional on the Phase-A freeze, not on the realized Phase-B covariates.

At the first Holm threshold, reject the mean component iff E_mix>=260. Use exact rational arithmetic for the defined serialized numerical probabilities, rational weights and binary completion sets when calculating the primary contrasts/products and threshold comparisons, or a certified lower enclosure of E with conservative p upper bound. Stable logsumexp is useful for display but alone does not certify rounding. A zero factor kills that component, retaining its 1/6 weight; never replace it by epsilon. Nonfinite or out-of-range contrasts are errors. The [exact analytical calculator](betting-mean-bounds.py) documents boundary cases; future implementation remains server-only.

Retain the following empirical-Bernstein calculation as a **named secondary sensitivity**, never a selectable primary alternative. Let **S_lower^2** be the usual unbiased variance of the observed t_lower,i values with denominator n-1. Do not reuse an all-attempt, available-case or imputed variance. Although reducing observations to lower bounds can change their variance, the concentration theorem applies directly to this bounded observed random variable; it does not require its confidence radius to be ordered relative to an unavailable complete-data radius.

The secondary lower empirical-Bernstein bound is

`L_EB(delta)=T_lower-sqrt(2*S_lower^2*log(2/delta)/n)-[7*1.8*log(2/delta)]/[3*(n-1)]`.

It has finite-sample one-sided coverage at least 1-delta under the stated iid bounded-source assumptions. It follows by rescaling and reflecting Theorem 4 of [Maurer and Pontil, COLT 2009, p.2](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~colt2009/papers/012.pdf); their sample-variance definition equals the unbiased variance used here. Theorem 4 and its Section 2 proof were inspected. This is a conservative concentration inequality, not an exact nominal-size or bootstrap test.

Invert the secondary bound to obtain p_EB: if T_lower<=0 set p_EB=1. Otherwise let `A=sqrt(2*S_lower^2/n)`, `c0=7*1.8/[3*(n-1)]`, `h=2*T_lower/[sqrt(A^2+4*c0*T_lower)+A]`, and `p_EB=min(1,2*exp(-h^2))`. This solves `T_lower=A*sqrt(log(2/delta))+c0*log(2/delta)`. It has no confirmatory decision slot. A Hoeffding result may also be shown as a labelled sensitivity. The uniform betting mixture is the sole primary mean test; no unadjusted p-minimum is allowed.

Set `p4=max({p_bet} union {p_cal,c : c is a fixed validation cell})`, and enter it in the 13-slot Holm family. If a structural prerequisite, fit qualification, freeze validity or robust point criterion fails, set p4=1 with its predeclared reason. Sporadic request-level forecast/outcome unavailability after a functioning path qualifies does **not** automatically force p4=1; use its A/Q/S bounds. The six-source pilot must establish required adapter, derivative and comparator paths for every required family; a missing model, unsupported JVP/adapter, family-wide undefined feature or later globally unavailable family is `UNEVALUATED_REQUIRED_PATH_UNAVAILABLE`, p4=1, retaining its planned weight. These structural reasons cannot be assigned after inspecting whether a bound would pass. This IUT allows arbitrary within-source dependence. Component intervals at the local level are not separately simultaneous; the globally adjusted decision carries the FWER guarantee.

### 5c. Delivery, identified scores and support are separate statuses

Record `delivery_status=full_delivery` only when every required **Phase-B** per-seed numerical forecast/measurement and every comparator probability was actually delivered with valid provenance. Otherwise record `partial_delivery_with_abstentions`, alongside counts by source/cell/seed/reason and `forecast_measurement_grid_complete=false`. A majority identified from two agreeing bits does not erase the absent third prediction. Record target-outcome completeness separately; full forecast delivery does not imply complete targets. Phase-A radius informativeness has its own status and is not a substitute for this delivery flag.

Record `point_grid_identified=true` only when all A and Q sets are singleton. Only then report the identified complete-source-grid all-attempt point scores and their numerical pass/failure. Such scores may be mathematically identified despite an absent third forecast bit; label this explicitly and retain the partial-delivery flag. Otherwise a full-delivery point Brier/bias that depends on a non-singleton set is **NA**; report the bounds, not invented a/q values. A component score that is fully identified may still be shown with its precise coverage and label. The primary denominator remains 200.

The inferential status is one of:

1. **UNEVALUATED_PREREQUISITE_FAILURE:** invalid/absent qualified fit, invalid freeze or a structurally unsupported required family/path; p4=1. An infinite q90 alone is not such a failure.
2. **ROBUST_POINT_CRITERIA_NOT_MET:** a worst-case .10/20%/positive-baseline condition fails; p4=1. Show any identified all-attempt numerical result, without claiming the worst-case failure proves that the unobserved actual completion fails.
3. **INCONCLUSIVE_SUPPORT:** robust point criteria pass but Holm does not reject H4. Distinguish forecast/outcome ambiguity, the conservative mismatch certificate and finite-sample uncertainty. Undefined full-grid point scores remain NA even when bounds are informative.
4. **STATISTICALLY_SUPPORTED_FULL_DELIVERY** if forecasts were fully delivered, or **ROBUSTLY_SUPPORTED_WITH_ABSTENTIONS** if they were not: qualified frozen paths and robust point criteria pass, and Holm rejects the worst-case H4 p-value. The latter is explicitly a conservative guarantee over all numerical forecast/outcome completions, not a complete-grid measurement or delivery claim. ## 7. Negative gates and honest specificity bounds

Freeze eight method-by-control-kind strata: F5/v5/WAM/VINE-R × C0/wrong-query. This yields n=200 source indicators per stratum, not 56 independent tests or thousands of independent calls. Additional per-cell/per-output counts are descriptive. The union's bound concerns the risk of any event in this declared query battery; it is not a universal all-queries or all-images FPR guarantee.
