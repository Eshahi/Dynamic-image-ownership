Author: Codex E2 IEEE venue researcher. Access date: **2026-10-25**. Research-only assessment; no submission, account access, experiment, acquisition or protocol amendment. Recommendations below are **researcher inference**, not editorial advice or an acceptance forecast.

## Decision in the present evidence state

**TIFS has the more direct subject fit; neither venue's submission readiness is established.** The current article is a bounded negative development case, not a validated prediction paper. A future successful, independently evaluated transport predictor would strengthen its scientific contribution, but those results do not exist. Keep the two cases separate when selecting the eventual destination.

The 60 development sources are not fresh confirmation. Whole-pipeline differences do not identify independent encoder training as the causal variable. Unequal native endpoints and clean qualities prevent an equal-payload, equal-FPR robustness leaderboard.

### Author requirements and format (verified, recheck before submission)

Shared SPS instructions require a clear problem, significant contribution, closest literature and explicit novelty. Scope/novelty deficiencies and insufficient experimental data can trigger rejection without review. **Regular Paper** is the appropriate format inference here. Comment Correspondence addresses an already published journal paper; Overview Articles have a separate preliminary approval route. Initial/revised limits: **TIFS 13/16**, **TMM 10/14** double-column pages, 10-point font. Initial limits include references and figures; supplements are separate. TMM supplements must be at most **four** double-column pages; the general recommendation is at most **six** without additional approval. These are maximum lengths, not evidence of a free-publication allowance. Source: [SPS author instructions, manuscript submission/length](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/information-authors).

## Conditional ranking and decision record

1. **Present dossier:** TIFS ranks first on subject fit, TMM second. Neither is certified ready; this ordering does not predict acceptance. TIFS's explicit negative-result language is relevant only with its reproducibility and novelty obligations, not an exemption from them.
2. **If the future transport prediction succeeds:** retain TIFS first when the paper's central contribution is watermark-removal/security analysis; TMM becomes a stronger alternative when the demonstrated contribution is a reusable multimedia processing predictor and the necessary account fits its limits.
3. **If the predictor fails or cannot be computed:** report that result and reassess the bounded case on its actual novelty. Do not promote the proposal, add new scientific work, relabel the development cohort as confirmation or switch claims to satisfy a venue.

No submission, journal contact, account creation, paid option or manuscript-format conversion is authorized or performed by this assessment.
