## 4. Phase A: feasibility, mechanisms and freeze

No Phase-B source pixels enter Phase A. First perform a bounded server-only, target-detector-sealed feasibility pilot on the fixed six development sources. It must verify every required adapter, explicit-noise replay, actual autodiff path, precision/save convention, peak memory, timings, storage and retrieval. Maximum pilot allocation is two instance-hours within the approved phase cap. A pilot can reject an infeasible path; it cannot tune a predictor, writer, reader, threshold, attack strength or source set on detection results. Preserve failed paths. Technical fixes must precede the full development release and carry exact code/config provenance.

The detailed twelve-source mechanism module measures each of four final residuals under each core encoder, against two norm-matched random directions and two Fourier-phase controls. It records mode and native-sampled displacements, perturbation energy with dimensional normalization, signed transport into the unchanged reader, actual posterior/noise terms and stagewise remainder. Noise-only decode and mode-versus-sampled native trajectories separate encoding, corruption and reverse dynamics as specified in [mechanism-server-spec.md](analysis/mechanism-server-spec.md). Comparisons are paired within source and explicitly descriptive; no norm-only correlation proves causality.

For the law, attempt C0/C1 chip vectors and full-chain tangents on all 60 sources × six R0/R1 cells × three seeds. Fit **one global scalar kappa** on the 30 designated fit sources by minimizing observed source/cell/seed-weighted squared chip error for `p_hat=b+kappa*v`. The planned weight is 1/18 per seed vector, total one per source. Unavailable vectors have no invented value and their missing objective weight is not redistributed. Kappa is weighted sum of `<v,p1-b>` divided by weighted sum of `||v||^2` over admissible complete vectors. Primary qualification requires >=2/3 available vectors in every source-cell triplet and >=81/90 in every cell. These are prospective coverage tolerances, not a missing-at-random claim. No exclusion by error, leverage or effect on the fit is allowed. Publish masks, original weights and complete versus qualified-incomplete status. No positivity restriction, family intercept, strength coefficient or selected clipping is added. Zero denominator means unidentifiable law. Both b and v arise from the C0 path; the marked INPUT supplies the exact encoder displacement.

Calibrate **one global q90 radius** on the other 30 sources: for each complete source take the maximum absolute decoded-margin error in **native score units** over its six cells and three seeds. Assign an incomplete source's score positive infinity, retaining all 30 source scores, then select their 28th order statistic. Here `M=D(p)-tau_dec` and the error is `abs(M_actual-M_hat)`, with no division by the threshold. At least 28 complete sources give a finite conservative radius; three or more incomplete sources give q90=infinity. Serialize infinity explicitly, not as a large finite number. An infinite radius is uninformative, never a successful uncertainty forecast; it does not alone invalidate H4's point predictor, which does not use q90 to decide C. This descriptive radius is not a Gaussian standard deviation, C probability, family-shift guarantee or second opportunity to tune kappa. No source-cell removal or radius tuning.

Map predicted 320-chip vectors through the unchanged reader using the C0 output's seven-view semantic descriptor as the explicit proxy for the unknown marked-output descriptor. Apply the real compound C rule and then the three-seed majority. The primary law predicts a deterministic bit, whose Brier loss is 0/1. Do not manufacture independent-seed probabilities or convert q90 into probabilities. Continuous margins, tangent remainders and semantic-proxy error are separate diagnostics; they cannot rescue C prediction failure.

Fit the primary **six-feature quality comparator** on the same 30 fit sources and six R0/R1 cells. Features are three C0-to-original quality medians (LPIPS/PSNR/SSIM) and three marked-INPUT-to-original qualities. Every required seed quality must exist for a row. Require >=5/6 feature-complete cells per source and >=27/30 complete sources per cell; retain weight 1/6 per observed row without redistribution. Use available-row training-only weighted moments, unconstrained logistic coefficients, unpenalized intercept and fixed `.5*sum(beta_j^2)` penalty. Both target classes and convergence are required. Publish masks, missing weight, preprocessing and seven coefficients. The law and comparator use their own admissibility masks; do not weaken either by discarding legitimately usable rows merely to force identical masks. This is stronger than the earlier provisional monotone one-feature comparator. Realized marked-TARGET quality is forbidden as a forecast feature. No family or strength label is included. See the exact [baseline contract](analysis/quality-baseline-contract.md) and [training/missingness design](analysis/training-missingness-design.md).

Develop and freeze both attacks **before any new target-detector release**. A uses equal strengths {.05,.1,.15,.2} in its two native passes. B uses the nine radius/penalty settings and fixed twenty steps in 05. One shared setting per attack applies to all four arms. Quality-only eligibility requires LPIPS<=.15, PSNR>=25 and SSIM>=.75 relative to the published marked input, at least 48/60 passing source majorities in every arm and, for A, at least 144/180 passing outputs per arm. Among eligible settings use the fixed worst-arm quality-budget-use score and deterministic ties. No eligible setting means inactive attack; do not relax quality, choose by detection, switch grids, or use an identity fallback. C0 selected attack outputs and fidelity/codec controls are evaluated after selection. These proxy attacks have no original, owner, payload or detector oracle at execution.

FLUX, if preapproved and accessible, follows the same fixed quality-only selection principle on its distinct one/two/three-update grid and all 60 development sources/four marks. No eligible setting means inactive FLUX before law fitting. Its released checkpoint, native img2img support, exact scheduler, precision, access and memory must all qualify. A codec-only five-input control is included if FLUX is active, as a descriptive extra outside H4. No Sana/SD3.5 substitution follows a failed FLUX audit without a new prospective protocol.

Phase A ends with a committed freeze containing: exact code/assets/config and source hashes; native settings and noise conventions; final cell/weight list; fit/calibration IDs; kappa and q90; quality coefficients/scales/convergence; attack eligibility tables and selected settings or inactive reasons; semantic-proxy definition; adapter/pilot records; fixed missingness/retry/analysis rules; and a revised measured resource forecast. Release development detector results only after their dependent choices are frozen. New development outcomes may falsify the law, but cannot trigger undeclared model rescue. **Stop for the user before Phase B.**

## 6. Confirmatory hypotheses, error control and honest non-success

The sampling unit is the source group, not seed, output, method or detector query. Use one fixed **13-slot Holm family at alpha .05**: H1/H2/H3/H4/H6 plus A/B × four arm-specific H5 claims. Unavailable attacks keep all four slots at p=1. Composite claims use intersection-union tests (maximum component p-value); there is no independence assumption across cells or hypotheses. Exact formulas, missingness bounds and numerical reproduction are in [confirmatory-statistics.md](analysis/confirmatory-statistics.md) and [stats-design.json](analysis/stats-design.json).

| Claim | Prospective criterion |
|---|---|
| H1 | F5 post-regeneration source-majority C probability <.10 at both R1 .1 and R1 .2, counting unresolved calls as potentially positive for loss inference. Clean positivity is reported separately. |
| H2 | F5 source-majority C probability >.80 at R0 .2. |
| H3 | More than .80 of sources jointly retain F5 at R0 .2, lose it at R1 .1 even under unresolved-call bounds, and have strictly milder R1 .1 median LPIPS, PSNR and SSIM against the same marked input, with complete qualities. |
| H4 | Every native cell has absolute prediction-rate error <=.10 and family-balanced law Brier risk at least 20% below the frozen quality comparator. Numerical and statistically supported status are distinct. |
| H5 A/B × arm | More than .50 of all sources have clean-positive, completed, quality-valid removal; more than .80 meet the quality region. For A, quality and nondetection must coincide on at least two of the same three outputs. |
| H6 | VINE-R known-message presence >.90 in each of R0 .2, R1 .1 and R1 .2; specificity and native quality remain separate. |

For H4, core validation weights are 1/9 per R0/R1 cell and 1/3 on K3; with FLUX, 1/12 per R0/R1 cell and 1/4 each on K3/FLUX. Training weights remain 1/6. The mismatch probability upper bound is a conservative sufficient certificate for absolute rate bias <=.10; it is not a replacement numerical requirement of 90% agreement. The paired Brier certificate uses the prospectively fixed uniform six-bet mixture on the source contribution `.8*loss_quality-loss_law`, range [-1,.8]. Missing target outcomes use the worst feasible majority completion, not convenient zero imputations, for inferential support.

Prospective WP5 availability amendment: a functioning frozen path may sporadically abstain because a request is blocked or technically unavailable. Retain feasible law-majority sets A from the available seed forecasts, comparator set Q={q} or [0,1] when missing, and outcome-majority sets S. Compute the worst cell bias/mismatch over A×S and minimum paired advantage over A×Q×S. A missing comparator is granted its best possible prediction; a missing law bit its worst. No absent prediction is fabricated. Apply the same .10/20%, CP/betting and Holm criteria to those observed bounds. A supported result with abstentions must say `ROBUSTLY_SUPPORTED_WITH_ABSTENTIONS` and report `forecast_measurement_grid_complete=false`; it cannot claim all derivatives or forecasts were delivered. When A/Q are not singletons, full-grid point losses stay unidentified/NA. Systematically unsupported families, invalid training freezes or missing required models still give p4=1. This replaces the earlier brittle any-missing-forecast veto before any new measurement, while retaining the original numerical tolerances and the separate full-delivery flag. See the exact [statistical contract](analysis/confirmatory-statistics.md).

At n=200 and the worst Holm threshold .05/13, sufficient complete-data counts are: H1 <=9 retained sources per cell; H2/H3/each quality component >=175; H6 >=191 per cell; H5 removal >=120. Actual decisions use exact p-values and Holm ranks, not these rounded planning shortcuts. Component power at true probability .90 for an >.80 claim is about .900; at .85 it is only .188. The WP6 fixed uniform betting mixture improves the stated analytical power scenarios, with EB retained only as secondary sensitivity. Under the explicit q=.5 shared-source-error scenario, full H4 first-threshold power is .99996/.99252/.91922/.45471 at error rates .01/.02/.03/.05. A perfect law against constant baseline Brier .04 still needs n=228 for the mean component at that threshold; the fixed n=200 can remain inconclusive. No uniform superiority over EB is claimed. There is no outcome-dependent sample enlargement.

Negative gates: eight strata = four methods × C0/wrong-query. For F5/v5 the event is any-carrier A under unmarked/correct owner or marked/wrong owner. WAM/VINE use known-message presence under C0 or wrong candidate message; do not call these owner verification. Collapse all declared cells/seeds/controls to one source-any event per stratum. Gate pass requires zero observed events **and no unresolved required negative calls**. Zero of 200 gives only a marginal one-sided 95% upper bound .014867; eight simultaneous bounds give .025057. No claim of exactly zero FPR or 1e-6. Historical VINE false detection cannot be erased by a fresh clean gate.

Do not create a single misleading all-hypotheses PASS. Report each adjusted claim, numerical failure, inferentially inconclusive result, unavailable path, specificity status and missingness separately. H1–H3 may support a bounded pipeline limitation while H4 fails. H5 absolute evasion does not establish superiority over its ablations, signal erasure, cryptographic forgery or universal attack effectiveness.

A claim of a **nontrivial high-versus-low retention forecast** additionally requires supported H1 and H2 under the same Holm family. H4 alone may certify a bounded prediction error without demonstrating that contrast. If all outcomes collapse to a floor, show the constant-floor diagnostic and withhold the nontrivial mechanism claim, even if a nominal numerical prediction criterion passes. This reporting gate adds no hypothesis slot and cannot increase false discovery.
