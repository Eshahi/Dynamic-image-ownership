# Venue fit for the bounded encoder-transfer article

E2, 2026-10-25. **The present work has a plausible negative empirical article frame, but submission readiness and Q1 eligibility are not established.** The proposed transfer predictor and attacks have not been measured. Journal fit is therefore assessed separately for the evidence that exists and for a conditional future successful study.

This is a research recommendation, not an editorial assessment or an acceptance forecast. Nothing is submitted, published, paid for or communicated to an editor. The current scientific verdict remains **FAILS, ROUTE C** for F5 independent-encoder transfer; the article cannot convert that negative finding into a successful watermark claim.

## Recommendation based on the present evidence

**TIFS is the strongest subject match to investigate; IEEE Access has the clearest verified article-type match for a bounded negative result.** These answer different questions, so a single prestige-based ranking would hide the decision. TMM and TOMM are alternatives if the paper establishes a reusable multimedia evaluation or prediction contribution. PRL is a possible concise empirical/methodological route. Signal Processing: Image Communication is a further processing-oriented alternative, with more scope/policy uncertainty.

TIFS's official deep-learning guidance explicitly welcomes negative findings contradicting published research, while imposing stronger reproducibility expectations. That provision does not turn failure of our own unpublished writer into a contradiction of the literature. IEEE Access explicitly provides a Negative Result category, but still requires a meaningful, technically sound and distinct contribution. A familiar regeneration vulnerability repeated on one method may be insufficient for either venue. ## What the article must contribute

The strongest present frame is **a bounded counterexample to treating codec-round-trip retention as sufficient evidence of full-regeneration robustness**, accompanied by precise detector semantics and source-level uncertainty. It is not a claim that any cited author made that universal sufficiency claim. The related-work section must identify overlap with existing encoder/latent-proxy/rinsing work, especially the inspected rank-ratio and MarkNull manuscripts. The case's value rests on its diagnostic specificity and reproducibility, not on being the first evidence that regeneration can remove a watermark.

The historical development data show F5 C retention of 55/60 through two foreign codec round trips and 0/60 through SDXL at the evaluated strengths, with a source-paired all-metric quality contrast. They do **not** identify encoder independence as the causal variable, establish universal information erasure, demonstrate cryptographic ownership, or support an equal-quality/equal-payload/equal-FPR method ranking. The proposed signed full-chain law is an untested falsifiable extension; it cannot supply present novelty merely by being described. [Article guardrails](article-drafts/README.md), [historical-result and claim summary](FINAL-REPORT.md)

If the separately approved server study supports the frozen H4 criterion, the article may add a conditional prediction result under its measured information/cost regime. If it rejects H4, cannot compute derivatives, or lacks enough evidence, the failure must remain visible. The negative empirical article can still be evaluated on its actual contribution; the law must not be refitted after confirmation or retrospectively described as validated.

Before a defensible submission, the authors need a complete integrated manuscript, a precise comparison to closest prior work, reproducibility materials with failure accounting, source-level uncertainty, and current journal-policy verification. Fresh confirmation is scientifically useful here, but no inspected publisher rule universally requires this particular server protocol. Submission, authorship, publication timing, fees and institutional decisions remain with the user. E3 provides a separate hostile review of this article frame.
