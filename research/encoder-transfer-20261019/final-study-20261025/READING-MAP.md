## Authority and provenance

The final protocol states its own precedence among the integrated contracts. Earlier provisional forms in derivations are preserved as history and are not alternate implementation choices. Later explanatory notes cannot override that precedence. Hypotheses were committed before the new raw-journal analysis; the source-selection rule was committed before the identifier draw. Both facts are recorded in PROGRESS, while the extensive prior use of the development sources remains disclosed.

Use the exact evidence type: **retained measured result**, **post-hoc analysis**, **conditional derivation**, **proposed future test**, **primary source report**, or **unverified lead**. None is interchangeable with a human judgment, a thesis lifecycle gate or authorization to spend money.
