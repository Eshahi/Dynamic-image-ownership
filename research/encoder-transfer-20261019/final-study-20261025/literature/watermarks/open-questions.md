# Bounded open questions

1. **Encoder independence:** Which released autoencoders are genuinely independently trained, rather than checkpoints, fine-tunes or reuses of one KL-f8 lineage? Model-family labels alone cannot settle this. The literature cards identify where papers do not establish that provenance.
2. **Causal localization:** Can F5's response be separated into encoder projection, decoder reconstruction and diffusion denoising effects under fixed noise and quality? Existing SDXL end-to-end failure does not answer which component causes failure.
3. **Insertion-site interaction:** Stable Signature and LaWa give different same-autoencoder outcomes. Is the relevant interaction a learned decoder artifact, multi-scale message placement or an encoder-aligned image residual? This needs a crossed design, not pooled watermark-family rhetoric.
4. **Null calibration:** How much do empirical wrong-owner, unmarked and near-duplicate rates depart from independent-bit/isotropic-feature models under the actual deployment distribution? Tiny analytic FPRs should not replace uncertainty bounds from declared negatives.
5. **Information matching:** What performance is attainable when inversion-based methods, learned extractors and registry-assisted checks receive comparable side information and compute? Report each arm's resource set before interpreting an accuracy difference.
6. **Content binding:** How does the registry arm compare with SEAL's content-derived initial noise under copied marks, semantically similar images and regenerate-and-enroll? This is a separate question from resistance to removal.
7. **Recent work:** DiffMark/BiSLW leads are unverified; recent primary venues and titles in the search log need follow-up before any novelty language. The present dossier intentionally makes no priority claim.

These questions guide a later approved server protocol. No experiments, model passes, software installs or code/model/data downloads were performed by this subtask.
