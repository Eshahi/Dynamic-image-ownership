**Threat and method — direct evidence (§§4–5):** A no-box adversary has the image and a clean public proxy diffusion model, but no target detector queries, target gradients, watermark key or paired clean images. MarkNull encodes and DDIM-inverts through the proxy, then optimizes a latent with a noise–latent alignment loss and LPIPS/MSE/SSIM fidelity terms. MarkNull-A amortizes this with a trained Restormer. Its post-hoc effect includes the proxy VAE bottleneck.

**Checked experiment (§7/Table 2):** Nine schemes, 100 images each at 512². Post-hoc images are generated by SDXL and then marked; default attack proxy is SD1.5. MarkNull's Table 2 bit accuracies are DwtDct 55.10%, RivaGAN 59.78%, HiDDeN 60.62%, and average across eight multi-bit schemes 53.14%; the ninth Tree-Ring is zero-bit and its postattack TPR@1%FPR is 43%. Appendix E.2 swaps in FLUX.1-dev proxy against SD2.1 targets: Gaussian Shading 56.95%, PRC 52.65%, Stable Signature 46.67% bit accuracy, while acknowledging fidelity cost.

**Limit / inference:** This is the closest inspected cross-backbone informed attack, but no F5 and no matched SDXL-img2img experiment. Do not treat mean bit accuracy as ownership verification or detector TPR. §6 Assumption 2 explicitly requires proxy and target to share the *same latent-space parameterization and discrete inversion scheme* (noise schedule and solver), plus a small vector-field discrepancy. Thus its theorem does not prove transfer across genuinely independent encoders; Appendix E.2's FLUX-to-SD2.1 experiment is empirical evidence, not a consequence of that theorem. §6's post-hoc proof further assumes imperceptible residuals predominantly lie off the semantic manifold. §5's inference of independence from high-dimensional orthogonality needs a distributional assumption beyond geometry alone. **Agent inference:** its cross-family success motivates a server-only proxy-latent attack against F5, but does not establish why F5 fails under SDXL regeneration.
