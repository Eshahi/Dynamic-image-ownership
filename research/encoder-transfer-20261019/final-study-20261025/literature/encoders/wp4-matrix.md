## Handoff for WP4

**Best practical new full-pipeline candidate, conditional:** SD3.5 Medium has an official strength-based img2img implementation and no visible FLUX access gate on the inspected model card, but its precise AE provenance and server memory must be checked before designating it an independently trained AE. SDXL and the FLUX.1 family have primary statements of *from-scratch AE weight independence*; the exact schnell released AE identity with the FLUX paper's evaluated AE still needs checking. Sber-MoVQGAN gives a separate training account and different codec, but Kandinsky 3 has already been run. Sana is the strongest architectural contrast for an AE-only mechanism arm, yet lacks a verified comparable strength-based full img2img pipeline. FLUX.1-schnell requires the user to accept its HF access conditions and is resource-heavy; do not imply Apache-2.0 means ungated.

**Comparability rule:** Archive exact model/card/config revision, AE weights provenance, scheduler config and actual start sigma; measure AE-only retention and full-pipeline retention separately. Match pixel-space distortion distributions within source rather than treating equal nominal strength as matched severity. Log resolution and prompting. A newly selected third family is an out-of-family challenge test, not a causal isolate of the AE.
