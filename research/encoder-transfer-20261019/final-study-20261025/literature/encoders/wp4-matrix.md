# Independent encoder and third-family feasibility audit (2026-10-25 UTC)

This is source inspection only. `V2` refers to method **and** experiment sections of the cited paper; model-card and API facts are separate source evidence. “Independent” means different AE training weights, not disjoint image datasets. None of these papers measures F5 or proves that identical image-to-image `strength` means identical perturbation severity.

**WP4 correction, 2026-10-25:** the completed [third-family audit](../../06-third-family.md) supersedes this initial discovery matrix. ## Handoff for WP4

**Completed WP4 recommendation:** conditional FLUX.1-schnell, with personally accepted access conditions, an expanded download allowance and server-only resource/severity checks. Otherwise retain Kandinsky 3 as a family excluded from law fitting, while disclosing its known development results. SD3.5 is also gated. Ordinary Sana lacks the required native img2img path; Sana-Sprint has one but its native few-step severity and exact AE lineage remain limiting. See the completed audit for that distinct candidate. No causal AE-only claim or unobserved-family claim is implied.

**Comparability rule:** Archive exact model/card/config revision, AE weights provenance, scheduler config and actual start sigma; measure AE-only retention and full-pipeline retention separately. Match pixel-space distortion distributions within source rather than treating equal nominal strength as matched severity. Log resolution and prompting. A newly selected third family is an out-of-family challenge test, not a causal isolate of the AE.
