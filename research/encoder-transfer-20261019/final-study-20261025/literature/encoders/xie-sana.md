# Xie et al. 2024 — Sana (V2)

- **Exact source:** Enze Xie, Junsong Chen, Junyu Chen, Han Cai, Haotian Tang, Yujun Lin, Zhekai Zhang, Muyang Li, Ligeng Zhu, Yao Lu, Song Han, *Sana: Efficient High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Linear Diffusion Transformers*, arXiv:2410.10629v1, 2024. https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10629v1. Accessed 2026-10-25 UTC.
- **Method inspected:** §2.1, §3.2. Sana's DC-AE uses f32, 32 channels and DiT patch size 1, versus typical f8 four/sixteen channels with patch 2. It replaces the generative backbone with a linear-attention transformer and uses flow training plus Flow-DPM-Solver sampling. §2.1.3 describes multistage AE training with a 1024px fine-tune.
- **Experiment inspected:** §2.1/Table 1 AE reconstruction and §5.1/Table 7 generation metrics. §5.1 compares token count and throughput when moving from f8c4p2 to f32c32p1. These are synthesis/efficiency tests, not watermark retention or matched img2img.
- **Direct evidence:** The authors describe training their new f32 DC-AE; architecture and dimensionality differ drastically from SD1.5. The exact initialization of the distributed Sana AE weights relative to SD1.x is **not stated** in this paper; source/checkpoint provenance should be checked separately.
- **Inference for this study:** Sana is potentially a high-separation third encoder. Its flow sampler, f32 compression, altered resolution and prompt model introduce multiple simultaneous changes. It should only be interpreted as a new full pipeline unless the server also measures the AE-only path.
- **Limits:** Published throughput on a 4090 is not a future img2img cost estimate. The 600M checkpoint's model card mentions Gemma-2 terms even though the model card labels the checkpoint Apache-2.0. No supported strength-based img2img path has yet been verified here.
