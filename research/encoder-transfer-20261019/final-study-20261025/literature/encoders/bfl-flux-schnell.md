# Black Forest Labs 2025 — FLUX.1 autoencoder (V2, with schnell checkpoint caveat)

- **Exact primary source:** Black Forest Labs (Stephen Batifol et al.), *FLUX.1 Kontext: Flow Matching for In-Context Image Generation and Editing in Latent Space*, arXiv:2506.15742v2, 2025, https://arxiv.org/html/2506.15742v2. Accessed 2026-10-25 UTC. **Checkpoint source:** official [FLUX.1-schnell card](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-schnell) and [repository tree](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-schnell/tree/main), same access date.
- **Method inspected:** Paper §2 says BFL trained the FLUX.1 convolutional autoencoder **from scratch** with an adversarial objective, using 16 latent channels. §3 says the Kontext model freezes this autoencoder while training an editing model from a FLUX.1 text-to-image checkpoint. Appendix A describes rectified-flow noising and shifted timestep distributions. The paper's statement is strong developer testimony for an AE with weights independent of SD1.5, though it does not supply a full training ledger or dataset overlap analysis.
- **Experiment inspected:** Paper §2/Table 1 and Appendix B compare reconstruction of FLUX-VAE, SD3-VAE, SDXL-VAE and SD-VAE on 4,096 random ImageNet evaluation images at 256×256. FLUX-VAE PSNR 31.1±0.08, SSIM .896±.004, perceptual distance .332±.003; SD3-VAE PSNR 29.6±.07; SDXL-VAE 25.9±.07; SD-VAE 25.0±.07. These measure reconstruction quality, **not mark survival**. The later paper's editing results concern Kontext and cannot be transferred to schnell.
- **Direct evidence for schnell:** Its official card calls schnell a 12B rectified-flow transformer distilled to 1–4 steps and lists Apache-2.0. BFL's [inference source `util.py`](https://github.com/black-forest-labs/flux/blob/main/src/flux/util.py) maps `flux-schnell` to `ae.safetensors` with `z_channels=16`, `scale_factor=0.3611`, `shift_factor=0.1159`; this strengthens family/config linkage but does not prove byte-identical AE weights with the Kontext evaluation. The card page says file access requires login and acceptance of sharing Hugging Face account information.
- **Inference for this study:** The FLUX.1 family now has explicit primary evidence of a fresh AE, stronger than model-card inference. **Exact schnell AE weight identity with the FLUX-VAE evaluated in Kontext Table 1 remains to be checked** from checkpoint revision/config/source. Distilled 1–4-step `FluxImg2ImgPipeline` may make low nominal strengths too coarse for a matched R0 comparison. Actual start sigma, achieved distortion and source quality are necessary.
- **Resource/access limits:** Official tree displays 23.8 GB transformer and 335 MB AE before text encoders/optional files. Peak VRAM and runtime unmeasured. A human account action is needed for the gate. The [Diffusers Flux API](https://huggingface.co/docs/diffusers/api/pipelines/flux) documents image plus strength, but an API contract does not prove scientific severity equivalence with SD1.5 DDIM.
