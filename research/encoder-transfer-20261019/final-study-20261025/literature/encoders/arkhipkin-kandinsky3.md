# Arkhipkin et al. 2024 — Kandinsky 3.0 and Sber-MoVQGAN (V2)

- **Exact source:** Vladimir Arkhipkin, Andrei Filatov, Viacheslav Vasilev, Anastasia Maltseva, Said Azizov, Igor Pavlov, Julia Agafonova, Andrey Kuznetsov, Denis Dimitrov, *Kandinsky 3.0 Technical Report*, arXiv:2312.03511v3, 2024. https://arxiv.org/html/2312.03511v3. Accessed 2026-10-25 UTC.
- **Method inspected:** §3.1, §3.4, §4. Kandinsky 3.0 uses the 270M Sber-MoVQGAN decoder, frozen during denoiser training; this is a VQGAN-derived architecture with MoVQ-style spatially conditional normalization and EMA. Authors say they developed/trained Sber-MoVQGAN on LAION HighRes; it is **not the Zheng et al. MoVQ model's checkpoint**. The base denoiser uses its own latent representation and U-Net. Reported image conditioning uses separate editing/inpainting mechanisms (§6), so distinguish ordinary img2img from those tasks.
- **Experiment inspected:** §3.4/Table 2 compares ImageNet autoencoder reconstruction: Sber-MoVQGAN 270M, 32×32 latent at 256×256, 16,384-entry codebook, FID .686, SSIM .741, PSNR 27.04. §7 human side-by-side compares generated image quality; neither tests cross-AE watermark retention.
- **Direct evidence:** Sber AE separately developed/trained, quantized VQ-family distinct from SD1.5 KL VAE. Paper gives training dataset and codebook size but not a complete checkpoint lineage proof; avoid claiming zero shared training images (LAION-derived image pools may overlap).
- **Inference for this study:** The Kandinsky path is a stronger structural mismatch than SDXL. A drop under full Kandinsky img2img cannot isolate quantization from its scheduler/denoiser. A MoVQ-only round trip can help separate these on the future server.
- **Limits:** Paper's “MoVQ” is an architectural influence, not evidence that it reuses Zheng's trained weights; exact deployed Kandinsky 3 checkpoint/config must be identified. Sber-MoVQGAN is quantized rather than Gaussian KL latent, making direct per-channel gain comparison nontrivial.
