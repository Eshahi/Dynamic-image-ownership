## Source digest (under 200 words)

SFW enforces conjugate symmetry to preserve a real inverse Fourier latent instead of discarding imaginary spatial components after arbitrary complex edits. HSTR combines this with Tree-Ring and a central 44×44 latent region. HSQR splits a QR pattern between real/imaginary free-half components and imposes signs while preserving sampled magnitudes. Its reported configuration is version-1, 21×21 QR, level H, 2×2 samples per cell; the paper calls nominal capacity 72 bits. Detection uses DDIM inversion and Fourier-region L1 distance; identification selects the nearest reference key, normally among 2,048. Generation/inversion use SD2.1-base and 50 steps. Regeneration comprises Bmshj18 and Cheng20 compression at quality 3 plus Zhao-style diffusion at 60 steps. MS-COCO HSQR identification is .980/.987/.999 for these three channels (Table 2); these are closed-set identity results, not unrestricted payload recovery. Supplement Table 10 varies diffusion noise step 20–180; it does not identify an independently trained attacker autoencoder. [Primary text, §§3–5, Eq. 1/3/4, Table 2; supplement §8.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07647v1).

## What the public implementation establishes

- **QR encoding versus operational correction.** [utils.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thomas11809/SFWMark/main/src/utils.py), `QRCodeGenerator` / `make_hsqr_pattern` (web lines 310–335), genuinely requests `ERROR_CORRECT_H` and encodes strings `HSQR{idx % 10000}`. Thus QR redundancy exists in the carrier. `get_distance_hsqr` (598–624) instead compares the Fourier query to a ±45 complex reference; it does not correct a recovered QR message. Importing `pyzbar.decode` is not evidence of its use. [detect.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thomas11809/SFWMark/main/src/detect.py), 50–61, 102–104, 133–145, loads reference patterns, inverts the suspect, then loops over every candidate and takes argmin. This evaluated path supplies no demonstrated QR-decoder error-correction radius or arbitrary-message decoding success.
- **Exact named checkpoints.** [generate.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thomas11809/SFWMark/main/src/generate.py), 28–35, names `stabilityai/stable-diffusion-2-1-base`; `detect.py`, 33–40, does likewise. [diff_wm_attack.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thomas11809/SFWMark/main/src/diff_attack/diff_wm_attack.py), 20–24, names `stabilityai/stable-diffusion-2-1`, `revision="fp16"`, and `noise_step=60`. The latter is not a statement of 60 reverse inference steps. No immutable model revision, VAE weight hash, or encoder-training lineage is established by these inspected files. Distinct checkpoint names cannot establish independent encoder training.

## Bounded author interpretation

A pool of 2,048 distinguishable identifiers conveys at most log2(2048)=11 identity bits; the largest reported pool, 8,192, means 13. These are derived pool entropies, not measured Shannon capacities. The nominal 72-bit QR claim, 441 modules, fourfold sample replication, identifier entropy and recovered user payload are different quantities. A successful candidate lookup does not demonstrate recovery of arbitrary 72-bit messages. Neither method establishes image-content binding or lawful ownership through those identification results.

For a real signal, the DFT must satisfy conjugacy at paired frequencies, with real coefficients at self-conjugate bins. Conjugacy prevents an avoidable representation loss; it alone implies neither Gaussianity nor survival through a nonlinear encoder/denoiser. Gaussianity additionally concerns joint distributions, not merely real-valued output. The paper's robustness gains are empirical within its evaluated channels.

F5 already applies an orthonormal **real DCT** to the SD1.5 posterior-mode latent; its real-linear coefficient operator does not have an imaginary-discard problem. Consequently, SFW is not a missing repair for F5. Its 32 blocks each choose between two ten-chip sign vectors; this is not QR ECC or a nearest-valid-codeword correction stage. A larger searched score can coexist with word changes and failed semantic binding.
