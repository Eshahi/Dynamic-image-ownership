## Source digest (under 200 words)

TSA pairs equal-norm angularly perturbed Gaussian seeds and uses 100-step deterministic DDIM. Final classifier labels define flip/stable cohorts. Secant responses decompose into longitudinal and transverse defects; exact-score linearization connects them to directional score-Jacobian/Hessian probes. Learned nonconservative denoisers require the empirical Jacobian interpretation. Reported transverse onset is signal fraction 1.7e-4, versus state RMSE near 9.5e-3. Cohort onset precedes useful individual classification. Leave-cache-seed-out linear probes and equal-covariance Mahalanobis/LDA give AUC-curve RMS disagreement 0.012. Mechanism/probe analysis uses LDM-CelebA, ten cache seeds, 1,000 pairs each. Evaluation is unconditional; causal steering and guided quantitative replication are untested. ## Interpretation for this dossier (our inference)

The relevant warning is statistical: a reliably nonzero conditional mean difference can coexist with strongly overlapping individual distributions. Increasing cohort size can expose a tiny effect while leaving individual rankings poor. Thus a mechanism plot stratified by completed regeneration outcomes cannot substitute for prospective prediction of an unseen source's detector outcome. Computing diagnostics after seeing all outcomes is legitimate exploratory analysis if labelled accordingly, but is not a forecast validation.

For the fixed F5 mark, the analogy concerns how to assess a response diagnostic. It supplies no evidence that F5 survives regeneration, that semantic commitment preserves a bit string, or that our proposed predictor works. Class flips between nearby generation seeds and detector margins for a marked image under img2img are different targets, perturbations and chains. Even their independent units differ: holding out cache-generation seeds cannot substitute for holding out image sources in our study.

The dossier's proposed full-chain JVP predictor is still unmeasured and privileged: target-model derivatives and C0 reference information are available to the analyst. A directional response of the denoiser alone is not the full encoder–denoising–decoder–detector response. Our predictor must be fixed using its permitted fitting data, then predict the later source-level detector outcomes under the dossier's existing split and rules. A retrospective relationship between derivative summaries and survival is insufficient. No new protocol amendment is made here.

Generic directional sensitivity analysis cannot be claimed as new on this basis. Any possible contribution must be narrower and measured, such as incremental prediction of the fixed F5 detector outcome over the frozen simpler comparators under independent-encoder transfer. This card establishes neither novelty nor that empirical result.
