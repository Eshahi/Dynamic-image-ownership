# Statistical primary-source locators

These are mathematical-method sources, not empirical watermark-comparison cards. Their proofs were inspected; no watermark robustness claim is imported from them.

- **Maurer and Pontil (COLT 2009):** [original PDF](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~colt2009/papers/012.pdf), Theorem 4, sample-variance definition and Section 2. The rescaled one-sided bound and its conditional iid assumptions are derived in [confirmatory-statistics.md](../../analysis/confirmatory-statistics.md). Its calculation is secondary after the WP6 prospective change.
- **Waudby-Smith and Ramdas, arXiv v7 (25 August 2022):** [original PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.09686v7), Section 4 and Appendix A.3–A.4; also the RSS discussion-paper version linked in [betting-mean-design.md](../../analysis/betting-mean-design.md). The present uniform six-bet mixture is a simple fixed-n specialization with its own displayed proof. No optimized strategy or time-uniform stopping rule from that paper is claimed to be implemented. Publisher-version full text was inaccessible.

Both source identities and exact access paths are retained in the shared search log and bibliography. The independent WP6 reviewer checked the new mixture proof and exact analytical boundary calculations separately.
