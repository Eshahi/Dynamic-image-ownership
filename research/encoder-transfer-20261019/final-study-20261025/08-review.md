## 4. Mathematics and forecasting contract

**Transport.** The paired sampled-posterior displacement includes both the mean difference and `(sigma_mark−sigma_clean)*eta`. Shared initial/per-step tensors remove direct paired differences in additive draws, not their influence on the nonlinear background trajectory. The proposed JVP is through the complete unmarked downstream program; for a multistep solver its state includes the required history. A seed integer alone is insufficient. The exact finite encoder displacement used by the main law differs from the differential encoder direction in the mechanism controls, explaining the distinct JVP counts.

The DCT chip transform is linear under its specified mode/scaling/normalization, but the unchanged decoder, word search and content binding are not. For a 320-sign vector, `w'p/||p||=sqrt(320)*cos(w,p)`; positive global scaling cancels. Disjoint weighted orthonormal DCT slots support the stated normalized row geometry. This does not make latent norm a sufficient detector statistic. The main text correctly says literal RGB8 rounding has zero derivative almost everywhere and treats the floating path as an approximation whose remainder must be measured.

**Fitting and information barriers.** The scalar weighted least-squares expression for kappa is correct for the specified observed-chip objective, without positivity clipping. It is not allowed to use realized marked-target quality. Its different legitimate feature/label mask is disclosed. Qualification and both-class requirements precede release. These procedures estimate the fixed fitted predictors' later risk, not an unbiased universal physical coefficient.

The calibration radius is rank 28 of all 30 source maxima; unknown maxima become positive infinity. Zero/one/two/three incomplete sources give the stated finite/infinite boundary.
