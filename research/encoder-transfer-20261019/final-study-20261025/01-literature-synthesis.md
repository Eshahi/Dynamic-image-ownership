# Literature synthesis: the missing quantity is readable transport

WP1, 2026-10-25. This is a bounded primary-source review, not a claim of exhaustive coverage or independent reproduction. Searches and versions are registered in [the search log](literature/search-log.md). Thirty primary papers in clusters 1–3 have method and experiment inspection: ten attack/benchmark cards, twelve watermark cards and eight encoder cards. Six further verified theory/transfer cards are retained; the rank-ratio lead is excluded because bibliographic identity remains unresolved. V2 means inspected, not necessarily peer reviewed or true.

## The question that survives the literature

The question is **when a fixed, image-domain watermark optimized through one encoder remains readable after another complete generative pipeline**, and whether a low-dimensional model predicts that outcome before observing it. F5 is such a writer; it is not initial-noise watermarking, not a decoder-trained mark, and not the proposal's unimplemented latent-insertion/lightweight-extraction method. The study is about T3 removal. Ownership, forgery resistance, T4/T5 and the separate registry system are outside its new evidence.

The retained development evidence is a useful challenge to a simple explanation: F5 source-majority semantic C survives SDXL and MoVQ round trips at 55/60, while SDXL full img2img gives 0/60 and Kandinsky full img2img .2 gives 5/60. Those are compound-pipeline observations, not an encoder-only causal contrast ([results](../results.md), [stage 2b](../results-2b.md), [T7 tables](../report/tables.md)). Any explanation based only on “an independent encoder destroys the residual” must account for the round-trip controls.

## Findings that change the design

**Own-encoder survival has no universal sign.** Stable Signature's own-autoencoder removal finding conflicts with a generic “same encoder protects a mark” story; LaWa reports different retention under its corresponding codec. The insertion site, trained objective and readout determine what “aligned” means. F5's objective is a signed, whitened, selected-chip margin through an encoder, not the largest singular value of that encoder. Box projection, quantization and its auxiliary tier further prevent identifying its final residual with a top singular vector. A chain rule for the complete detector is needed.

**MarkNull is close prior art, not a ready-made proof.** It is the closest inspected no-box proxy-latent manipulation attack. Its transfer theorem assumes shared latent parameterization and inversion scheme, and its post-hoc argument adds an off-manifold premise. Cross-backbone evidence is empirical. The final protocol should test a generic latent intervention with controlled distortion; it must not inherit an independent-encoder guarantee from that theorem ([card and exact locators](literature/attacks/marknull.md)).

**Rinsing is an established attack family.** Differences between Zhao's Tree-Ring results and WAVES rinsing are not clean contradictions: attack recipes, evaluation populations and operating points differ. A two-pass attack can be a useful stress test, but is not itself a new contribution. Multiplying single-pass retention probabilities assumes independence that repeated application to the same evolving image does not supply.

**Architecture independence is partly verified.** SDXL explicitly describes a from-scratch autoencoder. The FLUX.1 Kontext paper provides the same kind of primary statement for FLUX.1, but exact identity of the released schnell AE still requires a pinned-checkpoint check. SD2.x shares SD1.x codec lineage. SD3.5's released initialization, Sana's exact released lineage and Stable Cascade's stage provenance remain qualified in the [matrix](literature/encoders/wp4-matrix.md). A different latent width is evidence of architectural difference, not by itself a complete training-provenance record.

**Information theory does not supply the missing empirical parameters.** [Jeon et al.](literature/transfer-theory/jeon-limits.md) study a constrained latent embedding channel with specified distribution and renderer assumptions. F5 does not meet those premises. [Transfer theory](literature/transfer-theory/demontis.md) motivates directional alignment; [Tramèr](literature/transfer-theory/tramer.md) supplies counterexamples to inevitable transfer; [Ilyas](literature/transfer-theory/ilyas.md) shows shared brittle features can matter. None gives the F5 cross-family Jacobians, denoiser response or threshold-crossing distribution. [PhotoGuard](literature/transfer-theory/photoguard.md) establishes encoder-targeted image perturbations as prior methodology, with a different editing-prevention objective. [Radioactivity](literature/transfer-theory/radioactivity.md) concerns retraining on marked images, a distinct channel.

## Reporting requirements carried into WP2–WP5

1. Preserve native operating points and disclose side information. F5/v5 C are compound semantic/content-binding predicates; WAM/VINE known-message detection is not the same task. Do not rank them as equal-payload, equal-quality or equal-FPR systems.
2. Distinguish embedding quality, attack-to-mark quality and total-to-original quality. No metric replaces missing human judgments. Use source-level comparisons; the three seeds are repeated measurements.
3. Report carrier evidence, decoded content consistency and final C separately. A zero final endpoint can conceal a readable but inconsistent carrier or a failed auxiliary condition.
4. Use observed negative calls and exact source-level bounds. Analytic independent-bit tails and computational PRC guarantees do not calibrate this detector's fixed-owner, image-distribution FPR.
5. Fit only what is observed. Family labels can diagnose unexplained structure but cannot stand in for measured mechanistic transport. Leave unmeasured gain/alignment parameters missing until the approved server phase.
6. Keep failures, missing outputs, safety blocks and non-overlap visible. A tiny matched-distortion subset does not support a population-wide causal comparison.

The unresolved [rank-ratio manuscript](literature/transfer-theory/rank-ratio-unverified.md) is a close discovery lead. It prevents an unqualified priority assertion while primary bibliographic verification is unavailable; its numbers and claims are not adopted as evidence. New search leads marked V0/V1 remain outside the substantive synthesis.
