# Singh (2026) — V2

Pranav Singh. *What Fixed-Rollout pass@k Evaluations Can Identify*. [Primary v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09245v1). Cache ID `singh2026.html`; CC BY 4.0. Preprint, not peer-review certification.

Inspected 2026-10-20: sections 2-4 (conditional-iid mixture and moment identification), section 5 calibration, Appendix B numerical controls and Appendix C certificate/evaluation. Fixed rollout depth identifies only a finite set of mixing moments under the paper's pooled random-task experiment. Evaluation uses released repeated-rollout configurations and precision/control checks; the text distinguishes fixed labeled benchmark likelihood from a random-task count law. These distinctions matter more here than its numerical LLM examples.

Use: beta mixing supplies unobserved higher-order structure; it is an assumption, not identified merely from mean and ICC. Do not extrapolate three fixed image seeds into arbitrary regeneration sampling reliability. Our three-seed majority is directly measured, unlike the paper's beyond-depth pass@k target; its nonidentification theorem is not asserted to apply verbatim. We retain sharp finite-three-variable bounds derived for the actual endpoint.
