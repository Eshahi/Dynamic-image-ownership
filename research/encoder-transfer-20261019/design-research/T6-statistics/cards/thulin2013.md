# Thulin (2013 preprint; 2014 journal) — V2

Måns Thulin. *The cost of using exact confidence intervals for a binomial proportion*. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1303.1288). Cache IDs `thulin2013.html`, `thulin2014.pdf`. License: arXiv perpetual non-exclusive distribution license, not an unrestricted reuse license.

Inspected 2026-10-20: sections 2.1-2.3 (binomial-tail inversion and competing intervals), 3.1-3.3 and Figures 1-3 (expected-length and sample-size evaluation), plus one-sided formulas in section 4. Exact equal-tail intervals preserve minimum binomial coverage at the cost of width. Numerical comparisons assess asymptotic length/sample-size approximations against exact calculations, including a case requiring 329 trials versus 331 from the approximation.

Use: retain preregistered Clopper-Pearson intervals; check endpoint formulas and distinguish one-sided zero-count bounds from two-sided intervals. Our planning uses direct finite sums, not the paper's asymptotic sample-size approximation. The result assumes a binomial sampling model; it gives no guarantee for correlated sources or a deterministically selected development cohort. Replaces unrefreshed Brown-card reliance for this topic.
