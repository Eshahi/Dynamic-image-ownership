# Elmore (2020) — V2

Ryan Elmore. *Modeling Sums of Exchangeable Binary Variables*. [Primary full text, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.11657v2). Cache ID `exchangeable2020.html`; arXiv perpetual non-exclusive license.

Inspected 2026-10-20: sections 2.1-2.2, equations (1)-(3), section 3 simulation, and section 4 examples. The exact count law depends on joint all-success probabilities through all orders. A Laplace-gamma approximation is compared with beta-binomial, folded-logistic and q-power distributions; simulations use 1,000 samples per setting, with 10/15 trials and sample size 100, assessing success-probability and correlation estimation. Two empirical examples can fit several dependence models similarly.

Use: a seed mean and pairwise ICC do not identify the third-order probability needed for a 2-of-3 majority. T6 therefore supplies sharp finite-exchangeable bounds alongside beta-mixture sensitivity. The bounds are derived locally from nonnegative probabilities of 0/1/2/3 successes, not credited as a theorem proved specifically for image seeds in this paper. No LapGam model is fitted or recommended for our cohort.
