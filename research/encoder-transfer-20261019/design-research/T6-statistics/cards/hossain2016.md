# Hossain, Diaz Ordaz and Bartlett (2016) — V2

*Missing binary outcomes under covariate dependent missingness in cluster randomised trials*. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1608.05289). Cache ID `hossain2016.html`; arXiv perpetual non-exclusive license.

Inspected 2026-10-20: sections 3-4 (complete records, multilevel imputation and validity conditions), 5.1-5.3 and Table 2 (simulation design and results). Four scenarios cross common/differential missingness and covariate interactions; analyses compare cluster-level methods, random-effects logistic regression and GEE. Evaluation uses 1,000 repetitions and reports bias, standard errors and coverage; complete records can bias risk differences even when some other estimands remain valid under additional assumptions.

Use: missingness mechanism and estimand must be stated. Accordingly, T6 includes both seed-level and whole-source loss, plus outcome-dependent loss sensitivity. No complete-case estimate rescues the fixed failure-imputed rule. We do not adopt clinical randomization assumptions, multiple imputation, or claim that their coverage applies to image seeds. The loss-risk ratios in our tables are hypothetical, not estimates from this paper.
