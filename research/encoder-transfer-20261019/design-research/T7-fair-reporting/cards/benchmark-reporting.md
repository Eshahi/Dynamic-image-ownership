# Reporting evidence extension

WAVES, [arXiv 2401.08573v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08573v3), V2: method §§3.1–3.2, evaluation §4 and Appendices E/F; refreshed by parent. Its joint robustness/quality design and four-category normalization support separate quality panels here. Its large calibration population and full attack suite are absent, so this study cannot reproduce its normalized leaderboard or validate its low-FPR point. Extends T1's benchmark card and the dossier's An2024 card.

VINE/W-Bench, [arXiv 2410.18775v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18775v2), V2: §§3.3,4.1–4.3, Table1 and AppendixD. The native resolution adaptation and explicit payload/quality/detection columns motivate our operating-point table. Aggregates over multiple edits/severities are not direct predictions for SDXL .2. Extends T3's VINE card; all numerical claims remain there.

WAM, [arXiv 2411.07231v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.07231v2), inherited T3 V2 §§3–5 and AppendixG.3. Localization presence and candidate-message agreement answer different questions; the released MIT checkpoint's calibration remains unverified. The native paper/code discrepancy is documented in T3, not repaired by changing its decoder.

Design choices, not published standards: the exact LPIPS bins, three calipers, matching score, minimum20 sparse-subset label and fixed illustration IDs are preregistered by this study. They are not claims of human perceptual equivalence.
