# T4 pre-outcome prediction matrix

All entries below are falsifiable **working hypotheses**, not results, theorem guarantees, promised effect sizes or new pass/fail gates. Unless specifically stated, direction refers to descriptive counts at the frozen endpoint and all 60 development sources stay in the denominator. No power claim is made. A clear reverse direction is disconfirming for the stated mechanism; a tie often leaves the mechanism unresolved. Execution errors or unusable image quality cannot be relabeled as successful carrier removal. The core's sole primary endpoint and routing rule remain unchanged.

## Endpoints, shared controls and side information

F5 primary C is `semantic.read AND semantic.content_match`, source success at least two of seeds 12/13/14. S uses `found` instead of `read`; Q tests exact decoded content code. A is any semantic or instance `found` and is the conservative negative endpoint. Internal readers use the supplied OwnerID/profile and pinned feature models, without a clean reference. WAM32 and VINE-R100 common presence uses their frozen payload thresholds; native WAM detection, native decoding and exact recovery remain separate. External payloads, quality and detector side information differ from F5, so their comparison is a practical benchmark, not a matched-message-capacity experiment.

Per internal arm/channel/strength, report `missing_call`, `raw_unavailable`, `not_found`, `found_not_read`, `read_content_mismatch`, `C_success`. Missing/error calls and absent raw fields have their own coverage, never inferred negatives. For valid raw fields prioritize `read` and content-match first, then `found`; C can literally be true even if found is false. Add `read_without_found` as a diagnostic counter, and Q separately. No extra reader call is required. Readable-but-content-mismatched means verification failed; it does not mean the carrier disappeared. C-success without Q is not exact-message recovery.

Across **every active R1 strength**, F5 C0 and wrong-owner calls are predicted to have A=false. Any A=true disconfirms the specificity expectation and blocks TRANSFERS; a missing call leaves the gate unresolved. No majority rule can hide one such call. For the other channels and arms, negatives are descriptive and cannot silently enlarge or weaken this gate. A clean C0 image after regeneration may acquire accidental evidence; this is why C0 is regenerated too.

## P5 analytic check: why smoothing is not guaranteed

For four independent Gaussian vectors eta_j and unclipped affine E(u)=Au+b,

`(1/8) sum_j [E(u+eta_j)+E(u-eta_j)] = E(u)`.

Thus the same paired construction leaves `E(u+delta)-E(u)=A delta` exactly unchanged. For smooth nonlinear E, each pair cancels the odd noise term but leaves curvature terms; expected bias begins with `(sigma^2/2) Laplacian E(u)`. The derivative of this bias need not contract the watermark direction. Finite pairs have sampling error; clipping at image bounds changes the expansion. At high pixel dimension, per-coordinate sigma 2/255 is not a global L2 radius 2/255. Cohen's smoothed-classifier probability certificate does not apply to this continuous average. This derivation motivates the expanded mode control and makes an unchanged result scientifically informative.

## Additional discovered adaptive limitation, outside the proposal set

LFM (T4-11) motivates a future public latent-band replacement attack against the known F5 carrier. The testable lead is readable-carrier loss with scene utility retained, beyond AE-only loss. Survival or removal only at unusable quality would weaken practical exploitability. Its published mask search uses verifier access and calibration images, so a faithful test needs a separate frozen protocol, calibration split and cost. No P11, extra attack code or compute has been added. P5 smoothing is not a substitute for that different adaptive threat.
