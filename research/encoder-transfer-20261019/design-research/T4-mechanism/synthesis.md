## Mechanistic conclusion before outcomes

The justified question is whether **this fixed F5 writer's readable, content-bound evidence survives this independently trained SDXL regeneration pipeline**. F5 deliberately increases SD1.5 latent evidence per visible perturbation. It may survive by shared encoder directions, by features retained during denoising, by carrier redundancy, or by some combination. Independent encoder training makes the test valuable, but does not mathematically force those directions to be orthogonal. A transfer pass does not establish a genuinely semantic arbitrary-message channel; a failure does not isolate the encoder from the decoder, resizing, posterior sampling, denoising and content-binding reader.

Three competing explanations remain live. H-A: adequate amplitude in the attack encoder and final reader directions largely explains survival. H-J: the writer relies on directions unusually amplified by its SD1.5 encoder and loses them under SDXL. H-S: features that preserve scene/content information also preserve enough of the carrier to read its binding. These are not mutually exclusive. A fourth mechanism, H-N, is finite-channel noise crossing a fixed reader threshold. Sourcewise read/content/quality reporting can distinguish some signatures without pretending that the core identifies all four causally.

## Derivation from the actual F5 design

The historical [F5 design note](../../../f5-encoder-amplified-latent.md) specifies a pixel-space writer differentiated through a frozen SD1.5 posterior-mode encoder, channel-wise DCT, public radial weighting and signed chips. The retained r2 configuration uses radial indices 4 through 32, 3272 candidate slots, 320 chips, 150 hinge-gradient steps, target margin 4, a 52 dB robust-stage budget, one refinement and a separate fragile luminance stage at 47 dB. Seven-view CLIP soft binding is part of r2. These settings explain the mechanism; they do not make the final stored RGB8 image a perturbation bounded by 52 dB. Refinement, the second tier, clipping and quantization intervene. Use final-image quality in results.

Let x be a clean RGB vector and w=x+delta its final stored mark. Let U and V denote input/output resolution and normalization maps; E_a, D_a the attacking autoencoder; H_t the denoiser; E_r the pinned reader encoder; B the reader's band/chip projection. For a fixed stochastic realization, a local linearization of its carrier-score change is

`Delta score ~= B J_Er V J_Da J_Ht a_t J_Ea U delta`.

This approximation omits clipping/quantization discontinuities and higher-order effects; it is not a fitted model. If the writer initially moves approximately along `J_Ew^T B_w^T target`, transfer contains the cross term `J_Ea U J_Ew^T B_w^T target`. Large norm under E_w alone says nothing definite about its sign/projection after all later factors. The same latent norm can have large or zero final projection. Conversely separately trained encoders can share useful or nonsemantic sensitive directions. No Jacobian orthogonality, singular-value estimate or empirical gain was measured here.

Pixel amplitude, encoder gain, direction and reader margin must therefore remain separate. For an RGB vector of N scalar entries in [0,1], PSNR=P implies `||delta||_2 = sqrt(N) * 10^(-P/20)` for the measured final pair. Changing to [-1,1] doubles the norm. Changing resolution changes N and the spatial spectrum. Latent scaling and latent dimension differ across models. Do not compare unnormalized SD1.5/SDXL/FLUX norms or substitute the writer's stage budget for the final pair.

## Zhao's bound applied carefully

For the ideal channel `Z=a_t phi(x)+b_t epsilon`, with epsilon standard Gaussian and a common downstream randomized map, the exact equal-covariance separation is `mu=|a_t| ||phi(w)-phi(x)||_2 / |b_t|`. Zhao yields `FNR >= Phi(Phi^-1(1-FPR)-mu)`; using `||phi(w)-phi(x)|| <= L_x,w ||delta||` gives a conservative bound. Small separation relative to noise constrains *every* detector. Large separation only makes that impossibility bound weak; useful evidence may still be lost downstream. The historical phrase about a small L being "safe" must be interpreted as favorable to removal, not safe for the mark. No protected historical note is edited here.

Native img2img adds a complication. If its VAE posterior is `m(x)+s(x) eta`, then the corrupted latent has covariance `a_t^2 diag(s(x)^2)+b_t^2 I`, which may differ between clean and marked images. A mean displacement divided by one pooled sigma is not Zhao's exact theorem. Equal-covariance generalization uses Mahalanobis distance, but unequal covariance needs a different analysis. Coupled random draws can be useful diagnostics, not a proof that covariances match. D1 uses posterior mode with no diffusion noise; setting sigma=0 gives no useful removal certificate.

The postprocessor must also be common. A clean-source caption reused across arms is explicit extra information; a caption produced independently from each suspect can change between arms. Omitting such conditioning from the channel invalidates a simple latent-only distinguishability argument. Empty-prompt core R1 avoids a caption side channel. The utility theorem assumes useful denoising on unmarked inputs and cannot substitute for paired C0 quality reporting.

## What the controls identify

- **X1/X2:** one/two 512-to-1024-to-512 Lanczos cycles test resampling susceptibility. They do not simulate VAE reconstruction or denoising. Extra loss in X2 is an alternative explanation for some rinse loss.
- **D1 versus X1:** adds official SDXL posterior-mode encode/decode, at the same high-level resolutions. This tests a complete autoencoder round trip, not the encoder alone.
- **R1 versus D1:** adds diffusion, posterior sampling and native pipeline input rounding. T5's exact-code inspection finds native R1 first casts image input to prompt fp16 dtype before VAE fp32 promotion, while the manual D1 path normalizes original RGB in fp32. The VAE arithmetic is fp32 in both, but these input paths differ. Preserve native paths; describe the contrast as a compound intervention.
- **R0 versus R1:** changes independent encoder family plus decoder, denoiser/text system, resolution and numerical details. Shared DDIM labels/steps are useful controls, not complete model isolation. Seed numbers match reproducibility conventions, not noise tensors across different latent shapes/RNG consumption.
- **R1-rinse versus R1 .1 and X2:** repeats the full channel with a 512-pixel intermediate and new second-pass seeds. It tests cumulative channel effects; .1+.1 is not equivalent to .2. The scalar Gaussian attenuation theorem is motivation, not a numerical prediction.

Raw latent distances, extra Jacobian probes, perturbed marks and new control images are **not core work**. If later proposed, they need a documented, budgeted development amendment. Existing reader fields can be summarized without adding model calls.

## Endpoint discipline and causal interpretation

The code authority is `scripts/m1b_e3_s1_analysis.py:70-87`, imported by the transfer runner. `C=semantic.read AND semantic.content_match`; `S=semantic.found AND semantic.content_match`; `Q=semantic.read AND decoded_code==payload.q`; `J=Q AND content_match`; `A=semantic.found OR instance.found`. H/D/F keep their existing definitions. C is neither S nor Q. Q can fail while C passes. Recomputed-only S must not be counted as C.

For every channel, report missing/error status and unavailable raw fields separately; absent fields are not a negative. For valid raw fields, prioritize read then content-match, otherwise found: not-found, found-but-not-read, read-but-content-failed and C-success. Track read-without-found separately, since literal C ignores found. Report exact Q separately and retain existing raw fields. Among readable outputs, conditional content-match rates help localize binding losses, but selection into readability makes this a descriptive decomposition, not a causal mediator estimate. A carrier can be lost while scene content is retained; content can drift while a code remains readable.

The sole fixed routing endpoint remains F5 C at R1 .2, source success on at least two of seeds 12/13/14. At n=60, TRANSFERS requires at least 45 and complete zero A on all planned F5 R1 C0/wrong-owner calls; FAILS is below 30; all other cases are PARTIAL. The external margin remains 3 sources (0.05n). The full R1 negative gate has 540 C0 and 540 wrong-owner calls; missing calls are unresolved. No majority averaging hides a negative. Other channels are descriptive. The rule is not a significance test or statistical noninferiority claim, and seeds do not make n=180 independent sources.

WAM32 and VINE-R100 use their frozen common presence endpoint (27/32 and 69/100 respectively), with native results separate. These binomial random-message-null tails are not empirical image false-positive probabilities. Random-message independence and fixed-image/key quantifiers matter. Public chip construction, exact hash equality, near-zero empirical bit correlations or zero observed negative calls do not prove unforgeability, cryptographic ownership, population FPR or distributional undetectability. F5 is still an image-domain comparator with public OwnerID/profile, pinned SD1.5 reader and CLIP side information, without a clean-image reference; it is not the proposal's latent generator-integrated method or the registry experiment.

## Pre-outcome clarifications and optional amendments

1. Preserve the frozen core and its 15-hour/$15 ceiling. Full fp32 SDXL VAE arithmetic starts from official fp16-valued weights, not a different full-precision checkpoint; input rounding remains as described above.
2. Correct explanations that equate found with read, C with exact Q, high latent norm with survival, or independent training with unrelated Jacobians. These are reporting clarifications, not a change to the gate.
3. **P5 recommendation, not approval:** expand the old 900-output smoothing-versus-ordinary-R1 proposal to **1800 diffusion outputs**, comprising 900 smoothed-mode and 900 matched unsmoothed-mode outputs. The old comparison confounds smoothing with switching posterior sample to posterior mode; retain it only as a cheaper complete-attacker alternative. The expanded control must match preprocessing, latent scaling, diffusion noise, decoder and all other settings. D1 supplies a mode round trip but not these diffusion outputs. At assumed 6/12 seconds each, the additional 900 controls add 1.5/3 generation hours. Historical smoothing allowance 1.6-4.6 hours plus those controls plus an illustrative 0.4 hour for added readouts/setup gives **3.5-8.0 extra hours**, or **$3.50-$8 at an assumed $1/hour**. Recommend a separately approved 8-hour/$8 ceiling at that rate; the old 5-hour/$5 ceiling may not fit. This is unmeasured planning arithmetic, not a runtime guarantee or expanded authorization.
4. **P6 recommendation, not approval:** caption each arm's pre-regeneration suspect input once, including C0, with the same frozen captioner and settings; reuse that caption for all three seeds. This is 60 sources x 5 input types = **300 captions and 900 diffusion outputs**. Here the input is the published marked image for a marked arm, not the clean original used as an oracle. Conditioning is attacker-realistic but arm-dependent. A clean-source caption shared across arms would instead add privileged side information and is not the recommended design. Exact captioner revision, preprocessing, generation settings, prompt insertion and dependencies must be fixed by a pre-outcome proposal amendment before execution; no best-caption search.
5. P4 MoVQ's actual diffusers 0.35.1 call path encodes once to four-channel latents and decodes with `force_not_quantize=True`; no VAE posterior sampling/scale applies. It is a different autoencoder family, not evidence of hard vector quantization in this runtime. AE-only 300 outputs are a narrower alternative to the full proposal, not a substitute for regeneration.
6. P7 has no defensible fixed executable price yet. HiDream-O1's RGB pixel path and PRX's native 1024 interface lack the frozen SDXL strength semantics; neither is a drop-in. Runtime examples of 10/30/60 seconds for 900 outputs yield 2.5/7.5/15 generation hours before setup/adaptation, not measurements. P8 training and any LFM-inspired public-band attack remain separate research proposals.

### P5 construction retained from Stage1a

The attack sees only the suspect image and the public method description, with no clean original, registry, owner secret or detector queries. At the Lanczos-upscaled 1024 input `u` in [0,1] RGB coordinates, draw four fixed public Gaussian arrays `eta_j` with coordinate standard deviation 2/255 and use their negatives as the other four perturbations. The same four arrays are shared across all sources and arms, independently of outcomes. Compute

`E_smooth(u) = (1/8) sum_j [E_mode(clip(u+eta_j,0,1)) + E_mode(clip(u-eta_j,0,1))]`.

`E_mode` includes SDXL normalization and the declared latent scaling. Average the eight posterior modes, then apply the .2 DDIM20 schedule with the frozen empty prompt, guidance 1, decoder and seeds 12/13/14. For 300 source/input combinations, the smoothed latents require **2400 cached encoder evaluations**, reused across the three diffusion seeds. The recommended unsmoothed-mode control adds 300 cached mode evaluations, so the expanded design has 2700 encoder evaluations and 1800 diffusion outputs. The exact public perturbation seed, RNG/array construction and input/averaging precision still need a pre-outcome proposal amendment; they are not frozen core settings. Match the two mode paths' preprocessing so that smoothing is the intended difference. Keep the final readers fixed and report quality with detection. No radius sweep, checkpoint selection or best-seed attack is authorized.

## what we might still have missed

OpenAlex surfaced Jacobian-SVD transfer metrics, LiBRA, RAVEN and ProxyEraseAgent; arXiv surfaced FeatMark, SpreadMark and WaterVIB. Those discovery-only leads are not silently promoted to V2 or used for quantitative claims. Model-alignment primary reading closes the most direct cross-Jacobian gap; a full review of every adaptive attack, perceptual-information bottleneck or encoder-training method remains outside this bounded experiment. Search truncation (eight arXiv/OpenAlex and six GitHub records per query), indexing delay, differing terminology and release changes can hide relevant work. We did not establish a theorem linking CLIP content-match to carrier capacity, the actual SDXL/F5 local gain distribution, posterior covariance equality, calibrated low-FPR behavior, human visual utility, adaptive public-key forgery resistance or a usable pixel-regeneration API. Those are explicit unknowns, not assumed favorable facts.
