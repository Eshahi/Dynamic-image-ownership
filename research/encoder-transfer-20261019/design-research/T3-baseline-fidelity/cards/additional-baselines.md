# Additional baseline index

Updated 2026-10-20. This index supersedes the earlier short survey. There are 24 distinct V2 post-hoc method cards; this index is not a 25th method or source.

The complete comparison and release qualifications are in [synthesis.md](../synthesis.md), [methods.json](../methods.json) and [method-comparison.csv](../method-comparison.csv). The approved external core remains WAM and VINE-R. P1 TrustMark Q is the first proposed addition, P9 Pixel Seal the second; P2 StegaStamp and P3 PhaseMark remain deferred. No additional core arm is authorized.

Recent omissions now have their own inspected cards: [GenPTW](GenPTW.md), [DistSeal](DistSeal.md), [EditGuard](EditGuard.md), [OmniGuard](OmniGuard.md) and [SNW](SNW.md). [Chunky-Seal](Chunky-Seal.md) now correctly records the official VideoSeal model card/checkpoint under MIT terms. A second version, checkpoint or generation-time variant is not counted again.

## what we might still have missed

See the synthesis for inaccessible journal leads and scope boundaries. Missing full text or a release does not establish absence of a method, and a public repository does not establish a licensed faithful checkpoint. This index contains no new outcomes.
