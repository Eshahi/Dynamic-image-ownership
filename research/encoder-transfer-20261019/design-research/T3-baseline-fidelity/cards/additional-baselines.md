## Recent work and exclusions

- **SimuFreeMark (Nov2025)**: inherited V2 card `tang2025simufreemark.md`, [§§3–4/Tables1–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.11295v1). Low-frequency decomposition + frozen SD VAE + learned latent64-bit residual, restore high frequencies. Tests1000 UltraEdit; PSNR39.19/SSIM.990; SDIP+ bit accuracy97.64/94.05/92.25 at strengths.1/.3/.5. Code/weights/license not located by targeted official-source searches; no executable proposal until resolved. Particularly relevant methodological prior art, not an omission to hide.
- **SpecGuard (ICCV2025)**: inherited V2 [arXiv2510.07302v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.07302v1), §§3–4/Tables1–3,9/Appendix12. Do not present this as turnkey. No permission/checkpoint verified.
- **Pixel Seal (Dec2025)**: inherited V2 [§§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.16874v1),256bit post-hoc U-Net/ConvNeXt-v2, adversarial/JND training. No diffusion-regeneration result established in inspected sections. Public package/weight feasibility not separately verified here; useful future quality comparator, not first decisive T3 addition.
- **APT (Aug2026)**: inherited V2 [§§3.2,4–5/Table1/AppendixD](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30656v1), secret server DINOv3 anchors,150 Adam steps;42.52s reported average on RTX4090, PSNR32.80/AUROC.97/IoU.90 over100 COCO examples. Localization, not arbitrary-message detection, so would add another endpoint rather than directly replace WAM/VINE. Public executable checkpoint/license not verified.
- **MBRS** is an expected robust post-hoc comparison in VINE: Table1 reports99.53/99.35% stochastic/deterministic TPR but27.37dB PSNR,30bits; Table7 .0053s/image. Its primary paper/code/license were not newly V2-inspected here, so this is **VINE's baseline evidence only**, not a fully eligible executable proposal. This explicitly bounds the completeness claim.
- Tree-Ring, Gaussian Shading, PRC and ordinary Stable Signature implementations target generation-time marking and are not drop-in arbitrary-existing-image arms. Their post-hoc adaptations would alter the method. FreqMark retries remain closed by user direction. ReMark is a removal tool, not an added watermark arm.

## Costed proposals, with explicit uncertainty

The extra expense is predominantly rerunning the **attack channels on each extra marked arm**, not feed-forward embedding. C0 regenerations can be shared only where the exact input/channel/seed is identical. With n=60 and3generators×3strengths×3seeds, each extra arm adds1620 marked regeneration images plus diagnostics, clean queries and wrong-message scoring. At an assumed2–5s weighted average per marked regeneration, that is.90–2.25 GPU-hours before setup/detection. At n=120 double this component. These are planning assumptions, not measured throughput or live rental quotes; multiply hours by the actual user's quoted rate. At illustrative $0.50–1.00/hour:
