## What the evidence changes in our interpretation

**Regeneration is a family, not one strength value.** [Zhao](cards/zhao.md) uses a noise timeline and PNDM; [WAVES](cards/waves.md) separates diffusion regeneration, VAE compression and repeated rinsing; [VINE](cards/vine.md) separates stochastic and deterministic reconstruction. Their noise time, scheduled denoising steps, actual reverse updates, number of passes and image resolutions differ. A pipeline strength of .2 is therefore not a universal standardized attack dose. Our SDXL .1/.2/.4 cells are fixed operational conditions that expose transfer to a differently trained autoencoder. They are not exact reproductions of these benchmarks.

**Repeated weak regeneration is justified, but not calibrated equivalent to one stronger pass.** WAVES evaluates two/four passes, CtrlRegen compares a twice-applied Regen baseline, and the [AR benchmark](cards/arrobust.md) uses three rounds at its stated60-step setting. These establish the attack family. They do not prove that .1+.1 matches .2 in effective noise, quality or computational effort. Keep the already-frozen one SDXL .1+.1 rinse, intermediate image, paired pass seeds and twice-resize X2 control. Do not select pass count from detector outcomes.

**Encoder identity and resampling are causal variables.** VINE's paper describes an SDXL-Turbo encoder, while its pinned released constructor loads `stabilityai/sd-turbo`. That discrepancy must remain visible when explaining VINE's lineage. W-Bench stochastic and deterministic paths also use different SD2.1 variants. The [local benchmark](cards/local.md) shows that restoring pixels outside the edit mask can restore payload evidence lost during whole-image reconstruction; localization success does not imply payload survival. These observations support retaining D1 and X1/X2 instead of attributing every change to the denoiser.

**Adaptive attacks answer another question.** [CtrlRegen](cards/ctrlregen.md) trains conditioning networks; [UnMarker](cards/unmarker.md) optimizes spectral changes; [DIP](cards/dip.md) selects successful fitting iterates with decoder feedback; [MarkNull](cards/marknull.md) exploits a proxy latent model; [DRIFT](cards/drift.md) includes verifier-dependent adaptation; [WAW](cards/waw.md) routes attacks using predicted watermark family. These attacker-access assumptions cannot be silently imported into fixed, query-free regeneration. MarkNull, [WMForger](cards/wmforger.md), [LiBRA](cards/libra.md) and [MarkCleaner](cards/markcleaner.md) are evidence that independent model transfer can be attacked in several ways; none estimates F5's survival in the declared cells.

**Quality-preserving reconstruction is not necessarily same-instance preservation.** [RAVEN](cards/raven.md) changes viewpoint; [NFPA](cards/nfpa.md) predicts a frame; [partial regeneration](cards/partial.md) replaces regions. Distributional quality or semantic similarity can remain good while exact instance content changes. [forensic cost](cards/forensiccost.md) and [forensic stealth](cards/forensicstealth.md) add evidence about attack traces, but WARP itself has no forensic-accuracy evaluation. These are limitations on interpreting removal, not reasons to add unrelated forensic experiments now.

## Conflicts that remain explicit

- **VINE denominator:** Table1 caption states5,000 images per edit, whereas Sections4.2-4.3 specify1,000 per regeneration arm and averages over m x1,000 outputs. Neither denominator has been silently substituted for the other.
- **Competition score direction and calibration:** the [report](cards/competition.md) uses incompatible descriptions of detection/attack score and of calibration between main text and AppendixE. The [winner](cards/challengewinner.md) leaderboard scores are competition scores, not raw TPRs. There are300 images per track;2,722 submissions are not independent images.
- **NFPA reimplementation:** original NFPA uses10 inversion and10 denoising steps; MarkCleaner's comparator uses25 purification steps. Its NFPA row is not a direct reproduction of the original table. NFPA's FID is distributional, and image resolution is not supplied in the inspected setup/appendix.
- **MarkCleaner:** Table1 reports meanTPR.014, but Tree-Ring retains ACC.795 while calibratedTPR is.001. Low TPR does not establish randomized bit evidence. The prose says15 watermarks while Table1 has12 columns; the mismatch is preserved.
- **RAVEN and channel comparison:** RAVEN's main detection setup says1,000 pairs while its DiffusionDB appendix says1,001 prompts. [SNW/channel](cards/channel.md) Figure4 says1,000 images while AppendixH.2/Table6 says200. Aggregation denominators are not reconstructed by guesswork.
- **Critical editing papers:** [Ni](cards/editing.md), [Fu](cards/fuediting.md) and [Vanishing](cards/vanishing.md) mix exact-message recovery, bit accuracy and detection, with unresolved payload and quality-reference assumptions. Vanishing's interpretation of7.4%bit accuracy as chance is invalid for binary bits. They are included as inspected critical coverage, excluded from the positive floor, and cannot establish universal watermark absence.
- **Other critical benchmarks:** [Orthogonal](cards/orthogonal.md) combines non-equivalent byte-accuracy/MSE provenance scales without calibrated FPR; [SADRE](cards/sadre.md) has unresolved Tree-Ring BRA and adaptive-strength semantics; [FDDWAN](cards/fddwan.md) omits the test denominator; [Hide&Seek](cards/hideseek.md) has an equation/algorithm mask-loss discrepancy and template publication metadata. These issues remain attached to each reported number.
- **Endpoint mismatch:** [ProxyEraseAgent](cards/proxyerase.md) counts BER>=.1 plus LPIPS<=.05 as success. That permits90%correct bits and is not our complete detection/owner endpoint. AR latent optimization selects target-best iterates; its black-box proxy-model label does not mean evaluation without target feedback. SNW's capacity calculation needs stronger assumptions than whitening alone.

## Two bounded recommendations; no protocol edit

1. **Correct reporting and lineage labels.** Retain raw/model-normalized image sizes, scheduler class/configuration, actual selected timesteps, update counts, both rinse seeds, VAE identity and resize operations in the already-required provenance. Describe released VINE as SD-Turbo-based, while noting the paper discrepancy. Label the frozen attack as an SDXL adaptation and disclose all per-source failures and missing outputs. This adds no model calls.
2. **Keep endpoints and external comparisons separate.** Report fixed native/common detection and owner/payload outcomes alongside paired quality, with source-level uncertainty. Never replace the frozen primary endpoint with exact-message recovery, BER, a literature ASR cutoff or a quality-selected successful subset. Preserve denominator contradictions and reimplementation differences in citations. This is an analysis/reporting correction and adds no GPU attack variant.

No additional standard variant is recommended for the current run. Full core remains n60, F5r2/v5r3/WAM/VINE, R0SD1.5 and R1SDXL strengths.1/.2/.4, D1, X1/X2 and one .1+.1 rinse. Primary R1.2C thresholds remain >=45/60 transfer, <30/60 failure, margin3; complete required negatives remain necessary. The integrated7500-unit estimate and15hour/USD15 cap belong to the protocol; this literature search supplies no measured server throughput. Training-heavy or adaptive attacks cannot be presumed affordable from another paper's runtime. Caption conditioning remains outside this frozen study and is not silently reinstated from the historical T1 sheet.

## what we might still have missed

The ICDMW2025 paper *Evaluating the Robustness of Invisible Watermarking Against Adversarial Attacks* remains inaccessible: DOI retrieval failed, catalog entries exposed no full text, and title/DOI/author-PDF/arXiv searches found only metadata and an official conference program. It is not counted. Erasing Shadows remains a metadata-only adjacent lead. Video benchmarks, screen-camera datasets and watermark-construction-only leads are explicitly scoped out; their exclusion does not imply robustness against those attacks.

Search indexing can lag new proceedings and October2026 releases. OpenAlex and official repositories improve breadth but do not establish exhaustive recall. Some full texts omit sampler, resolution, attack prompt or optimizer learning rate even after main/appendix searches; exact omissions are in the settings rows. DIP's official recursive-tree route failed with403 while its paper gives the500-iteration grid but no learning rate. These gaps limit reproducibility of external comparisons, not the frozen local specification.

Finally, none of these external studies tests F5 under our paired independently trained encoder conditions. A failure or success here remains a bounded development result, with no held-out or universal security claim. Future author releases, corrected benchmark versions, more powerful adaptive attackers or non-diffusion generators could change the broader assessment and should be evaluated only under a separately declared follow-up.


## Late-lead coverage addendum, 2026-10-20

The original accepted 34 readings remain intact. Five newly surfaced leads each received an own T1 V2 recheck, card, complete settings row and cached-primary checksum. The unsuccessful BrushOff search is uncounted, although its supplied primary was accessible and read. The original three-category search floor remains satisfied.

BrushOff reports VINE failure under painting re-rendering even where diffusion-edit robustness is strong. Its non-generative stroke bottleneck, repainting-specific quality metrics, fixed payload and painting sample do not estimate F5 performance under independent diffusion encoders. Main repainting fidelity is borrowed from VINE for other systems; subset and clean-failure exclusions remain explicit. TrustMark has 61 payload bits plus 35 BCH parity and 4 header bits; its 39 overhead bits are not all BCH. BrushOff's paired relative-brightness probe is exploratory and trades embedding quality; it does not reopen a closed F5 design line. Re-rendering is an untested limitation on broader robustness claims.

Boundary shows why indistinguishable latent samples may expose useful sign-boundary information. The 15x headline concerns synthetic bit flips; image results have separate 100/30-source denominators and same-pipeline inversion conditions. Printed budget/indexing inconsistencies prevent adopting the pseudocode without clarification. The SDXL table reports ASR .19 at epsilon = 8 despite stating 30 images; several table rates are not rounded binary counts out of 30. Aggregation/repetitions remain unexplained, so we preserve the reported rates and stated sample size without reconstructing integer counts. No attack code was downloaded or run. The official CCS accepted-list retrieval yielded a client-rendered shell rather than a verifiable title; this card relies on the inspected arXiv v3 and leaves venue unverified.

The taxonomy's reused editing Table 4 must not be conflated with the newly found eight-method editing benchmark. The taxonomy is an English translation/reorganization, mostly reusing earlier tables; its harmonized HSTR addition is stated separately. Neither proves a semantic watermark carrier. The new benchmark distinguishes edit-only TPR from post-distortion composites, missing-output exclusions and native operating settings. Its score-separation diagnostic is reporting context rather than a replacement for direct testing, our frozen endpoint or attack severity. Native settings are tabulated despite its conservative adjunct classification.

PECCVAI supplies an SDXL paraphrase comparison, but its native WDP mixes bit accuracy and confidence and takes a maximum over patches/channels. Those means cannot be treated as calibrated image-level TPR. Its embedding PSNR is not matched post-attack quality. The T1 recheck used the retained official CVF PDF; the live CVF re-open returned 403. Supplementary settings/MOS were not inspected and remain unknown.
