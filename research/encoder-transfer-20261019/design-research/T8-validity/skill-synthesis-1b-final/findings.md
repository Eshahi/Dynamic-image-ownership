# Evidence synthesis

This report preserves reported evidence; source hashes establish identity, not scientific truth.

- T8-waves-1: WAVES separates presence detection from message identification, measures TPR at 0.1% FPR, and combines eight fidelity measures into dataset-dependent normalized quality. Its attack families include reconstruction, partial-noise diffusion, repeated regeneration and stronger access conditions. Repeated settings share the original sources. These are actual benchmark choices, not interchangeable definitions of survival. [T8-waves; supporting; direct-evidence; confidence high]. Limitations: The normalization and attack grids are benchmark-specific. No F5 result, theorem of encoder independence, or calibrated SDXL operating point is supplied.

- T8-waves-2: Three watermarks and three image sources are evaluated across 26 attacks. Reported aggregate performance over severities is not the detection count at a single frozen channel. Similar versus different autoencoder experiments motivate a transfer question but cannot determine F5 behavior. [T8-waves; supporting; direct-evidence; confidence high]. Limitations: The normalization and attack grids are benchmark-specific. No F5 result, theorem of encoder independence, or calibrated SDXL operating point is supplied.

- T8-waves-3: Keep attack fidelity, total fidelity and fixed survival endpoints separate; a broad benchmark motivates explicit scope limits rather than copying its aggregate score. [T8-waves; inconclusive; agent-inference; confidence medium]. Limitations: The normalization and attack grids are benchmark-specific. No F5 result, theorem of encoder independence, or calibrated SDXL operating point is supplied.

- T8-vine-1: VINE uses a generative-prior image writer, residual adaptation to higher resolution, a 100-bit extractor, and blur/noise augmentation as a proxy for regeneration. W-Bench distinguishes stochastic regeneration, deterministic inversion, global/local editing and video. The paper describes an SDXL-Turbo prior, while the inspected official released constructor uses SD-Turbo; preserve that disagreement. [T8-vine; supporting; direct-evidence; confidence high]. Limitations: This is an author evaluation, not a native universal cutoff. Training on editing proxies and paper/release lineage mismatch limit the inference of unseen encoder transfer.

- T8-vine-2: OpenImages training uses 256-pixel inputs and testing applies residuals at512. Both denominators remain recorded rather than repaired. TPR at fixed empirical FPR is more specific than mean bit accuracy or AUROC. [T8-vine; supporting; direct-evidence; confidence high]. Limitations: This is an author evaluation, not a native universal cutoff. Training on editing proxies and paper/release lineage mismatch limit the inference of unseen encoder transfer.

- T8-zhao-1: The attack corrupts an embedding with additive equal-covariance Gaussian noise and applies a common downstream generator. - T8-prc-1: PRC watermarks encode pseudorandom-code signs in initial generative latents and recover them through inversion. Theoretical undetectability is computational and assumption/parameter dependent. The false-positive theorem quantifies over a random independent key for a fixed image; it is not a fixed public-key image-distribution theorem. [T8-prc; supporting; direct-evidence; confidence high]. Limitations: Public deterministic F5 chips, content-derived codes and expected-message checks do not inherit PRC secrecy, undetectability, unforgeability or random-key quantifiers.

[T8-prc; supporting; direct-evidence; confidence high]. - T8-wam-1: WAM predicts a per-pixel presence mask and per-pixel message logits.
