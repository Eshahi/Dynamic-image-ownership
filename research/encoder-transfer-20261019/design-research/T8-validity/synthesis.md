## Allowed language by outcome

- TRANSFERS: “On the fixed consulted development cohort, F5 met the prespecified source-majority C criterion under the pinned SDXL .2 channel, with complete zero observed A negatives across the retained R1 grid.” This is not a lower confidence bound of75% population survival.
- ROUTE A: a method-led article remains a development possibility; novelty, matched-quality breadth and fresh confirmation remain open.
- ROUTE B: transfer occurred with weaker common-point robustness than the best external count by more than the fixed margin; any tradeoff explanation needs its own quality/capacity/cost evidence.
- FAILS/ROUTE C: the prespecified operational criterion failed for this SDXL configuration. Count detector misses separately from missing/blocked attempts. Do not claim a theorem of encoder-specific failure across all encoders.
- PARTIAL: evidence is insufficient under the fixed rule; no post-hoc threshold relaxation, larger cohort, new seeds or held-out follow-on.
