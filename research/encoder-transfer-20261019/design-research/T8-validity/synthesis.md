## What the design can identify

[SDXL section 2.4 and Table 3](cards/sdxl.md) explicitly support training the new autoencoder from scratch while retaining the original architecture class. This resolves trained-weight ancestry more convincingly than SD2's decoder fine-tuning. It does not establish independent data, feature directions, Jacobians or generative priors. The experiment changes a complete attack pipeline: encoder, decoder, denoiser, resolution and conditioning. F5's writer and reader remain SD1.5-based. A pass is transfer through this SDXL channel, not an encoder-independent reader or universal semantic watermark.

D1 and X1/X2 improve interpretation without creating a pure encoder intervention. D1 uses the posterior mode and direct fp32 input normalization. Native R1 samples the posterior and rounds normalized inputs through the pipeline's fp16 dtype before full-VAE upcast. R1 minus D1 therefore confounds diffusion, sampling and input rounding. X1/X2 isolate resize-only loss but cannot subtract nonlinear resize/model interactions. Full fp32 VAE execution preserves the pinned fp16-file checkpoint values; it is not a different fp32 checkpoint. These are [direct pinned-code observations](cards/official-and-local-contract.md), consistent with accepted T5.

[Zhao's bound](cards/zhao.md) is conditional on the separation after an embedding and equal-covariance additive Gaussian noise. Its utility theorem assumes a generator that denoises the clean counterpart well. Neither small pixel distortion alone nor a large latent gain proves the final robustness outcome. Independent training likewise does not imply orthogonality. Thus a failure identifies susceptibility of the declared compound channel, while a pass measures retained readable, content-bound evidence on the fixed development cohort. It does not establish which hypothesized mechanism caused that result.

## What survival and failure mean

The primary endpoint stays historical C, literally `semantic.read and semantic.content_match`, with two-of-three seeds per source. It is not interchangeable with any-found A, exact Q/H, bit accuracy, common queried-message presence or native WAM mask detection. The read-first carrier-state report is valuable: binding mismatch can coexist with a readable carrier, and unavailable raw fields cannot become carrier absence. F5 exact-H diagnostics use clean decoded H because intended H is not exported; this weaker target basis remains disclosed and never defines primary C.

Detection, decoding, attribution, mean bit agreement and semantic consistency are different events. [DREW](cards/drew.md) uses enrolled database retrieval and a reliability fallback; those side-information advantages are outside this reference-free transfer test. [WAW](cards/waw.md) shows why loss of an expected payload need not mean a watermark-free output. No result here supplies cryptographic unforgeability or legal ownership.

Public expected messages make the external common point reproducible, but its fair-independent-bit tail is not an empirical image-FPR guarantee. ## Why clean quality, denominators and clustering matter

The planned roster is deterministic and consulted. It is not a random sample of images, owners, prompts or encoders. Three seeds are one content cluster; hundreds of negative calls are not hundreds of independent source images. Model-based CP intervals and the three paired Holm-adjusted tests remain exploratory. The development route is an operational rule, not population validation, a noninferiority test or evidence that a Q1 article is ready. Seed marginals and pairwise correlation do not identify the three-way majority law, and marginal external rates do not identify their maximum; accepted T6's hypothetical sensitivity tables retain those qualifications.

Missing positive attempts fail the prescribed denominator; missing negative calls remain unresolved. An incomplete operational FAILS label therefore needs observed coverage and the optimistic sensitivity alongside it. Clean failures must remain: [BrushOff IV-C](cards/brushoff.md) excludes fifteen of five hundred RivaGAN clean failures from its attack denominator. Separately, its DwtDct trial detects 349/500 clean marks and is excluded from attack evaluation; **DwtDctSvd detects 500/500**, and RivaGAN 485/500. These names and denominators cannot be collapsed.

Quality is measured on each arm using explicit references: mark/source, attack/original mark, and total/source. Rinse also reports its incremental second pass. BrushOff compares to the clean painting and borrows VINE-only fidelity for other marks after a pilot assumption; we do not adopt that borrowing. The paper is painting-only with one stroke renderer, fixed payloads, a smaller non-VINE 2048-canvas subset, and additional VINE strengths and exploratory probes. Its result is a newly discovered attack-family limitation, not evidence against F5. Parent separately owns the T1 settings addendum.

Native arm quality can differ; no single operating point supplies an equal-quality frontier. Detection-blind finite-grid matching can support a conditional comparison only where the fixed calipers overlap. Sparse or missing support stays explicit. Good PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS does not prove imperceptibility or same-instance content; [RAVEN](cards/raven.md) changes viewpoint while retaining scene similarity. Human ratings stay missing. No new metric, additional image set or attack is inserted through this validity analysis.
