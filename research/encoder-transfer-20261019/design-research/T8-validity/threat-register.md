# T8 Stage1b threat register

Documentary, pre-outcome analysis, 2026-10-20. These **68 distinct threats** exceed the 25-mechanism floor. A row is not a measured failure. Mechanisms are agent inferences unless identified as direct source observations; a mitigation reduces a threat without proving its absence. Sources link to own V2 cards with inspected method/evaluation locators. `Contract` means the exact local protocol/config argument, not a published finding. No new experiment, threshold or arm is proposed for adoption here.

| V02 | Architecture mistaken for learned independence | Independently initialized weights may learn aligned features in the same architecture. | Say independently trained, retaining related KL-f8 architecture. | Training-data independence, Jacobian orthogonality and feature independence are unproved. | [SDXL](cards/sdxl.md); alignment is an explicit open assumption. |
