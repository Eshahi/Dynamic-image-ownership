## Versioned native public-projection attack

Use attack version `ac-native-projection-v1` for the27 public transfers and20 T4 shams. It is one fixed analytic white-box transplant, with no score feedback, optimizer, repetition loop, amplitude grid or attack success selection. Public projection receives donor saved C1, recipient saved C0, public alpha owner and pinned models; it does not receive donor unmarked C0. The sham substitutes donor saved C0. Source-paired residual attacks remain a separately labelled stronger-access arm.

1. Let donor input A be C1 for a real projection or C0 for a sham; recipient B is C0. Obtain their primary fp16 scaled mode latents `z_A,z_B` and recomputed public descriptors `E_A,H_A`. These are observations of the supplied images, not their embedding checkpoints. Work in flat C-order16384 coordinates.
2. Construct the donor template with alpha. Embed `w_j=r_j*v_j` at its channel coordinates and zero elsewhere. Each full-length w has unit norm and the two are orthogonal because their supports are disjoint.
3. Compute `a_j=dot(z_A-z_B,w_j)` and `z_star=z_B+a_s*w_s+a_i*w_i` in CPU float64. This is the minimum-Euclidean-norm displacement matching the donor's two **unnormalized projections**. It does not copy the score denominators or guarantee a score threshold. Save both projection residuals; before decoder dtype conversion they must agree to numerical tolerance. Never replace a_j by a target threshold or the embedder's margin.
4. Decode `z_star/.18215` and `z_B/.18215` through the pinned fp16 VAE, clip each decoded RGB float to[0,1], and take their difference R. Let `B_float=B_RGB8/255`; form `uint8(rint(255*clip(B_float+lambda*R,0,1)))` using the same deterministic36-step quality-only cap at35.2dB. No watermark score enters lambda. Record the unclipped projection displacement, fp16 conversion error, R, lambda, output/source hashes and decoding operator. Final re-encoding can lose the projected mark; that is measured, not assumed away.

The attack uses original recipient pixels through an explicit residual bypass; it is not a pure decoded recipient. Both the real and sham arm use the same output operator and cap. Donor mode/CLIP extraction and known-template linear algebra are public white-box computations. Report **zero adaptive detector-decision queries**, while separately recording these analytic projection computations and all model calls; do not describe the adversary as ignorant of the detector. The cap is a prospective attack distortion constraint, not an assertion that the recipient meets SSIM/LPIPS or human perceptual validity.

Original A/v5 used three-pass image-DCT chip projection. Consequently this adapted arm supports a mechanism-appropriate evaluation, but its outputs are not a controlled same-attack-byte comparison with their projection results. Only the two residual arms share exact construction. Do not count failure of this one-shot attack as exhaustion of adaptive public forgery; anyone knowing the public owner/maps can attempt direct re-embedding or optimize a recipient-template mark.
