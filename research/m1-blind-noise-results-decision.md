# B3-C result, mechanism diagnosis and branch decision

Version `m1-blind-noise-results-decision-v1`, 2026-10-04. Method escalation: gpt-6-astra/xhigh. Exploratory development evidence from two previously examined photographs; no held-out data, new model run, threshold change or human verdict. This document follows the completed experiment, so its diagnoses and proposed amendment are not retrospective preregistration.

**Decision:** retain B3-C as a failed, genuinely initial-noise, blind dual-content-template experiment. Do not expand its amplitude grid or its cohort. Its tested fixed-photo channel is too inefficient at the source-quality budget. Investigate the same continuous blind reader through direct terminal-latent optimization, frozen separately in `m1-terminal-continuous-design.md`. That is an amendment within family A, not a fifth family and not a repaired B3-C result. Initial-noise methods, learned extractors and better inversions are not exhausted.

## What failed, and what the saved evidence rules out

**Units, placement and rounding are consistent with the design.** The inverted noise RMS is.962956/.957087, with per-channel RMS.929–.988/.908–.975. It is not erroneously multiplied or divided by the VAE factor. At amplitude.025, perturbation RMS is2.596%/2.612% of the noise RMS; at.1 it is10.385%/10.448%. Supplied perturbation/template cosine is at least.9999679. fp16 rounding RMS is only.20–.80% of intended perturbation RMS, and99.1–99.8% of noise coordinates change. Source templates match the core exactly after the declared float32 cast. A hypothesis of a missing mark, wrong tensor layout or wholesale fp16 erasure is contradicted by these checks.

The source-template scores **on supplied initial noise**, an evaluator-only observation, grow from(.596,.509) to(9.726,9.989) for1675, and from(-1.180,-.100) to(8.366,9.027) for4795 at.1. Thus the desired initial correlation is present. These are not image-reader results: they cannot qualify as successful extraction, nor do they ensure the terminal VAE points in the same direction.

**Small-dose hybrid1675 fails despite stable content geometry.** Its clean source CLIP cosine is.996935 at.025 and.990426 at.05; each pHash changes one bit. The compressed instance template cosine is.934977/.928986. Actual SRHT normalized-kernel error is only.000351/.000461. At.1 CLIP cosine remains.984629 and the instance cosine.861773, with error.000223. The semantic and instance score denominators on these cells change by less than1% relative to C0. Low-dose failure here cannot reasonably be blamed mainly on descriptor drift, sign quantization of CLIP, the compression from16384 to8192 dimensions, or score normalization. The carrier does not acquire enough terminal alignment at affordable distortion.

**Source4795 is much more sensitive to denoising perturbation.** At.025 the float decoder residual RMS divided by initial-noise amplitude is2.04978, versus.54051 for1675, about3.79 times larger. Doubling amplitude does not double the same image displacement: the.025/.05 residual cosine is.7055 and the proportional-response relative error is1.0213. For.025/.1 these are.4979 and1.7255. The corresponding1675 values are.8886/.5042 and.6613/.9505. These are strongly nonlinear finite differences, not an estimated Jacobian.

4795's clean CLIP cosine drops from.91653 at.025 to.59654 at.1; its instance kernel drops from.69240 to.42000. The semantic score is consequently nonmonotone. Instance projection error remains at most.009878 on hybrid clean cells, too small to explain the much larger observed score deficit. Float clipping affects.84–3.38% of RGB components across the six hybrid outputs; RGB8 rounding RMS is.00111–.00113. Clipping and rounding are real transformations, but correct residual composition plus these measurements provides no evidence of an implementation mistake sufficient to explain the entire failure.

**No unjustified causal decomposition.** A C1-minus-C0 blind-score difference changes the latent, rederived template and normalization together. The report retains those differences and numerator/denominator changes, but does not call them fixed-template channel gains. Terminal VAE tensors were not retained, so exact source-template oracle scores on the final images cannot be reconstructed from JSON alone. Re-encoding saved PNGs would supply that diagnostic; it is not legitimate to infer it from a favorable kernel value.

For scale only, a deliberately crude origin-constrained extrapolation of each dose's hybrid distortion/score gives a35.2dB amplitude of.0333–.0417 for1675 and.00867–.0173 for4795, and predicted scores far below4. The report labels these post-hoc extrapolations explicitly. Their disagreement across doses and the large proportional-response errors prohibit treating them as bounds, optimal doses, or justification for a new selected amplitude. The reason to stop this grid is its joint measured result and poor signal efficiency, not an extrapolated impossibility theorem.

## Why GS success does not contradict this failure

The GS lightweight result starts from marked iid-like generated noise, uses256 bits with64 repetitions and an enrolled whitening nonce/reference payload, and measures a generated-image carrier. B3-C starts from an inverted photograph, adds a small continuous content template, asks it to retain a particular direction through50 denoising steps, and queries the suspect's own features. Its source bypass further changes the final image being encoded. Those are material changes to the channel, amplitude and side information. GS's four generated-image successes demonstrate that an initial-noise signal can be visible without inversion; they do not guarantee that a small isotropic analog displacement transports efficiently around an arbitrary photo inversion.

NoisePrints and OSI, already inspected and recorded in the literature inventory, support direct terminal readout as prior art. They do not establish B3-C's quality or blind dual-content-binding claim. The fixed conditional random-owner tail argument in the B3 design also remains a null model, not a carrier power guarantee and not resistance to an attacker who computes the public templates.

## Chosen next experiment and limits of the decision

Use terminal-latent optimization against the **unchanged B3 continuous reader** to remove the untested assumption that the initial direction is also an efficient terminal direction. The encoder and decoder are differentiable, so the embedder can explicitly adapt to their local response while the readout remains one VAE encode plus CLIP/hash. Retain a declared source-pixel bypass and a deterministic35.2dB residual cap. Clean semantic/instance and after-VAE semantic surrogate scores receive gradients, with source features frozen only inside the embedder. The final detector recomputes its own features from saved RGB8.

This choice combines three actual findings: A has demonstrated quality-preserving terminal-latent residual optimization; B3 full-CLIP template geometry is numerically sound and1675's content drift is small; D's capped latent-derived residual can retain a VAE-readable carrier. The now-inspected `m1-phase-residual-expansion-results.md` reports D clean presence9/12, VAE presence9/12, and their **intersection8/12**, with clean quality12/12. These are that independently checked report's figures, not measurements recomputed by the B3 CPU script. A's complete T3/T4/T5 assessment is still pending here. None of these facts predicts the new pilot's result.

The separate prospective specification fixes the two sources, objective, optimization cap, observation boundary, endpoint selection, controls and promotion rule **before its implementation/run**. Keep existing A, B3 and D code/config/results intact. Do not call this a new design family, claim original pure-decoder quality, or select the better past source. If the new pilot fails, distinguish unattained frozen-source surrogate targets from an attained surrogate that fails the actual blind reader; that distinction is more actionable than another unstructured dose sweep.

An optional **readout-only B3 audit** may accompany the next GPU load: re-encode all32 unique retained B3 PNGs, retain their scaled posterior-mode latents and hashes, recompute all original blind scores, and add explicitly oracle source-E/H scores. Require exact PNG hash parity and numerical agreement with the old reader within declared floating-point tolerance; do not replace original results. This entails zero U-Net calls, no new image, no target selection, at most60 seconds and10GiB GPU allocation. It can locate feature-versus-latent losses more precisely but is not a prerequisite for the chosen structural amendment and cannot retrospectively rescue B3's blind gate.

The broader initial-noise family remains open: better inversion, channel-adapted noise optimization, spatial/frequency coding, or trained readout were not tested. B3-C is meaningful negative evidence for one explicit proposal-compatible content-template adaptation, alongside positive component evidence from GS. It is not evidence that no lightweight content-bound initial-noise method exists, and it does not on its own satisfy M1's three-family exit clause.
