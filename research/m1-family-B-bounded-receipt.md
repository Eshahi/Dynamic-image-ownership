## Bounded stop and threat applicability

All six fixed two-source route/dose gates fail a necessary clean/VAE condition; stronger B3 attacks are structurally not triggered. No further amplitude grid, cohort expansion or favorable-source substitution is justified by these records. The concrete mechanism amendment is already being tested as A-C: optimize the observable terminal channel directly, with the same continuous blind reader. It remains family A, not a third variant counted as another design family.

GS supplies a published latent-noise baseline and full bounded regeneration evidence, with measured native/one-VAE mean readout times2.185464/.053589 seconds. Its enrolled nonce/reference and generated-image task prevent a matched existing-photo or dual-content-security comparison. T4 donor identity and T5 semantic consistency are not implemented by its shared reference-message check; no three-state security labels are invented. B3's absent clean detections likewise cannot be called successful transfer rejection. All public-template methods lack an authenticated secret or signature verification; carrier statistics alone establish no ownership credential.

Untested alternatives include better inversion, noise optimization through the entire sampler, spatial/frequency codes, other foundation models, and trained extractors. They are not declared exhausted. Their additional learning/model/compute mechanisms lie outside this finite tested branch. Positive GS transport is retained explicitly and contradicts any blanket claim that lightweight initial-noise extraction is impossible.
