# Pure arithmetic for the bounded confirmatory T5 draft

`scripts/m1_confirmatory_t5_pairs.py` implements the preferred disjoint30-pair rule already proposed in `m1-confirmatory-draft.md`. It is a pure function module: no image, annotation, model, filesystem or network access, and no scientific worker or authorization mechanism. Five generated-metadata tests pass. No held-out observations were supplied during preparation.

Only after approval may the eventual worker populate the frozen300-source cohort's operational category signatures (sorted unique COCO category IDs with noncrowd instances) and source32-bit pHash. Empty signatures, missing observations, same frozen groups, different signatures and distances below8 remain enumerated with reasons. The code ranks eligible unordered pairs by SHA256 of the fixed tag, a zero separator and two UTF-8 UIDs individually framed by four-byte big-endian lengths; UID byte ordering breaks digest ties. It greedily chooses at most30 pairs with no reused source group, then reports the entire eligible/rejected/selected ledger and shortage. It never broadens the cohort or lowers a threshold to fill the count.

The interface contains no detector score, output-quality criterion or marked-image feature. Input provenance and scientific unlock remain obligations of the future authorized worker: this module cannot determine whether a caller truthfully supplied source observations. Category agreement is an operational screen, not a human semantic judgment; pHash distance8 is not a guarantee of rejection by either dual reader. All pair results remain descriptive with explicit eligibility shortfall and missing human labels.

Tests cover independent length-framing arithmetic including UTF-8, ordering invariance, exact distance8 versus7, missing/empty observations, noncrowd category handling, disjoint frozen groups and rejection of unknown sources or extra score fields. This is one scheduling component; candidate-specific worker, manifest, budgets and full rehearsals are still required before M1 readiness.
