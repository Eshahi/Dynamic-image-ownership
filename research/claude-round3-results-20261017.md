## Verdict

**As written, the pre-registered bar fails.** The protocol listed synthetic siblings as negatives without condition, and 2 of 6 were accepted. Their D1 scores are .077 and .060, just above τ1 .0594. Every other condition passes: .2 is 39/40 on both regenerators, and the 40 different look-alikes, 59,280 unrelated pairs and 80 transfers are all rejected.

**Under the user's definition applied uniformly, the bar passes.** The synthetic sibling pairs are 26.6 dB apart, closer than 95% of strength-.2 regenerations are to their originals. By the definition they are ambiguous, not different. The protocol was therefore internally inconsistent: it applied the definition to real look-alikes but not to synthetic ones. That inconsistency is Claude's design error and is reported, not silently corrected.

## Other findings

- **The D1 floor has too little margin against texture siblings.** Across 14 synthetic seeds, sibling D1 ranges .015-.100 (seed 13: .100). Calibration had only 6 siblings, with a maximum of .052. Regenerated positives have D1 of at least about .32 at .4. A floor of about .15 would cost almost nothing but must be tested on new data.
- **Overlapping footage.** Codex flagged that the round-3 shot 04_5b shares frames with calibration pair tos-02_3d. All three 04_5b pairs are "different" and rejected; excluding them changes no count.
- **Secondary (median, 23.6 dB) definition:** 55 different look-alikes with 3 accepts (18.8, 20.4 and 22.6 dB), plus the 2 siblings. It fails.
- **Cross-check against Codex:** its own D1-only scoring of the same data matches at .2 (30 and 33 source majorities; seed counts 91 against 91 and 98 against 99). At .4 Claude's seed counts are 1-3 higher, because Claude's F_R omits Codex's correct-record ranking. That ranking cannot change the round-3 rule, which accepts no wrong-source pair.
