# Channel geometry and the remaining binding question

Primary researcher note, 2026-10-04. This is an interpretation of frozen development designs, not a replacement protocol, new experiment, acceptance verdict or impossibility theorem.

For an image perturbation d with RMS at most epsilon and n pixel components, a differentiable scalar reader s has first-order change g^T d, where g is its image gradient. Cauchy–Schwarz gives |g^T d| <= epsilon sqrt(n) ||g||. If the perturbation is constrained to a decoder tangent range V, replace g by its orthogonal projection onto V. This is only a local linear calculation: clipping, RGB8 quantization, finite steps, feature recomputation and diffusion are nonlinear. It is not a measured global capacity bound. Two requested scores also depend on their projected gradient alignment; improving both is not equivalent to optimizing one carrier twice.

The source bypass J=I+D(u)-D(u0) restores the original photograph at u=u0 while restricting the optimized residual to decoder-produced changes. It removes baseline reconstruction error from the delivered image by construction. It does not establish that those changes survive a separate encoder or a stochastic regeneration channel. A-C therefore differentiates through the actual VAE reader and a float VAE cycle, while separately checking saved-image blind scores. Neither a source-template surrogate score nor terminal predecode bits count as extracted image evidence. C-L100's exact complementary-word recovery at terminal, PNG and extra VAE stages is a useful channel witness; its failed generated-counterfactual quality remains a separate failure.

The continuous instance template before compression has inner product

`<E1 tensor b(H1), E2 tensor b(H2)> = <E1,E2> * (1 - 2*HD(H1,H2)/32)`.

Thus robustness to pHash drift and distinct-instance separation compete. At CLIP cosine near1 and Hamming distance8 the ideal template correlation is0.5. A coherent instance signal with score8 might consequently produce approximately4 under another such template, exactly the pilot threshold. Native background, normalization and compression prevent treating this approximation as an actual score prediction, but they do not justify a guarantee of rejection. A larger embedding margin cannot be credited as better binding without T4/T5 evidence. The projected kernel errors are retained to distinguish compression effects from actual content ambiguity. The confirmatory draft's Hamming>=8 pair screen supplies a difference criterion, not a security theorem.

Regeneration can preserve broad semantics while altering the pHash and carrier. `semantic_only` consequently records observed component alignment; it does not identify the cause. Likewise `both_match` is a public OwnerID/content-pattern consistency result. Anyone able to compute the same public maps can optimize a forgery. Conditional random-owner concentration bounds describe a nonadaptive fixed-image null, not that attacker. A cryptographic signature would require an authenticated signing key/enrollment protocol and robust recovery of a signed content statement; those are different resources and must not be silently inferred from public hash maps. A secret PRF would change key access and verifier assumptions, not automatically solve noisy content binding.
