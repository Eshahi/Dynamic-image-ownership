# WP2: candidate contributions and author ranking

Authoring escalation, Codex, 2026-10-28. This is an idea portfolio, not the hostile review or the primary-selection decision. No images, scientific code, models, metrics or timing experiments were run. All positive claims below are hypotheses. No candidate currently establishes TIFS readiness.

The four requested seeds are I01–I04. I05–I12 are eight additional proposals developed here. They span writer design, prediction, attacks, mechanism, coding, evaluation and verification protocols; they are not twelve settings of one writer. Weak or pre-empted candidates are retained explicitly so that selection cannot silently forget why they were rejected.

## Common interpretation and evidence

The starting evidence is the authoritative MAIN STATE and the [brief](BRIEF.md), not a new reanalysis. F5 retains 182/200 source-majority detections on SD1.5 .2 but 0/200 on SDXL .2; K3 gives 61/200. Foreign codec round trips retained 55/60 development sources. These observations motivate questions but do not identify a unique causal mechanism. The failed first-order transfer law stays failed.

All Stage S proposals use only the existing 60 development sources and documented spares. The 200 Phase-B sources, 40 reserves and other held-out images are excluded. K3 is **optimization-excluded, historically observed**, not an unseen discovery family. A fresh family for confirmation must be chosen and frozen without using its results. Standard distortions are a separately fixed, small secondary article family, included for all compared arms; they cannot select the main contribution.

Costs below are alternative **standalone** Stage S envelopes, including a two-source server rehearsal, setup, reading, quality, a small secondary-distortion section and retrieval. They must not be added together and advertised as one 15-hour study. Cost ranges are planning estimates, not laptop measurements; each requires 1.5-times measured server extrapolation and a 15-hour hard cap. At USD .45–.60/hour, a 15-hour screen is approximately USD 6.75–9 before provider-specific charges. No spending is authorized by this document.

“Positive dev probability” is this author's subjective probability that the specified practical screen succeeds, conditional on correct implementation, available retained assets and completing its inventory. It is **not** a publication probability, calibrated posterior or result. Fit scores are 1 (unlikely as a standalone contribution) to 5 (strong security-journal relevance if validated). A high probability of finding a weakness can coexist with low novelty.

The source distinctions below rely on inspected primary versions and locators in [WP1](02-prior-art-delta.md), its [audit receipt](literature/wp1-delta-audit.md), and the [October 25 map](../encoder-transfer-20261019/final-study-20261025/article-drafts/close-prior-art-map.md). SemBind, Guidance Watermarking and VINE were also reopened in this authoring pass. “Boundary” means a question not settled by those inspected sections; it is not an exhaustive novelty certificate. ForenMark and the other inaccessible primary texts remain an unresolved submission risk.

## I01 — Native-injection-aware writer with a fixed content-bound reader

**One-sentence claim.** Under a fixed source-distortion budget, optimizing a post-hoc residual through family-specific noisy codecs improves native regeneration attribution beyond both codec exposure and generic augmentation, including a family excluded from writer optimization.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [Guidance Watermarking §§4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22126v2) occupies reader-guided, augmented-gradient insertion; our test is a fixed photograph and unchanged F5 reader with a controlled native-noise term. [VINE §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18775v2) occupies generative priors and blur-surrogate robustness; its surrogate is an essential competing explanation. [DrawMark, method/experiments](https://msf-hnu.github.io/resource/DrawMark_Defeating_Regeneration_Attacks_by_Embedding_Watermark_into_Predicted_Noise_of_Diffusion_Models%20(1).pdf) occupies noise-aware embedding; its learned in-generation mechanism differs from this frozen-reader post-hoc intervention. This is a narrow empirical distinction, not “first EOT” or “first noise-aware watermark.”

**Decisive screen and cost.** Twenty-four fixed sources, three seeds; SD1.5 .2, SDXL .1/.2 and K3 .2. Compare F6-noise, matched F6-codec, matched F6-generic augmentation, F5, v5, WAM, VINE-R and C0: 2,304 native outputs. Require >=18/24 native source-majority C on each SDXL cell and K3; >=6 more than F5 in each SDXL cell, >=4 on K3; >=4 more than each writer ablation in each SDXL cell; no more than two lost SD1.5 successes. All clean sources must pass strict quality, >=23/24 both-match, and paired median LPIPS/PSNR changes must be <=+.005/>=-1 dB against F5. T4/T5 negatives must be complete with no false attribution. **12–15 hours, but unverified**; extra augmentation arm tightens the earlier six-marked-arm budget. Rehearsal failure is a resource stop, not permission to omit the control or silently shrink the study.

**Failure and kill.** Noisy-codec inputs may be outside decoder training support; native denoising may undo any improvement. Kill if codec or generic augmentation explains the gain, clean quality fails, K3 does not improve, binding weakens, or the complete design cannot fit 15 hours. Fit **4/5**, positive dev **0.25**.

## I02 — Owner/content binding on a robust existing carrier

**One-sentence claim.** On identical VINE-R-marked outputs, a declared content-verification layer reduces false donor attribution under T4/T5 while retaining most cross-family T3 carrier successes.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [SemBind §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20310v1) already binds latent carriers to semantics. [MetaSeal §§4–7 and Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.10766v2) already gives signed content-dependent attribution but loses verification after regeneration. [DREW §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02836v2) already couples watermark identifiers with retrieval. Only a measured security/robustness gain in this fixed-photograph setting remains; changing the carrier is not a new primitive.

**Decisive screen and cost.** Twenty-four sources, the four native cells above, three seeds. Use the same VINE outputs for binding-on, binding-off and fingerprint-only verification; explicitly choose known-record registry assistance or a fully specified decodable payload before running. At least 12/24 negative source units must deliver the donor carrier, otherwise the binding comparison is unidentifiable. Necessary practical bar: >=50% reduction in false attribution on delivered-carrier negatives, <=10 percentage-point loss of T3 attribution, and strict clean quality. **6–10 hours** using existing VINE assets.

**Failure and kill.** Regeneration can invalidate the descriptor, while near-copies can match it. A public owner label is not authentication. Kill as a research contribution if only a registry plus carrier swap remains, if carrier delivery is absent, or decoding/authentication is assumed from 69/100 presence. Fit **2/5**, positive dev **0.55** for engineering benefit, not novelty.

## I03 — Finite reader-response forecasts instead of a transfer law

**One-sentence claim.** A small frozen model using finite noisy-codec reader responses forecasts native watermark retention better than family/strength, clean-margin, encoder-gain and quality controls on prospectively sealed outcomes across multiple readers.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [Drift Kernel, inspected paper/card](../encoder-transfer-20261019/final-study-20261025/literature/drift-kernel-2026-primary.md) predicts reconstruction drift, not signed reader acceptance. [Shallow Diffuse §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.21088v5) uses local structure for insertion and inversion detection, not this fixed-reader out-of-sample forecast. [WAVES §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08573v3) occupies quality/attack evaluation; a sealed increment over its natural quality comparators is the distinction. No guarantee follows merely from using an SNR feature.

**Decisive screen and cost.** Split 60 sources deterministically into 30 fit/10 calibration/20 locked evaluation. Four retained writers, three families, fixed strengths/seeds; estimate four finite noisy-codec draws per feature cell, then seal forecasts before releasing new native outcomes. Practical bar: Brier improvement >=.05 over the strongest prespecified simple comparator on locked sources, positive improvement in >=3/4 readers and on K3; no native-outcome quality metric in a predictor advertised as pre-regeneration. Report source-cluster uncertainty and leave-one-reader/family stress tests. **10–15 hours**, using retained development clean marks only after verifying their identity.

**Failure and kill.** Reader-score scales may be incompatible, base rates may explain performance, and the denoiser is unobserved by the proxy. Kill if gains disappear against family/strength or clean margins, forecasts are released after outputs, or leave-family performance reverses. Fit **4/5**, positive dev **0.35**.

## I04 — The actual RQ-02 latent insertion test

**One-sentence claim.** A watermark inserted into a source image's autoencoder latent can retain the same lightweight fixed-reader content attribution after decoding and foreign-family regeneration while preserving the source photograph.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [OptMark §§2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.21727v1) occupies optimized latent insertion. [ONRW method/experiments](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17388v1) occupies inversion/noise optimization with semantic preservation. [Shallow Diffuse §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.21088v5) already includes shallow and post-generation variants. The only useful distinction is jointly satisfying source-relative strict quality, unchanged lightweight readout and foreign-family content binding; latent insertion itself is not new.

**Decisive screen and cost.** Twelve fixed sources; first test unmarked VAE reconstruction quality as an eligibility ceiling, then latent-chip optimization, unchanged F5 pixel writer and a decoded unmarked control. At least 11/12 clean both-match and all strict source-relative quality gates; three seeds at SDXL .1 and K3 .2, with >=9/12 source-majority C on both. Add VINE/WAM task controls if this first gate passes within the frozen inventory. **5–9 hours**. A fail at the reconstruction ceiling ends the candidate before diffusion.

**Failure and kill.** VAE reconstruction alone can violate the source-quality contract; latent optimization may encode an easily erased direction. Kill on that ceiling, failure to beat F5 on both foreign cells, or any need to relabel a pixel writer as latent. Fit **2/5**, positive dev **0.15**.

## I05 — Carrier-conditioned attribution laundering

**One-sentence claim.** A bounded transplant-and-regenerate adversary can deliver a donor's watermark into an operationally different recipient while exploiting content-gate tolerance, and separating carrier delivery from binding exposes failures hidden by unconditional rejection rates.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [SemBind §4 and Appendix D](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20310v1) already studies imprinting, reprompting and adaptive forgeries; our potential delta is an exact-record/near-copy task with explicit delivered-carrier denominators, not first forgery. [WARP §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.40031v1) already evaluates re-embedding and regeneration. [Soft-binding benchmark §§3,6–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04151v1) already tests near-copy verification. A new result must show a reproducible, previously unmeasured failure or a real defense improvement under matched access.

**Decisive screen and cost.** Freeze 24 donor/recipient units from development identifiers, including documented operationally distinct near-copy units where available. Primary T4 uses only the marked donor and recipient: a fixed patch-transplant grid and at most six native candidates per pair/family, shared across defenses; no result-selected pairs. Donor-clean residual subtraction, if separately studied, is a stronger-access diagnostic and cannot substantiate the primary threat claim. T5 reads the distinct, unmanipulated target under the donor claim; transplant/regeneration results remain separately labelled T4/composed attacks. Evaluate F5, a declared VINE-plus-record binding control and fingerprint-only; native VINE/WAM presence are carrier controls. Require >=12/24 carrier-delivered eligible units, >=6/24 unconditional false donor attributions for a vulnerability claim, and >=3 distinct source pairs rather than one repeated pair. Exact donor q/record and the frozen content gate are required for donor attribution; F5 C alone is insufficient, and instance h is a separate endpoint. Compare unregenerated transplant and the same candidate schedule without carrier conditioning. **8–12 hours** for two families with serial models; query ceilings include every attempted candidate.

**Failure and kill.** A removal success is not a forgery. A different record ID alone does not prove operationally distinct content. Missing near-copy labels make the T5 portion unavailable. Kill as a TIFS primary if attacks only break the local method, if no applicable external binding baseline is implementable, if quality fails, or if a known SemBind/soft-binding attack already explains the result without a new security finding. Fit **4/5 if generalized**, positive dev **0.45** on the bounded local screen.

## I06 — Finite denoiser rescue versus erasure

**One-sentence claim.** Noise injection and subsequent denoising have separable, reader-dependent effects, so low directly decoded noisy-latent scores cannot in general be treated as irreversible watermark destruction.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [Drift Kernel, inspected card](../encoder-transfer-20261019/final-study-20261025/literature/drift-kernel-2026-primary.md) treats image drift. [Early Semantic Commitment, qualified inspected card](../encoder-transfer-20261019/final-study-20261025/literature/early-semantic-commitment-primary.md) concerns classifier response along trajectories and has partial access, not verified acceptance. [MarkNull §§5–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10166v1) studies manipulated proxy latents. Our object is finite **signed watermark-reader** rescue/erasure on coupled native trajectories; stagewise curves alone are not causal proof.

**Decisive screen and cost.** Twenty-four sources, four writers, two design families plus K3, three seeds. Save a fixed small set of native trajectory checkpoints; compare final native output, direct noisy decode, and matched starting-latent interventions with the same noise tape. Prespecify a rescue event as proxy negative/final positive and an erasure event as proxy positive/final negative. Require both events on >=6/24 source units for at least two readers and a reproducible family interaction; propose no post-hoc checkpoint. **8–12 hours**; no trajectory backpropagation.

**Failure and kill.** Intermediate decodes are distribution-shifted artifacts, and altered latents may be off-manifold. Kill the mechanism claim if only one local reader exhibits the effect, if checkpoint decoding changes the native trajectory, or if the study remains a descriptive curve without predictive/interventional value. Fit **3/5**, positive dev **0.45**.

## I07 — Attribution ceiling from accepted regeneration equivalence

**One-sentence claim.** For a precisely specified legitimate-transformation distribution and distinct-record alternative, their observation overlap imposes a quantitative limit on simultaneous T3 acceptance and T5 rejection, independent of the chosen watermark detector.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [SemBind §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20310v1) studies a robustness/forgery trade-off. [Soft-binding benchmark §§6–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04151v1) studies near-copy errors. [Dual-Guard §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.19090v1) studies reference-assisted closed-set authentication. Our proposed contribution is an operational hypothesis-testing ceiling for specified equivalence classes, not the classical total-variation inequality itself.

**Decisive screen and cost.** Freeze 12 distinct-record development pairs and a common native T3 grid; map positive and negative outcomes through each declared detector observation. Construct exact observation collisions where possible, and report finite-test-set deterministic lower bounds on unavoidable conflicting labels. Require collisions across >=3 independent pairs and at least two observation systems, with all labeling assumptions explicit. **6–10 hours**. A learned discriminator's failure is **not** an upper bound on total variation and cannot certify population impossibility.

**Failure and kill.** Semantic closeness is not equality of observations or distributions; small data cannot establish overlap for unrestricted readers. Kill any population impossibility claim without a valid distributional bound; if only exact-hash collision in a lossy descriptor is shown, restrict the result to that descriptor. Fit **3/5**, positive dev **0.40** for a finite operational conflict, much lower for a broad theorem.

## I08 — Search-budget-aware attribution rather than per-call FPR

**One-sentence claim.** A fixed per-message threshold can materially understate false-attribution risk when a verifier searches many owner/record candidates, and an explicit roster/query budget restores a defensible decision guarantee.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [Guidance Watermarking Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22126v2) already distinguishes random-image and random-key nulls. [DREW §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02836v2) performs database retrieval. [WAVES detection/identification sections](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08573v3) already distinguishes detection and identification. The possible new finding is a measured operational failure and task-correct repair, not Bonferroni, union bounds or a new cryptographic theorem.

**Decisive screen and cost.** Freeze roster sizes 1/16/256/4096 and a public-query policy, using 60 development source units and fixed random messages. Compare retained fixed thresholds with a separately named roster-aware rule, reporting genuine acceptance at each roster size. Require >=10 percentage-point genuine-acceptance advantage over a uniformly overstrict threshold at equal empirically evaluated global error, or demonstrate a substantial uncorrected risk gap. **3–6 hours**, dominated by one set of reader outputs; no repeated diffusion is needed.

**Failure and kill.** Random-key validity need not hold for chosen keys; millions of comparisons are not millions of independent images. Kill as a main contribution if the conclusion is only a standard multiplicity correction or all evidence depends on an invalid null. Fit **2/5**, positive dev **0.70** for a reporting discrepancy, not originality.

## I09 — The verified-payload frontier behind robust known-message presence

**One-sentence claim.** The retained carrier's regeneration-resistant known-message acceptance does not imply useful exact authenticated-payload capacity, and a measured decoding frontier determines whether registry-free binding is feasible.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [VINE §3 and Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18775v2) supplies the carrier and statistical presence test. [MetaSeal §§4–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.10766v2) explicitly carries signed content and suffers regeneration failure. [DREW §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02836v2) addresses coding of a retrievable pointer. The new question is the joint native-regeneration/decoding/binding frontier at the retained 100-bit carrier, not a new coding bound or a criticism of VINE's actual task.

**Decisive screen and cost.** Freeze three short codebooks with stated minimum distances and decoder radii before outcomes; compare presence, exact decoded pointer and verification on the same 24-source, four-cell, three-seed VINE outputs. Do not fabricate a 36-bit tag as public-key authentication. Require at least a useful declared pointer size and >=90% of presence-positive T3 cases decoded correctly, with zero wrong-pointer acceptance in the finite negatives; report abstentions and the small-source uncertainty. **5–8 hours**. Registry-free signatures need their actual bit budget and may be ruled out before running.

**Failure and kill.** At 69/100 presence, accepting 31 errors is not guaranteed unique decoding of arbitrary 100-bit strings: disjoint decoding balls need distance >=63, and the binary Plotkin bound allows at most four such codewords. This standard bound rules out a naive worst-case 100-bit interpretation; it does not bound average-channel capacity. Kill the authenticated-payload claim if it relies on presence alone, ideal independent errors, or payload lengths excluding ECC/signature overhead. Fit **3/5**, positive dev **0.65** for finding a substantive task gap, low for a standalone novel theorem.

## I10 — Family comparisons at native injection coordinates

**One-sentence claim.** A preregistered native-noise intervention can distinguish nominal-strength/scheduler confounding from a true family-dependent reversal in watermark survival.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [WAVES Appendix F.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08573v3) already evaluates attack strengths and quality. [W-Bench §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18775v2) includes diverse regeneration settings. [Drift Kernel, inspected card](../encoder-transfer-20261019/final-study-20261025/literature/drift-kernel-2026-primary.md) relates drift to diffusion parameters. The possible contribution is a prospectively validated explanatory intervention, not “equal strength is unequal” or simply changing axis labels.

**Decisive screen and cost.** Twenty-four sources, four writers, three families; choose three native timestep settings per family by a fixed latent-noise-coordinate rule before outputs, retaining the native scheduler. Compare with the original nominal-strength grid, source quality and family-only models. Require the frozen coordinate to explain a prespecified substantial portion of held-out-family ranking changes beyond quality and nominal strength, without suppressing contrary cells. **10–14 hours**, with code/step counts frozen from scheduler inspection.

**Failure and kill.** Latent scale and dimension are not directly comparable across codecs; a fitted rescaling can merely memorize family identity. Kill if no operationally justified common coordinate exists, matched coordinates violate the native pipeline, or an equally simple family intercept explains the result. Fit **3/5**, positive dev **0.55**.

## I11 — Robust/fragile interference as a separate channel design problem

**One-sentence claim.** Measuring and constraining interference between F5's robust semantic channel and subsequent fragile/quantization stages can increase joint clean recovery without weakening T4/T5 rejection or foreign-family retention.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [Dual-Guard §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.19090v1) occupies dual complementary channels. [SLICE §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12749v1) occupies structured semantic subchannels. [Guidance Watermarking §§4.4–4.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22126v2) occupies multi-objective gradient handling. A new result would have to isolate actual robust/fragile interference under the fixed photograph and readout; another refinement schedule is insufficient.

**Decisive screen and cost.** Twenty-four sources; fixed writer snapshots before/after quantization, fragile insertion and final refinement; compare a newly named joint constraint against unchanged F5 and a matched-compute clean-refinement control. Require >=4 recovered clean both-match source units without a strict-quality loss and no native/T4/T5 regression; then use the four-cell native screen. **8–12 hours**. The older closed microvariant line is not reopened as a sequence of tuning rounds; this is one preregistered mechanistic test under the session brief.

**Failure and kill.** Existing clean recovery may already be near ceiling, and interference cannot plausibly explain total SDXL loss by itself. Kill if there are fewer than four baseline clean failures to repair, if the effect disappears after saved-RGB8 evaluation, or if it is just more optimization. Fit **2/5**, positive dev **0.40** conditional on non-ceiling baseline units.

## I12 — Ambiguity-aware verification with explicit abstention

**One-sentence claim.** A known-record verifier that rejects ambiguous owner/content matches can lower T5 false attribution while preserving a predeclared minimum fraction of valid regenerated-source decisions.

**Three closest sources and boundary.** [DREW §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02836v2) already uses retrieval and fallback search. [Soft-binding benchmark §§3,6–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04151v1) already evaluates second-stage verification and near-copies. [Dual-Guard Full mode §3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.19090v1) already assumes reference-assisted closed-set authentication. Our possible distinction is a risk/coverage operating point for competing valid records; neither a registry nor “abstain when uncertain” is new.

**Decisive screen and cost.** Freeze a development registry and one ambiguity-margin rule using only a fit partition; evaluate on source-disjoint development sources, T3 outputs and documented near-copy negatives. Compare binding alone, ambiguity rejection and fingerprint-only at matched coverage. Require >=50% reduction of false attributions with >=80% of baseline true T3 acceptances retained, and no hidden transfer of ambiguous cases into successes. **6–9 hours** including VINE/F5 carrier controls.

**Failure and kill.** Removing every difficult example trivially lowers risk; public descriptors can admit adversarially chosen ambiguous records. Kill if the gain vanishes at matched coverage, if provenance wording becomes a legal ownership claim, or if registry assumptions alone explain the benefit. Fit **2/5**, positive dev **0.50**.

## Author recommendation before hostile review

Proceed to hostile review of I01, I03 and I05. I01 has the most direct route to a useful positive method result, but its generic-augmentation control adds unmeasured cost and could remove its apparent novelty. I03 is a coherent alternative study, not an extra analysis silently attached after I01 fails. I05 must earn external relevance; the known local registry weakness is not a new discovery. I09's coding distinction should inform any binding proposal regardless of selection.

None justifies the title “robust ownership” without separating carrier presence, exact donor/record recovery, content consistency, operational instance identity and lawful ownership. Missing human assessments stay missing. The final selection and any fixed Stage S parameters belong in `04-selection.md` and the protocol after independent review.
