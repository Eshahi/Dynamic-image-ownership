## 2. Blocking findings before protocol freeze

### B1. The proposed attribution claim is stronger than the primary endpoint

In `m1b_e3_s1_analysis.predicates`, C is `semantic.read AND semantic.content_match`; it does not require recovery of the original semantic code. Q is exact original q recovery, and J is Q plus current-content agreement. A writer that raises C while decoding another compatible q has not demonstrated retention of the original payload. Moreover, q is a content-derived semantic code, **not a unique record identifier**. J alone does not establish original-instance identity, and `both_match` alone is not an evaluator comparison against the donor's original q and h.

**Author action:** make J co-required with historical C for the new writer's foreign-family practical gate, while retaining all C/Q/J/H/D/F/A counts. Name the positive claim **original semantic-code retention with a fixed content-consistency reader**. Do not call it exact image identity, authenticated ownership or the proposal's latent insertion. For an original-instance T4/T5 attribution row, explicitly report exact donor q, exact donor h and the declared core acceptance predicate together. A declared full-instance event can be `D AND F`; report J and F separately as well. F5's clean-decoded h reference remains an acknowledged historical limitation, not an independently known intended payload.

An exact donor q collision may expose a coarse equivalence class, not a cryptographic forgery. The task definition must say which differing contents are prohibited members of that class. The public-derived profile and public owner label supply no secret-owner authentication.

### B2. A generic augmentation control is necessary, but an arbitrarily weak one is insufficient

Guidance Watermarking already optimizes existing reader gradients through augmentations; VINE already motivates inexpensive blur-like surrogates for generative editing. A codec-only control isolates encoder exposure but cannot exclude ordinary low-pass robustness as the explanation. Conversely, winning against one weak Gaussian-noise setting would not establish a general advantage over generic EOT.

**Author action:** retain all three new writers. Give the generic writer the same clean loss, original target q, 150 surrogate updates, 50 clean refinements, projection budgets, quantization and fragile stages. Its fixed transform set must contain a meaningful low-pass/pixelation condition as well as noise, with every parameter committed before outputs. Report the result as superiority over **this specified generic control**, not all generic augmentation methods. Do not drop the arm or reduce only its steps after a cost forecast.

A concrete admissible small control is a uniform cycle of (i) separable Gaussian blur, sigma 1.5 pixels, kernel 11, reflection padding; (ii) bilinear downsample 512 to 256 and bilinear return, with interpolation/antialias settings explicitly pinned; and (iii) additive independent RGB Gaussian noise, standard deviation .02 on [0,1], followed by clipping. This is a locally specified control, not a reproduction of VINE's full training pipeline. The author may choose a different fixed set **now**, with its rationale, but it must not be outcome-selected.

### B3. The finite surrogate is not native img2img, and its intervention is incompletely frozen

The mode-encoded channel followed by immediate noisy decoding omits posterior sampling and the entire denoiser. Its decoder sees a noisy latent outside its usual clean input distribution. An apparent objective improvement can be an off-manifold reader response which denoising later destroys. The author correctly gives counterexamples; these must survive into the claim and protocol.

**Author action:** use posterior mode in both codec and noisy-codec writers, call the latter a **finite noisy-codec surrogate**, and retain the exact native posterior-sampling path for evaluation. Freeze actual scheduler-derived a/b, family scaling, resolution, resizing and clipping. Do not divide by a, normalize noisy latents, use straight-through RGB8 rounding, or substitute nominal strength for a timestep without declaring a different method. Freeze the 150-update family/cell cycle as SD1.5 .2, SDXL .1, SDXL .2 repeated 50 times; the codec control uses the identical family sequence with a=1 and b=0. K3 is excluded from all writer optimization and writer diagnostics used for selection.

Use the author's 150 clean-plus-surrogate updates followed by 50 clean-only refinements, with the inherited two fragile writes. That is **350 reader-gradient evaluations**, plus 150 codec encode/decode gradient evaluations for each codec-based writer. It is not 150 F5-equivalent calls. Record the counts and actual server time. Use disjoint predetermined random streams for optimization and diagnostic proxy draws. Native saved-PNG detection, not proxy loss, decides success.

### B4. The 24-source cost claim is stale after adding the third writer

The reuse note's 2,016 outputs, 48 new marks and approximately 466 seconds/new-mark ceiling describe seven arms, not the current eight-arm proposal. At n=24 the current inventory is 2,304 native outputs, 72 new marks and 168 marked clean images. Seven hours of marking minus unchanged F5 at the historical 119 seconds/source leaves less than 310 seconds/new mark before v5/WAM/VINE and overhead. A 1.5-times safety factor and rehearsal consume more slack. None of this establishes 24 GB feasibility.

**Author action:** adopt n=16 now. The corresponding inventory is 1,536 native outputs, 48 new marks and 112 marked clean images; original C0 files are additional. Under the same illustrative seven-hour marking allocation, subtracting F5 leaves less than 485 seconds/new mark before other costs, still unverified. The two rehearsal sources are additional and disjoint. The rehearsal must exercise **all three** writers, all native families including K3 load, reads, quality, T4/T5 and secondary distortions. Extrapolate the complete inventory, not just diffusion calls.

Freeze `one-time cost + 1.5 * measured variable cost + retrieval reserve <= 15 hours` as the launch condition, with the paid rehearsal included in the accounting. Preserve a reserve for the next uninterruptible unit and result retrieval. On failure record RESOURCE_STOP; do not shrink n, steps, controls or native cells after seeing outcomes. Serial graphs and unloaded readers are required during the K3 prompt-encoder peak. Allocated GPU memory alone is not a device-fit measurement. If safe serial execution still does not fit, the design does not fit this hardware.

### B5. T4/T5 can currently produce reassuring but uninformative zeros

F5's robust donor-carrier delivery, donor semantic-code acceptance and donor-instance acceptance are different events. A patch may never deliver enough donor signal; zero downstream acceptance then says little about binding. Also, a composite really contains a donor patch. Detecting that genuine component is not automatically a false whole-image provenance claim.

**Author action:** define the protected unit as the whole recipient image for the false-attribution task, and report partial donor-content detection separately. Primary T4 gets only published donor, recipient and public owner label. Keep known-clean residual transfer a separately labelled stronger-access diagnostic, not a substitute. Include a fixed published-image patch grid and no-transfer/unmarked-donor shams. Freeze geometry, blend, candidate count, target-relative eligibility and all evaluator calls. No detector-guided choice of pairs or candidates is allowed in the nonadaptive primary experiment.

Primary T5 is an **unmanipulated target** read under the donor claim. All ALPHA-marked different images are not wrong-owner negatives. Exact donor-code diagnostics, current-content C, core F, true wrong-owner reads and unmarked semantic neighbors must remain separate. Donor q/h can be evaluator references without secretly becoming detector side information.

Use disjoint donor/target source identities when reporting independent pair-level intervals. A practical allocation is 16 screen donors, 16 other development targets and two disjoint rehearsal sources. Select target assignments without replacement by an original-only, fixed semantic-neighbor rule before new marks; retain duplicate checks and ambiguous labels. This is an algorithmic neighbor set, not a human-certified near-copy or distinct-instance set. Do not pretend 16 overlapping directed edges give 16 independent pairs. Missing distinctness labels make the relevant attribution conclusion unavailable.

Report both unconditional pair success and carrier-conditioned acceptance on the same fixed candidate set. If delivery is rare, say the binding test is uninformative; do not call zero events a demonstrated defense. This need not erase a valid T3 writer improvement, but it prevents an unconditional security/TIFS verdict.

### B6. Missingness and comparator failures can manufacture a winning screen

Counting an absent comparator outcome as failure can create an apparent paired improvement. Counting an absent C0 outcome as a negative creates nonexistent specificity evidence. Dropping blocked sources changes the fixed sample. These are especially material at n=16.

**Author action:** retain every source and seed. For each source-majority predicate define a lower value from observed positives and an upper value allowing unresolved outcomes to be positive. Use candidate-lower minus comparator-upper counts for all gain gates, or require the relevant paired comparator outcomes complete. A source with two observed positives has a known majority even if its third call is unresolved; a source with one positive, one negative and one unresolved call does not. Negative checks remain incomplete when their required outcomes are unresolved. Distinguish SCIENTIFIC_KILL, RESOURCE_STOP and INCOMPLETE; no partial-inventory GO. Safety blocks remain authentic missing outcomes with no changed prompts or replacement seeds.

### B7. I03 needs stronger null models and an unambiguous prediction target

Family/strength-only and a global intercept are too weak if each watermark has a characteristic survival rate. A normalized reader score can merely encode method identity. Reusing historically inspected sources also means sealed new seeds are not a previously untouched source evaluation.

**Author action if activating the backup:** fix whether the predicted outcome is a single native seed or the three-seed majority. A per-seed Brier target with source-first aggregation is the simpler choice. Add a prespecified smoothed **method x family x native-cell base-rate** comparator, plus nested models adding clean margin, encoder gain and proxy quality. Freeze their fitting, smoothing, feature normalization and penalty using the 30 fit/10 calibration partition only; score the prespecified strongest comparator or every comparator on the 20 evaluation sources without choosing one from evaluation outcomes. Keep native-output quality an outcome-informed diagnostic. The .05 improvement criterion is against the strong comparator, not whichever baseline loses most.

Do not reuse a fitted K3 intercept for a leave-family claim. In leave-reader evaluation, the held-out reader cannot contribute fitted labels or normalization estimated from its evaluation outcomes. Report observed-family interpolation and leave-family/reader extrapolation separately. If the comparator's Brier score is below .05, the proposed .05 absolute improvement cannot pass even with a perfect predictor; report that failure rather than changing the target afterward. New-seed sealing remains useful, but fresh sources and a fresh family are necessary before broad generalization.

### B8. I05 has no demonstrated contribution beyond an already known failure class

The identity `P(F) = P(E and M) * P(B | E and M)` is correct when all events use the same experimental unit and the conditioning denominator is positive. It is bookkeeping, not a security theorem. It is undefined at zero delivery. Using per-candidate delivery in the first term and any-of-six false attribution in the second breaks the intended interpretation.

**Author action:** do not select I05 now. If revisited later, it needs a credible applicable external binding defense, documented operationally distinct units, the same saved candidates for binding-on/off, pair-level any-of-K and per-candidate results, and the full query count. A wrapper assembled from VINE plus the already failed local registry is not an independent published binding defense. SemBind is close prior art but its generated-image, inversion-based task cannot simply be substituted for arbitrary-photograph post-hoc marking. No new external-baseline acquisition is required for the current I01 screen merely to keep this rejected candidate alive.

## 4. Mathematical checks and non-blocking warnings

- **I01 objective: conditionally verified.** The 52 dB initial RMS is approximately .002512 and the RGB 512-square L2 radius approximately 2.23. These are the first robust-stage constraints, not final-image PSNR. The local signed-margin derivative is mathematically appropriate where differentiable. The positive sum of clean and surrogate losses need not preserve every family; the cellwise native gates are therefore essential. The denoiser counterexample is valid and rules out the claimed universal implication.
- **I03 counterexamples: verified within their assumptions.** Coupled additive noise cancels in a latent pair difference. The linear-reader Gaussian acceptance formula holds for b>0 and a nonzero readout vector. Its scalar sign information explains why energy is insufficient, but does not establish sufficiency of the proposed feature vector. Two denoisers sharing the same codec can still yield different labels.
- **I05 factorization: verified with the unit/denominator qualifications in B8.** Conditioning on successful carrier delivery can select easy images; the conditional figure is descriptive, not a causal estimate of binding strength. Whole-source or genuinely disjoint-pair dependence must be preserved in uncertainty calculations.
- **I09 coding caution: verified as a worst-case bound.** Guaranteed correction of 31 errors in a binary length-100 code requires distance at least 63; the stated Plotkin argument gives at most four codewords. It does not bound typical-channel capacity or invalidate known-message testing. Keep that distinction; no new coding study is necessary for this selection.
- **Specificity remains weak at this n.** Even 0/16 independent negative source units has a one-sided 95% upper binomial bound `1 - .05^(1/16)`, about .171. For 20 and 24 independent units the bounds are approximately .139 and .117. Repeated seeds/patch scales do not create independent source units, and these binomial illustrations do not apply unchanged to overlapping pairs. A zero screen is not a small false-positive guarantee.
- **K3 is optimization-excluded, historically observed.** It is not discovery-unseen. SD3.5 or another genuinely unobserved family remains a conditional Stage C requirement, with frozen selection, native schedule, access, storage and 24 GB feasibility. Architectural difference alone is not proof of independent initialization or training history.
- **Prior-art access gaps persist.** The inaccessible ForenMark full text and other documented leads are submission risks. They do not require abandoning a cheap screen, but prohibit declaring novelty cleared. The prior-art survey did not establish that the narrow remaining idea is new.
- **Rehearsal verification is not available locally.** Syntax and pure-logic tests cannot verify gradient flow, family scaling, fixed-reader dispatch, RGB8 behavior, native noise matching, GPU memory or runtime. Those remain server rehearsal checks. The F6 label must explicitly route to the F5 reader; the old adapter's label fallthrough selects the wrong behavior.
- **Historical evidence is context, not fresh validation.** Preserve all negative results and complete source/seed inventories. No trend in the old held-out results may choose a Stage S source or threshold. The intended h export may improve F6 documentation but must not silently redefine retained F5 endpoints.

## 5. Prior-art inspection supporting this review

These are primary texts reopened during this review. The comparisons are reviewer inference from the cited sections, not an exhaustive priority finding; no paper was reproduced.

- [Guidance Watermarking v2, §§4.2–4.5 and 5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22126v2): existing-reader guidance, augmentation gradients and gradient aggregation already occupy the generic optimization machinery. Its in-generation task differs from fixed-photograph residual optimization. This supports B2, not a finding that I01 is already identical.
- [VINE v2, §§3.1 and 4.3–4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18775v2): the paper uses blur-like surrogate distortions and a generative embedding prior, including an ablation showing why a surrogate control matters. A local Gaussian-noise-only strawman would not confront that explanation. This supports the low-pass generic arm and retention of VINE as a strong carrier baseline.
- [SemBind v1, §§2.3 and 3.1, Appendix D.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20310v1): semantic binding and black-box transfer/imprinting/reprompting defenses are already explicit research questions, with access assumptions unlike our published-photo patch task. Its overlay-spoofing experiment also narrows any claim that a donor-patch semantic attack is new. This narrows I05 and generic binding novelty without certifying our own binding.
- [Soft-binding benchmark v1, §§6–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04151v1): expected-key checks, registry scale and near-copy failures are already central, and reference fingerprints must correspond to the published asset. Registry-assisted verification is therefore not a new contribution merely because its carrier changes.

DrawMark, OptMark, WARP and the other sources in the author's map were not independently reopened in this bounded review; their detailed coverage remains documented author evidence, not an additional reviewer inspection claim. Nothing in the four reopened sources supplies a theorem for native regeneration survival or validates F6.
