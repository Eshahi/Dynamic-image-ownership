## Original analytical guardrail for injection SNR

For an additive Gaussian native channel z_t = a_t E(x) + sigma_t epsilon, using the same epsilon for a marked/control pair makes their difference exactly a_t [E(x_w)-E(x)]. A paired-difference norm therefore contains no realized noise cancellation failure by itself. A candidate signal ratio must specify whitening, sigma_t, latent scaling and dimensional normalization; zero-noise codec retention is a separate endpoint.

Even a correct norm ratio does not encode the direction of the fixed reader's decision boundary or the semantic-binding gate after nonlinear denoising. Two equal-energy residuals can have opposite signed reader responses. Prospective leave-family-out prediction with reader-margin and family/noise controls is consequently necessary. This is a mathematical diagnostic, not a new theorem of final regeneration survival, and no claim of first SNR analysis is justified.
