# Evidence synthesis

This report preserves reported evidence; source hashes establish identity, not scientific truth.

- bar-lvmark: Method: 3D-wavelet/RGB decoder and importance-selected latent-decoder modulation. Eight named baselines (despite text saying seven): HiDDeN, WAM, Blind, REVMark, Stable Signature, WOUAF, VideoShield, VideoMark. Panda-70M: 10K training videos; Vidprom:100 prompts;100 messages; Open-Sora/DynamiCrafter. Attacks: image distortions, frame manipulations, H.264, weight noise/pruning/retraining. Ablations: modulation, transforms, fusion, patches. Quality: PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS/tLP/FVD;100-person AMT study. Formal security guarantee and calibrated false-positive analysis: UNKNOWN. Code: https://github.com/kuai-lab/TIFS26_lvmark . [tifs-b-lvmark; supporting; direct-evidence; confidence high]. Limitations: Adjacent VIDEO; inspected arXiv v4, not final typeset article. Journal 21:4908-4923 (16 pp). Author order differs between repository citation and preprint; preprint order retained.

- bar-mantlemark: Method: pose-dependent spherical frequency patterns and Fourier projection-slice derivation. Nine image baselines: HiDDeN, MBRS, CIN, FIN, TrustMark, Robust-Wide, InvisMark, WAM, VINE; three 3D comparators: GaussianMarker, GuardSplat,3D-GSW. Data: Synthetic NeRF(8 scenes), LLFF(8), Mip-NeRF360(9);600 rendered views, five repeats,95% intervals. Instant-NGP/TensoRF/3DGS; six image manipulations. PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS, ROC/AUC and TPR at validation-calibrated 1% FPR. Ablations/strength sweeps reported; human study UNKNOWN. Synthetic 3DGS TPR .941 at alpha .4,1.000 at .8; Mip360 .742/.942. Code: https://github.com/luo-ziyuan/MantleMark . [tifs-b-mantlemark; supporting; direct-evidence; confidence high]. Limitations: Adjacent 3D reconstruction, not diffusion regeneration. Inspected institutional author manuscript (16 pp); journal 21:256-271, online 21 November 2025. Venue verified in CityU institutional record.
