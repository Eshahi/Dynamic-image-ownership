## Bounded paper summaries

Tang et al. improve a conventional robust reversible watermark by modeling which polar harmonic moments remain stable under chosen attacks. Stable carriers receive adaptive normalization, and a multi-level STDM quantizer adjusts embedding strength; reversible coding stores recovery information afterward. The evaluation separates parameter/component work on 1000 BOWS2 images from another 1000-image comparison. Five external RRW comparators, two payload sizes, seven distortion families and several controlled ablations make the mechanism empirically assessable. Final mean PSNR39.09dB is reported for RRW-PCET. The paper is a useful precedent for matched payload and quality, explicit attack strengths and mechanistic ablations. It is not evidence of diffusion robustness: attack simulation has acknowledged transfer limitations, and extraction BER does not quantify false attribution. **Evidence kind:** direct evidence for design/protocol; final relevance judgment is agent inference. **Locators:** II–III; IV-A–E; V.

Chen et al. target protection of individually selected objects when a user photographs part of a screen. A mask-gated adapter embeds separate100-bit messages into disjoint instances; a watermark locator and pretrained instance segmenter then fuse their predictions for extraction. The study uses COCO training and validation groups, LVIS transfer, four external methods, a separately adapted FPSMark comparator, real device changes and multiple component ablations. Five-instance clean quality reaches36.00dB PSNR and.964 SSIM; partial-capture accuracy depends on instance coverage. Manual perspective correction in comparisons and the heavy segmentation detector are material side information and cost. This accepted TIFS author version demonstrates demand for a specific operational threat and evidence beyond an aggregate accuracy score. It supplies no diffusion or semantic-collision evidence. **Evidence kind:** direct evidence for design/protocol; publication-bar inference is agent inference. **Locators:** III; IV-A–H; V.

## Synthesis for the proposed TIFS article

**Agent inference:** these two precedents support a scoped claim plus explicit operating conditions, strong task-matched comparators, quality/payload controls, separate mechanism ablations, and candid failure cases. They do not justify treating missing FPR or human-study analysis as desirable. The planned article should improve on that limitation with source-level uncertainty and explicit wrong-owner/unmarked controls. Standard distortions can be an article-only secondary table with frozen strengths; neither paper makes them evidence for T3 regeneration or T4/T5 binding.

**Excluded leads, not counted:** *New Visible Watermark Protection Mechanism Based on Information Hiding* (TIFS2025, DOI10.1109/TIFS.2025.3592572) had author code/metadata but no inspected method+experiment full text. *Client-Side Embedding of Screen-Shooting Resilient Image Watermarking* (TIFS2024, DOI10.1109/TIFS.2024.3397043) has an indexed primary title page, but its author PDF returned404; do not count it as full-text inspected. An exploratory Lancaster PDF proved to be the already-covered MantleMark and is not an additional paper. No third paper is claimed.
