# Evidence synthesis

This report preserves reported evidence; source hashes establish identity, not scientific truth.

- delta-optmark: Inference-time initial and intermediate latent optimization uses a fixed DINO reader, adjoint gradients and quality regularizers. Experiments use SD2.1, 1,000 generated images, 48-bit messages and 1,200 optimization rounds; regeneration TPR is .872 at a stated 10^-6 FPR operating point. This pre-empts generic latent insertion, dual-scale robustness and gradient-efficient diffusion optimization. [optmark; supporting; agent-inference; confidence medium]. Limitations: Inspected v1; final conference version may differ.

- delta-onrw: Null-text inversion followed by latent optimization combines decoder loss, self-attention preservation and attention-derived foreground masks. A fixed HiDDeN reader extracts 48 bits. COCO/ImageNet experiments report approximately one minute/image on RTX4090; COCO PSNR27.11 and SSIM.88 do not meet our strict clean gate. This pre-empts generic inversion-noise embedding with a cheap fixed extractor. [onrw; supporting; agent-inference; confidence medium]. Limitations: No independent reproduction; clean source-relative quality differs from this project's strict gate.

- delta-drawmark: Trainable adapters modulate a frozen SD2.1 U-Net's predicted noise; a latent decoder extracts 48/64 bits. Training uses 10,000 DiffusionDB images for ten epochs; evaluation uses 5,000 COCO/LAION prompts and four baselines. Table1 reports .975 diffusion-attack bit accuracy for 48 bits, without establishing our compound ownership endpoint. Generic noise-aware generative watermarking is occupied; a fixed-reader post-hoc multi-family objective needs a narrower distinction. [drawmark; supporting; agent-inference; confidence medium]. Limitations: Author-hosted five-page IEEE paper; no code/reproduction checked.

- delta-softbinding: Watermark payload lookup, fingerprint lookup and second-stage content verification are compared under source-level evaluation and a controlled false-match budget. Near-copy catalogue variants and adversarial descriptor forgery expose limits. This pre-empts generic registry-pointer-plus-fingerprint novelty and requires separation of payload validity, record retrieval and visual binding. [softbinding; supporting; agent-inference; confidence medium]. Limitations: Preprint; security and calibration claims are limited to its tested pipelines.

- delta-vine: VINE uses blurring as a generative-edit surrogate and adapts SDXL-Turbo for 100-bit watermark embedding, decoded by ConvNeXt-B. W-Bench evaluates eleven watermark methods. A mechanism-inspired surrogate and pretrained generative prior are prior art; a new writer must demonstrate a distinct controlled effect beyond this strong baseline. [vine; supporting; agent-inference; confidence medium]. Limitations: Inspected arXiv v2. This project's VINE endpoint is presence/expected-message verification, not ownership.

- delta-tifs-guidance: Official guidance asks for enough architecture, loss, preprocessing, split, optimization, stopping and hardware/software detail for reproducibility. Code and data release are encouraged; this is not a fixed eight-baseline or mandatory human-study rule. Negative findings need scientific significance and rigorous reproducibility. [tifs-guidance; supporting; agent-inference; confidence medium]. Limitations: Publication year not stated; 2026 is access year, not asserted release year.
