## Six primary hypotheses and analysis

The source endpoint is ≥ 2 of 3 J-positive seeds. Repeated seeds share a source; n remains 120. Test the following one-sided alternatives with six fixed Holm slots at familywise .05:

| Slot | Alternative |
|---|---|
| H1 | Paired J(F6-noise)>J(F5), SDXL .2 |
| H2 | Paired J(F6-noise)>J(F6-codec), SDXL .2 |
| H3 | Paired J(F6-noise)>J(F6-generic), SDXL .2 |
| H4 | Paired J(F6-noise)>J(F5), SD3.5 .2 |
| H5 | Pr[J(F6-noise)]>.70, SD3.5 .2 |
| H6 | Pr[J(F6-noise)]>.70, SD1.5 .2 |

For paired tests let B=candidate-positive/comparator-negative and C=reverse; p=Pr[Bin(B + C, .5)≥ B], or 1 if no discordances. For absolute tests p=Pr[Bin(120, .70)≥ observed successes]. Use inclusive exact tails, no midp. Sort p ascending, slot order breaks ties; compare ranks 1..6 to .05/(7−rank) and stop at first failure. Unavailable slots retain p=1; never shrink the family. Report raw/adjusted p, paired 2 × 2 tables, effect sizes, source-cluster confidence intervals, all C/Q/J/H/D/F/A counts and denominators. A complete primary efficacy confirmation requires all six rejections. Individual supported slots remain reportable if others are inconclusive; lack of rejection is not proof of no effect.

Power from the inspected exact calculation uses a=.05/6, sufficient for every Holm slot. At n=120 absolute critical count is 97; marginal power is .4636 at p=.80, .9165 at .85, .9993 at .90. Paired power is .9589 for (p10, p01)=(.25, .05), .2923 for (.20, .10), .9995 for (.35, .05). These are unconditional powers integrating the random discordance count; a marginal difference alone does not specify paired covariance/power. Source independence/exchangeability is an assumption and creator grouping does not prove it. Do not convert per-seed rates into source-majority probabilities using 3r²−2r³ without a conditional-independence model. Any future conditional-power calculation must condition on a declared discordance/joint model, not pretend the expected discordant count is fixed.

These are marginal lower bounds for Holm rejection, not all-six power. Under four (.25, .05) paired alternatives and two .85 absolute rates the dependence-free all-six union lower bound is only .6685; quality/specificity gates reduce total GO further. This is a large-effect confirmation with limited power for modest effects. No increase/decrease in n or weakened .70 bar follows interim results.
