# Recent TIFS evidence bar: inspected studies and the remaining gap

Audit date: 2026-10-28. This is a research synthesis, not a prediction of acceptance or a list of universal journal requirements. Method and experiment sections were inspected for **nine distinct TIFS-linked works** from 2023–2026: **seven core image studies and two adjacent studies (video and image-to-3D)**. StyleMark appears in both earlier A and B inventories and counts once. The request for at least eight recent TIFS studies is met only with the adjacent studies; **eight core image studies is not yet met**. Nor are eight final journal PDFs established: several inspected artifacts are author preprints, and StyleMark has an unresolved final-version/title/author reconciliation. These limits must accompany any use of the count.

The retained [A cards](literature/tifs-bar-a.md), [B synthesis](literature/tifs-b/synthesis/findings.md), [B evidence ledger](literature/tifs-b/synthesis/evidence-ledger.jsonl), and [C cards](literature/tifs-bar-c.md) contain artifact paths/checksums and fuller locators. This audit reopened primary online text where accessible and inspected the cached method/experiment text for the author-hosted Tang, Chen and MantleMark PDFs. Source hashes identify artifacts; they do not validate the scientific claims. No model, image, dataset, metric, timing or encoder run was performed.

## What the comparison supports

**Agent inference:** recent accepted work repeatedly makes one concrete operational problem testable, describes what the detector knows, compares task-matched methods, shows quality/robustness tradeoffs and tests the proposed mechanism through ablation. Several studies deliberately test model/dataset/device transfer. Comparator counts range from two families to twelve unique names; counted datasets range from a small fixed image set to multiple model tasks. Thus neither “at least N baselines” nor “at least N datasets” is established as a universal TIFS rule. Sixteen pages is common here, but there is no inferred page entitlement or page requirement.

Theory, user studies and attribution FP calibration vary. CA-QIM supplies a channel-specific bound; WFM's binomial calculation needs bit assumptions; MantleMark calibrates a1% operating point; IAP reports3000 negatives; LVMark uses100 human raters. Their absence or UNKNOWN status elsewhere is not permission to omit evidence needed by **our** ownership claim. Report image sample sizes, seeds and repeated captures separately: correlated views/messages are not independent negative sources.

IAP already couples a watermark pointer to an enrolled original. Chen already handles semantic instances with masks and learned segmentation. StyleMark's inspected preprint already tests six unseen style-transfer models. WFM already uses perturbation-robust generator optimization. None is a license to claim that generic content awareness, a registry, latent insertion, or cross-model testing is itself new. The [current prior-art delta](02-prior-art-delta.md) is the controlling novelty map; the nine rows here characterize evidence packages rather than exhaust recent novelty.

## Audit disposition and unfilled eighth core-image slot

Additional access attempts did **not** supply an eighth core image full text. Screen-Shooting Robust Watermark Based on Style Transfer and Structural Re-Parameterization (DOI10.1109/TIFS.2025.3542992) also remains an uninspected lead. Do not count these.

RAW (arXiv2403.18774) was not verified as TIFS; GenPTW's author repository identifies AAAI2026. DART's located publication is Pattern Recognition, not TIFS. TrustMark is ICCV2025. They may matter scientifically but cannot fill a TIFS-only slot. Division and Union: Latent Model Watermarking is TIFS2025 but protects model ownership and its full text was not inspected here; it is not used to pad the core image count.

**Disposition:** the general nine-study evidence survey is usable with the stated source/version limits. A strict eight-core-image deliverable remains incomplete by one paper, and an eight-final-journal-fulltext criterion is also unfulfilled. These bibliographic gaps do not repair or worsen the measured F5 result: a prospective positive contribution plus fresh confirmation remains the substantial scientific gap.
