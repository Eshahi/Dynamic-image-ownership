**6. PASS — M1 is a checked missing mechanism, reasonably retained as an evaluation/comparator question.** SOURCE: RT16/C18 buys source/action observations; TD03 I06 uses a fixed common noise tape. Neither specifies adaptive selection of the joint noise law under unchanged native marginals. M1 adds that intervention rather than merely proposing common seeds again. Independently inspected OSCC §§3.1–3.6 and §§4.3/5 define admissible coupling, policy information restrictions, covariance-based selection and calibration fallback, with finite-game evidence. DERIVED/JUDGMENT: Preserving both marginals can improve paired contrast precision through covariance. Better scalar contrast variance need not improve a multi-action gradient or final learned policy. Fixed common random numbers, fixed coupling search and supervised selection therefore remain serious controls. The 09-review disposition leaves task-specific utility UNKNOWN rather than asserting that OSCC forbids all future novelty. That is the correct reading of the generic-novelty rejection. A diagnostic allowance is not evidence that fitting, safety checks and useful variance reduction will all finish within it.

**7. PASS with a narrow clarification — M2 changes the estimand and trajectory measure, not just the sampled source.** SOURCE: RS14/D3 samples finite target units with inclusion probabilities; RO11/C08 searches for adverse outcomes; TD03 I06 diagnoses checkpoint effects. M2 instead estimates a prespecified channel's failure probability using weighted trajectories. Corso et al.'s methods use state-dependent disturbance proposals, trajectory weights and policy/value-based learning; their experiments compare Monte Carlo and cross-entropy sampling on a disturbed pendulum, and report important high-dimensional limitations. Generic RL importance sampling is therefore occupied, while watermark utility remains unestablished. [Corso et al. v1, Methods and Experiments](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.12470).

DERIVED: The indispensable quantity is a valid nominal/proposal likelihood ratio on the sampled probability space. It need not be an explicit density of the model's output image. For a shared deterministic map `Y = F(Z)` with known input-noise laws p and q, `E_q[(p(Z)/q(Z)) 1_failure(F(Z))] = P_p(failure)` under support/integrability conditions. Common unknown transition factors can also cancel. Thus steelman-r1's isolated phrase that an unknown black-box kernel density immediately kills the estimator is too broad if read without its preceding ratio-contract condition. The current 09-review disposition already requires a valid density/weight contract rather than an image-density model, so this is a nonblocking clarification, not a reason to reopen WP8. Deterministic DDIM suffixes still cannot be given new random branches and called the same nominal channel.

JUDGMENT: Defer until the ratio and support are justified, then compare classical sampling at total cost. This preserves a potentially useful estimator without turning a sampled zero into a rare-error certificate or an average-case estimate into worst-case security. No proof of poor RL efficacy follows from deferral.
