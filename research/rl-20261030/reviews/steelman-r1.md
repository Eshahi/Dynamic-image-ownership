A small fixed coupling grid, ordinary common random numbers, antithetic sampling and supervised covariance selection are the strongest non-RL baselines. RO11/C08 maximize adverse outcomes, and TD03 I06 measures checkpoint interventions; neither is this probability estimand.

**Mechanism and actual RL burden.** DERIVED: For target path law p and proposal q with suitable support, `E_q[(p/q) 1_failure]=P_p(failure)`. Classical cross-entropy importance sampling, fixed Gaussian tilts, stratification and non-RL splitting are mandatory alternatives. The plausible RL advantage is a history-dependent proposal for a long path with multiple failure modes; for a single initial Gaussian draw followed by deterministic DDIM, that argument becomes much weaker. One cannot inject new suffix randomness into deterministic DDIM and still call it the original native law.
