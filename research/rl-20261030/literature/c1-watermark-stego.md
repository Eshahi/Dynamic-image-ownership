## Cluster synthesis

**JUDGMENT — Occupied ingredients.** Generic RL selection of embedding blocks, coefficients, costs, token sets and utility/detection reward mixtures is well occupied. C01–C04 directly concern image allocation; C05–C08 occupy generator/detector co-training and robustness-versus-utility optimization in text/code. C06 is especially relevant to content-dependent acceptance: preserving detection under paraphrase while rejecting semantic manipulation already exists as a text objective. None of this establishes the identical image task or construction; U05/U08 are unresolved novelty risks that should prevent an absolute “first” claim.

**JUDGMENT — Equal total budget remains the missing causal test.** The inspected image studies compare embedding systems, architectures or cost designs. They do not isolate RL against the strongest gradient, discrete coordinate-search, or random-search alternative with matched training, tuning, evaluator calls and deployment cost. The text papers show that learning can help, but often train a new detector, add correctness training, or change the available side information. Such comparisons cannot settle SideA for a frozen image reader. The negative variants and tradeoffs retained in the cards argue against transferring headline improvements wholesale.

**JUDGMENT — Policy placement.** P2 has direct antecedents. P1 contextual selection is often the more accurate name for one-shot decisions. P3 pre-encoder transform control is not demonstrated by these inspected embedding papers; that question belongs with augmentation/optimization evidence. P4 sequential allocation has a real MCTS antecedent, but its useful horizon and evaluator cost must be established. “Uses a policy gradient” does not by itself imply sequential state dependence; C02/C04/C06 distinguish that issue explicitly.

**JUDGMENT — T3/T4/T5 and unseen families.** Steganographic security asks whether a mark is detectable at all; robust watermark verification asks whether it can still be detected after regeneration. Optimizing one does not prove the other. Text paraphrasing/refactoring is a useful analogy, not image diffusion evidence. None of the12 cards demonstrates post-hoc content-bound image watermarking against unseen diffusion families at this project's strict quality gates, with T4/T5 evaluated and equal total budget. This is a bounded retrieval finding, not proof that no such work exists. The14 access-limited leads and indirect-search-only coverage materially limit novelty confidence.

No new claim of RL superiority, image-regeneration success or publication novelty follows from the count itself.
