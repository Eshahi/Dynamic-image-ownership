# When a policy could outperform the available alternatives

WP4 synthesis. **DERIVED/JUDGMENT:** RL has no demonstrated advantage yet. Its strongest plausible role is a low-dimensional decision whose next useful action depends on informative feedback, where gradients, a small enumerated search or a supervised policy do not provide the same result more cheaply. Direct score-function search over the existing continuous pixel perturbation is a weak default. This is a conditional method-selection conclusion, not a theorem that gradients always win.

Evidence is the frozen implementation, inspected literature and WP3 descriptive saved-number analysis. F6 remains blind and unchanged. No science, policy training or new timing was run. Exact derivations and source locators are in [estimator note](theory/estimator-note.md), [short-horizon bandit note](theory/bandit-note.md) and [family/reader note](theory/family-reader-note.md).

## 1. A scalar reward discards directional information, but may reach a better objective

For reparameterized action a=theta+sigma*z with z standard Gaussian, expand the reward at the fixed current point theta0 as `R=c+h^T(a-theta0)+epsilon`, where epsilon has mean0, variance v and is independent of z. Evaluating at theta=theta0, with optimal constant baseline c, the score-function estimator is `g_SF=((h^T z)+epsilon/sigma)*z`. Its expectation is h and its trace covariance is `(d+1)||h||^2+d*v/sigma^2`; averaging n independent samples divides this by n. The first term exists even without seed noise. With fixed total exploration energy rho²=d*sigma², the noise term is d²v/rho². Reducing action dimension can therefore matter substantially.

For the same differentiable linear reward with action-independent additive noise, the exact pathwise estimator is h and has zero variance. Real gradients need not be constant or noiseless. Different estimators can also optimize different objectives: native thresholded C/J versus a differentiable surrogate. Surrogate bias is not cured by taking more cheap gradients, and score-function access to an informative true reward could win despite its variance. Conversely, a zero C reward supplies no useful direction merely because its empirical variance is zero.

MEASURED/CONDITIONAL: WP3 F5 R1 .2 within-source decoded-margin variance is v=.15674. The independent-noise term alone, with sigma=1 and target trace RMS .1, requires at least126,1,004 and16,051 samples for d=8,64 and1024, respectively. At sigma=.1 these lower planning counts grow100-fold. This transfers a fixed-arm noise estimate to a new local policy under an explicit assumption; it is not a measured policy-gradient variance, an iteration count or an improvement forecast. Existing-carrier feedback costs2.19–3.63 seconds, whereas a changed F5-style carrier adds about122 device-equivalent seconds per rollout. Multiplying by all training updates, tuning and validation is still required.

The complete equal-compute comparison is estimator MSE, not sample count: for budget T, per-sample costs c_S/c_P, covariance traces V_S/V_P and biases b_S/b_P relative to the same target gradient, compare `||b_S||²+c_S*V_S/T` with `||b_P||²+c_P*V_P/T`, subject to consistent units and local approximations. The gradient signal, native/surrogate bias and backward timing are all unmeasured. Thus no numerical crossover is identifiable. A learned residual correction or model-based controller can be a legitimate hybrid, but must beat the corresponding non-RL control-variate, line-search and derivative-free baselines with the same access.

A controller choosing discrete surrogate, timestep, step size or stop action is not equivalent to policy gradients over RGB. It can use existing pathwise gradients inside its environment. To establish that sequential RL adds value, compare a fixed schedule, random actions, state-shuffled policy and an equally informed supervised/myopic rule. An early action must change later feasible state or information; a numbered list of independent choices does not establish this.

## 2. Short horizons, full information and amortization

The saved arm table is full information: every retained source has outcomes for all fixed arms/cells, with explicit missingness. It permits oracle ceilings and supervised action prediction without inventing logged propensities. It does not support off-policy evaluation of new actions or a new adaptive trajectory. A one-time configuration choice is best-arm identification or contextual selection; the existing8→4→2→1 Stage P is already successive halving. Renaming it RL adds no method.

Cumulative regret charges lost rewards during learning; simple regret evaluates the final selected arm. A manuscript must choose which problem its deployment requires. For a one-off per-image mark, exploratory rewrites are often the dominant cost. A shared policy might amortize training over many future images, but equal-total-budget comparisons must specify that future count before observing results. The full cost is training plus tuning plus validation plus deployment, not policy inference alone. See the bandit note for explicit regret bounds and the20-hour inventory calculation.

DERIVED: for K fixed [0,1] arms with n independent draws each, empirical-best selection has simple regret at most `min(1,sqrt(2*log(2K/.05)/n))` with probability at least95%. For K=8/64/1024, a sufficient .1-regret plan takes1,154/1,570/2,125 draws per arm; these conservative sufficient counts are not lower bounds. The explicit UCB1 cumulative-regret corollary is vacuous for K=8,T=573. Under a deterministic one-good-arm construction with no informative context and T<K distinct queries, some problem has expected simple regret at least `1-(T+1)/K`: at T=24, .6094 for K64 and .9756 for K1024. This demonstrates a need for action structure, not a measured watermark learning rate.

The concrete cost contrast is24 hypothetical fresh sources×8 candidates×3 seeds×one cell: cached F5-style writing and high-end feedback total7.0875 hours ($3.91); rewriting on every call totals20.1008 hours before controls. Twenty hours buy at most573 high-cost rewrite rollouts, versus19,834 feedback calls on available carriers, before overhead. Neither ceiling is an adequate scientific design by itself. A20-hour training investment would need591 future deployments to repay even a hypothetical full122-second writing saving per source, or5,902 for a10% saving. No such saving is measured. This separates a plausible amortized controller from a one-off search whose training costs exceed its available benefit.

WP3 limits the immediate retained-arm opportunity: cell-known C selection gains only1/420 beyond a simple cell-specific fixed choice; unknown-cell per-source selection gains13/420 beyond one global fixed arm, while worst-family C does not improve. Source survival can be predictable without the optimal action changing by source. A new action space might have more headroom, but that is an untested hypothesis and usually adds exploration cost. Short horizons favor small menus, cheap cached carriers and information-rich feedback; they do not mathematically rule out all amortized controllers.

## 3. Few families cannot identify arbitrary excluded-family behavior

With no structural relation to a new family, two response models can agree on every training observation and differ on the excluded family's rewards. Therefore source-only policies, gradients and RL all lack a distribution-free extrapolation guarantee. Three or four families are three or four clusters; strengths and seeds do not multiply that count.

Even an optimistic iid family-level zero-failure model would give a one-sided95% upper failure bound of63.2% for three families and52.7% for four. Our selected families and overlapping LOFO fits do not justify that iid model, so those numbers illustrate weakness rather than estimate actual project risk. Leave-one-family-out is meaningful as a finite falsification test, with the full family vector reported. Repeatedly selecting methods on it consumes it as development evidence.

A transferable guarantee would require an explicit assumption: for example uniformly bounded total-variation distance from a trained channel mixture, or a margin/Lipschitz bound under output perturbation. No valid numerical bound is available here. The failed first-order transfer law particularly cautions against assuming a local encoder surrogate controls the full noisy denoiser. Positive robotics domain-randomization evidence is a possibility argument; recurrent target observations available to a robot are absent from a writer acting before future regeneration. Matched robust randomized PGD is a necessary comparator to a learned curriculum.

## 4. Fixed reader, carrier and quality are separate bottlenecks

For any source, the relevant question is whether a perturbation satisfying final strict clean quality can survive the channel and satisfy the fixed F5 C/J/binding tests. Optimization cannot recover information unavailable to that reader, but one failed writer does not prove the feasible set empty. Local margin bounds would need native-channel gradients and remainder control; neither is inferred from saved reward variance.

VINE-R establishes survival with its own carrier, reader,100-bit payload and quality operating point. Its clean PSNR median34.7 and31/60 strict-quality development passes prevent treating that result as proof of a strict-quality F5-reader solution. Conversely it prevents a universal claim that regeneration always erases all watermark information. The F5 PhaseB median45.1 corresponds to3.20 times less RMSE than the35dB boundary, but this median scalar slack does not certify any all-source scaling under SSIM, LPIPS, fragile-tier interference and binding.

A future factorial comparison could distinguish writer/objective mismatch, representation/reader mismatch and quality-constrained channel loss by holding access, payload, final quality and sources fixed. This session does not run or authorize it. F6 GO would show a same-reader feasible operating point on its evaluated scope; fail would reject that particular package; RESOURCE_STOP would leave its scientific outcome unresolved. None should be reinterpreted as universal evidence for or against RL.

## 5. Conditions carried into candidate screening

The positive case needs all of: an informative action/state structure, an attainable secure operating point, a reward aligned with C/J or a declared attack objective, sufficient exploration/amortization budget, and an advantage over the strongest matched non-RL alternative. An excluded-family claim additionally needs finite independent evidence and a narrow stated scope. Low-dimensional discrete control, declared-feedback red teaming and useful adversarial responses remain plausible; high-dimensional scalar-reward rewriting, free personalization and automatic new-family transfer remain unsupported.

No candidate has been scored in WP4. WP5 will first obtain independent raw ideas, commit a screening rule, and only then compare their merits. The theory narrows what must be demonstrated; it does not supply novelty or an empirical success probability.
