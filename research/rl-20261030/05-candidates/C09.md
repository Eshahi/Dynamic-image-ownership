Original donor residual subtraction is outside the primary contract.

## WP7 cost clarification, after forecast elicitation

The original six-procedure inventory does not separately fund every named control. Patch grid, beam, fitted NES/SPSA portfolio, permitted PGD, random, history-shuffled and learned policy make **seven** procedure slots. At the stated 1,400 calls each plus 1,600 control/failure calls, the corrected total is **11,400 units**, not 10,000. DERIVED additional feedback cost is `1,400*(2.19..3.63)/3,600 = 0.852..1.412 h`; the conditional first screen becomes **11.21–15.77 h**, about **$6.18–8.69**, before any additional fitting beyond the original allowance. The hard cap remains20h and can yield RESOURCE_STOP. A three-seed independent final evaluation requires further calls and is still not included in this small diagnostic.

A prespecified combined non-RL portfolio may legitimately share paid training observations and use fewer slots, but the paper must state that allocation and cannot claim each constituent was independently fully trained. The tenfold broad-study anchor correspondingly increases by8.52–14.12h before new fitting/external-baseline costs. This is a visible bookkeeping correction, not a measured runtime or a new efficacy result. The screening rule/scores are unchanged; the maximum20h first-screen ceiling and unresolved full-study risk remain.
