# C20 — Robust/fragile repair order and saved-RGB8 reconciliation

The shared data, endpoint and cost definitions in [the author bundle](../ideas/sheets-systems.md) are part of this sheet. Final selection is in [the candidate table](../05-candidate-table.md).


**Class / points / raw IDs / analogy.** R-full for repair sequencing, P9/P4; RT17, RO07, RS16. RS16's standalone phase choice is a horizon-one R-bandit subcase, not identical to sequential repair. Outside analogy: robotic assembly where a later operation disturbs an earlier component. Benefit type: possible direct defense or clean-quality/encoding efficiency, plus mechanism evidence.

**Mechanism and information.** State: current saved/reloaded RGB8 residual, robust and fragile reader margins, last operation, remaining update budget and three separate quality slacks. Action: one bounded robust repair, fragile repair, prescribed rounding/dither phase, reconcile/re-read or stop. Transition genuinely changes pixels and future repair margins; horizon four blocks. Terminal reward is C/J with exact h/both-match separately, never an alternative easier q/h. The fixed source-derived content code and public owner stay unchanged. At deployment only source, current clean mark and available local diagnostics exist; no future native regeneration outcome is available. Training native feedback is charged, and evaluator true q/h remains separate from the operational reader. Any dither phase needed by the decoder must be fixed public information or explicitly signaled and charged.

**Strongest equal-total-budget alternatives.** Four fixed repair orders crossed with two fixed quantization phases (eight profiles); exhaustive schedule/phase selection; coordinate descent; most-violated-margin greedy repair; quantization-aware PGD/straight-through gradients; supervised next-operation prediction; random order and state-shuffled policy. Equal update/gradient budget, final RGB8 materialization, all observations and total training+tuning+native feedback+deployment budget. The strongest control gets the same final re-read and quality constraints. More refinement cannot be called an RL gain. If a larger horizon is proposed, charge its tree/search and compare beam search before neural RL.

**Necessary-condition evidence / effect.** SOURCE: D13 and the reader-semantics note show actual robust→round→fragile→refine→round interactions, so N1 discrete operations and N2 changing physical state hold structurally. There is no project evidence that harmful interference explains SDXL failure; codec-only survival and noise-stage collapse caution against that explanation. JUDGMENT smallest worthwhile effect: recover >=4/24 baseline clean both-match failures while preserving strict quality and all native C/J cells, or achieve >=4/24 additional foreign-cell J successes beyond the best matched schedule. These alternatives must be preregistered separately, with no post-result switch; no effect size is measured here.

**Data and gates.** Apply F to24 fresh groups for one finite-menu falsification, without selecting images because F5 fails on them. No old F5 microvariant campaign is reopened and the approved F6 code/results are untouched. Same q/h and retained reader; final saved-image PSNR >35, SSIM >.9, LPIPS <.1 on every source; report C/J, exact h, both-match, T4/T5, C0/wrong-owner and all clean failures. Retaining a reader does not prove unchanged security when the carrier distribution changes; repeat negatives. A new reader or recalibration would be a distinct candidate with unknown FPR, not a repair quietly folded in.

**First decisive falsification / cost / kill.** L24x8 evaluates192 fixed complete marks,2304 C1 native outputs,288 C0,2304 wrong-owner calls and384 T4/T5 pairs, plus clean checks:13.605h/$7.50 with reserve/setup. Add at most2h for intermediate snapshot reads and phase/controller fitting, **15.605h/$8.60 conditional cap**. Individual repair-block timings are unmeasured; the122-second whole-writer anchor is valid only if all eight schedules genuinely share its fixed total update budget. RESOURCE_STOP if they do not fit. Before training a policy, use all eight outcomes as a finite ceiling: kill the clean-repair objective if fewer than four baseline failures exist or the oracle cannot repair four, and kill the T3 objective if no schedule reaches its preregistered J margin. Kill the RL attribution if greedy/enumerated/quantization-aware PGD gives the same result or gains vanish after saved-RGB8 rereading.

**Later evidence / cache.** L240x8 gives127.051h/$70.01 before intermediate trajectory materialization;8h extra yields135.051h/$74.41 conditional planning, with no neural reader training. Learning a new reader would add an unmeasured cost and alter the method contract. The later full study is EXPENSIVE. Cache a completed fixed schedule across native seeds; a controller that changes pixels at a branch requires its own write trajectory and cannot reuse another branch's final image as if free. Policy amortization must beat simple coordinate descent in total measured writing time over a declared deployment count.

**Closest three inspected works and residual claim.** [Dual-Guard, §§3.1,4.1–4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.19090v1) already combines complementary provenance/content channels with reference-assisted verification. [Guidance Watermarking, §§4.4–4.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22126v2) already treats multiple reader/augmentation objectives with gradient handling. [AdaptiveISP, §§3–4/Tables3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.22939v1) already learns sequential discrete image-processing modules and cost tradeoffs. TD03 I11 explicitly proposes robust/fragile interference and RGB8 control; a new result would have to show noncommuting state-dependent repair that survives matched-gradient and finite-schedule controls. Scheduling or dither alone is occupied engineering.

**Publishable claim, conditional.** A reproducible carrier-interference mechanism makes action order matter after actual quantization, and a stateful controller improves joint recovery beyond matched coordinate descent at strict quality without weakening T4/T5. If the best fixed order explains it, report the engineering/mechanism result without an incremental RL claim.

**Steelman.** The actual pipeline contains discrete quantization and mutually disturbing carriers, so pathwise gradient descent over one continuous residual does not automatically solve the joint final-pixel problem. Assembly-style state feedback is a coherent possibility.

**Hostile assessment.** TD03 already proposed this, clean recovery may be near ceiling, and total SDXL collapse has a different observed location. Eight schedules can be enumerated. RL may merely rediscover quantization-aware coordinate descent after paying for many full122-second marks, with no meaningful channel-transfer gain.
