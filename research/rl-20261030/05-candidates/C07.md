# C07 — Sparse pulses along an actual latent reconstruction trajectory

The shared data, endpoint and cost definitions in [the author bundle](../ideas/sheets-writers.md) are part of this sheet. Final selection is in [the candidate table](../05-candidate-table.md).


**Class, point, provenance and benefit.** R-full, P2/P3; RT20. Outside analogy: sparse impulse control of a dynamical system. This is an explicitly **new latent writer**, neither F5 nor F6 relabelled. Potential direct defensive efficacy and an honest RQ-02 mechanism test; it has no established benefit today.

**State/action/transition/reward/horizon.** A fixed source-inversion/reconstruction pipeline exposes current latent, predicted clean image, fixed original q payload, remaining pulse/quality budget and trajectory time. At two prospectively selected insertion times choose no pulse, +q-conditioned fixed-basis pulse or −pulse; nine paths result. Subsequent denoising changes how an earlier pulse survives and interacts with the second. Initially retain the unchanged F5 reference-free reader/q32+h32 contract; fragile insertion/finalization is explicit and charged. Reward is final C/J after native T3, conditional on every Q gate. A changed decoder is a separate method amendment and cannot rescue this fixed-reader claim silently.

**Information and leakage.** Training sees only approved source-model trajectories and native design-channel outcomes on D sources. Deployment can invert/reconstruct the clean image using the same public model, but has no future regeneration observations. No source-dependent inversion optimization may query an excluded family. All current P/S/B/reserve/C sources and frozen F6 outputs are excluded. Inversion/model access and any prompt or reconstruction side information must be shared with baselines; detection remains reference-free in this candidate.

**Evidence/conditions.** N1 insertion-time/action choices are discrete, and N2 subsequent denoising depends on the inserted latent. N4 usable native reward, N5 training amortization, N6 transfer and especially N7 source-relative feasibility are unproved. VINE's own reader/payload/quality results do not prove feasibility for this reader. Unmarked reconstruction failure is a warning, **not a mathematical quality ceiling**: allowed pulses might also repair reconstruction. Only failure across the declared feasible pulse/control set, or a separately justified bound, can kill that set.

**Strongest fair alternatives.** No-pulse reconstruction, best fixed early/late pulse, random pulse timing/sign, enumerated nine paths, pathwise amplitude/trajectory optimization and non-RL constrained optimal control using exactly the same latent access. Include a supervised source/state-to-pulse policy and, when fair under Q, a frozen image-domain writer comparator labelled as such. Charge inversion, every trajectory, backward memory/time, pulse search, final fragile insertion and all training/tuning; “frozen generator weights” does not mean free rollout.

**Effect/claim target.** JUDGMENT: effect and feasible quality frontier are unknown. Direct-study target remains +10 pp primary C over the strongest equal-budget non-RL, J non-regression, final strict PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS and preserved T4/T5/C0/wrong-owner. Publishable claim: a finite, controlled insertion-time interaction supports a source-preserving latent writer with the same declared lightweight readout; an honest infeasibility result is limited to the tested pipeline/action set. “First latent watermark” and “first RL diffusion steering” are not available claims.

**First decisive falsification and kill.** Eight D sources × nine fixed pulse paths (including no pulse) plus one tuned pathwise and one non-RL optimal-control path = **88 trajectories**, each finalized and evaluated on two cells × three seeds: **528 marked T3, 48 C0, 528 extra wrong-owner, 88 clean checks, 176 T4/T5 units**. Random/fixed/supervised/oracle selection can reuse the nine-path table; there is no learned-RL efficacy result. Kill this bounded fixed-reader pulse design if no admitted path is strictly source-feasible, if feasible native C/J shows no headroom beyond non-RL controls, or if decoding requires extra undeclared information. A pulse-path failure does not prove impossibility of all latent methods. No reconstruction-only shortcut is called a decisive defense test.

**Cost and later evidence.** Let g be the fully measured cost of one inverted, controlled, finalized trajectory, including any optimizer search allocated to that arm. With B's feedback/control/reserve convention the screen is **3.1767 + 0.030556*g hours** for g in seconds, plus any arm-specific training not included in g. Completing within 20 h needs g <=550.58 s before additional learning; g is entirely UNMEASURED. The **122 s F5** and **361–723 s unmeasured F6** figures are comparisons, not valid latent-trajectory estimates. **EXPENSIVE/unresolved, F=1:** no credible complete <=20 h inventory is established. Repeated seeds on a fixed final image cache; changed pulses require new trajectories. Later L's check inventory plus inversion/control training and three independent policy seeds is required; substitute measured g, never F5 timing, and report full-study cost UNKNOWN until then.

**Three closest inspected works.** [ONRW, §§3.1–3.2/4.2–4.5, Tables 1/3/4](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17388v1) already optimizes inversion noise for watermarking; reported COCO PSNR 27.11 dB is below this contract and bit accuracy is not C/J. [DSRL, §4 and §5.2/Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.15799v1) already learns latent-noise steering of a frozen diffusion policy; robot return, initial-noise action and unmatched pretraining costs do not demonstrate source-preserving image watermarking. [DRaFT, §4/Algorithm 1, §5 and Appendix B.8](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.17400v2) is a strong differentiable trajectory/reward alternative and documents reward-collapse concerns; generative preference alignment is not ownership binding. Cards C12-05, C08-06/04.

**TD03 comparison.** I04 explicitly proposed the actual source-preserving latent insertion test and already named reconstruction feasibility as the central risk. The sole residual here is sparse state-contingent timing with an identical non-RL trajectory-control comparison. If fixed pulses/pathwise control work equally well, credit a latent-method result and reject the RL-specific claim.

**Steelman.** This places the intervention inside the dynamics that subsequently transport or erase it. A delayed pulse or later compensating pulse could avoid the immediately destructive part of reconstruction, giving a genuine sequential control problem rather than a noisy substitute for a pixel gradient.

**Hostile reviewer.** The source may already be damaged by reconstruction, and no latent action can be assumed to preserve the original photograph. ONRW and diffusion control occupy much of the concept. Pathwise control has direct gradient access; the proposed tiny action set is exactly enumerable, while a richer learned policy multiplies expensive rollouts.
