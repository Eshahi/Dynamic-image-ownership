# C06 — Progress-aware counterexample curriculum game

The shared data, endpoint and cost definitions in [the author bundle](../ideas/sheets-writers.md) are part of this sheet. Final selection is in [the candidate table](../05-candidate-table.md).


**Class, point, provenance and benefit.** R-game, P3/P8; RO05/RO20. Outside analogies: curriculum learning near the learnability boundary and counterexample-guided program refinement. Potential diagnostic/article evidence and, only if demonstrated, direct defensive efficacy. A changing sampler by itself is not proof of an advantageous game.

**Players/state/actions/transitions/reward/horizon.** A teacher observes fit-only progress, uncertainty and surrogate/native disagreement by registered mechanism; a shared low-dimensional writer-policy learner responds. Teacher actions choose a known-channel surrogate mixture/difficulty or a feasible T3 counterexample; learner actions update its writer policy using ordinary pathwise gradients. Teacher utility is subsequent **feasible** native improvement or a reproducible falsification of its surrogate prediction, not the largest unlearnable loss. Learner utility is C/J subject to Q; false-attribution/quality failures are separate veto components, not exchangeable scalar rewards. This is a general-sum teaching game with an adversarial counterexample component, not a claimed zero-sum equilibrium. A bounded first game has three response rounds, with policy state carrying between rounds.

**Information and leakage.** Teacher and learner see only fresh fit-source model responses; frozen check sources and an optimization-excluded family are invisible to both. Deployment uses the frozen learned writer policy, not the teacher or a future-channel oracle. D excludes all P/S/B/reserve/C pools. T4/T5 negatives and their labels are predeclared training data; no repeated use of a check failure as a new curriculum example. A reproducible counterexample may diagnose a surrogate without yielding a better defense.

**Evidence/conditions.** N2 strategic response changes which training task is informative, and N1 discrete curriculum choices exist. Project first-order surrogate failure and saturated F5 SDXL C make informative-reward allocation a relevant diagnostic (E=2). They also make an all-zero reward curriculum plausible. N4 learnability, N5 amortization, N6 excluded-family structure, N7 secure feasible tasks and N8 matched training cost are unresolved. A teacher that selects impossible strict-quality cases is not discovering useful robustness.

**Strongest fair alternatives.** Uniform/stratified random domain sampling; fixed curriculum; non-RL progress-based sampling; CVaR/worst-case robust PGD; classical counterexample-guided search and refinement; finite-game/double-oracle allocation without a learned teacher. Strongest comparison trains the same writer learner, uses the same surrogate/task menu and native counterexample calls, and receives equal total teacher+learner+tuning budget. Include random teacher and frozen-teacher ablation; supervise a next-task predictor on the same acquired data as another control where appropriate.

**Effect/claim target.** JUDGMENT: no projected gain. Direct benefit target is +10 pp primary C beyond the best equal-budget robust/randomized non-RL, J non-regression and all Q gates. Diagnostic benefit instead requires reproducible, prospective surrogate counterexamples with a mechanism-specific correction; it cannot be counted as defense efficacy. Publishable claim: progress-aware counterexample selection isolates and repairs a specific simulator blind spot beyond classical refinement, with finite excluded-family tests and an informative null if repair fails.

**First decisive falsification and kill.** A single batch can show reward saturation but cannot test reciprocal adaptation. The smallest proposed *game-response* screen is 24 D sources (16 fit/8 sealed check), five arms (learned progress teacher, classical counterexample rule, CVaR robust PGD, random teacher, fixed curriculum), three response rounds, two cells × three seeds. All five arms run their complete inventory; check rows are sealed until the final comparison, not curriculum feedback. Conservative count: **360 writes, 2,160 marked T3, 144 C0, 2,160 extra wrong-owner, 360 clean checks, 720 T4/T5 units**, plus unmeasured teacher/policy updates. Kill the bounded game if feasible training rewards stay saturated, responses merely cycle through already solved counterexamples, fixed/classical curricula match its sealed benefit, or Q regresses. A preliminary saturation probe is not a positive game comparison.

**Cost and later evidence.** Applying B to three rounds gives **22.00 h/$12.12 even at the F5 anchor**, or **51.87–97.12 h/$28.58–53.51 at UNMEASURED F6 anchors**, before teacher fitting. **EXPENSIVE; F=1.** Each changed writer-policy/round rewrites; only repeated attack seeds of the same round's fixed mark cache. A 20 h cap would be a resource screen, not enough for the stated first decisive game comparison. Later L adds a check-stage floor and independently replicated teacher/learner seeds, plus full counterexample discovery, failed responses and policy training. No justified full-study upper cost exists yet.

**Three closest inspected works.** [Active Domain Randomization, §§2–5.1 and Appendices C/F/H](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v100/mehta20a/mehta20a.pdf) directly occupies a learned task sampler and documents impossible-region degeneracy. [EPOpt, §§3.1–3.2/4.2–4.4, Appendix A.5/Table 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.01283v4) occupies lower-tail ensemble robustness; target-informed adaptation is additional access and the same objective can drive non-RL gradients. [SPARED, Method/Eqs.1–3, Experiments/Table 3 and adversarial-round ablation](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.12876v1) already alternates detector/editor adaptation; image-origin detection and its fidelity gate do not establish watermark binding or this strict quality contract. Cards C11-04/03 and C08-07.

**TD03 comparison.** I01's surrogate writer, I03's prediction and I06's rescue/erasure diagnosis already cover the ingredients. New evidence must be a useful adaptive counterexample-to-response relationship, not generic adversarial co-training or another failed surrogate law. I11-style repair alone does not establish curriculum value.

**Steelman.** The current surrogate may fail in a structured subset of cases. A teacher that selects correctable disagreements rather than maximum loss could keep learning away from both easy saturation and impossible cases, yielding a reusable mechanistic lesson even when it does not win every endpoint.

**Hostile reviewer.** This adds a second expensive learning problem to an unvalidated first one. Progress bonuses and counterexample distributions can overfit the same three families. Classical robust optimization may explain all gains, while familiar adversarial-training structure leaves only a narrow novelty margin.
