# C14 — Least-favorable genuine/forged observation game

The shared data, endpoint and cost definitions in [the author bundle](../ideas/sheets-security.md) are part of this sheet. Final selection is in [the candidate table](../05-candidate-table.md).


**Class / point / origins / analogy:** R-game; P6/P7 with a fixed P5 observation interface; RS15. Outside analogy: adversarial hypothesis testing and counterexample-guided abstraction analysis.

**Benefit type:** Security discovery and article evidence about a bounded observation bottleneck. WP6 A2 is **not applicable by design**; a collision witness or impossibility argument does not improve defense.

**State/action/transition/horizon/reward:** A genuine player selects an admissible T3 transformation of a marked source; an alternative player selects an operationally distinct unmodified T5 candidate or separately labelled T4 construction. A verifier responds using a frozen interface phi. Search alternates for at most three rounds/Q24 per source, with a prespecified Q16 checkpoint. Reward seeks opposite truth labels with identical phi, or minimizes distance as a search heuristic only; a learned discriminator's confusion is never the proof. Exact witnesses are retained with all admissibility checks.

**Information/leakage:** Specify phi before results: primary code-and-status interface contains decoded q/h, read flags and content statuses; it deliberately omits continuous internal scores and is a restricted named verifier interface, not a claim about every reader observation. Also evaluate the full exported score interface as a separate diagnostic. Training/evaluator know source identity; deployment policies see only public images, declared API and previous replies, not true labels or original q/h. No F6 or protected outcomes, test-trained discriminator or selected-family re-use.

**Necessary conditions:** N1 discrete semantic/code observations support a collision search, and legitimate/near-copy ambiguity motivates an opportunity. N2 sequential learned responses are not established: exhaustive finite pairing or constrained optimization may suffice. N4 valid witness reward, N5 reuse, N6 generalization and N8 matched budget remain unresolved; N7 here means valid opposite-label examples, not an established secure operating point.

**Strongest equal-total-budget non-RL:** Exhaustive pairing on the same finite observed library, nearest-neighbor collision search, direct constrained gradients for T4/T3 surrogates where access permits, beam search and classical double oracle. Random admissible pair sampling is mandatory. The learned player receives no extra observations/truth labels or training/evaluation budget. T5 candidates cannot be gradient-edited; such actions belong only to the separately named T4 branch.

**Fresh data:** [Fresh-data contract](../ideas/fresh-data-contract.md),48 genuine-source groups24/8/16 and128 fixed-rule eligible alternatives per group, with licensed acquisition, independent operational-identity labels and custodian exclusion against P/S/B/reserve/C/all consumed pools. Related positives/negatives form indivisible split groups. No protected IDs opened; missing human labels make a purported collision unverified rather than a success.

**Security/fidelity:** Record native T3 C/J and P separately from the restricted-interface experiment. T4 requires exact donor q/record plus acceptance, exact h separately; T5 remains unmodified. Strict clean writer quality, legitimate T3 fidelity, alternative T4 recipient fidelity, C0/wrong-owner A and abstentions are separate constraints. Q>0 search/composed attacks expand original access. Trusted-record phi would add registry side information and define a separate result.

**First decisive falsification / kill:** Require exact equal-phi/opposite-label witnesses on at least three independent evaluation groups and an advantage of at least two additional witness groups over strongest non-RL pairing/search. A finite witness supports only that declared interface and those cases. For one balanced exact observation pair, any deterministic decision errs on at least one member; randomization has average error at least1/2 on that pair. Kill a broad ceiling claim if only approximate similarity, discriminator failure or a deliberately impoverished trivial accept-bit interface is available; kill the RL claim if exhaustive search matches it. JUDGMENT witness targets are worthwhile effects, not expected collision rates.

**First cost:**48 cached F5 marks=1.63h. Three search procedures×1,000 feedback units, plus1,000 controls/rechecks/failed calls=4,000 native-equivalent units,2.43–4.03h;6,144 unmodified candidate reads at1.4s add2.39h. The1,000 per procedure includes384 evaluation queries =16×Q24 diagnostic allowance (three rounds×8), with primaryQ16 results also reported;616 remaining fit/selection calls include three training seeds. Reserve4h for features, fitting, exact matching, setup/retrieval:10.45–12.05h/USD5.76–6.64, cap20h. **Not EXPENSIVE for this bounded witness screen**; a large learned generator's full training is not covered. F6's48 writes would cost4.81–9.64h, unmeasured and excluded.

**Later full evidence:**480 groups/61,440 alternatives/40,000 native-equivalent units with tenfold reserve imply104–120h/USD58–66, plus unknown larger training. This buys finite witnesses, not a population total-variation bound or universal impossibility theorem. Fixed observations can be cached for exact pairing, but newly generated genuine/T4 outcomes require new calls; do not charge only successful witnesses.

**Three closest inspected works / delta:** [Detection Games Under Fully Active Adversaries §§II–IV and Appendix II](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.02850) already formalizes two-hypothesis attacks under explicit source/observation assumptions. [SemBind §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20310v1) covers robustness/binding trade-offs. [Soft-binding benchmark §§3.3,6–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04151v1) covers near-copy verification errors. Residual is an operational exact-observation witness on a declared interface; neither classical hypothesis-testing bounds nor generic learned collision search is new. DRIFT preempts any generic RL removal subroutine.

**TD03 / publishable claim:** I07 already proposes this attribution-ceiling idea; the only additional hypothesis is learned witness-search efficiency over exact pairing/direct optimization. Possible claim: “Explicit genuine/forged observation collisions impose a finite decision conflict for a named verifier interface, and learning does or does not accelerate their discovery.”

**Steelman:** Exact witnesses avoid conflating a weak trained discriminator with an information-theoretic ceiling and can reveal why certain attribution tasks conflict. **Hostile:** Restricting observations may manufacture an unsurprising collision; continuous full-reader observations may never coincide, and a finite nearest-neighbor table can produce the same evidence without a game learner.

**Screens / provisional verdict:** G1 PASS discrete observation premise; G2 PASS exact search/gradient/random alternatives; G3 PASS prospective contract with identity labels unresolved; G4 PASS only for concrete new witnesses, I07 already owns the generic idea; G5 PASS restricted interface and finite-scope claims explicit. ## WP7 cost clarification

The three adaptive-search slots are a bounded diagnostic allocation, not separate full funding for every named gradient, beam, double-oracle, learned and random method. Exact pairing/nearest-neighbor checks may reuse the same paid fixed observation table; new adaptive outcomes cannot. Each additional1,000-query procedure costs **0.608–1.008h** at the stated feedback anchor, plus its fitting/feature cost. A complete comparative study must enumerate those slots and independently evaluated policy seeds before claiming matched total compute. The original10.45–12.05h screen and104–120h broad-study figures are conditional lower inventories, not guaranteed all-comparator costs. No witness or population error bound has been observed; the ARTICLE-ONLY disposition is unchanged.
