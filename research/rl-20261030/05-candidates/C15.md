# C15 — Active verification views and public counterclaim reads

The shared data, endpoint and cost definitions in [the author bundle](../ideas/sheets-systems.md) are part of this sheet. Final selection is in [the candidate table](../05-candidate-table.md).


**Class / points / raw IDs / analogy.** R-full, P5; RT10, RS10, RS20, RO09. Outside analogy: active sensing with costly measurements and a stopping certificate. Benefit type: verification efficiency and possibly direct defensive efficacy, only if new sensitivity survives a separately valid acceptance rule; cheaper reads alone are not Side A's defensive-improvement event.

**Mechanism and information.** State: suspect image, claimed public owner, acquired view/owner responses, chosen-action history, remaining observation budget and candidate q hypotheses. Action: acquire one of seven predefined semantic views, read one of three fixed public counterclaim owners, or reject/abstain/stop. Transition reveals another dependent observation; no pixel changes. Horizon at most ten acquisitions. Training reward is correct C/J with negative query cost under hard quality/security constraints; acceptance is controlled externally, not granted by the RL reward. The retained seven-view mean is one descriptor, not seven independent trials. At deployment there is no original image, enrolled record, true payload or regenerator identity; evaluator-only q/h never enter state. The decoy roster is declared extra public side information. Train, freeze the entire policy/statistic, then calibrate on disjoint source groups. Chosen-owner and attacker-selected nulls are not random-key nulls.

**Strongest equal-total-budget alternatives.** Fixed seven-view F5; every fixed prefix/order within the ten-action menu; a cheap-to-expensive cascade; greedy expected information gain and dynamic programming on the same estimated observation model; supervised nonnegative view aggregation; random view/decoy order; state-shuffled policy. All receive the same features, candidate roster, fitting/calibration data and **training+tuning+evaluation+deployment** budget. Enumerating seven-view subsets (128) is a serious cheap alternative once features are cached. Give both methods any training-table full information actually purchased. Pathwise weight fitting is allowed; learned discrete acquisition must add benefit beyond better aggregation.

**Necessary-condition evidence / effect.** SOURCE: D15/D16 provide N1 discrete acquisition and N2 action-dependent information; the current reader already has seven views. JUDGMENT: no inspected project result establishes redundant views, useful counterclaims, N3 conditional acquisition value or N7 a valid more-sensitive reader. Smallest worthwhile effect: >=25% reduction in measured total verification cost at matched source-level C/J and coverage, or >=10 percentage points J gain at a fixed justified global false-acceptance operating point. Both are prospective judgments, not expected measured gains.

**Data, final gates and calibration.** Apply F to 72 fresh groups, 24 train/24 calibrate/24 locked development evaluation, with all acquired-negative rows retained. Strict final marked-image PSNR >35 dB, SSIM >.9, LPIPS <.1; compare C, J, exact h, T4/T5, C0 and wrong-owner rejection separately. Use the same final saved RGB8 carriers across readers. No new neural reader is necessary for this first screen; small policy-fitting cost remains charged. An anytime-valid null law for dependent selected views is **unidentified**. Full-policy split calibration is a possible route under stated exchangeability, not a proven adversarial guarantee; 24 null source groups cannot certify a 1e-6 rare-event rate. G5 therefore remains unknown. Keep abstentions and misses in denominators.

**First decisive falsification / cost / kill.** L72 plus exhaustive four-owner materialization adds five extra owner reads across each paired C1/C0 output (two additional C1, three additional C0), plus two extra reads for every clean and T4/T5 row: `[5×864+2×(72+144)]×1.4/3600=1.848h`. Upper subtotal with reserve/setup is `1.2×(4.7014+1.848)+1=8.859h`; cap policy fitting/tuning at2h and unmeasured view/pair preprocessing at2h: **12.859h / $7.09 conditional cap**, with RESOURCE_STOP if either cannot fit. This is a bounded utility falsification, not a rare-FPR certification. Compare all policies on the same complete acquired observations, then charge only their allowed online acquisitions plus their full share of training. Kill if even the valid fixed-policy/full-observation ceiling lacks J headroom, if greedy/prefix rules match the policy's cost/accuracy frontier, or if acceptance requires an invalid adaptive score. A failed validity construction kills the claimed secure verifier before a positive efficacy conclusion.

Actual amortization must use measured savings and a preregistered deployment count.

**Closest three inspected works and residual claim.** [Howard et al., §§2,4.1,6](https://arxiv.org/html/1810.08240v9) occupy time-uniform error control under explicit assumptions; they do not calibrate these views. [Better Aggregation in TTA, §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2011.11156v2) occupies learned view weighting without RL. [DRL-FAS, III-C/D and IV-C3/Table IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2009.07529) already learns sequential anti-spoofing patches and compares random/score selectors; its task has no ownership-binding guarantee. Cards C9-01/C9-05/C10-S1. TD03 I08/I12 occupy multiplicity and abstention; C15's possible residual is a tested acquisition mechanism under a valid full-policy null, not first active verification. UNINSPECTED close image-RL leads remain a general novelty limit.

**Publishable claim, conditional.** A history-dependent evidence-acquisition policy changes a source-disjoint C/J-versus-cost frontier under a specified false-attribution contract, beyond fixed, greedy and supervised rules. If validity cannot be supplied, publish only a diagnostic comparison of raw evidence acquisition, with no security improvement claim.

**Steelman.** Early disagreements may identify exactly which view or counterclaim resolves ambiguity, making the optimal continuation state-dependent. The policy acts on information rather than repeatedly paying for an F5 rewrite, so the economics are better than RGB score-function search.

**Hostile assessment.** Seven views and a tiny roster are almost tailor-made for fixed cascades and exhaustive subset selection. A learned rule can harvest chance maxima across correlated views or chosen owners; the apparent improvement may vanish after valid calibration. No retained false-positive number can rescue a newly defined statistic, and saving a fraction of a 1.4-second read may never repay training.
