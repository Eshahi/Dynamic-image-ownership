# C05 — Low-dimensional residual, repair and stopping controller

The shared data, endpoint and cost definitions in [the author bundle](../ideas/sheets-writers.md) are part of this sheet. Final selection is in [the candidate table](../05-candidate-table.md).


**Class, point, provenance and benefit.** R-full, P4; RT08/RT09/RO06/RO08. Outside analogies: residual robotics control and industrial control with several constraints. Potential deployment/experiment efficiency; direct defensive efficacy requires a distinct demonstrated endpoint gain.

**State/action/transition/reward/horizon.** Keep the ordinary pathwise writer as the base controller. Expose iteration fraction, residual norm, gradient alignment, recent decoded-margin change, rollback history and measured PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS slack. At four registered checkpoints choose continue, smaller trust region, clean repair, rollback once, or finalize; finalization is absorbing. Updates and rollback change subsequent state. Terminal feasible C/J is reward; time is a separately reported cost constraint, not a reward that buys false acceptance. No policy emits a high-dimensional RGB update.

**Information and leakage.** Train on new D trajectory records and terminal design-channel responses. Deployment observes only its own local progress unless an explicitly priced known-channel check is enabled for all arms. Checkpoint terminal outcomes used to train stopping are not available to the deployed stopper. Do not equate WP3 clean-margin survival prediction with prediction of *incremental benefit from another block*. Existing P/S/B/reserve/C sources are excluded; frozen F6 stays unchanged.

**Evidence/conditions.** N1 stopping/rollback is discrete and N2 continuation changes residual/remaining work. F5's supplied 122 s write establishes a material cost, but no retained learning curve establishes avoidable work; E=1. N3 predictable future improvement, N4 terminal reward alignment, N5 amortization and N7 recoverable quality remain unknown. A high intermediate margin can still predict failed foreign-family C. Intermediate quality feasibility is not final RGB8 feasibility, and a local safety filter does not certify T4/T5.

**Strongest fair alternatives.** Tuned PGD/Adam schedules, trust-region/backtracking and primal-dual constrained optimization, fixed early-stopping rules, sequential improvement tests, non-RL restart allocation, supervised stopping/continuation-value regression, and a differentiably trained learned optimizer. Include random legal actions, a fixed shorter schedule, and shuffled-history control. All use the same intermediate measurements and full training/tuning budget; thresholds are never tuned against check sources.

**Effect/claim target.** JUDGMENT: plausible benefit is cost reduction, magnitude unknown. Smallest worthwhile cost effect is 20% lower total deployment seconds at matched C/J and Q, plus a declared amortization horizon. A direct-defense claim instead needs +10 pp primary C, J non-regression and Q. One publishable claim would show a transferable signature of delayed constraint recovery/continued native benefit, rather than merely discover a shorter constant iteration count.

**First decisive falsification and kill.** Twelve D sources, seven preregistered conventional trajectories: fixed PGD, tuned Adam, trust-region, greedy constraint repair, random legal actions, fixed rollback schedule and fixed short-budget schedule. Save five checkpoint images per trajectory, including terminal. Charge **84 full trajectory writes, 420 checkpoint clean checks, 2,520 marked T3 calls, 72 C0, 2,520 extra wrong-owner and 840 T4/T5 units**. Fixed/greedy/supervised stopping and an oracle can be compared on the checkpoint table; a future learned residual trajectory is not contained in it. Kill the stopping subcase if no checkpoint can save the worthwhile work while preserving Q/C/J, or if source-disjoint supervised/myopic stopping exhausts its advantage; kill a claimed safety mechanism if its repairs violate final Q. This can falsify stopping headroom or a specific delayed-recovery hypothesis; it cannot rule out every unseen residual action or establish RL efficacy.

**Cost and later evidence.** Charging one 122 s F5-equivalent trajectory rather than rewriting at every checkpoint, and charging every checkpoint's controls/feedback, gives **10.98 h/$6.05** with the same 25% reserve and 2 h allowance. Additional instrumentation/learned fitting is unmeasured; cap at 20 h and retain RESOURCE_STOP. Cache trajectory checkpoints only because stopping selects prefixes of an unchanged trajectory. A learned rollback/update sequence requires new computation; replay cannot fabricate it. Full evidence uses L plus actual trajectory training/tuning. Even a hypothetical full 122 s deployment saving needs about 591 future marks to repay 20 h training; a 10% saving needs about 5,902 (WP4 arithmetic). No saving has been measured.

**Three closest inspected works.** [Learning to Optimize, §§3.1–3.4 and §4.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.01885) occupies RL update policies and finite-horizon acceleration, with unavailable total meta-training cost. [D-Adaptation, Algorithm 1, §§6–7.2](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/defazio23a/defazio23a.pdf) offers an analytic adaptive-step comparator without learned-policy training; convex theory does not cover this constrained writer. [Celo2, §3/Algorithm 1, §4 and §5/Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19142v1) shows a compact non-RL learned optimizer can have modest meta-training cost, but its neural-training results and 4.5 GPU-hour figure do not transfer to costly watermark rewards. Cards C06-01/05/08.

**TD03 comparison.** I11 already asks whether refinement/interference can be repaired, and I01 provides robust writer gradients. This route must demonstrate delayed control or a real compute crossover; another fixed step-size/refinement setting is a comparator outcome, not a new RL contribution.

**Steelman.** Most writer dynamics can remain gradient-controlled. A small controller could learn when a locally promising step will consume irrecoverable quality slack, and spend work only on sources with recoverable terminal gains.

**Hostile reviewer.** No saved trace shows that fixed iteration counts waste enough time to repay training. The model may learn a constant stop rule or memorize seen families. Backtracking, constrained gradients or supervised continuation prediction may reproduce every benefit more cheaply.

**Screens/verdict.** G1 PASS (N1/N2); G2 PASS; G3 PASS (D); G4 PASS for bounded delayed-recovery/cost evidence, not a new optimizer concept; G5 PASS (Q, unchanged readout). Provisional **WATCHLIST**: E=1 no project response traces, M=2 genuine state control, N=2 focused provisional residual, F=2 conditional instrumentation/training, V=2 useful cost or null result. Direct Side A remains unproved even if stopping saves time.
