# C18 — Allocate fresh experiments to learn action advantages

The shared data, endpoint and cost definitions in [the author bundle](../ideas/sheets-systems.md) are part of this sheet. Final selection is in [the candidate table](../05-candidate-table.md).


**Class / points / raw IDs / analogy.** R-bandit, P8 supporting P1/P2/P3; RT16, RO16, RS14. Outside analogy: adaptive experiments for heterogeneous treatment effects. RS13/RO10 mechanism diagnosis is related but distinct. Benefit type: experiment efficiency and article evidence; **not direct defensive efficacy** and not by itself Side A's event of improved C/J with preserved T4/T5.

**Mechanism and estimand.** State: uncertainty about source-feature-by-configuration advantages, completed paired trials, positive action/source sampling probabilities and remaining experimental budget. Action: purchase a source/stratum, one of four frozen configurations or a paired contrast, and a native cell. Transition reveals measured reward/cost and updates uncertainty; it does not change underlying fixed-arm outcomes. Reward is reduction of locked-source final policy simple regret or improved action-advantage prediction per total cost, not higher observed survival in the acquired sample. Horizon is a finite allocation budget. Four configs would be separately frozen non-F6 schedules with the same payload, final reader and gradient budget; no approved F6 parameter or output is retuned. Two-cell first screen is R0 .2 and R1 .1, three fixed seeds.

**Training/deployment information and leakage.** Only prospectively acquired training contexts, outcomes and genuine randomized propensities are available during allocation. A downstream source-only selector must use features available before its chosen action; expensive probe/mark features are charged. Locked source outcomes evaluate decisions only, never acquisition choice. Existing complete historical tables have no required behavior propensities and do not identify new action effects; do not invent them. Mechanism identification among writer/carrier/reader hypotheses needs a different controlled intervention design. RS14 target-population T4/T5 risk estimation needs a separately frozen target distribution and justified adaptive estimator. One successful allocation study proves neither.

**Strongest equal-total-budget alternatives.** Balanced source×arm×cell factorial design; prestratified/Neyman allocation; classical optimal/Bayesian experimental design; non-RL adaptive importance sampling with logged positive probabilities; greedy uncertainty or treatment-difference active learning; random allocation; and **existing Stage P successive halving** for its global-best-arm objective. Compare Stage P on that objective and separately report that it does not target individualized action advantages. Full-information supervised action prediction is the downstream baseline, with the same purchased outcomes and features. No pathwise derivative exists through buying a source outcome; derivative-based writer optimization remains identical across arms. Same total writing/feedback cost, training, tuning, estimator fitting, validation and downstream deployment count applies to all allocators, not just the number of acquired labels.

**Necessary-condition evidence / effect.** N1 is supported by discrete experiment purchases; N2 applies to information, not a changing physical image. MEASURED/DESCRIPTIVE WP3: source survival can be predictable while within-cell finite-menu C personalization adds only1/420 beyond a cell-specific fixed choice. This directly motivates testing differential action value rather than easy survival labels; it is caution, not evidence new heterogeneity exists. N3 is unresolved for the four new configurations. Prospective smallest worthwhile effect: >=25% lower **total** acquisition+fit cost to achieve the same locked-source decision regret (within .05 C/J utility) and uncertainty target as the strongest non-RL allocator. No measured saving or defense gain is claimed.

**Data and endpoint contract.** F supplies48 new source groups,24 acquisition-fit/12 model-selection/12 locked development evaluation, with no overlap in near-copy groups. The first complete four-arm table is an exploratory ceiling/algorithm-falsification instrument, not a claim of online savings. All final saved marks must pass PSNR >35, SSIM >.9, LPIPS <.1; score C and J separately and veto quality/security-ineligible arms using complete T4/T5, C0 and wrong-owner data. Fixed F5-style reader, public owner conditioning and evaluator-only payloads are declared. Twelve locked sources cannot certify rare security risk or small effects; report source-cluster intervals and non-success missingness. Safety diagnosis/coverage cannot be counted as more secure marking.

**First decisive falsification / cost / kill.** L48x4 includes192 writes,1152 C1 native outcomes,288 C0,1152 wrong-owner calls,384 T4/T5 pairs and192 clean checks. Core8.319–8.895h; reserve/setup11.674h/$6.43. Cap allocator/supervised fitting and repeated masked-table comparisons at2h: **13.674h/$7.53**, conditional on the122-second F5-style cost and unchanged reader; no new neural reader is trained. The fully purchased table bounds gains and supplies the same accessible outcome universe to every simulated allocator; charge its collection once equally as study cost, and report masked reveal costs separately, never as actual saved GPU hours. Kill if a constrained oracle has negligible held-source action headroom, if action-difference prediction fails out of source, or if balanced/importance/Stage-P-appropriate controls equal the adaptive policy after fit cost. If cell saturation makes all contrasts zero, report the null rather than reallocating to convenient cells.

**Later evidence / cache.** A genuinely prospective allocation comparison needs new sources and randomized acquisition: L240x4's complete-audit ceiling is54.368h/$29.96 before fit; an8h total allocator/analysis allowance gives62.368h/$34.36. Each allocator must either share a prospectively randomized supported log under stated assumptions or receive independently budgeted data; separate physical runs multiply costs. A full audit table remains useful to assess selection regret, but cannot itself demonstrate online compute savings. Cache a fixed source/configuration write across requested cells/seeds; purchasing another configuration pays another write. If deployed once to choose one study, policy training may never amortize; declare that cold-start case as primary.

**Closest three inspected works and residual claim.** [Active Testing, §§2.2–3.4, Algorithm1, §5](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/kossen21a/kossen21a.pdf) already uses non-RL adaptive importance sampling for model evaluation and is a mandatory comparator. [Doubly Robust Policy Evaluation and Learning, §§2–5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1103.4601) already combines outcome prediction with genuine logging propensities. [Contextual Bandit Bake-off, §§2,4–5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.04064) shows simple supervised/greedy rules are strong and method rankings depend on tuning. TD03 I03/I06 concern forecasting/diagnosis, not this advantage-learning objective; I08 concerns multiplicity, and existing Stage P already occupies successive halving. Residual novelty is a tested action-advantage acquisition objective with expensive cached writes and honest nulls, not a new general bandit principle.

**Publishable claim, conditional.** For a frozen watermark action space and fresh image distribution, acquiring uncertain **action differences** improves downstream decision accuracy per total experimental cost beyond balanced factorial, adaptive importance, greedy and halving alternatives. Alternatively, a rigorous null can establish that apparent survival predictability offers no useful policy-selection headroom. Neither says a new watermark is more robust unless a later locked deployment actually shows it.

**Steelman.** The project currently pays for many measurements that can predict difficulty without teaching which action to take. Explicitly spending on disagreements could answer the RL feasibility question sooner and produce a general lesson about experimental cost dominated by reusable writes.

**Hostile assessment.** This may simply be ordinary optimal experimental design under a new name. The retained menu's tiny within-cell oracle headroom suggests little benefit, and learning an acquisition policy for one small study can cost more than completing its factorial. A replayed complete table makes allocation look cheap because the expensive counterfactuals already exist; only the later prospective study can settle that concern.

**Screens / provisional verdict.** G1 PASS (discrete acquisitions with evolving information); G2 PASS (balanced factorial, non-RL adaptive importance, Stage P, supervised/random); G3 PASS (F); G4 PASS (narrow expensive-action-advantage evidence question, provisional); G5 PASS (fixed endpoints/access, efficiency distinct from defense).
