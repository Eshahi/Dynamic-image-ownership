## Deferred or separately limited mechanisms

These are JUDGMENT dispositions after the frozen rule, not claims of empirical failure. They remain available for the steelman reviewer to rescue. They are not counted among the21 full sheets.

- **D1, RT07 coordinate-transform sequence:** not equivalent to C03's surrogate-block ordering. Fixed/random transform ensembles and differentiable expected-loss optimization already expose those coordinates. No project observation establishes useful sequence-specific response to reversible basis/resampling transformations, and non-native transforms could amplify surrogate mismatch. Retain as a C03 follow-up only after a source-disjoint action-order diagnostic supports an effect; no claimed independent novelty or full candidate score.
- **D2, RO10/RS13 mechanism-identification design:** C18 targets action-advantage learning, whereas these seek causal diagnosis of reader/carrier/channel bottlenecks. A fixed factorial design or Bayesian experimental design is the direct non-RL baseline. Hypotheses/interventions currently change multiple contracts and are not yet identifiable. Useful as the non-RL diagnosis component of an article, not automatically an RL contribution; no claim that C18's result would validate it.
- **D3, RS14 risk estimation under adaptive discovery:** policy-learning allocation and unbiased target-risk estimation are different estimands. Recorded positive inclusion probabilities, complete support and valid adaptive uncertainty would be required. This established adaptive-sampling idea is retained as an evaluation safeguard for C09/C10/C18; no new estimator or RL superiority is claimed.
- **D4, RS16 quantization phase:** C20 includes saved-RGB8 reconciliation, but a standalone phase selector needs its own factorial grid against exhaustive small-menu choice and quantization-aware gradients. The existing data do not identify phase-dependent regeneration headroom. Keep this narrow variant inside the interference diagnostic; do not reopen a sequence of writer microvariants or count it as a separate contribution.
- **D5, RS19 writer abstention:** declining hard sources changes coverage rather than meeting the all-source improvement event. A supervised calibrated reject rule at matched coverage is the strong comparator. It may be a useful product disclosure or separate selective-prediction study, but cannot count declined sources as successful marks or satisfy Side-A defensive efficacy by changing the denominator.
- **D6, RO17 version-change canaries:** the current research question has no observed service-version sequence or prospective maintenance deployment. Classical change detection with fixed sentinels is the immediate baseline. Without that separate task/data contract, it is an evidence-maintenance idea rather than a candidate for the present C/J improvement event. No monitoring or automation is created.
- **D7, RO18 batch budget controller:** this couples sources through a common remaining budget, unlike C05's within-image controller. It could be worth rescuing if future learning curves reveal predictable marginal gains, but none are logged here. Compare dynamic programming/index scheduling with identical curve observations and count all unfinished sources. Its cheapest necessary diagnostic can accompany C05, but a C05 gain would not establish batch-policy superiority. No further full sheet is added merely to increase the count.
