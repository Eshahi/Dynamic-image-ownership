# Prospective fresh-data contract for candidate screening

JUDGMENT: this is an acquisition rule for a later authorized study, not an acquired dataset, a licence acceptance or a source of evidence. No image, protected identifier or held-out journal is opened now. Candidate sheets specify their own required counts; WP8 will select at most two designs and freeze their counts before any new outcomes exist.

1. At a later acquisition stage, freeze a timestamped Wikimedia Commons metadata snapshot of natural photographs with explicit per-file CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA terms and sufficient native dimensions for the unchanged writer's input. Record the exact licence version, author, attribution, source URL and revision. Exclude unclear licences, illustrations, synthetic images and images requiring additional permission. Commons membership alone is not a licence guarantee.
2. Freeze a deterministic ordering before outcomes: sort eligible canonical file identifiers by SHA-256 of `rl-20261030-fresh-v1` followed by the canonical identifier. This seed is a reproducibility convention, not a statistical claim. Work down that list; log exclusions and reasons, never replace a difficult image because of watermark performance.
3. Before scientific use, an authorized custodian compares identifiers and downloaded file hashes against every consumed or assigned pool: the old 60, Stage P/S, Phase B, reserves, Stage C, COCO/DIV2K and any other retained project pool. The present dossier neither enumerates nor reads the protected lists. A matching or uncertain item is excluded. Commons' metadata SHA-1 can aid identity matching, but compute and record SHA-256 after later authorized acquisition; they are different hashes.
4. Group alternate revisions, crops and perceptual near-duplicates before splitting. Allocate whole related-image groups to training, policy selection, evaluation and any later confirmation. A numeric ID/hash exclusion alone does not establish independence. Pretraining contamination remains unknown even for newly acquired public images.
5. Fix quotas and source rules for T4 donors and T5 operationally different look-alikes independently of observed accepts. Related negatives are a separate challenge population; document their relationship and annotation procedure. Human judgments remain missing until people provide them. Neither CLIP similarity nor detector output supplies a human semantic label.
6. Any development evaluation or excluded-family result revealed during selection becomes consumed development evidence. A later confirmatory comparison needs a separate acquisition freeze and user decision. No protected reserve is repurposed. If eligibility, duplicate checking, permissions or enough sources cannot be established, stop that design as DATA_UNAVAILABLE instead of silently borrowing held-out data.

SOURCE: [Commons reuse guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia), inspected 2026-10-30, sections on checking individual file licences and complying with attribution/share-alike terms. [MediaWiki Imageinfo API](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Imageinfo), inspected 2026-10-30, parameters for timestamp, URL, dimensions, SHA-1 and extended metadata. These sources support metadata/licence handling, not the proposed sample's scientific representativeness.

No API enumeration, image acquisition, downloading, licensing commitment or scientific preprocessing has occurred in this research session. Any size, GPU time or success estimate in a sheet remains conditional on these later checks and the stated compute envelope.
