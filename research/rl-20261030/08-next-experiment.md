## Common conditions

JUDGMENT: Every proposed run is later work requiring a user decision about paid compute and data. No source has been acquired, no model has been run and no server code has been written here. The approved F6 experiment remains frozen and separate. Its outcomes do not change a protocol retrospectively; any subsequent amendment must precede the new study's outputs and preserve the original proposal.

The [fresh-data contract](ideas/fresh-data-contract.md) applies to both designs. Later source acquisition must exclude every consumed or assigned pool, including Stage P, Stage S, Phase B, reserves and Stage C, using an authorized custodian without exposing those protected identities here. Related images form whole split groups. Missing licences, compatible implementations, adequate eligible groups or independent T4/T5 labels stop the relevant stage; they are not inferred from an absence of detector acceptance.

SOURCE/DERIVED: Cost anchors are the retained approximately 122 device-equivalent seconds per F5 write, 2.19–3.63 seconds for a native/read/quality unit, and 1.4 seconds for an extra reader call. The 361–723-second F6-like writer anchors are unmeasured. Historical USD uses $0.551 per instance-hour, not a live quotation. These are conditional device-equivalent estimates; parallel task elapsed times must not be summed as though each occupied the entire device. The protocols itemize writes, native outcomes, reader/quality/negative calls, fitting, validation and reserves. New acquisition, annotation, engineering, licence and publication costs have no verified monetary estimate.

Each first screen has a hard 20-instance-hour ($11.02 historical) ceiling, including its unsuccessful attempts and controls. The two ceilings are separate and not an automatic 40-hour purchase. If both were separately authorized, their maximum combined ceiling would be 40 hours/$22.04; finishing an earlier screen does not authorize the other or a later learning stage. Unmeasured runtime or missing controls can yield RESOURCE_STOP below scientific completion.

JUDGMENT: Equal budget means equal allowed information and complete accounting of fitting, hyperparameter search, failed seeds, acquired outcomes and deployment—not merely equal policy inference time. Cached observations are reusable only when the physical writer/action/outcome is unchanged. A full counterfactual table may support masked replay or a finite oracle; the cost of buying that table cannot be reported as a realized saving from an online allocator.

Kandinsky 3 at strength .2 is the named optimization-excluded family in both protocols. It is historically known to the project. Its exclusion is from all policy fitting, reward, tuning and selection in the new study; it is not an assertion that no researcher has ever seen its behavior. Once its new evaluation is revealed it is consumed development evidence. Later confirmation needs independently frozen sources and cannot recycle that evaluation for selection.

Metrics remain C (content-compatible detection), J (exact original q plus content consistency), and strict final quality (PSNR>35 dB, SSIM>.9, LPIPS<.1). Exact fragile h, raw carrier presence and lawful ownership are not synonyms. T4, T5, C0 and wrong-owner events are separate; missing calls do not count as safe outcomes. Regeneration seeds, policy-training seeds, source groups and model families are different replication units.

The protocols distinguish a finite-screen falsification rule, a positive comparative result, a precision-supported null, and RESOURCE_STOP. A failure to reject zero gain is not equivalence. Conservative sufficient sample-size bounds are not impossibility lower bounds. Selecting favorable sources, cells, policies or failed seeds after inspection cannot satisfy any positive gate.
