# Prospective screening rule for WP5

Rule version1, fixed before candidate scoring. The commit containing this file and its SHA-256 will be recorded in PROGRESS and the candidate table before any full sheet receives a score or verdict. This rule does not select an experiment, authorize compute or alter F6. The three independent raw-idea lists are unfiltered inputs; a raw idea can survive, merge into a documented candidate or be declined with a reason. No quotas require a positive verdict.

## Hard screens

For every full candidate sheet, answer G1–G5 explicitly. A failed hard screen excludes it from the shortlist; unknowns remain visible and cannot be counted as established facts.

1. **G1, a supported necessary condition.** Name at least one of WP1 N1–N8 and identify actual evidence for its structural premise (for example a discrete action without a pathwise derivative, or an action changing later observations). Merely saying that RL is adaptive does not pass. The other necessary conditions remain obligations; one supported condition is not evidence of overall efficacy. Outcome variance alone does not establish differential action value.
2. **G2, a strongest fair non-RL comparison.** Name a concrete non-RL comparator with the same action set, source/model access, feedback and total training+tuning+evaluation+deployment budget. Include random policy/action allocation as a diagnostic. Where relevant include tuned PGD/expected-loss or robust gradients, finite-grid/BO/racing, supervised action prediction and the existing Stage P rather than only the old F5 writer. If a fair comparator cannot be specified, fail.
3. **G3, admissible fresh evidence.** Specify future licensed development data by a fixed acquisition rule and exclusion-by-ID/hash against all consumed/assigned/protected pools, with a separate later confirmation decision. No current protected source identities or held-out outcomes are needed to construct the hypothesis. Keep F6 frozen and blind. Any design depending on retrospectively retuning its outputs, Phase B or an excluded-family test fails.
4. **G4, a residual claim beyond occupied work.** Compare the closest three inspected works and the relevant TD03 idea. State exactly what remains testable/new; generic RL watermarking, RL diffusion removal, fixed-reader PGD, binding, registry pointers and successive halving are occupied. A known ingredient can pass for a specific unoccupied mechanism, rigorous matched null or new bounded evidence, but novelty is labelled provisional. Fully occupied application-only claims fail. An inaccessible close paper prevents a cleared novelty claim and lowers the novelty score; it does not justify pretending the paper is absent.
5. **G5, coherent endpoint and access contract.** Identify C/J versus presence, payload, strict final clean quality, T4/T5, C0/wrong-owner and side information. Writer/verifier routes cannot trade false acceptance or quality violations for reward. Attacker routes declare query access and attack-output fidelity separately from the clean writer bar. Game routes specify players, observability and feasible payoffs. A benefit that relies on undeclared privileges, incompatible tasks or weakened gates fails.

Every sheet labels its benefit as direct defensive efficacy, experiment/diagnostic efficiency, security discovery, and/or article evidence. An attacker finding more false acceptances does not satisfy WP6's event of improving the defense while preserving T4/T5; a cheaper diagnostic does not itself improve C/J. Keep those distinctions when forecasting and summarizing Side A.

## Scores after the hard screens

Five ordinal scores0–4 guide comparison; they are judgments, not calibrated probabilities. Record the evidence behind each. Their sum is a transparent selection aid, not a theorem.

| Axis | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E: project evidence/headroom | Contradicted or no plausible reachable benefit | Outside analogy or unmeasured action response | Direct project diagnostic supports a relevant opportunity, not efficacy | Project action-dependent evidence plus a plausible mechanism | Fresh prospective equal-budget improvement (not available in this research session) |
| M: need for the stated RL/bandit/game mechanism | Mislabelled or unnecessary | Same result likely available by a tiny fixed/full-information rule | Nontrivial partial feedback, state change or strategic response; simple alternative remains serious | Concrete reason adaptive history should beat a matched simpler policy | Direct ablation establishes that advantage (not available here) |
| N: residual publication novelty | Fully occupied claim | Mostly engineering or access to close prior art unresolved | Specific residual question/mechanism supported by inspected comparison, novelty provisional | Clear differentiated question with strong inspected comparison and meaningful general lesson | Independently cleared novelty plus compelling evidence (not available here) |
| F: feasibility of decisive evidence | Inadmissible or no bounded test | Decisive first test exceeds20h, or major unresolved runtime/data dependency | <=20h bounded falsification is plausible but key runtime/training cost uncertain | <=20h arithmetic grounded in measured dominant costs, with cap and clear kill rule | Existing reusable evidence suffices (cannot establish a new RL efficacy result) |
| V: scientific/use value if resolved | No meaningful claim | Narrow optimization with weak relevance | Useful bounded performance/cost/security evidence or informative null | Changes method choice or exposes a reproducible mechanism across relevant cases | Broad decisive result justified by evidence (not assumed here) |

An estimate of expected effect size is always JUDGMENT unless it is an observed finite-menu ceiling. Do not convert analogy effects into project gains. A sheet can give a prospective smallest worthwhile effect and uncertainty instead of an invented point forecast. The subsequent fresh forecasters estimate probabilities; WP5 scores do not substitute for WP6.

## Costs and decisions

- Show the first **decisive falsification test**, its actual inventory, and the later full evidence cost separately. A cheap software check or toy alone is not a decisive efficacy test. Use the supplied measured122s F5 device-equivalent write,2.19–3.63s feedback and$0.551/h with explicit scope; F6's361–723s and all per-step/learned-training costs remain unmeasured. Cache fixed candidate writes only when the action semantics allow it. Include comparator/random arms, failed calls, controls and reserved runtime or state which cost is unidentified.
- Mark **EXPENSIVE** when the first decisive test exceeds20 instance-hours ($11.02 at the retained rate), or cannot credibly fit the cap. Keep the candidate in the table and show why. A strict prospective cap can make an unknown-cost diagnostic affordable only by allowing RESOURCE_STOP; it cannot promise a completed comparison within that cap.
- Shortlist at most6 candidates that pass all five hard screens, score at least11/20, have E>=1,M>=2,N>=1,F>=1,V>=1, and offer a falsifiable incremental claim. A shortlist is **CONDITIONAL**, not a recommendation to spend or proof of RL benefit. EXPENSIVE candidates may enter if they otherwise qualify, but retain that label and cannot receive F>1 without a justified <=20h test.
- Rank eligible candidates by total score, then stronger project evidence E, then stronger mechanism M, then lower upper-bound first-test cost, then stable candidate ID. Select the first six; select fewer if fewer qualify. Do not alter weights or thresholds to obtain a preferred result. Report any excluded near-ties and why.
- Verdicts: SHORTLIST-CONDITIONAL; WATCHLIST (plausible but fails numeric threshold or unresolved hard-screen requirement); DO-NOT-PURSUE (hard failure or a clear dominated/occupied route); COMPARATOR/ARTICLE-ONLY (useful evidence but no incremental RL claim). A valid negative verdict can still support a rigorous null route in WP7.

Write at least20 full sheets spanning at least7 policy points, all R-full/R-bandit/R-game classes, P1/P2/P3, and at least4 explicit outside-field mechanisms. Coverage requires consideration, not passing a screen. Each sheet records raw idea IDs, TD03 comparison, all requested state/action/reward/access/transition/horizon/cost/security fields, three nearest inspected works, publication claim, steelman and hostile paragraph. The table links sheets and the raw idea mapping. Any later rule amendment must be separately committed, justified without selective rescoring, and trigger rescoring of every sheet; the original rule and scores remain preserved.
