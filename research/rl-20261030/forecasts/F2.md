Reasons and reference classes

- C03: Sequential constraint recovery is a plausible RL advantage when a temporarily adverse action changes which later repairs are effective, and informative state can generalize those choices across sources. This is a stronger premise than merely predicting which sources survive. AdaptiveISP, recurrent control and learned optimizers establish that such mechanisms can exist, but are weak quantitative analogies for this task. My principal simpler-method explanation is that robust PGD plus a fixed, beam, greedy or supervised schedule captures the same useful action sequence. AA/AB versus BA/BB differences alone do not discriminate these explanations. No action-response data support the residual RL advantage yet. I place more mass on a publishable restricted mechanism than on satisfying every A2-A4 gate, but do not assume either. Current-evidence B2 remains low because the proposed positive mechanism has not been tested.

- C04: A first probe could change whether a second probe is worth its cost and which writer should be committed, giving a real sequential information-acquisition problem. The finite action/horizon and fully shared observation interface also make Bayesian experimental design, MPC and greedy information gain unusually formidable controls. A probe that chiefly recognizes intrinsically easy sources adds little decision value. The retained within-cell writer menu has almost no measured C headroom; the four new configurations must establish new headroom rather than inherit it from source variability. OWN_SUCCESS is higher than A2 because useful cost reduction need not create a ten-point defense improvement, although fully charged training, probes and final evaluation make even the cost event difficult.

- C09: Informative binding-specific replies could guide complementary edits that ordinary donor copying misses, and an external verifier may reveal an exploitable sequence. Exact donor attribution on independently labelled distinct recipients is much harder than watermark presence or breaking the already weak local registry. Beam search, public-surrogate gradients and NES/SPSA can plausibly explain all attack gain without a learned policy. Semantic-watermark forgery, soft-binding work and DRIFT make generic novelty weak. Labels, fidelity adjudication and a modern external binding baseline are substantial completion risks under 200 hours. A2/A3/A4 are null because this is attack discovery, not defensive improvement; OWN_SUCCESS carries its separate stronger statistical attack event. A failed F6 writer does not directly establish or refute that event.

- C18: Adaptive sampling can concentrate on source/configuration contrasts that change a downstream decision, and this can differ substantially from easy-source selection or survival prediction. A plausible RL advantage is delayed value from a contrast that improves a shared learner over many source groups. Stratified/Neyman allocation, Bayesian design and greedy difference learning can plausibly obtain the same saving. Four arms and a small source-only feature interface favor these simpler controls. The replay table is a useful falsification tool but offers no prospective cost-saving evidence. I give this route relatively better future publication prospects because a tightly demonstrated distinction between prediction and decision value could support a concise PRL article even when 25% savings fail. A2/A3/A4 are null because allocation efficiency is not direct defensive performance.

- OVERALL: These are dependent opportunities, not four independent lottery tickets. C03 and C04 share the writer, strict quality, limited action headroom, data acquisition and strong non-RL competitors. Screening followed by a prespecified choice can modestly improve A2, but screen costs and multiplicity prevent an oracle maximum. A1 covers the full shared 600-hour program, not the first diagnostic. The publication program can select at most two routes and can retain an informative null; this helps FUTURE_ACCEPT more than it helps defense. Its current-evidence B2 is still low because every RL-informed route lacks a central proposed experiment. N0/R08 and N1/R09 are excluded from OVERALL B.

The gate-specific history changes my caution, not a numerical prior. Three fully assessed comparative families include two qualified and one failed; three other families stopped at earlier failed screens with full M1a unassessed. These were selected, adapted, dependent methods, including E3's F5 ancestry. I do not pool them into 2/6. The sole independent-encoder decision failed despite good in-family results; that is strong qualitative reason to discount A3, not a one-trial probability estimate. Both confirmation studies met their own different primary endpoints, but neither preserved strict clean quality on every fresh source. That makes the present universal final-mark quality gate a material A4 concern; it does not retrospectively fail those studies or yield a 2/2 prior. The correlated failed F5 modifications suggest a local plateau but do not rule out a richer action space.

The other six routes

- R02: A tight equal-budget null can be useful, particularly with a clear mechanistic explanation. No actual RL-versus-control null exists now. TIFS does not automatically reward failure of one's own candidate; a broad, well-controlled scientific lesson must survive prior art. A small unsuccessful screen or an underpowered difference is inadequate.
- R05: Opponent populations could expose complementary failure modes, but PSRO/SPARED and exact finite or non-RL double-oracle responses occupy much of the obvious contribution. Broad study costs already consume much of 600 hours before extras. This produces low current and prospective estimates.
- R06: A concrete genuine/forged observational witness for a nontrivial declared interface could be incisive. An exact finite-pair impossibility statement without the pair is not that result, and ordinary constrained search may produce any witness as well as RL.
