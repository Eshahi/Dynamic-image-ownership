The central difficulty is the conjunction in the actual defense contract. A policy must exceed the strongest equally funded non-RL by at least 10 percentage points, establish a positive paired lower bound, preserve the specified C/J endpoints, satisfy strict quality on every attempted final mark, and meet adequately powered negative-rate conditions. Training, three independent policy seeds, all controls and fresh confirmation must fit one budget. A small pilot improvement or a safe fallback to an unmarked image does not meet this event. A2 already includes completion risk; A3 and A4 are nested conditional events as instructed.

**Reference classes.** The history shows two conditional qualifications and one failure among three fully assessed comparative families, with three other families stopped at earlier, different screens. It also records three failed correlated F5 modifications, one independent-encoder primary failure, and two positive confirmations of different primary endpoints that each failed strict quality on some fresh sources. These are stage-specific, selected and dependent records. E3's F5 ancestry particularly weakens independence. I do not pool the six families or treat either pair of confirmatory studies as exchangeable Bernoulli trials. The useful lesson is that local development progress is attainable, but quality conjunctions and transfer can deteriorate when evaluation expands. There is no directly comparable RL success-rate series or reliable named-venue acceptance-rate series.

**C03.** This is the strongest direct-defense candidate in my forecast, but the margin over equally informed non-RL remains unlikely. Native-noise steps and repair steps can alter later feasible directions; state-dependent delayed repair is a plausible low-dimensional sequential advantage. The competing explanation is that one fixed order, beam search, an AdaEA-like conflict rule, or supervised action prediction captures the benefit. Noncommutativity alone establishes no necessity for learning a sequential policy. Analogies to sequential image processing and learned optimizers support existence, while their training bills and the absence of a measured action-response advantage reduce confidence. The retained writer menu's tiny within-cell oracle headroom is a warning, not a numerical ceiling on these new actions. A1 is substantially below one because several fully fitted comparators and independent confirmation are additional costs, not free uses of the illustrative 1,500 RL writes.

**C04.** A second probe can have genuine conditional information value: its usefulness may depend on the first reply, and that reply may change which writer should be committed. That gives a credible advantage over fixed probing. It is much harder to beat greedy information gain, Bayesian design or MPC with the same observations and costs. Two probes and four terminal choices also form a small search problem where exact or nearly exact non-RL planning may be strong. A probe that merely recognizes an easy image creates no action advantage, and expensive probes can consume the putative saving. I therefore put its cost-reduction OWN_SUCCESS above its A2 defense event, while requiring all quality and negative conditions for both. Prediction improvements from completed F5 marks supply no direct estimate of either probability.

**C09.** A history-dependent probe can distinguish which binding constraint currently rejects a composite and guide the next edit. A simpler beam or public-surrogate search may exploit the same feedback, and DRIFT and prior binding attacks narrow the contribution. The already observed registry weakness cannot count as a new discovery, and presence/C cannot count as exact-donor false attribution. Obtaining valid independently labelled recipient groups and integrating a modern external baseline are material completion risks within 200 hours. Missing human fidelity/identity labels are unresolved work, not assumed passes. Its A2/A3/A4 are null because attack efficacy is not defensive watermark improvement. Its OWN_SUCCESS concerns only the separately specified, valid, matched exact-donor attack event; it does not estimate lawful ownership or key authentication.

**C18.** This is comparatively feasible because the mechanism can be tested with a small fixed action space and a common downstream learner. Allocation targeted at uncertain action differences could avoid wasting calls on sources that are difficult but whose best action is already obvious. Neyman allocation, Bayesian design, greedy contrast learning and balanced sampling are the strong simpler explanations. The retained menu offers little within-cell action-choice gain, so a successful new menu is itself uncertain. A fully purchased table is only a replay diagnostic; prospective independent acquisition arms, failed calls and fitting consume real additional budget. A concise PRL paper with a general lesson about difficulty prediction versus decision value can survive an informative null, which is why future acceptance exceeds OWN_SUCCESS centrally. Its A2/A3/A4 are null because allocation efficiency does not itself establish the defensive contract.
