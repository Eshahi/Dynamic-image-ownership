## Theory and literature, with evidence in both directions

WP4 derives a local linear-Gaussian illustration: score-function gradient covariance trace `(d+1)||h||² + d*v/sigma²` with an optimal constant baseline, divided by the number of independent samples. Under fixed exploration radius the noise term grows as d². A true pathwise gradient has zero variance only in that particular toy; surrogate bias and real backward cost are unknown. An indicator reward can have zero pathwise derivative almost everywhere, so “gradients exist” does not universally defeat RL. Low-dimensional, discrete and history-dependent actions remain possible. A paired/antithetic benefit needs covariance assumptions; saved source/seed variance cannot determine policy gradient signal-to-noise.

At most three historically observed family clusters give no assumption-free bound on an arbitrary excluded family. Even optimistic iid-family zero-failure examples have one-sided95% upper failure bounds.632 at three and.527 at four; these are **not project confidence intervals**. LOFO can falsify a mechanism but gives a weak, selected-domain extrapolation estimate. K3 has been historically observed; excluding it from new policy fitting is an optimization exclusion, not never-before-seen discovery. A later new family/source freeze must not be repeatedly mined for selection.

The search contains133 full-text-inspected cards,335 exact-distinct queries and ten valid indirect scholarly-host families. C1 has12/15 cards under documented69-query/six-index retrieval exhaustion. SPAR-RL, HFRW, KAIST and several close image-RL leads remain inaccessible/uninspected; there is no first-RL-watermark claim. Generic binding, registry pointers, fixed-reader PGD, noise-aware insertion and successive halving are already occupied.

Positive analogies: MCTSteg models non-additive embedding costs; image-processing policies such as AdaptiveISP use sequential actions; TIFS DRL-FAS reports learned patch EER1.79 versus random2.14 and score selection3.29 in a different spoofing task; recurrent sim-to-real control can exploit dynamics observations; learned optimizers can amortize training. None supplies this project's strict-quality/content-binding/foreign-regenerator result.

Counterevidence/strong controls: RandAugment/TrivialAugment and random architecture search can match elaborate search. Pathwise DRaFT/related diffusion optimization can beat expensive score-function methods when their reward is differentiable. Learned-optimizer training can be enormous and step-count gains can disappear in wall time. Robust/ordinary domain-generalization baselines can beat specialized algorithms. Finite games, supervised portfolios, Bayesian design, beam search and greedy information gain may capture all proposed benefit.

Direct close prior art: DRIFT (arXiv2609.08213v1) already uses history-based RL diffusion-watermark removal/refinement and verifier gating. Its RL refinement reports LPIPS.136→.114 and PSNR24.95→25.19 on225 images; this is not an equal-total-budget proof of benefit for the present task. L2T learns transform selection; AdaEA adapts gradient ensembles without RL. SemBind, semantic-watermark forgery and the soft-binding benchmark already cover binding/spoofing/near-copy weaknesses. PSRO and SPARED occupy learned opponent populations and editor/detector co-training. Active Testing, doubly robust learning and contextual-bandit benchmarks supply strong non-RL/allocation precedents. Conditional probe value and matched action-order mechanisms remain testable residual questions, not cleared novelty.
