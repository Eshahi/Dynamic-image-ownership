STATUS: PROSPECTIVE; research only; no execution or expenditure authorized.
RECOMMENDATION: CONDITIONAL, initially the 48-write necessary-condition screen only, capped at 20 instance-hours ($11.02 at the historical rate).

JUDGMENT: This is one WP8 design, implementing [C03](../05-candidates/C03.md) and the bounded R01/R02 questions in [WP7](../07-article-routes.md). It does not change F6, retained F5, their readers, or their data. F6's outcome remains unknown here. Nothing below reports new empirical evidence.

## 1. Question, hypotheses and scope

JUDGMENT: Does the state left by one robustness/repair block change the useful next block enough to improve native source-majority C, beyond the strongest selected non-RL controller under the same information and total-cost opportunity?

H-order: With equal A/B counts, initialization, gradient exposure and finalization, AB and BA have different feasible terminal outcomes, with source-dependent preferred order.
H-headroom: The feasible four-path oracle exceeds the best simple screen controller; this is a necessary condition for this particular two-block policy, not a learned result.
H-policy: Each of three independently initialized fitted policies improves primary C by at least 10 percentage points, with a positive paired lower bound and all registered preservation gates.
H-null: For each of those three frozen policy fits, a paired upper bound excludes a +10 pp advantage over the independently selected finite non-RL portfolio. This is a bounded procedure/budget null, not a theorem about RL or all policies.

AB differing from BA establishes neither H-policy nor an RL contribution. Native/surrogate mismatch motivates the test but supplies no measured order advantage. A fixed order, supervised controller or analytic conflict rule winning is a legitimate non-RL result.

## 2. Operator and information contract

Name the new writer C03-P1. Keep F5 q32+h32 encoding, reader, thresholds, OwnerID handling and final RGB8 conversion fixed; label the result an image-domain writer.
Use two equal-length blocks. A applies the registered native-noise robustness gradient; B applies the registered clean/content-constraint repair gradient, with fit-only T4/T5 counterexamples. Discount is 1; horizon is exactly two; actions are only A and B.
All four paths AA, AB, BA and BB start from the same source, initial residual and writer seed. AB and BA have identical operation counts. Neither path receives an extra end-of-path repair; any shared refinement/fragile insertion occurs identically once.
All paths obey one fixed projection and gradient-call ceiling. No path receives more gradient steps because its quality is poor. Final failure remains a failure.

The later implementation freeze must specify the numerical loss terms/signs, their normalization, step schedule, block length, constraint radii, counterexample construction and deterministic finalization before acquiring outcomes. Reuse inspected fixed-reader primitives where valid; do not import a selected F6 result or silently equate an approximate security loss with native T4/T5 acceptance.
This numerical operator freeze is an explicit readiness dependency: absent a valid differentiable B, matched gradient ledger and measured total writing cost, status is DESIGN_NOT_READY, not permission to improvise during the screen. This dossier supplies no server implementation.

The bounded controller observes eight scalars: block index, decoded-q margin, content-consistency margin, PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS slacks, A/B gradient cosine, and previous action. Normalize only with fit statistics. No pixels, future regeneration responses or excluded-family scores enter the policy.
Charge every gradient needed to form these observations, even when its action is not selected. State extraction and counterexample computation have no assumed zero price. All learned and analytic controls receive the identical interface.
Deployment may use the frozen local surrogate models and the disclosed fit-only counterexample bank; it cannot query the actual future attacker. Check/confirmation recipients and T5 targets never enter that bank.
The counterexample bank, projection and quality contract are part of the newly named method's side information. The detector remains reference-free; no registry or original image is added at detection.

## 5. Stage 1: the only immediately recommended screen

For every screen source collect AA, AB, BA, BB, fixed-mean robust PGD, and analytic gradient-conflict control: 8×6=48 complete writes. The latter two receive the same gradient ledger, observations, finalization and native feedback.
The mean control uses equal normalized A/B exposure; the conflict control removes the opposing gradient component by its frozen zero-cosine rule. Numerical normalizations and clean-only step calibration belong to the pre-outcome operator freeze. No uncharged native hyperparameter search is called “tuning.” Broader mean/conflict tuning is explicitly funded in Stage 2.
Uniform random and best-fixed A/B schedules are exactly computable from the complete four-path table; the oracle chooses only a fully feasible recorded path. They do not require invented extra trajectories or constitute partial-feedback savings.

SCREEN_CONTINUE requires all four conditions: (i) no quality/negative violation in the path selected by the oracle; (ii) feasible oracle primary C exceeds the best of the six global fixed screen arms by at least 2/8 sources; (iii) AB beats BA on at least one source and BA beats AB on at least one other source on primary source-majority C; (iv) the selected oracle does not reduce R0 C or J relative to the best primary-C global arm, tie-broken by primary J then lower arm ID.
These are deliberately coarse necessary-condition rules; the 2/8 threshold is not a confidence interval or a +10 pp generalization estimate. Report intermediate state differences, all six-arm C/J vectors and every violation even when the rule fails.
If complete data fail any condition, stop C03-P1 as NO_FINITE_HEADROOM or ORDER_NOT_SUPPORTED, naming the actual failing condition. Do not enlarge the horizon/action space after this result. Such an enlargement is a new design.
If resources or essential observations are missing, return RESOURCE_STOP or INCOMPLETE, never a scientific rejection. At 20 instance-hours stop regardless of promising partial rows, retaining failed/unfinished attempts. No post-screen policy training is authorized by SCREEN_CONTINUE.

## 6. Stage 2: a later, deliberately small learned comparison

This stage requires a separate implementation freeze, measured feasibility and user decision. It is specified now to expose the full evidence/cost obligation; the current recommendation does not fund it.
On each of the 96 fit/tune sources collect 11 terminal paths: the four A/B sequences; mean-gradient controls with normalized robustness weights .25/.50/.75; conflict controls with cosine thresholds 0/.25/.50; and one frozen myopic worst-constraint controller. Every control respects the same total gradient and finalization ceiling.
Stateful discrete selectors can reuse the four-path portion only when their selected trajectory exactly matches one of those paths. A continuous-controller change outside the recorded 11 paths cannot be evaluated by replay and is outside this protocol.

RL is a two-action linear-softmax policy on the eight states. Optimize its exact finite-table expected two-step terminal return, using all four path probabilities and registered intermediate states; this is a small offline/model-known policy-gradient comparison, not an online exploration or large neural-RL experiment.
Reward is -1 for any final Q or observed-negative violation; otherwise `C(R1 .1) + .05*J(R1 .1) + .01*C(R0 .2)`, with each term a three-seed majority indicator. Native C/J, not a surrogate margin, supplies these terminal labels.
For each seed fit four configurations: learning rate .001/.01 × entropy coefficient 0/.01, 1,000 full-table updates each. Choose one common configuration by mean tune reward over all three seeds, then lower learning rate and lower entropy. Preserve every failed fit; no replacement seed.

The non-RL portfolio includes best fixed A/B schedule, all three mean-gradient and three conflict settings, myopic worst-constraint selection, supervised source-to-path selection, and supervised state-to-next-action selection trained on the complete native return table.
For the two supervised learners use the same eight-dimensional observations where available, linear logistic models, cost-sensitive complete-table targets, regularization {.001,.01,.1,1}, and 1,000 updates. Source-to-path prediction uses initial state only; the stateful learner sees the selected first-block state. No policy receives a future-path state at deployment.
Select the single strongest non-RL portfolio member on tune by complete quality/negative feasibility, then primary C, primary J, R0 C and lower registered method ID. All methods and ties are frozen before check. “Strongest” means this tuned finite portfolio, never an oracle over every possible non-RL algorithm.
This finite portfolio is not automatically the strongest available completed F6 or other external writer. A broad claim of beating the project's best non-RL method requires adding that eligible method prospectively with its own fully charged fitting/evaluation inventory and unchanged access/quality contract. That extra arm is not funded by the table below. Without it, report only the bounded controller-class comparison; do not equate its success with WP6's broader A2 event.
The RL arena and the entire non-RL selection arena each have the identical complete fit/tune table and a six-instance-hour fitting/tuning allowance. All stated grid fits must finish; premature truncation is RESOURCE_STOP rather than an underfunded baseline. There is no private reward data or extra gradient allowance for RL.
Report each arena's total acquisition+fitting+tuning+evaluation cost and the physically shared acquisition cost separately. Sharing this finite table buys information equally for both arenas; it proves no real online query saving. Unused time is not evidence of efficiency unless the same evidence endpoint has actually been reached.

The seven sealed-check policies are all three frozen RL fits; the selected non-RL winner; uniform random; the 7101 policy with intermediate state features permuted across source groups; and a supervised open-loop source-to-path ablation fitted within the non-RL allowance. The shuffled policy retains its own stage/previous-action fields.
All seven receive 200 source groups, four cells, three regeneration seeds and complete negative/quality checks. No checkpoint or seed is selected using check. The two ablations are diagnostics, not additional efficacy hypotheses; one shuffled seed cannot establish stability of the ablation across training seeds.
If development passes, independently freeze/acquire the 200 confirmation groups and evaluate the same seven policies unchanged with the new regeneration seeds. Confirmation reproduces these trained policies on fresh sources, not a new training population. No checking result is reused as a confirmation observation.
Report cold-start costs. A fixed illustrative deployment workload is N=1,000 future sources at the same declared operating conditions; training is amortized over that N only, with measured per-source observation/write cost. This is not a forecast that such a workload exists or a claim of deployment savings.

## 7. Endpoints, uncertainty and multiplicity

For every source/arm/cell, C and J require at least two of three regeneration seeds. C is decoded semantic read plus content consistency; J also requires exact original q, never exact fragile h. Report fragile h and clean both-match separately.
Assess every attempted final mark against its source for PSNR >35 dB, SSIM >.9 and LPIPS <.1. Every attempted check/confirmation mark of a policy making a positive claim must pass individually; missing outputs and quality failures remain in its denominator with C=J=0 and prevent that claim. Preserve failed fit/tune exploration too, without pretending every exploratory path was feasible. Do not tune reader thresholds, discard failed seeds or trade quality for robustness.
Negative endpoints are source-level indicators: any exact donor false attribution on the registered T4 pair; any erroneous content-compatible attribution on the unmodified T5 pair; any C0 acceptance across registered cells/seeds; any wrong-owner acceptance across clean/marked native rows. Preserve every row as well as these group maxima.
C0 receives both correct-owner and wrong-owner reads. T4/T5 use the correct claimed-donor code; their composition/label checks are separately recorded. One negative pair per source bounds this operating condition only, not all possible attacks.

For an RL seed versus the selected non-RL winner, let x+ and x- be the number of paired source wins and losses on an endpoint, n=200. Define `L = CP_lower(x+,n,.0125) - CP_upper(x-,n,.0125)` and `U = CP_upper(x+,n,.0125) - CP_lower(x-,n,.0125)`.
CP_lower/upper are one-sided exact Clopper–Pearson limits with the displayed tail probability. Bonferroni over the two binomial components gives each directional difference bound coverage at least 97.5%, despite dependence between wins and losses. This conservative construction needs no independent-regeneration assumption.
For a positive development result require, for **each** of the three RL fits: primary R1 .1 C point gain ≥.10 and L>0; primary J L≥-.05; both C and J L≥-.05 on R0 .2 and R1 .2; strict quality on every attempted mark; and all four negative gates below.
In addition, every listed primary-J and preservation-cell C/J point difference must be nonnegative. A negative point difference cannot pass merely because it lies inside the .05 noninferiority margin.
For each negative class require no new observed false acceptance relative to the selected control, and a one-sided 97.5% CP upper bound on the probability of a new RL-only acceptance ≤.05. This bounds excess risk conservatively because new-acceptance probability upper-bounds the paired rate increase. Report absolute rates and bounds too.
For the excluded-family step require K3 .2 C point gain ≥.10 and L>0, K3 J L≥-.05 and the same quality/negative gates, for each RL fit. This is the finite optimization-exclusion claim. Requiring only K3 non-regression does not pass this transfer event.
K3 J must also have a nonnegative point difference. The confidence-bound margin supplements, rather than replaces, point non-regression.
Fresh confirmation repeats all these conditions separately. A positive check followed by missing/failed confirmation stays a development result; it cannot be called reproduced.

For H-null require U<.10 on primary C for every frozen RL fit, plus the same J, quality and negative-preservation gates. A failed efficacy test alone never passes H-null. A null with both arms infeasible is a shared feasibility failure, not evidence that competent non-RL optimization suffices.
Allocate .025 to the positive claim and .025 to the bounded-null claim. Within a claim, all listed conditions and all three fits must pass: this is an intersection-union decision, not an opportunity to select the best seed/cell. Sequential transfer and fresh-confirmation gates cannot rescue failed primary evidence.
The tune-stage portfolio selection is separated from check, so the primary comparison is against one frozen selected control. Do not claim significant dominance over every exploratory arm or every cell; any such new contrast needs a new multiplicity plan. Screen selection, 11 paths, 12 RL fits, supervised grids and two diagnostics are disclosed in full.
Policy-training uncertainty is limited to these three named initializations; report their full range and each bound, never treat regeneration seeds or source counts as extra training replications. The estimand is conditional on those fits; no population-of-RL-seeds confidence claim follows.

DERIVED precision examples, not power forecasts or empirical outcomes: at n=200, 20 wins/0 losses gives L=+.03615; 20 wins/20 losses gives U=.09994. Thus the specified positive and bounded-null rules are attainable, but there is no guaranteed power under unmeasured discordance/effect rates.
With no new negative acceptance in 200 independent groups, the one-sided 97.5% upper bound is `1-.025^(1/200)=.01828`; with only eight it is .36942. The screen therefore cannot certify the +5 pp security margin. Wide bounds at the planned n mean INCONCLUSIVE; there is no outcome-dependent sample expansion.
