# Excluded families and the fixed reader

WP4, 2026-10-30. DERIVED arguments and JUDGMENT unless marked SOURCE or MEASURED. No new experiment or F6 outcome is used. This note separates an optimization limitation, a reader limitation and an identifiability limitation.

## What three or four families identify

Let u(pi,G) in [0,1] be the source-level success probability of a fixed policy under family G, with its clean-quality and binding contract fixed. Training observes G1,...,Gm. Without an assumption connecting an excluded G* to these families, the training observations place no nontrivial bound on u(pi,G*). Two response models can agree on every observed family/action/source and assign different rewards to all actions under G*. A learner has the same observations in both worlds and therefore cannot identify which unseen response applies. This is a statistical non-identifiability argument over unrestricted response models, not a claim that every such response is a physically realizable quality-preserving diffusion attack.

More source images reduce uncertainty about a given family's performance; more seeds reduce conditional regeneration uncertainty. Neither increases m. Strength changes, posterior-mode versus sampling, smoothing and different seeds inside SDXL are not independently trained encoder families. SD1.5, SDXL and Kandinsky provide at most three distinct observed family clusters here. A future fourth family would add one cluster, not thousands of independent domains.

Even under the stronger and currently unjustified assumption that m families are iid draws from a specified population and the policy was fixed independently, zero family-level failures gives one-sided95% upper failure probability `1-.05^(1/m)`: .632 for m=3 and .527 for m=4. A one-sided Hoeffding lower bound on mean bounded utility is `mean-sqrt(log(20)/(2m))`, subtracting .707 or .612. These deliberately optimistic iid illustrations are weak. Our selected popular families and overlapping leave-one-out fits do not satisfy that sampling story, so these are **not confidence intervals for this project**.

Leave-one-family-out remains useful as a falsification stress test: train on two of three (or three of four), apply a previously fixed selection rule, and report the complete family-specific vector including failures. It is not a precise estimator of universal new-family success. The folds share training data, train different policies, and may share model lineage. If repeated LOFO inspection chooses the algorithm, those folds become development evidence. A genuinely excluded later family is still needed for a confirmatory extrapolation claim; source holdout alone does not supply it.

## Assumptions that could support transfer

For a fixed writer and source distribution, let P_G be the distribution of its observable regenerated output and P_mix that of a source-family mixture. If total variation `TV(P_G*,P_mix)<=epsilon` is established, any bounded success indicator has `E_G* u >= E_mix u-epsilon`. To support policy selection uniformly, the closeness assumption must hold across the allowed writer/action set; closeness checked at the old F5 mark does not cover optimized adversarial marks. No such epsilon is measured here. A small encoder-only distance also does not bound the complete noisy denoising channel or a discontinuous decision.

Alternatively, a score s may be L-Lipschitz and a coupled target/surrogate output error bounded by e, giving `s(target)>=s(surrogate)-L*e`. Robustness follows only for margins above L*e, with analogous conditions for content-code compatibility and other required endpoints. Neither a valid local-to-global L nor output error e is available. MEASURED: Phase A's SDXL tangent mismatch (8–15 times larger and nearly orthogonal) and retention loss at noise injection directly undermine importing the old linear surrogate as that certificate. They do not disprove every higher-order or learned surrogate.

SOURCE: [DomainBed v1, §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.01434v1) makes model selection part of domain generalization and shows how strong matched ERM changes comparisons. Its LOFO/oracle distinction is directly relevant; it supplies no watermark guarantee. [Peng et al., §§IV–V/TableII](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.06537v3) demonstrates positive recurrent sim-to-real transfer, but the robot receives observations of target dynamics during operation. A writer that marks before the unknown regenerator acts has no equivalent target feedback. [EPOpt, §3 and AppendixA.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.01283v4) improves a lower return tail through ensemble training, at a mean-return tradeoff; its optional target-informed adaptation changes the information contract. Randomized and worst-family objectives can also train ordinary differentiable optimizers. These full texts were reopened for this note; detailed cards are C11-02/03/08.

The strongest case for RL is therefore conditional: a low-dimensional adaptive controller could learn useful state-dependent optimization behavior shared across families. It must demonstrate that behavior beyond a tuned random schedule, robust expected-loss PGD and a supervised controller given the same training observations. More simulator episodes cannot compensate for a missing target-relevant mechanism.

## What keeping the F5 reader fixes

Define the clean feasible set Q(x) by PSNR>35, SSIM>.9 and LPIPS<.1 for the completed marked RGB image, including the fragile tier. For fixed reader D_F5 and channel G, a necessary question is whether any delta in Q(x) makes `D_F5(G(x+delta))` satisfy C, J and the intended T4/T5 binding behavior. An optimizer chooses within this feasible set; it cannot create a detectable code outside the reader's accepted representation. However, failure of one optimizer does not show that the set of successful deltas is empty.

SOURCE: the retained implementation maps the SD1.5 VAE/DCT representation to320 chips, decodes a32-bit semantic q, and carries a separate32-bit fragile h. C requires read plus content match; J requires the original q as well. The generic recomputed branch and maximum decoded correlation are not equivalent to a correct original code. Public OwnerID selects a public pattern and does not authenticate a legal owner. Any policy reward that ignores these distinctions may optimize carrier presence while weakening the actual verification task. See [code context](../context/wp0-code.md) and [reader semantics](../context/wp1-reader-semantics.md).

For a scalar necessary margin m(delta), a local expansion is `m(delta+d)=m(delta)+g^T d+remainder`. If a justified remainder bound is `|remainder|<=L||d||^2/2`, the maximum local improvement within radius r is at most `r||g||+L*r^2/2`. If even that cannot reach zero, that local neighborhood is infeasible for the scalar necessary event. We do not know g or L after native regeneration, and the saved across-seed variance does not estimate them. The observed F5 R1 .2 mean decoded margin -3.9109 motivates a material gap to study, but it is not a per-source impossibility bound or a causal response to a proposed action. Correct q and content compatibility add constraints even after this margin passes.

DERIVED: with normalized pixel peak1, PSNR>35 imposes image RMSE<10^(-35/20)=.017783. F5's PhaseB median PSNR45.1 corresponds to RMSE.005559, about3.20 times smaller; the MSE ratio is10.23. This suggests numerical room in **that median scalar budget only**. It does not permit scaling every existing perturbation3.2 times: all-source PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, RGB rounding, fragile interference and channel nonlinearities still bind. The budget applies to the final composite mark, not solely the robust stage.

MEASURED: VINE-R survives more native regeneration, but has another learned reader, another100-bit carrier and clean PSNR median34.7; only31/60 development sources pass all three strict clean constraints. It proves existence of information surviving those tested channels at its own operating point. It proves neither fixed-F5-reader feasibility at the strict bar nor fixed-F5-reader impossibility. WAM/VINE message presence also does not establish content binding.

Three hypotheses remain distinguishable only with future controlled evidence: (i) optimizer/objective mismatch, where a better writer with the same reader works; (ii) carrier/reader mismatch, where changing representation helps at equal payload and quality; (iii) quality-constrained channel loss, where even a suitable reader cannot recover the desired code at the required distortion. A factorial comparison would hold payload, access, final quality and source set fixed while changing writer and reader separately. It is a prospective diagnostic, not authorization or an experiment run in this session. F6 GO would support a feasible same-reader operating point on its evaluated cells, fail would reject its specific surrogate/optimization package, and RESOURCE_STOP would leave science unresolved. None alone identifies a universal capacity limit.

## Implication for the next package

Keep P1/P2/P3 open as different mechanisms, but demand an explicit stable information source, a feasible secure operating point and a non-RL comparator. A zero-shot pre-encoder policy cannot identify a future hidden regenerator merely by being recurrent. An attacker with declared feedback may adapt to the current channel/reader, which is a different information problem. No candidate is scored here; WP5 must screen the actual state/action/reward and total cost.
