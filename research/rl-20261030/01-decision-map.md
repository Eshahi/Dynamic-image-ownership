## Necessary conditions and testable failure modes

These are conditions for a **possible** RL advantage, not claims that they hold. WP5 must identify at least one structurally supported condition and separately acknowledge every unresolved requirement. Matching a condition is necessary for screening, never sufficient to predict success.

| Condition | What must hold | Where structure exists / what could falsify usefulness |
|---|---|---|
| N1: discrete or inaccessible objective | Decisions cannot be optimized as cheaply/reliably with available pathwise derivatives. | D01–08,11–22 contain discrete choices; D09–10 primarily do not. If a small grid or differentiable relaxation equals the policy, RL has no demonstrated advantage. |
| N2: informative evolving state | Early choices alter later feasible states or observations, and a history-dependent policy improves over a fixed schedule. | D05–11,13,15,17 chains,22. Test state-shuffled/open-loop/constant policies. A sequence of independent arm draws is not enough. |
| N3: predictable heterogeneity | Predecision features predict differential option value, beyond mean arm/cell effects and seed noise. | D01–04,11,17,19,21. WP3's oracle ceiling and leave-source-out prediction are descriptive diagnostics; no oracle may be deployed as a policy. |
| N4: usable reward at affordable variance | A dense, correctly oriented reward improves terminal C/J or attack success and does not reward quality/binding violations. | Needed everywhere. Proxy margins might help; loss saturation, surrogate mismatch or seed noise can kill the route. Costs include rewriting, controls and failed calls. |
| N5: amortization | Repeated future use repays training and deployment cost relative to the best non-RL learner. | P1/P2 policies, optimizer controllers, attackers/verifiers. A one-off eight-arm selection offers little horizon for this; existing full-information data favor supervised learning. |
| N6: information about domain shift | Training variation includes a stable causal/task feature useful in an excluded family. | D03–08,17,22. Three observed families cannot establish general unseen-family robustness; more episodes within one family do not add domains. |
| N7: feasible secure operating point | A policy's action set contains solutions meeting strict quality, fixed C/J semantics and preserved T4/T5/negative requirements. | All writer/verification routes. VINE presence is not proof of fixed-F5-reader feasibility, nor is median quality proof of all-source compliance. |
| N8: comparable access and total budget | RL and its strongest comparator receive the same sources, observations, allowed feedback, model calls, search/training budget and deployment accounting. | All routes. A controller with native feedback cannot be compared to a blind fixed writer and called an algorithm-only gain. Report one-off and amortized comparisons separately. |

## What is decided now

JUDGMENT: do not substitute policy gradients for the existing high-dimensional RGB pathwise gradient without a concrete advantage in access, variance or objective alignment. Conversely, do not reject all RL merely because RGB gradients exist: discrete simulator choices, observation acquisition and strategic opponent responses are separate decisions. P1 (per-image configuration), P2 (spatial/frequency allocation), and P3 (pre-encoder transform/surrogate scheduling) are distinct readings of the user's proposal and will all enter WP5.
