## What the results establish

MEASURED, Phase A sections 3–5: the F5 reader's content-compatible source majority survives R0 .1/.2/.4 on 60/57/39 of 60 sources, and R1 on 0/0/0. Its encoder gain is largest in all three encoders tested; SDXL normalized chip retention drops from .66 through the codec to .08 after noise. These are descriptive paired observations, not encoder-only causal identification. The pooled first-order law is falsified; the quality comparator is unavailable because one fit source lacks sufficient complete features. Quality-ineligible attacks remain inactive, not successful attacks.

These facts cannot be retuned into new confirmation.

MEASURED, Phase B section 6: clean median PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS is 45.1/.982/.035 for F5 and 34.7/.995/.008 for VINE-R. JUDGMENT: VINE-R's survival shows that regeneration does not erase every carrier under its own operating conditions, but it does not establish a feasible carrier at this session's all-source strict PSNR >35, SSIM >.9, LPIPS <.1 bar. Different payloads, readers and operating points preclude an equal-task dominance claim.
