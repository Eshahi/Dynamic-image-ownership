**JUDGMENT — reader relevance.** A security researcher needs to know whether robustness depends on the encoded signal, the decision rule or the optimized objective before adopting a more expensive search method. Separating those explanations can produce a stronger contribution than winning a local policy race, provided the components are actually interchangeable and the endpoints remain identical.

**JUDGMENT — residual novelty.** The possible contribution is a valid identification of component-specific and interaction effects under one attribution contract, including an informative failure of factor separability. A generic factorial analysis, new reader, carrier mixture or insertion objective is not itself novel. [TD03 I02/I09/I11](../tifs-20261028/03-ideas.md) already cover robust-carrier binding, verified-payload limits and interference. The RL arm is a small test of residual optimizer value after those confounds are addressed, not the route's organizing novelty.

**MEASURED versus future.** The existing VINE/F5 contrast changes trained reader, carrier, payload semantics, quality and training resources together. VINE's presence endpoint and F5's C/J are not interchangeable; 200/200 presence does not establish exact q recovery or universal secure capacity. No factorial data exists. Neither a favorable F6 scenario nor existing VINE-B engineering supplies the missing causal separation.

**JUDGMENT — evidence required before a factorial claim.** A later design must establish which reader/carrier/objective combinations are compatible, match exact original decoded payload and error-correction/security overhead, hold final quality and threshold calibration fixed by declared rules, and disclose original-reference/registry/key access. If a reader change necessarily requires retraining the carrier, that intervention is a joint method factor; do not estimate a nonexistent independent reader effect. Randomized compatible interventions and paired source/cell/seed contrasts can support effects only within the tested design. Infeasible crosses remain documented missing cells, not zero robustness or data to impute.

**JUDGMENT — role and cost of controls.** The strongest gradient/greedy/supervised controller is required for the small RL arm with identical observations and full matched training/tuning resources. Separate component retraining from policy fitting, and include all failed combinations and any calibration. Decoder or carrier training can dominate the existing writer costs; no assumption makes it free because published weights are available. All relevant final arms still need T3, T4/T5, C0, wrong-owner, clean-quality and exact-message evaluation, plus independent source confirmation and an optimization-excluded family.

**DERIVED — symbolic inventory, not an invented factorial design.** With n source groups and k valid frozen terminal policies/combinations, four cells and three seeds require nk writes, 12nk marked outputs, 12n C0, 12nk extra wrong-owner reads, nk clean checks and 2nk initial T4/T5 units. The common conditional cost is `2 + 1.25*[nk*w + 12nk*5.03 + 12n*3.63 + nk*3.05 + 2nk*1.65]/3600` hours. For n=80,k=7, this is L's 86.38–156.76 h ($47.59–86.38) at w=361–723 s, **only if those writing anchors apply**. Each additional policy at n=80 adds 80 marks/960 marked outputs plus its controls; a changed reader requires its actual read timings rather than 1.4 s by assumption.

**JUDGMENT — full cost and first-screen limit.** Factor levels, compatible crosses, retraining demand and precision are not frozen, so the full R10 evidence inventory and required hours/USD are **UNKNOWN**. The 600 h/$330.60 forecast ceiling is not an estimate that a factorial study fits. L and the common training/confirmation subtotal are transparent sensitivity anchors, not a priced valid factorial. A ≤20 h compatibility or mechanism screen could only test necessary conditions; no such screen is claimed designed here, and it cannot establish factorial effects. A cost or compatibility failure ends that proposed scope rather than allowing arbitrary omitted cells.

**JUDGMENT — F6/data dependencies.** GO would demonstrate one same-reader objective intervention on F6's own cells, but would still not isolate the full reader/carrier/objective contrast. FAIL would reject that frozen package, not prove the reader is the bottleneck; RESOURCE_STOP leaves that comparison missing. R10 can be scientifically useful in any branch, but it depends on lawful, compatible model/decoder access and fresh licensed/group-disjoint data; trained external systems' historical data and compute must be disclosed where known and otherwise marked unknown. Exact payload compatibility, T4/T5 labels and separately approved confirmation may block a stronger claim in every scenario.

**JUDGMENT — exactly five hostile objections and answers.**

1. **“Your factorial crosses are not well-defined because the reader and carrier are co-trained.”** Establish compatibility before outcomes and treat inseparable changes as one joint intervention. If valid crosses do not exist, restrict to paired method contrasts or a documented separability failure; withdraw independent factor/interaction claims.
2. **“VINE wins because you compared presence with exact content attribution at different distortion.”** Agree that existing numbers are confounded. Require identical exact payload/acceptance semantics, strict final quality and declared detector side information, keeping unmatched published operating points only as contextual controls.
3. **“The new objective or decoder received vastly more training than the baseline.”** Record inherited training where known, all new retraining/tuning and full matched optimization opportunities; unknown legacy cost remains a limitation. Report conditional fixed-pretrained-system effects separately from end-to-end efficiency claims.
4. **“The RL arm is decorative and will distract from the actual diagnostic.”** Keep it explicitly small and secondary; compare with equally informed gradients/greedy/supervised controls. Remove RL from the title if it adds no independently supported effect; the valid factor-separation result can stand on its own.
5. **“Three families and selective feasible crosses cannot establish a general capacity or causal law.”** Report only identifiable contrasts in the compatible finite design, with interaction/selection uncertainty and a separately sealed family/source check. Do not infer universal channel capacity, reader impossibility or arbitrary-family transfer from those data.
