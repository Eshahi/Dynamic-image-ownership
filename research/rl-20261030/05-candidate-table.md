## Selection and integration audit

The root retained all author scores after reading every sheet and checking the shared cost formulas; no threshold, weight or hard screen changed. C18 ranks first by total. C03/C04/C09 tie on total, E and M. Their unmeasured extra runtime makes the common 20-hour resource cap the comparable upper first-test spending bound; conditional arithmetic envelopes are not guaranteed runtimes. The stable-ID tiebreak therefore orders C03, C04, C09. There is no excluded eligible seventh candidate and no positive quota was filled.

Near-threshold exclusions are C06, C08, C11 and C14 at 10/20. C06/C11 have expensive reciprocal-learning tests; C08 is close to DRIFT with no established history advantage; C14 additionally fails M>=2 because finite pairing can produce the same witness. C15/C16 cannot currently supply their altered acceptance rule's validity; C19/C21 have unresolved adjacent novelty or new-reader contracts. The retained F5/v5 selector, small finite coding/schedule games and static mixtures remain useful comparators but do not earn an RL contribution by relabeling.

The 21 individual sheets are the counted deliverables. The three linked author bundles retain the initial judgments and shared contracts, not 21 extra candidates. All sheets have state/action/transition/reward/horizon, access and leakage, strongest non-RL plus random controls, necessary conditions, effect target and uncertainty, future data, first/full costs, endpoints, kill rule, three inspected neighbors, TD03 comparison, publication claim, steelman, hostile case and verdict. Root's final selection here supersedes the sheets' provisional ranking wording.

For security screens, a multi-seed training-call allowance is not three independently evaluated final policies: the small first inventory permits selecting one policy using selection-only data before locked evaluation. Preserve every training seed and failure. A later efficacy claim requires independent policy-seed evaluation with its multiplied cost. Aggregate control allowances in those screens are ceilings to be itemized in a future protocol; missing controls prevent the relevant endpoint from passing. C09 still needs a modern external binding comparator before a broad publication claim; a known local registry failure alone is insufficient. These limitations do not count as completed evidence.
