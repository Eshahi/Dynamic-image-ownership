# Petrov2025Coexistence

**Source:** Aleksandar Petrov, Shruti Agarwal, Philip H. S. Torr, Adel Bibi, John Collomosse. *On the Coexistence and Ensembling of Watermarks*. [arXiv:2501.17356v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.17356v1), 29 January 2025. Accessed 2026-10-19. **V2:** §§2–4, Table 1, Figures 7–8 and augmentation appendix inspected. Later venue information is not substituted for this inspected version.

**Verified edge:** reference entry Bui et al. (2023a), *TrustMark: Universal Watermarking for Arbitrary Resolution Images*, cites arXiv:2311.18297. This is an older-preprint edge, not a final-TrustMark edge.

**Direct evidence:** the study composes watermarks in series and parallel and varies strength, clipping and error correction. Table 1 reports zero first-message recovery on the same-method diagonal, while heterogeneous combinations can coexist. Figure 7 uses 1,020 Adobe Stock images. Figure 8 shows that a stronger TrustMark-Q with error correction can outperform an ensemble; benefit is conditional on the operating point.

**Interpretation:** authors motivate increased capacity and alternative quality/robustness tradeoffs. My inference: layering is established prior art, not a general robustness guarantee. These conventional augmentation/coexistence results do not isolate registered-original attribution after diffusion, or the thesis's q/content benefit behind an owner gate.
