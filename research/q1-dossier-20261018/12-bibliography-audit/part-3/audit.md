# Bibliography metadata audit, part 3

Access date: 2026-10-19. Scope: exactly the 29 keys in `assigned.bib`. All 29 keys are preserved in `corrected.bib`; `audit.csv` has exactly one row per key. This audit accessed bibliographic metadata and publisher landing pages only. It did not refresh experimental claims or establish final-fulltext equivalence.

## Result and interpretation

All 28 scholarly entries have a directly retrieved arXiv or Crossref metadata record. All 29 titles and all 28 scholarly complete author lists match their selected cited-version records after harmless case/brace/typographic normalization. The remaining entry is Adobe living documentation with no printed byline or date: its title and host are corroborated by the publisher's repository; an explicit Crossref title query found only unrelated results. A failed or nonmatching search is not proof that a DOI does not exist.

The four year mismatches below are **version-policy mismatches**, not evidence that the stated publication years are false. The supplied `@misc` entries cite arXiv DOIs and inspected preprints but use later publication years. This deliverable consistently retains those entries as preprint citations with their first arXiv-publication years, adds explicit arXiv venue/eprint fields, and keeps the later publication venue/year and any verified publication DOI separately in notes and CSV columns. An equally valid manuscript-wide alternative is to convert them to publication citations with the verified publication year and venue; do not change year alone without selecting the cited version. Citation keys remain unchanged regardless of year.

DOI `absent` means a missing original field now filled from a verified record. DOI `N/A` means this audit did not establish a publication DOI for that source type; it is not a universal claim of no DOI. Exact original and verified strings, complete publication author lists, source URLs, access dates, version differences and unresolved items are in the CSV. Crossref's raw name-token split for Cameron is retained in the metadata JSON and explained below rather than copied as an incorrect author spelling.

## Files and remaining uncertainty

`arxiv-metadata.json` contains 19 retrieved records including the inspected versions and submission/update dates. `crossref-metadata.json` and `crossref-retry.json` contain publication records and the original retry failures. `crossref-title-search.json` records the three title searches used to find publication DOIs. `datacite-metadata.json` records arXiv DOI metadata and three failed calls; the corresponding DOI links for Stable Signature, WOUAF and SERUM were verified on opened arXiv abstract pages instead. `dblp-metadata.json` preserves the HTTP 429 search attempts. `adobe-crossref-query.json` preserves the unindexed-document query and nonmatching results. `publisher-corroboration.json` records the supplementary opened landing-page metadata. A temporary metadata-assembly helper was removed after generation and QA; it ran no experiment and changed no file outside this directory.

Unresolved items are explicit in the CSV: Adobe's byline/date/immutable commit; publication venues for RAIN/NAWE; final publication landing/DOI refresh for Watermarks in the Sand; publication DOI/final-fulltext refresh for MetaSeal; and all originally documented fulltext or exact-version-equivalence gaps. No fulltext PDF, image, dataset, model, source code or package was downloaded to disk.

The four year-status mismatches are editorial consistency decisions, not false publication-year claims: pure arXiv citations use the first-release year while their selected inspected versions remain explicit; final publication citations use the publication year. The later venue and year remain separately documented in the CSV and corrected BibTeX notes.
