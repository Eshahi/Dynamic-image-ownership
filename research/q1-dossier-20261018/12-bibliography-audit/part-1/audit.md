# Bibliography metadata audit: part 1

Access date: **2026-10-19**. Scope: all **29** entries in `assigned.bib`. This is a metadata audit, not a new paper inspection, literature synthesis, result audit, or claim of equivalence between manuscript versions.

## Coverage and interpretation

- All 29 entries have one CSV row and one corrected BibTeX entry with the original key.
- 25 arXiv works were retrieved in one current Atom metadata request. Forensic-stealth v1 was also queried explicitly because the current unversioned record is v2.
- Crossref supplied matching publication metadata for 11 assigned works: StegaStamp, Craver, Kutter, RoSteALS, FreqMark, UnMarker, SpecGuard, TrustMark's final publication, InvisMark's final publication, PhaseMark's final publication, and Müller. Seven supplied arXiv DOIs were additionally verified in **DataCite**, their actual registry; they are not Crossref-validated DOIs.
- Thus 28 entries have directly retrieved Crossref or arXiv records. **GROW has CVF publisher metadata and a DBLP indexed author-page search hit, but its direct DBLP record/API could not be opened.** Its DOI remains unresolved. Do not report all 29 as having successful direct registry retrieval.
- Two complete-name discrepancies were found: omitted middle initials in Craver and Kutter. Every assigned author list contains all named authors; no missing person was found. All supplied titles and years match the selected edition or the explicitly stated conference year after harmless title capitalization/markup normalization. SpecGuard has a real author-order conflict between editions, described below.
- Four absent DOI fields can be filled for the selected publication: StegaStamp, Kutter, SpecGuard, and Müller. Separate final-publication DOIs were also found for retained TrustMark, InvisMark, and PhaseMark preprints, but are not silently substituted for their preprint identity.
- `match` means support for the selected citation edition, not equality across all versions. Venue statements in supplied notes count as supplied venue metadata. `absent` for DOI means the bibliography field is missing although a matching selected-publication DOI was retrieved. `not-applicable` means the selected preprint has no claimed DOI or formal venue to compare in this audit; it **does not assert that no DOI or later publication exists**. All arXiv acceptance comments are recorded as author-supplied metadata, not independent proof of final text.
- Current exact source titles, complete ordered authors, publication/update dates, venue strings/comments, DOI values, source URLs and edition distinctions are in `audit.csv`. `arxiv-metadata.json`, `crossref-metadata.json`, `datacite-metadata.json`, `FreqMark.json`, `TrustMarkFinal.json`, and `supplemental-metadata.json` retain metadata evidence. Temporary XML and HTML responses were reduced to metadata-only JSON and removed; no manuscript was downloaded.

## Limits and retained uncertainty

The CSV is an audit of supplied metadata, so a missing DOI or absence of a later venue claim is not treated as evidence that publication has never occurred. The listed retrievals do not establish completeness of all possible later publications. Current arXiv metadata is sufficient to verify preprint identity, but author-supplied conference comments alone do not certify final publication text. The GROW direct-registry gap, SpecGuard author-order conflict and forensic-stealth version change remain explicit. All original keys are preserved; protected bibliography and other project files were not edited.
