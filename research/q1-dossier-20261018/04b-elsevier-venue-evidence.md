## Shared publisher policy and unresolved submission facts

Elsevier's [research-data guidance](https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/research-data) treats software/code, models, algorithms and protocols as research data. It encourages repositories, linking datasets to articles and data statements explaining availability or restrictions. It explicitly says options vary by journal. This establishes a publisher baseline only; it does **not** verify a mandatory public code/data-release rule for any of these four titles. A reproducible release plan should disclose registry enrollment information, descriptor implementation/version, thresholds, split separation and unavailable material without releasing protected data.

Elsevier's [decision-time support page](https://www.elsevier.support/publishing/answer/when-can-i-expect-a-decision-from-the-editor) explains that journal metrics vary. It does not supply a verified current value for these titles. First decision can include desk decisions and must not be interpreted as external-review duration; submission-to-acceptance and acceptance-to-publication are separate metrics.

Do not budget an APC or promise a submission timeline from this dossier. A final venue choice still needs an accessible current official guide/Insights/OA record and an accepted ranking-system category/year row. No paid subscription or access workaround was attempted.
