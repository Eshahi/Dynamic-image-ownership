- **Identity:** Hager A. Gharib; Noha M. M. Abdelnapi; Khalid M. Hosny. *Robust zero-watermarking for color images using hybrid deep learning models and encryption*. Scientific Reports 15, 28906 (7 August 2025). DOI `10.1038/s41598-025-09290-7`.

- **Mechanism / side information:** Fuse LBP texture with VGG19 conv5_4 features, binarize, and use DWT/DCT feature-space processing with scrambled watermark. Lorenz/logistic transformations and XOR produce the externally stored zero-watermark. Verification needs stored share, feature extractor and scrambling parameters. Feature-space insertion must not be described as a physical image watermark.

Detailed recovery results use NC and BER; they do not report operating-point owner-attribution FPR for realistic look-alikes. No diffusion editor/regenerator identified.
