- **Mechanism / side information:** Move image vectors away from separating hyperplanes in a balanced hierarchy. The modified content plus stored index tree yields a database index; that index retrieves an arbitrary associated message. Decoder has the tree and association table. This is an active watermark/retrieval hybrid, not pure zero-watermarking.
- **Capacity / evaluation:** One identity among database entries, not a fixed independent payload. Gaussian-source experiments and 2,048 natural images downsampled to 64×64 grayscale, normalized to unit energy. Under the paper's AWGN convention, 1,000 query-noise repetitions returned correct index for all trials at noise variances 1 and 2; 655 at variance 4. Do not map those variances to image-domain PSNR or diffusion strength.
- **Stated limits / access:** Security of associations explicitly excluded; AWGN only. Database fixed during encoding/query; online enrollment left for future work. Full index information is side information and communication/storage is discussed.
- **Relevance:** The paper explicitly explains that identical content associated with different messages is ambiguous under retrieval alone and that physical marks can separate the instances. Thus even the broad rationale for adding a physical owner pointer to registered content is old. Thesis value requires a new, precisely evaluated generative/adversarial setting, not an architecture-only claim.
