# rocek2021critical — Medical zero-watermarking criticism

- **Identity:** Aleš Roček; Michal Javorník; Karel Slavíček; Otto Dostál. *Zero Watermarking: Critical Analysis of Its Role in Current Medical Imaging*. Journal of Digital Imaging 34, 204–211 (2021), published 7 January. DOI `10.1007/s10278-020-00396-0`.
- **Primary source / access:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10278-020-00396-0 ; 2026-10-18. **V2**, selected-method descriptions, Methods, Results, Discussion. MAIN cache `C4/critical-2021.html`, SHA256 `45A0C1B518C8A5B54946F6D03C715E223E26AFE92AA5840E88D348CC983099BA`.
- **Setting / mechanism / information:** Four tested families: DT-CWT visual cryptography, DWT/SVD, completed LBP, contourlet/SVD with QR. Features and a watermark produce an ownership share; a trusted authority stores a dated/signed record. Verification needs share, keys and the relevant feature procedure. Payload is method-dependent logo/QR; no common bit-capacity claim.
- **Evaluation:** CT abdominal tumor images from cooperating Masaryk Memorial Cancer Institute; total patient/image count not reported in inspected text. Tests progressively modify content, compare neighboring slices from one patient, and compare corresponding anatomy across patients. No generative regenerator.
- **Findings / limits:** High recovered-watermark correlation persists for different medical images. Neighboring slices have PSNR below 40 dB; different-patient comparisons below 30 dB, yet watermark correlation can remain misleadingly high. No reliable decision threshold emerged for the tested examples. This is evidence about these CT methods/data, not a universal impossibility theorem for all zero-watermarking.
- **Thesis relevance / inference:** Strong prior warning for E2: robustness and discrimination must be measured jointly on realistic near duplicates. A recovered owner logo and reliable storage do not imply instance specificity. This paper supports reporting the look-alike limitation rather than silently defining all close images as the enrolled work.
