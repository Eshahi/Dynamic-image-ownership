# hung2026signcryption — Registered shares with signcryption

- **Identity:** Pham Thai Hung; Ta Minh Thanh. *A robust zero-watermarking and signcryption scheme for image copyright protection and license verification*. Scientific Reports 16, 9103 (14 February 2026). DOI `10.1038/s41598-026-38991-w`.
- **Primary source / access:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-38991-w ; 2026-10-18. **V2**, proposed scheme, registration/reconstruction, experimental setup and attack results. MAIN cache `C4/signcryption-2026.html`, SHA256 `2E7E86F988432D69662918B6931CDB5725EC4EBD2E0811D91A2D8424E89F7D94`.
- **Mechanism / detector information:** Entropy and SIFT select local regions. Y-channel DWT/DCT binary features combine by XOR with an Arnold-scrambled logo. ElGamal-style signcryption protects local shares stored by a semi-trusted certification authority. Verification requires encrypted shares, cryptographic keys, keypoint metadata, feature procedure and local-vote reconstruction. It does not require embedding in image pixels.
- **Payload / data:** 32×32 binary logo; 512×512 sources. Twelve TCIA medical and three SIPI color images (15 total).
- **Threat / conditional findings:** Geometric transformations, cropping, JPEG and filtering/noise; no generative regeneration identified. Gaussian-noise settings labelled 1%, 2%, 5% give proposed NC approximately .82, .76, .68 in the comparison, despite broadly favorable abstract language. These are watermark correlations, not false-attribution rates.
- **Limits / relevance:** Protecting a registry record and verifying its signer are distinct from establishing legitimate ownership of enrolled visual content (our inference). Competing-owner enrollment and selected look-alike false accepts were not tested in the inspected evaluation. Licensing is outside the thesis scope; cite only the established separation between robust feature matching and record authentication. Do not inherit the paper's broad security claims without independent cryptographic analysis.
