# seenivasagam2013qr — QR registration for medical-image authentication

- **Identity:** V. Seenivasagam; R. Velumani. *A QR Code Based Zero-Watermarking Scheme for Authentication of Medical Images in Teleradiology Cloud*. Computational and Mathematical Methods in Medicine 2013, article 516465, 16 pages. DOI `10.1155/2013/516465`.
- **Primary source / access:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3732617/ ; publisher https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2013/516465 ; 2026-10-18. **V2**, §§3–6, Tables 2–6. MAIN cache `C4/qr-2013.html`, SHA256 `919D9DD36158EFB234486377B22ED05C2C590A946C3AC0246BA3312303B6F55F`.
- **Mechanism / information:** Contourlet low-frequency blocks, SVD and signs of Hu moments form a master share. Scrambled QR and master share form a secret share. Radiologist retrieves the secret share from an EHR server, reconstructs a QR from the suspect image, then decodes patient identification and an EHR URL. Detector requires the share and block-selection/Arnold parameters; no pixel embedding.
- **Payload / evaluation:** QR encodes sample HL7 patient metadata plus URL; trimmed logo 77×77, not 5,929 independent payload bits. Seven 512×512 example images, one per CT/mammogram/MRA/nuclear/PET/ultrasound/X-ray modality. Checkmark and Matlab distortions; no diffusion. Table 2: JPEG quality 50 gives NC .9854 for mammogram and .9999 for CT. These are recovery correlations on examples.
- **Limits / relevance:** High invariance of moment signs can also suppress instance specificity (our inference, consistent with Roček's later critique). No hard-negative population or adversarial re-enrollment evaluation found. A QR content pointer recovered using a registered feature share is explicit early prior art; the thesis's physical pointer must be distinguished from this external association.
