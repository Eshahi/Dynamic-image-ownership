- **Setting / mechanism:** Unmodified-image registration. SD1.4 VAE reconstruction supplies training targets: patch pairs whose ViT feature distances change least. An MLP learns stability; inference retains top-50 pair indices. Suspect verification measures intersection with the registered set, divided by 50.
- **Side information / capacity:** ViT-B/16, trained predictor, enrolled edge set and optional storage key. 196 patches at 224 pixels; 50 edges are an intrinsic signature, not 50 arbitrary payload bits. No physical owner signal is inserted. Appendix B proposes Arnold scrambling of stored indices.
- **Evaluation:** COCO training (count not stated in inspected setup). §5.1 allocates 10,000 UltraEdit/MagicBrush instances: 1,000 regeneration, 1,000 global editing, 4,000 local editing, 4,000 ordinary degradations. This differs from the exploratory allocation in §3.1; do not combine them. §5.3 separately samples 1,000 images per each of COCO/UltraEdit/MagicBrush for all-pair uniqueness.
- **Conditional findings:** Table 1 reports TPR at nominal 0.1% FPR: deterministic regeneration 85.13%, InstructPix2Pix 89.65%, MagicBrush 95.63%, UltraEdit 96.55%, ControlNet inpainting 97.43%. Appendix G uses DPM-Solver with 25 regeneration steps; editors use 50 steps, global guidance 8/1.5, local guidance 7.5/1.5 and 10–60% masks. This is not a matched thesis SD img2img strength comparison.
- **Threat / limits:** Evaluated editors and random unrelated-image collisions; no competing-owner enrollment or selected same-shot look-alike test found in inspected sections. Appendix F assumes independent edge activations with probability 0.5 and a binomial null, then selects the threshold. The claimed FPR is therefore conditional on that model; it is not demonstrated hard-negative FPR. Top-K dependencies and selected near duplicates require separate justification (our assessment).
- **Relevance / inference:** Directly defeats broad novelty claims of zero-pixel, registered descriptors robust to generative editing. Its intrinsic signature does not by itself establish which claimant legitimately enrolled an identical image. A physical pointer may supply an additional observation; its useful contribution must be measured against fingerprint-only enrollment under explicit adversarial access. Scrambling is not accepted here as a cryptographic ownership proof.
