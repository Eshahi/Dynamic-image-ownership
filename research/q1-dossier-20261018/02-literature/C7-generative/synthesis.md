# C7: Generation-time mechanisms and the natural-photo boundary

Inspection date: 2026-10-18. Ten distinct works receive V2 method-and-evaluation refreshes/new inspections in `matrix.csv`; V2 does not mean reproduced. Seven refresh existing cards; AquaLoRA, WOUAF and SERUM add full primary inspections. SEAL, GROW and DiffMark are cross-references, not additional C7 counts.

The comparison needs four separate axes: where insertion occurs, which model access it requires, what the detector observes, and whether the task is model provenance, user identity, or enrolled-image verification. Calling all these methods “latent watermarks” hides the decisive differences.

Tree-Ring, Gaussian Shading, PRC and RingID control initial generation noise and recover it through diffusion inversion. ROBIN moves insertion to intermediate denoising and reduces inversion work, while adding optimization. These mechanisms do not directly supply a writer that minimally changes an arbitrary existing photograph. Their detection/inversion machinery and key banks must be charged as side information and compute. PRC's cryptographic guarantees also require the stated adversary/input assumptions; Appendix Figure 8 is a proposed robust/private-VAE mitigation discussion, not a measured robustified-regenerator result. See the corresponding cards for inspected sections and threat-specific metrics.

There are important exceptions to a blanket natural-photo exclusion. [WIND §4.3 and Appendix D](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.04653v5) uses inpainting to watermark existing images, but the reported photo-fidelity result misses this thesis's strict gate. [AquaLoRA §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.11135v1) explicitly inserts a residual into an encoded existing image before VAE decoding. Its photo stage is more directly relevant to proposed latent insertion than an initial-noise watermark. Reconstruction-relative perceptual loss must not be substituted for distortion relative to the original photograph.

Inversion-free extraction is established prior art. Stable Signature and WOUAF train RGB extractors coupled to modified generation decoders. [SERUM §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13396v1) is a sharper novelty conflict: a VAE encoder feeds a learned lightweight detector, while the generator remains unchanged and insertion uses initial noise. Thus “VAE encoder plus lightweight detection” alone is not a new thesis contribution. Cheap detection must also be separated from training and generation cost. SERUM's attack results use specific regenerators and separately calibrated perturbation thresholds; they do not establish an unknown-attack operating point for minimally changed natural photographs.

Registry use alone is also insufficient novelty. WOUAF registers user fingerprints; [WIND §6](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.04653v5) proposes storing prompts/seeds for past generations to investigate edits that preserve the watermark. [SEAL](../C3-forgery/cards/arabi2025seal.md), already inspected by C3, further constrains semantic-binding claims. A record holding continuous descriptors of a published image must be compared on its exact observation model, threat boundary and false-association problem, including a fingerprint-only arm. A user-ID registry and a content-descriptor registry are different, but neither the word “registry” nor lookup establishes cryptographic content binding.

For this thesis, F5 remains an RGB image-domain writer optimized through the SD1.5 encoder; this is not the proposal's latent insertion. The strongest defensible research question is whether that explicitly declared observation model yields a useful natural-photo fidelity/verification tradeoff under independent attacks, after accounting for record side information. SD2.1 is an unseen diffusion checkpoint, not evidence of an independent encoder family; the separate Q-VAE memo handles SDXL's encoder-training evidence.

Do not pool bit accuracy, binary TPR, nearest-key identification and content verification. WOUAF's headline “attribution accuracy” is per-bit agreement; its database false-assignment test is a separate experiment. Existing-photo quality and paired generation quality are likewise distinct. No present dossier reading establishes a new positive thesis result. C1's [GROW](../C1-posthoc/cards/Luo2026GROW.md) and [DiffMark](../C1-posthoc/cards/NguyenLe2026DiffMark.md) remain mandatory neighboring precedents, counted in C1.

Parent addition: RAIN v2 is now V2 (Li2026RAIN), adding a prompt-free learned extraction boundary. Its reported two-GPU extractor latency excludes VAE encoding and GS decoding; it does not satisfy the proposal's image-DCT hypothesis.
