**Direct evidence.** Watermarks and fingerprints support matching transformed assets to remotely retained manifests. Soft bindings cannot substitute for hard cryptographic bindings. Resolution can use a value, manifest identifier or asset; located assertions must match the discovery algorithms and values according to algorithm-specific rules. Recovered manifests can have expected hard-binding validation failures after asset transformation. Side information includes manifest repositories, algorithm definitions, certificates/trust infrastructure and signed assertions. The format specifies algorithm identifiers, values, parameters and scope; no fixed watermark payload or numerical false-match guarantee is prescribed.

**Inference/relevance.** Pointer-based recovery is standard architecture, not a thesis contribution. A thesis registry is not automatically C2PA compliant. Signed-manifest authenticity, image similarity and legal ownership are different conclusions. No dataset, attack count or experimental performance applies to this standard.
