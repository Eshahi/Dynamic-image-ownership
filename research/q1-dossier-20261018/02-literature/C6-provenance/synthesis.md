# C6 synthesis: architecture is established; empirical attribution remains the question

Access date: 2026-10-18. Six unique V2 sources: four empirical/theoretical research papers, one normative specification, and one technical guidance report. These are **not six experimental papers**. One additional Adobe documentation card stays V1 under the dossier's strict rule. All decisive 2025–2026 papers were read in their current arXiv versions. Verification level V2 must not be confused with arXiv v2.

**Novelty verdict.** Reject “first watermark pointer plus registry,” “first watermark plus continuous descriptors,” and “first two-factor watermark/fingerprint verification” as contribution headlines. [DREW](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02836v2) (`saberi2024drew`) routes retrieval using watermarks; [Fairoze et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.04901v2) (`fairoze2025publicwatermark`) explicitly allow deferred payload storage; [SynthID-Image](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.09263v1) (`gowal2025synthidimage`) describes conjunctive payload/embedding verification. These are direct prior-art observations, not extrapolations from abstracts.

The thesis's potential contribution is narrower: an openly specified image-domain VAE-amplified carrier evaluated as an owner-and-record pointer under a frozen regeneration protocol, with explicit trusted-registry information and negative attribution results. This is an **agent inference about possible positioning**, not a novelty proof. C1 must decide carrier novelty; C3/C4 must decide security and registration antecedents. C6 alone does not establish that a full attribution game is new. DREW already includes diffusion purification, so “first hybrid tested under regeneration” is also unsafe. Its transformations and retrieval task cannot be equated numerically with the thesis's DDIM owner-attribution protocol.

**The newest close benchmark changes the reviewer baseline.** [Kazempourradi et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04151v1) (`kazempourradi2026softbindings`) is a strong comparison anchor for hybrid failure modes and near-copy specificity. However, its complementary-success union is explicitly not a measured deployed cascade; its watermark track is not a regeneration study. Cite the paper precisely rather than presenting it as either a complete scoop or irrelevant. Its negative geometric-verification result rules out promising that adding geometry will automatically solve the thesis's look-alike failures. That is a research hypothesis requiring later testing.

**Security and standards boundary.** [C2PA 2.4](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/specs/ContentCredentials.html) (`c2pa24contentcredentials`) separates soft recovery from hard binding. The thesis's trusted registry is detector side information. Correct record selection does not establish legal ownership, signer authority, originality, enrollment chronology, or universal authenticity. Nor does it inherit Fairoze's conditional security theorem: similarity and collision resistance under adaptive attack are materially different properties. An attacker-enrolled competing record and an attacker who optimizes pixels against the verifier are different games.

**What the manuscript should do now.** Describe candidate discovery, selected-record verification and final attribution as separate operations. State what happens for missing, ambiguous, conflicting or overwritten registry entries, and distinguish measured behavior from proposed handling. Keep the frozen E2 limitation and the exact E3 completeness caveat supplied by WP1; do not use literature to repair those outcomes. All thesis numbers must be imported from WP1 EV identifiers, so none are repeated here. [NIST AI 100-4](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-4.pdf) (`chandra2024nist1004`) supports distinguishing robustness, adversarial security, quality and operating-point evidence, without supplying a publication threshold.

**Future comparative evidence, not run here.** A common-protocol comparison should include a DREW-style cluster/retrieval arm, a direct watermark-plus-record conjunction, fingerprint-only matching and the thesis rule, with matched registries and attribution endpoints. It must preserve near-duplicate negatives and report abstention/coverage, not just conditional accuracy. This is a candidate experimental requirement for WP6; it is not authorization to change thresholds or execute anything in this session.
