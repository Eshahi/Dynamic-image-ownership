# cameron2015cluster — V2

Accessed 2026-10-18. Verification denotes primary method and evaluation/proof inspection, not reproduction.

**Metadata.** A. Colin Cameron and Douglas L. Miller. *A Practitioner's Guide to Cluster-Robust Inference*. Journal of Human Resources 50(2):317–372/373 (2015; final endpoint pagination to resolve in bibliography audit); DOI [10.3368/jhr.50.2.317](https://doi.org/10.3368/jhr.50.2.317). [Author index](https://faculty.econ.ucdavis.edu/faculty/cameron/research/papers.html), [February 2015 final author PDF](https://faculty.econ.ucdavis.edu/faculty/cameron/research/Cameron_Miller_JHR_2015_February.pdf). Locators: §§II, IV–VI and VIII.

**Method/evaluation.** Explains within-cluster dependence, multiway clustering and inference with few clusters. Empirical examples use CPS individual and state-year data; the cross-sectional example contains 65,685 individuals. Cluster asymptotics depend on the number of clusters, not merely rows.

**Side information/payload/threat.** Not a watermark design. The relevant object is the dependence structure of evaluation units.

**Limit/relevance.** Regression sandwich formulas are not automatically a valid rare-event confidence bound for this registry. They explain why pair reuse and a shared record bank need explicit modeling. Sixteen shots from one film support a narrow descriptive statement; a cluster-aware formula cannot manufacture a diverse population or repair missing comparisons.
