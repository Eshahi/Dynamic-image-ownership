# brown2001binomial — V2

Accessed 2026-10-18. Verification denotes primary method and evaluation/proof inspection, not reproduction.

**Metadata.** Lawrence D. Brown, T. Tony Cai and Anirban DasGupta. *Interval Estimation for a Binomial Proportion*. Statistical Science 16(2):101–117 (2001); DOI [10.1214/ss/1009213286](https://doi.org/10.1214/ss/1009213286). [Full published article mirrored at Portland State](https://web.pdx.edu/~ihd/stat563.S17/brown.pdf). Locators: §§2–4, particularly §4.2.1, and §5. Publisher endpoint did not expose text; the mirror is the primary article, not a secondary summary. Pages 117–133 in some repositories include discussion/rejoinder.

**Method/evaluation.** Compares binomial interval coverage and length analytically and numerically. Wald intervals can have poor coverage. Clopper–Pearson inverts binomial tails, guarantees at least nominal coverage under that model, and can be conservative.

**Side information/payload/threat.** Not a watermark design; these fields are not applicable. The input is a binomial success count with its sampling assumptions.

**Limit/relevance.** Exact arithmetic is not exact inference when trials are dependent or selected. The thesis retains its prespecified one-sided Clopper–Pearson reporting and separately states cluster, missingness and multiplicity assumptions. This paper supplies no license to treat millions of shared-image comparisons as millions of independent trials.
