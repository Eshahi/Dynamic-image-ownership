# C9 evaluation norms

The target journal literature uses several incompatible endpoints. FaceSigns evaluates selective fragility and ROC AUC; Dual Defense jointly evaluates disruption and watermark recovery. Neither inspected preprint calibrates the thesis's wrong-record attribution game. Their journal versions are bibliographically verified but not fully refreshed; do not present their preprint protocols as exact2024 final-paper requirements.

Across these inspected protocols and the recent journal zero-watermarking papers in C4, the useful norm is an explicit data/attack/metric contract, not a universal minimum image count. A300-source positive confirmation can estimate a roughly90% recovery rate with useful uncertainty. It cannot establish a one-in-a-million false-attribution probability, especially when millions of pairs share those300 sources. Sixteen shots from one film provide a narrow specificity check, regardless of the number of neighboring frame pairs.

A suitable article should report: image-level true attribution at frozen thresholds; bit recovery only as a diagnostic; all failed/unavailable attempts; source/shot dependence; quality of clean marked images separately from attack output; side information for every arm; matched settings for direct comparisons; and end-to-end extraction/lookup time separately from cached matrix operations. Regeneration attacks need named checkpoint, autoencoder, scheduler, strength and seed, with input/output definition. Current cross-paper tables must be qualitative because their payloads, resolutions, training and operating points differ.

Recent post-hoc SpecGuard and SimuFreeMark add attack breadth and latent-feature baselines, but high average bit performance is not calibrated attribution. The theory and benchmark clusters supply stronger false-positive discipline than merely copying a journal table. C1/C2 also show that regeneration and repeated purification are expected comparisons for a regeneration claim, while C3/C6 require active forgery and false-binding analysis for a security claim.

For the current evidence, an honest empirical boundary paper is more credible than a new-state-of-the-art carrier paper. Journal access gaps are tracked explicitly and must be resolved before a detailed numerical prior-art comparison. No new experiment, model download, or scoring was performed.
