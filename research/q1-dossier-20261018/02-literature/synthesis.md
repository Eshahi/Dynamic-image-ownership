## 1. Broad architectural novelty is already pre-empted

The system cannot credibly claim to introduce watermark identifiers, retrieval-assisted watermark verification, watermark–fingerprint hybrids, trusted feature registration, or content binding. StegaStamp encodes identifiers; Roy and Chang's retrieval-assisted watermarking predates current diffusion systems; DREW couples a watermark with retrieval; SynthID-Image explicitly describes checking both recovered payload and stored image embeddings. TrustMark's published version discusses verifying a retrieved fingerprint or thumbnail against the query. C2PA treats watermarks and fingerprints as soft bindings to externally stored provenance. The October 2026 soft-binding study examines their complementary strengths, false bindings and second-stage verification. These are direct antecedents, not peripheral related work.

Zero-watermarking is especially important: registered content features can support later identification without modifying the original image. Rel-Zero extends this line to generative edits through stable patch relationships. Adding a carried pointer changes the observation and may reduce competing matches, but does not create the general idea of registration-based verification. The precise question is whether that pointer provides useful disambiguation at an explicitly measured cost in recovery and missingness, for a declared adversary and candidate bank.

Relevant V2 cards: Roy/Chang in C4; `saberi2024drew`, `gowal2025synthidimage`, `kazempourradi2026softbindings`, `c2pa24contentcredentials` in C6; TrustMark in C1 and its final-version refresh. Unavailable recent zero-watermarking journal manuscripts prevent a comprehensive priority claim even where their abstracts appear less directly relevant.

## 2. VAE-aware existing-photo marking is a crowded method space

RoSteALS, ZoDiac, FreqMark, VINE, APT and PhaseMark already make different uses of latent representations, generative priors, differentiable encoders or regeneration-aware training. SimuFreeMark is particularly close to the original latent-insertion proposal: it processes the low-frequency part of an existing image through a frozen VAE, inserts a learned latent residual and reads the message with an image-domain extractor. SpecGuard adds a spectral regeneration-oriented comparator. F5 therefore needs an exact algorithmic comparison, including where optimization occurs and which quantities its objective amplifies. “Uses a VAE,” “robust to diffusion,” and “no inversion at extraction” are insufficient contribution statements.

Generation-time methods have different privileges. Tree-Ring, Gaussian Shading, PRC, SEAL, RingID and ROBIN often control initial noise, sampling or model parameters. WIND's existing-image route introduces substantial reconstruction distortion. AquaLoRA and Stable Signature modify a generator's decoding path; RAIN and SERUM use learned extraction. Their strengths matter for related work, but an arbitrary clean photograph cannot be treated as if its generation process were controllable. A methods table must distinguish generation-time control, post-hoc optimization, model-specific learned extraction and stored-record access.

F5's retained evidence supports a measured implementation under fixed conditions, not a new family or a lightweight latent extractor. Its writer operates on the image through a VAE encoder; the proposal's DCT extraction obligation remains open. The fixed selection of F5 over E3 on an unseen diffusion checkpoint establishes a development choice, not statistical equivalence or an independent-autoencoder result.

## 3. Regeneration robustness does not establish attribution security

Zhao et al.'s removal analysis is conditional on its smoothing and distortion assumptions. It is not a theorem that every practical mark must fail at a stated PSNR. WAVES, W-Bench and CtrlRegen nevertheless show why evaluation needs quality-controlled regeneration, several model families and transparent attack settings. UnMarker supplies a distinct adaptive removal threat. Rinsing or controlled regeneration can preserve useful semantics while changing watermark-bearing details.

The local SD1.5 and SD2.1 VAE file hashes differ, but a serialization or decoder difference cannot establish different encoder tensors. Primary SDXL lineage documentation identifies SD2.x as decoder-fine-tuned from the SD1.x family; see `Q-VAE.md`. Accordingly, the retained transfer result is “unseen diffusion checkpoint.” Independent-encoder robustness remains unmeasured. SDXL's independently trained VAE is a justified future test candidate, subject to a later model-version and resource check; it was not run here.

Security also requires the attacker's capabilities. Craver's ownership ambiguity and Kutter's copy attacks precede diffusion. Modern black-box, single-image and one-shot watermark forgery demonstrate that a positive detector result can be transplanted or induced. MetaSeal signs content-dependent messages but does not make a signature automatically bind to every possible visual instance; its regeneration example fails verification. SEAL and SemBind add semantic dependence with different generation-time access and residual limitations.

Passive regenerate-and-enroll, active re-marking under another identity, residual transplantation and chosen-query forgery are distinct games. The retained registry E3 examines the first under its frozen enrollment procedure. Zero observed competing attributions there cannot be advertised as security against the other three. Nor does an earlier timestamp prove original creation or legal ownership.

## 4. The hard part is defining and measuring instance identity

SSCD targets copies of the same two-dimensional source and explicitly separates that task from nearby video frames. DINOv2 and CLIP representations capture semantic or structural relations that may merge distinct images. ICDiff evaluates graded diffusion replication, whereas fixed-FPR instance verification demands a declared binary endpoint and genuinely independent negative sources. NeuralHash and black-box hash attacks show that learned similarity can be adversarially manipulated; the practical-hashing counterevidence also cautions against saying every perceptual hash is cheaply broken under all budgets.

The frozen D1+B1 rule uses a native fine-detail vector and a centered 32×32 gray-layout vector; neither is CLIP or DINO. Its 18.126 dB operational cutoff is a calibration-derived eligibility rule, not a semantic or legal definition. The three held-out accepts below that cutoff are negative evidence for the record check. Closely related frames share a shot, so 45 pairs do not supply 45 independent scene contexts. The registry's success on millions of correlated unrelated comparisons cannot outweigh this hard-negative failure.

Local geometric verification is a plausible proposal, not an established cure. DELG already combines global retrieval with local evidence, and the recent soft-binding benchmark did not obtain universal improvements from a geometric second stage at matched false-binding rates. A future verifier must preserve regenerated positives while rejecting same-shot negatives on fresh calibration and evaluation pools. The dossier should propose one discriminating test, not a catalogue of descriptor variants.

## 5. Evaluation norms and claim boundaries

Recent journal examples vary widely: some report bit error or normalized correlation, others TPR at a fixed FPR, and dataset size can hide dependence among views, frames or repeated attacks. A journal's acceptance of a weak metric is not a reason to adopt it. Our strongest reporting choice is source-level recovery, attempt-level availability, ambiguity as its own outcome, shot-level hard-negative uncertainty, and query-bank false-attribution events. Bit accuracy, message recovery, correct record routing and correct attribution must be separate columns.

For zero events, one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson bounds are approximately 3/n only under the declared independent Bernoulli unit. Reusing the same 300 images across millions of pairs does not create millions of independent queries. Multiple cells and absent records further constrain claims. Fuzzy extractors and secure sketches provide formal tools under entropy and distance assumptions; they do not supply the missing empirical distribution or certify a 32-bit perceptual pointer.

The recommended direction is a bounded empirical study of registry-assisted attribution under regeneration and competing enrollment, with F5 as the carrier and the E2 failure as a central result. A stronger method-paper claim requires fresh matched baselines, an independent encoder, active forgery and adequate hard-negative diversity. Broad ownership, universal regeneration robustness, first hybrid architecture and cryptographic security claims are unsupported. No experiment, model loading, image inspection or threshold change was performed in this literature session.
