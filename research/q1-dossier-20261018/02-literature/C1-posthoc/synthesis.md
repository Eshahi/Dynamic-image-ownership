# C1 synthesis: post-hoc watermarking and the encoder-gradient novelty boundary

Date: 2026-10-18. Research only; no methods were run, scored or compared locally. Sixteen unique works have version-specific V2 cards: fourteen current primary inspections of the latest identified paper/venue version, TrustMark's current inspection of its older arXiv v1 with final-version limitations, and one explicitly inherited StegaStamp inspection. V2 denotes method plus experiment reading, not reproducibility or a positive judgment.

The closest prior work forms several distinct neighborhoods. [VINE](cards/Lu2025VINE.md) is the strongest directly relevant learned post-hoc editing benchmark: it embeds in existing images using a generative prior and evaluates regeneration and global/local editing. [FreqMark](cards/Guo2024FreqMark.md), [ZoDiac](cards/Zhang2024ZoDiac.md), [PhaseMark](cards/Lee2026PhaseMark.md) and [APT](cards/Ha2026APT.md) establish different forms of post-hoc latent or feature-aware marking. FreqMark optimizes latent frequencies, ZoDiac optimizes a diffusion reconstruction with a ring, PhaseMark modifies latent phases without optimization, and APT aligns features to secret anchors for localization. These are sufficiently close that “VAE-aware,” “post-hoc,” “semantic features,” “frequency carrier” or “regeneration robust” cannot alone define novelty.

The specific F5 question remains narrower: optimizing an image-domain variable through a frozen VAE encoder to retain a decodable carrier, followed by a trusted continuous D1+B1 record check. [PhotoGuard](cards/Salman2023PhotoGuard.md) already demonstrates image-gradient steering of diffusion encoders, but aims to disrupt editing rather than preserve recoverable ownership bits. Thus encoder sensitivity is a precedent, not the proposed contribution. A defensible novelty discussion must distinguish the optimization variable, objective, decoder observations and verification decision. This reading does not establish that their exact combination is unprecedented.

A roughly 32-bit pointer is also not independently novel. [StegaStamp](cards/Tancik2020StegaStamp.md) encodes external hyperlinks; [RoSteALS](cards/Bui2023RoSteALS.md) explicitly motivates exact provenance-database lookup; [TrustMark](cards/Bui2023TrustMark.md) connects public identifiers to provenance. The potential contribution lies in a precisely scoped verification rule and its failure boundary under regeneration, copy-paste and look-alikes. A trusted enrolled descriptor is additional detector side information. It must be named in every comparison and cannot be equated with reference-free watermark extraction or cryptographic ownership.

Generation-time methods require a separate comparison category. [GROW](cards/Luo2026GROW.md) and [DiffMark](cards/NguyenLe2026DiffMark.md) modify diffusion sampling, rather than preserving a supplied photograph. Both already avoid iterative diffusion inversion at extraction, but require learned/model-based components. Consequently, “inversion-free” does not mean lightweight DCT-only, and their generation-quality metrics are not marked-photo distortion metrics. RAIN and MetaSeal are routed to the generation-time/semantic-boundary clusters; this C1 package makes no independently verified claims about them.

[WAM](cards/Sander2025WAM.md) separates local messages and detects marked areas, which is relevant to copied content. Its appendix also shows degradation under stronger regeneration. Localization, bit accuracy, whole-message recovery, TPR at a fixed FPR and correct record attribution answer different questions. [InvisMark](cards/Xu2024InvisMark.md) supplies a small regeneration study; [Pixel Seal](cards/Soucek2025PixelSeal.md) supplies recent high-capacity fidelity evidence but no established T3 result in the inspected sections. No cross-paper numeric ranking is defensible from these heterogeneous protocols.

The highest-priority scholarly gaps are a matched existing-photo comparison with VINE and representative latent post-hoc methods; an explicit adaptive mark-transfer/re-marking threat; and separation of checkpoint transfer from genuinely independent-encoder transfer. Those are future study questions, not authorization to execute experiments or reopen closed baseline work. SD2.1 checkpoint novelty must not be called independent-encoder evidence without checking the encoder lineage. The current thesis record's whole-registry confirmation remains failed because of E2; this literature review neither repairs nor reinterprets that outcome.

Coverage limitations: TrustMark's 2025 final experimental section still needs version-specific refresh; several 2026 works are preprints/workshop papers, not established main-conference results. The October 2026 soft-binding benchmark and September 2026 local-watermark benchmark were routed to other clusters as leads, not counted as C1 V2. The matrix and cards preserve these distinctions.


Additional direct antecedent: SimuFreeMark (arXiv2511.11295v1) inserts a residual payload into a frozen VAE representation of low-frequency photo content and decodes without diffusion inversion. It invalidates generic existing-photo latent-insertion novelty. SpecGuard (ICCV2025/arXiv2510.07302v1) adds spectral-feature post-hoc marking and regeneration/rinsing evaluation. Their average bit/performance reports are not matched wrong-record attribution operating points.


Final-version refresh: use Bui2025TrustMark (ICCV2025), now fully read in access-refresh. Its Section 4.5, p.18636, explicitly proposes checking a retrieved fingerprint or thumbnail against the watermarked query. Pointer-plus-record content verification is therefore direct prior art. The old Bui2023TrustMark card remains a historical-version record, not another unique source. NAWE adds an analytical carrier with neural host suppression; it does not establish regeneration security.
