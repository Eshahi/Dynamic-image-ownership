# C9 journal evaluation refresh

Inspected on 2026-10-18 PDT (2026-10-19 UTC). This bounded refresh adds three V2 method/evaluation reads in TMM, TIFS and TCSVT. V2 describes inspection depth, not final-version identity or methodological endorsement. These papers are a convenience sample found through the logged searches, not a representative survey or a universal acceptance threshold. No quartile claim is made here.

[MarkPlugger](cards/zhang2025markplugger.md) has the strongest explicit preprint-to-journal link: its revised arXiv record names TMM2025 and the journal DOI. [MantleMark](cards/luo2026mantlemark.md) has matching university-hosted manuscript and author-affiliated journal metadata. [Reversible Unlearnable Examples](cards/wang2026reversible.md) has matching authors/abstract/mechanism across the author upload and publisher/institutional metadata, but an explicit arXiv journal DOI is absent and its title differs by “Toward/Towards.” Treat its manuscript numbers as version-qualified. None was checked byte-for-byte against a publisher PDF.

The main implication is metric discipline. Payload resemblance, character recovery and BER answer recovery questions. A record-attribution claim additionally needs a frozen decision rule and negatives appropriate to that claim. The presence of BER-only journal papers does not make BER sufficient for owner attribution. Likewise, an either-of-two-extractors rule needs calibration for the combined decision. A known mark or pose-conditioned template must be listed as detector side information.

Dataset volume must be decomposed. MarkPlugger distinguishes a training corpus, inference pairs and an external corpus. MantleMark distinguishes source scenes from hundreds of rendered views and repeats. Reversible reports validation use and classifier repetitions, but the inspected text does not provide a complete independent watermark split accounting. The useful reporting template is: independent source count, generated derivatives per source, calibration negatives, held-out negatives, split construction, and duplicate/near-duplicate exclusions. Confidence intervals should follow the independent source unit. Repeated renderings cannot substitute for independent scenes.

These examples support reporting matched baselines, external transfer, quality trade-offs, attack parameters, ablations, hardware and resource costs. They also expose comparison pitfalls worth avoiding: fixed versus variable payloads; unavailable code; different image-size feasibility; component FLOPs versus complete runtime; and per-image versus whole-scene time. Public repositories were linked by two manuscripts, but no repository contents, license, executable state or data release were audited in this research-only pass.

For the dossier, the recommended future evidence table should put source-level attribution at fixed FPR beside quality, payload recovery, and complete verification runtime, with side information and attacker access in adjacent columns. Evaluate the thesis's named threats directly; conventional JPEG/noise tests in other papers do not fill a missing regeneration, transfer or look-alike attribution test. This is a reporting and future-design recommendation only: no experiment was designed for execution or run here.

Do not use this refresh to declare that any sample size is inherently journal-ready. Sufficiency follows from the target endpoint, independence, error rate and uncertainty, not a journal logo. No thesis performance number is repeated here because the evidence ledger remains the authority.
