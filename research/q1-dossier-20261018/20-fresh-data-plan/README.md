## Proposed units and grouping

Use separate identifiers for dataset record, original work, distributed byte variant, creator/production, video or photo session, shot/capture group, and pair. A **pair** is two specified observations for an endpoint; it is not an independent source, creator, or production. Multiple pairs or frames from one shot cannot increase the number of independent source groups. Ordinary photos can share a creator/session even without a video.

The conceptual hierarchy is creator/production dependency component → original video/photo session → shot/capture group → frame/file variants → pair observations. The true data can be a graph: co-creators, compilations, reused footage, reuploads and derivatives can join apparently separate branches. Build conservative connected components from supported shared-creator, shared-production, shared-original and duplicate/derivative links. Split these components together. Record a source/capture group separately for the estimand; the larger component is the split and clustering guard, not a claim that all its contents depict one event.

Resolve creator aliases only from attributable profile/source evidence. Same display name alone neither merges people nor proves difference. A uploader account, distribution platform or dataset publisher does not establish the original creator. For compilations record all attributable component origins; otherwise quarantine. Unknown or conflicting creator identity remains quarantined from the confirmatory cohort rather than being assigned a new apparently independent creator ID. Unknown shot boundaries are not silently treated as distinct shots; conservatively keep the whole video/session together. More conservative merging reduces effective diversity and must remain visible.

**Bounded learning of the grouping rule:** a future metadata-only pilot may reveal provider fields, aliases and derivative relationships. Log those discoveries and freeze a single source-evidence-driven grouping policy before confirmation allocation. No detector scores, learned descriptor similarities, owner/payload outputs, or marking success may inform grouping. Later verified links may merge components, never split one for favorable results; cross-split links trigger the breach procedure below. This is provenance resolution, not adaptive learning from held-out outcomes.

3. Compare only **already supplied metadata or existing historical hashes**, recording algorithm, encoding, byte variant and coverage. Open Images' base64 MD5 and Commons' SHA-1 cannot be directly compared. Absent comparable hashes means unknown, not clean. ## Proposed allocation and deterministic selection contract

Before any future acquisition/evaluation, record the target population, precision objective, source families, quotas/caps, ordinary-photo versus temporal-pair roles, metadata snapshot revision, exclusion-register revision, grouping-policy version and outcome-independent eligibility rules. Fix quality eligibility before method outputs: permitted media type, verified real-camera provenance where required, documented native dimensions/duration/frame rate, and a later blinded content-integrity rubric. Numeric cutoffs are left for that protocol; this document does not choose them. Metadata-absent dimensions are unresolved, not inferred from file size. Markability, detector success, descriptor distance, favorable look-alike failure or regeneration quality must never become source eligibility filters. Subsequent operational failures belong in all-attempt accounting.

After provenance/overlap resolution, assign whole dependency components to development, confirmation and a separately reserved replacement order. A future protocol must fix the split proportions/counts, seed, canonical encoding and tie rule before ranking. Proposed ranking is a cryptographic hash of the committed seed plus canonical component ID with a domain tag; sort digest ascending, then canonical ID for ties. Apply predeclared source strata and creator caps in that order. This is an allocation design only: no seed, ranked asset list, split assignment, acquisition list or run file is created here.

Within an assigned component, deterministically rank eligible source groups and use a predeclared temporal-offset or still-sequence rule to propose pairs. Freeze offsets, timestamp/frame interpretation, direction, boundary handling, allowed pair reuse and pair count before viewing method outcomes. Native timing differences and shared frames remain recorded dependencies. Do not choose pairs because they fool the method or look easier to reject. Ordinary photographs and temporal negatives have different populations; pooling requires an explicit estimand and weights rather than a larger convenient denominator.

Freeze selected membership and reference/query roles before any outcome. Use replacements only for predeclared administrative ineligibility discovered before evaluation and retain the rejected candidate/reason in screening counts. Unavailable files or later experimental failures remain in all-attempt denominators; no successful-output replacement. A later discovery of license/provenance error can require withdrawal, with transparent missingness rather than silent substitution. A grouping/overlap breach after outcomes invalidates the affected confirmatory interpretation and requires separate analysis or a new untouched cohort; it cannot be repaired by reassigning successful observations.

## Identity labels and frozen historical labels

The prior gray-PSNR operational definition and its retained labels/results stay frozen. It is a historical endpoint, not ground truth for perceptual identity. A new label policy is prospective and separate: provenance-supported exact copy, derivative of same capture, distinct temporal capture in the same shot, distinct shot/capture, or uncertain. Distinct capture and same underlying scene are compatible; the policy must state which notion of identity the target claim uses. Do not collapse this relationship taxonomy to a desired positive/negative label without a declared mapping.

Any later human annotation needs a written rubric, independence from method/owner outputs, at least an explicitly specified disagreement/adjudication procedure, and project authorization as appropriate. Keep individual labels, uncertainty, adjudication and missingness; do not invent human judgments. If historical gray-PSNR is reported alongside new labels, use separate columns/endpoints and report disagreements. No relabeling the old E2 result. Curator familiarity with sources is logged; confirmation labelers and outcome analysts should be blinded as the eventual protocol specifies.
