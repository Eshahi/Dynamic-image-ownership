### F1. Matched external control for the empirical attribution claim — submission-critical

**Hypothesis:** the joint pattern of recovery, false binding, competing-owner ambiguity and abstention contains an informative difference from an established fingerprint/hybrid control, rather than only a consequence of this system's weak descriptor or threshold.

**Design:** propose F5+registry, the identical frozen descriptor-only arm, an SSCD reference arm, and one externally documented hybrid (DREW preferred if its implementation/profile can be vetted). This is a small comparison, not a new baseline sweep. Use the same 300 fresh photographs, same source groups, two fixed regeneration checkpoints at .2 with three seeds, and the F2 hard negatives; retain clean/unmarked/wrong-owner controls. Match input information, registry size, payload/candidate semantics where meaningful, quality budget and calibration data. If a method cannot be matched, document that difference rather than normalize away its cost. Thresholds come only from separate development data; do not calibrate to held-out zero errors. Report record acceptance, any-beta acceptance, ambiguity and abstention, with confidence intervals on appropriate groups.

**Mandatory component controls, refined in R7:** EV-77 shows beta owner detection already fails for every completed retained E3 query. Preserve native owner detection O, native read flag R, decoded-code validity V, record-dependent distance test Q and frozen descriptor rule F as distinct quantities. The six controls are owner-only (owner outcomes only), F, OF, ORVF, ORVQ and Full=ORVQF. The added ORVF control requires readable valid code but does not match it against the record. It separates read availability from code-distance discrimination. V exposes an existing input-validity requirement; it does not change the frozen full rule. Record correctness is undefined for owner-only output. Keep all accepted records, multiple records per owner, same-owner wrong-record outcomes and passive/active enrollment separate. These are future decision-rule controls, not writer/descriptor variants or newly executed arms. The [component contract](23-component-study-contract.md) gives exact semantics, including invalid outputs and missingness.

**External comparison contract, before implementation:** DREW's native Algorithms 1–2 use cluster routing, thresholded top-1 retrieval and a whole-database retrieval fallback; they do not implement this dossier's all-accepted-owner rule. Its enrollment also marks each registered item, whereas retained E3 enrolls an unmarked-by-beta derived copy. The later protocol must report two clearly labeled questions: native DREW behavior under its own supported enrollment/decision rule, and an explicitly adapted candidate-set analysis only if meaningful and faithfully implementable. Do not call the adaptation published DREW performance, silently suppress its fallback, replace top-1 output by all matches, or force a beta re-marked enrollment into the passive setting. Unsupported configurations are not applicable. Compare task-compatible controls on common operational success/error/abstention metrics, and retain decision-rule/access differences in the table. Source: inspected DREW v2, §3.3 Algorithms 1–2 and §4, `saberi2024drew`.

**Falsifiable question and outcome map:** does record-code distance selection add useful discrimination while retaining recovery, for a declared bank/population with multiple records per owner? The primary local contrast is Full versus ORVF: only Q is removed. Full versus OF removes both reading prerequisites and code matching and is a composite contrast, not q-specific evidence. ORVF versus OF examines readable-code availability (with validity failures disclosed); Full versus ORVQ examines F. These are paired decision-rule comparisons on common outputs and the same bank. Nested acceptance sets mechanically reduce both any-wrong-record acceptance and inclusion of the correct record; a reduction in errors alone does not establish useful discrimination. Report exclusive correct attribution, co-acceptance, ambiguity, all-attempt recovery and missing-outcome bounds as well. R10 replaces the earlier provisional five-point loss allowance for the selected local study: require at least .05 qualified same-owner episode resolution, at most .02 any-seed correct-record-loss incidence and at least .85 delivered majority recovery, all with simultaneous bounds in both checkpoints. The generic external-comparison outcome map below does not enlarge this finite-frame claim.

- **Supports an added record-code contribution:** the predeclared paired interval clears the useful discrimination margin while recovery meets its noninferiority margin, with the pattern replicated in task-compatible external or independent-channel evidence. Only the tested population/access is claimed.
- **Refutes the proposed useful increment:** the interval rules out the predefined useful discrimination improvement, or recovery loss exceeds the tolerated margin. Report a bounded negative component result; do not continue describing q as an established useful binding contribution. A negative article needs adequately precise and externally relevant replication, not merely a nonsignificant comparison.
- **Unresolved:** the interval includes both worthwhile gain and no useful gain, cohort clustering leaves inadequate precision, baseline semantics are incompatible, or unavailable banks prevent the chosen estimand. Do not relabel this “stable” or submission-ready. Refine scope or propose a new separately approved study, without tuning the completed data.

Creator/production is the highest source sampling level; camera groups and pairs are nested observations. ### F2. Fresh, diverse hard negatives and complete-bank confirmation — submission-critical

**Hypothesis:** the frozen D1+B1 specificity failure and pointer/descriptor distinction persist beyond one film; the complete-bank version of E3 has the claimed operating point.

**Design:** freeze the existing method unchanged; acquire approximately 100 independent source groups across at least 20 creators/productions, with three temporal pairs per group, and 300 fresh ordinary photographs. Keep a distinct development pool for logistics only; no tuning on this confirmation. Evaluate both the descriptor-only negative endpoint and the full pointer-plus-record endpoint, passive enrollment with complete banks, and accepted-owner-set outcomes. Record every attempted source and missing comparison. Use both original operational labels and prospective blinded identity labels if approved; disagreements remain visible. Primary claim is source-group risk, with creator/video dependence explicitly modeled or bounded, not a count of independent pairs.

**Success:** all planned comparisons are accounted for and the prospectively selected specificity/recovery contrast yields one of F1's adequately precise supporting or refuting patterns at the declared creator/source hierarchy. Merely obtaining another failure or a similar point estimate is not sufficient replication. **Kill:** evidence is driven by a few creators, labels cannot be adjudicated consistently, or missingness prevents complete-bank interpretation; restrict the population/claim and do not fill in outcomes.

**Precision:** even zero events in 100 iid source groups yields an approximately 2.95% one-sided 95% upper bound. About 299 independent zero-event units are required for a 1% bound, about 2,995 for .1%; correlated frames cannot supply those units. If a tighter claim is essential, use F5 before collecting outcomes.

**Data/cost:** Commons/V3C2 candidate groups, Open Images ordinary photos; 3–10 researcher-days for licensing/curation; laptop 12–36 h or rented 12–36 h (**USD 10.68–32.04**). Shared F1 artifacts must not be counted twice. **Unlocks:** external-population and complete-bank evidence; never legal ownership or universal specificity.

**Design:** SDXL is the first candidate because the inspected SDXL paper states its autoencoder was trained from scratch. Do not retune F5. SD3/FLUX are optional later replications, not required to initiate an expensive sweep.

**Success:** source-majority recovery lower bound clears the prospectively chosen floor (the existing .85 is a candidate) while quality and false-binding criteria remain met. **Kill:** recovery collapses at comparable image fidelity, memory/runtime makes the scoped study infeasible, or the encoder is not independent; restrict the claim accordingly.
