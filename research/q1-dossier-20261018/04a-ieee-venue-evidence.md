## Rules shared by SPS journals

**G1 — TIFS/TIP/TMM author rules.** [Current SPS author instructions](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/information-authors): two-column, single-spaced PDF, 10-point font, one-inch minimum margins. Initial/revised limits: 13/16 pages, TMM 10/14. References count. Initial appendices/proofs count; revised ones are supplementary. Review: single-anonymized, minimum two independent reviewers. Supplements: six-page recommendation, more needs EiC approval; TMM four-page maximum. Abstract: 150–250 words. Code/data supplements are encouraged. Insufficient experiments, inadequate novelty and routine combinations risk desk rejection.
**F1 — 2026 charges.** [IEEE 2026 APC list, updated 31 March 2026](https://magazines.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/10/IEEE-Article-Processing-Charges-List.pdf), pp. 2, 6, 7 and 9: all four titles are hybrid, with optional OA APC **USD 2,800**; regular no-overlength thresholds are TIFS/TIP 10 pages, TMM 8, TCSVT 11. Overlength charges are TIFS/TIP/TMM USD 220/page; TCSVT USD 125/page. [IEEE Open 2026 pricing](https://open.ieee.org/for-authors/article-processing-charges/) says price depends on submission date and applicable local taxes are additional. Society/council members may get 20%, IEEE-only members 5%, with student exclusions; discounts cannot combine or reduce overlength fees. Institutional agreements may cover eligible APCs. No payment was authorized or initiated.

**F2 — optional OA versus traditional route.** The [SPS author rules](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/information-authors) and [TCSVT submission rules, section F](https://ieee-cas.org/tcsvt-submission-manuscript) allow traditional subscription publication without the OA APC. Mandatory overlength charges still apply to hybrid OA. SPS requests voluntary USD 110/page for the first ten standard pages; this is distinct from mandatory charges. [IEEE OA licensing](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/choose-a-publishing-agreement/open-access-rights-management/) describes CC BY and CC BY-NC-ND options. Do not assume that a country-based waiver covers a hybrid title: [IEEE's current fee explanation](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/your-role-in-article-production/about-potential-article-processing-charges/) says the low/lower-middle-income program does not apply to hybrid journals.

## Recommendation within this four-journal subset

TIFS has the strongest thematic fit, conditional on a sharper security question and adequate evidence; it is not submission-ready on this venue check. TMM and TCSVT are plausible backups requiring a substantive empirical contribution; their wider scope does not imply easier acceptance. TIP is a poor first target for the present bounded registry-assisted negative finding. Final cross-publisher ranking belongs in `04-venues.md` after combining the other fact sheets.
