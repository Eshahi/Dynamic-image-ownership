## New findings that prevent misclassification

1. **ConZWNet access metadata conflict.** Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex describe hybrid open access / CC BY-NC-ND and point only to the DOI. OpenAlex has no PDF URL and reports no repository full text. The public Elsevier API instead returns `openaccess=0`, `openaccessArticle=false`, and no article body. Neither a definitive closed-access verdict nor successful open full-text access is justified. Record this as an access discrepancy, not a literature result. See `metadata-evidence.json`.
2. **ConZWNet author spellings.** Publisher-deposited Crossref metadata and the author repository agree on Deyu Tong and Hongxin Han. Semantic Scholar's De-Yu Tong and Hongxing Han are not adopted as bibliographic corrections.
3. **Pseudo version distinction.** The 2021 chapter [DOI 10.1007/978-3-030-77424-0_36](https://eudl.eu/doi/10.1007/978-3-030-77424-0_36) has authors Le Danh Tai, Cao Thi Luyen and Ta Minh Thanh. It is not the 2022 journal article. EUDL requires a free account for its download; the chapter was not acquired, merged or substituted. No relationship beyond similar title/abstract is established here.
4. **ResearchGate label is insufficient.** The Pseudo author-profile label is contradicted by the actual article page's explicit publisher-preview presentation. Its first page verifies identity, not the remaining 17 pages.
