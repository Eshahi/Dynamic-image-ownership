Checked 18 October 2026 during WP9. This note was added after the WP9 review snapshot. The direct publisher/ranking-provider evidence in 04 remains the basis of the shortlist. These are older, agency-reproduced records. They do not establish 2025-metric/June-2026 quartiles, do not turn JISA into a Q1 venue, and do not substitute AIS for JIF. The [agency's database index](https://uefiscdi.gov.ro/scientometrie-baze-de-date) separately lists JCR 2025 as the June 2026 edition, confirming that the PDF above is not its latest metric year. No inference from the agency's national assessment rules to the user's institution is made.

The practical decision is unchanged: TIFS remains a conditional scientific target; TMM and TOMM are backups; the university's ranking system, accepted edition and category remain unanswered. Direct Clarivate/SCImago category verification for TIFS remains open despite this corroboration.
