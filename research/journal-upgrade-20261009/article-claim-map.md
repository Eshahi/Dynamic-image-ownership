## Honest paper boundaries and progression

Current E3 writes RGB pixels and extracts through VAE/CLIP; it is image-domain and not a lightweight DCT-only latent method. q is content-conditioned, h operational from a fresh bootstrap, and the detector does not receive the clean source or intended evaluator targets. Report these inputs, the alpha-only owner limitation and all construction/detection/model-loading cost. Semantic payload recovery is distinct from presence, content agreement, exact instance identity and authorization.

The original twelve sources are adaptive prior evidence; the completed 300-source M2 is frozen historical confirmation and excluded from all new tuning. The 24-source cohort is now consulted development data, not final confirmation. VAE SDXL controls are already seen and cannot supply unseen-model transfer. The fixed .4 T3 channel is exploratory and may be visually inadmissible; humans have not supplied judgments.

The bounded mechanism round is closed with H2 unused, as the [new decision memo](h2-article-viability-decision.md) explains. The [endpoint atlas](endpoint-interpretation-atlas.md) supports a conditional empirical argument, not a novel taxonomy. Next scientific progression requires adequate baseline fidelity, a meaningful external tradeoff or transferable failure, independent scope controls, and only then new frozen confirmation at its proper human milestone. The user made human ratings optional; technical work proceeds using explicitly constructed controls while human-perceived admissibility remains unestablished. More samples cannot create novelty.
