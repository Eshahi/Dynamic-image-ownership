## Honest paper boundaries and progression

Current E3 writes RGB pixels and extracts through VAE/CLIP; it is image-domain and not a lightweight DCT-only latent method. q is content-conditioned, h operational from a fresh bootstrap, and the detector does not receive the clean source or intended evaluator targets. Report these inputs, the alpha-only owner limitation and all construction/detection/model-loading cost. Semantic payload recovery is distinct from presence, content agreement, exact instance identity and authorization.

The original twelve sources are adaptive prior evidence; the completed 300-source M2 is frozen historical confirmation and excluded from all new tuning. The next 24 sources are fresh reserved development, not final confirmation. VAE SDXL controls are already seen and cannot supply unseen-model transfer. The fixed .4 T3 channel is exploratory and may be visually inadmissible; humans have not supplied judgments.

Progression is fresh simplification → at most two controlled mechanism hypotheses → small closest-baseline feasibility → independent domain/model transfer and binding positive/disagreement tests → matched external comparison and human admissibility → new frozen confirmation only at its proper human milestone. If simplification alone survives, retain it as engineering and reconsider article scope. If the mechanism is prior art without a meaningful controlled advantage, reject the methods headline. A failure/mechanism article is viable only if it adds reproducible knowledge beyond known distinctions; more samples cannot create novelty.
