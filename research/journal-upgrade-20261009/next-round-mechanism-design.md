## Why this question, and what is not established

H1 has exact q in 18/18 identities at both .2 and .4, checked q in 18/18 and 16/18, but exact h and F in 0/18. M2 likewise has strong q recovery and zero regenerated full acceptance. Moving q margins again attacks the wrong bottleneck. The missing diagnosis is whether h fails because its full-resolution carrier disappears, its recomputed descriptor changes, or both. Those mechanisms require different remedies.

The existing h is not merely low-frequency pHash. `revised_watermark_v4._perceptual_hash` combines coarse DCT structure with local gradient detail; the latter can receive three quarters of the feature energy. A perfectly transmitted old h can therefore still disagree with regenerated content. The stage below measures that obstruction instead of assuming it away.

RC1 demonstrates public construction and independent predicate effects on three quality-qualified pairs. Its failed all-six quality gate does not imply an authentication theorem or invalidate those three observations. Root's retained-pixel diagnosis indicates accumulated perturbation and an absent canonical quality selector, not an LPIPS implementation defect. Repairing that writer can improve experimental controls; it cannot explain away the fragile-instance bottleneck or create authorization evidence. This design does not rerun RC1 or claim a causal isolation of its quality loss.

## Conditional algorithm if the oracle gate passes

Call the experimental method **E3-DV1**. Keep the exact 32-bit q and operational 32-bit h targets, BCH(59,32,t=5), q semantics, instance descriptor, radius 6/far 10, public owner/profile, pinned VAE and CLIP. Do not split the existing 320 q chips into two 160-chip banks: that changes the successful q code's chips per symbol from 5/6 to 2/3 and confounds the question with a weakened reader. Nor add a high-frequency disjoint band and assume equal regeneration survival.

Instead retain every native q slot, its allocation to 320 chips, weights and normalization. Within chip k, write its normalized coefficient vector as a_k. Its supports are disjoint across k. Construct a deterministic vector r_k over the same slots using a new domain-separated public stream of (owner, profile, decoded q, `E3-DV1-instance-orthogonal`). Form b_k = normalize(r_k - a_k*(a_k dot r_k)). Reject a degenerate chip before science; use one deterministic fallback coordinate basis orthogonalized to a_k, selecting the first nondegenerate coordinate in native slot order. Record fallback count. This yields 320 instance projections with a_j dot b_k=0 for all j,k in exact arithmetic. Test the numerical Gram matrix before running images. The fallback is geometry-based, never image/outcome-based.

Normalize both sets in the *same coefficient coordinates used by native Layout*, including radius whitening in a_k; b_k is orthogonal to that actual weighted q vector, not merely to an unweighted sign pattern. Domain-separated h chip signs and the same 59 BCH segment boundaries encode h. q-conditioned layout routing preserves the dependency that an h signal belongs to the decoded q route. This is public domain separation, not a secret PRF or cryptographic authorization.

There are 640 linear projection constraints but still only 64 payload bits and 118 coded bits. At least two physical coefficients per native chip are required. No capacity is created by orthogonalization: remaining coefficient dimension is being used, at added perturbation cost. At fixed ideal projection amplitude, two orthogonal banks need approximately twice the latent residual energy (3 dB); nonlinear encoder gradients can cost more. Orthogonality predicts lack of direct *linear latent* interference, not independence after encoding, rounding or diffusion.

Construct from the **canonical RGB source once**, never by writing onto a fullmarked image. Joint objective is the equally weighted mean of the two existing squared chip-margin hinges, each with margin 4. Use one canonical-anchored RGB L2 budget at **43 dB**, 200 normalized-gradient steps, native learning-rate fractions .2 for first half and .05 for second half, clipping and rounding as native. The 3 dB change from the native first 46 dB bank is the fixed two-bank energy allowance, not a parameter search. Do not restore the old fragile carrier, add minimax refinement, or perform iterative rewrites. The inference reader still uses one VAE encode; it first decodes q, then constructs the q-conditioned h layout and decodes h. If q is not readable, h is unavailable, not supplied from an evaluator target.

Apply the existing fixed quality selector [1,.8] against canonical pixels, preserving failed outputs. Do not restore payloads after contraction. The saved RGB image must pass PSNR>35, SSIM>.9, LPIPS<.1, exact q/h, and both content matches. A quality or clean-payload failure is a method failure, not a reason to tune that image. This short writer is a capacity feasibility screen, not a claim that its cost matches full E3.

Keep native q decoding/threshold unchanged. For the new h bank, use bounded-distance decoding only, no recomputed-message fallback and no expected target in detection. Freeze a conservative score threshold `sqrt(2*log(2*2**64/alpha))`, where alpha is the unchanged profile target; the 64-bit multiplicity accounts for possible q-dependent layouts and 32-bit h messages. This is a score convention, **not an established FPR theorem**: orthogonalization produces dependent weighted directions, natural images are not isotropic, and an adaptive public writer violates random-null assumptions. Do not inherit native sign-bound claims or multiply q/h false-positive probabilities. Report scores and observed negatives explicitly. A stronger threshold can itself kill feasibility; do not lower it after results.

## Security, observability and novelty boundaries

A verifier observes suspect pixels, public owner/profile, VAE/CLIP and two recovered words. It sees neither private authorization nor an enrolled original. Therefore F_DV1 means **dual content consistency under a public mark**, never a source owner's signature. A public attacker can compute both current descriptors and run this same writer. Robust transport may increase persistence of those reissued marks. Cryptographic hashes of q/h do not fix the missing secret; a truncated signature does not fit a secure authorization claim in the present 64 bits. Signed enrollment references would change side information/task and are not added here.

The mechanism should survive only insofar as two low-frequency VAE projection banks survive the same channel that already preserves q. It does not assume stable full-resolution coefficients. Its distinction from the previous chip-versus-symbol ablation is allocation of a second independent content payload into previously unused latent directions; q's successful readout remains intact. Orthogonal spread spectrum and BCH are established tools. A local success is not sufficient for a novel paper; a failure still cleanly separates descriptor stability, transport capacity and quality cost. Human equivalence/admissibility remains unassessed.
