## Three conclusions that must remain separate

**Control-construction failure.** RC1 requires every RR image to pass strict quality against both the marked recipient and its canonical source, exact q/h delivery and combined acceptance. All six RR images deliver both targets, are nonidentical to their recipients and pass combined F. All pass PSNR/SSIM. Recipients 30, 436 and 459 fail canonical LPIPS; recipient 30 also fails LPIPS against the marked recipient (.113084, versus .258140 against canonical). Only three of six pass both envelopes. Thus RC1 fails the preregistered positive gate because its fixed reissue procedure did not construct the full required quality-qualified panel. No RR was rejected by the detector. Dropping failed sources or weakening an envelope cannot convert this result to the frozen gate's success.

**Demonstrated local component effect, with a failed study-level gate.** For pairs 73-89, 127-165 and 309-370, every RR/DR/RD/DD image passes both strict quality envelopes and exactly delivers its supplied q/h. All three DR images pass perceptual-only F and fail combined F through the semantic predicate while their own-instance predicate matches; semantic status is uncertain, mismatch and uncertain respectively. Their three RR counterparts are accepted. All three reciprocal RD images pass semantic-only F and fail combined F through own-instance mismatch. These are measured, paired, local component observations. Across the full fixed N=6 DR panel, perceptual-only F is 6/6 and combined F is 0/6, but only 3/6 qualify by quality; the same 3/6 quality coverage applies to every cell. The preregistered repeated-witness criterion remains false because it also required all six RR positives to pass quality. It would be wrong either to erase these three qualified observations or to claim the full study passed. The old M2 all-reject panels still show no added semantic-binding value on those particular constructions; they do not override RC1's local effect.

**Donor-source authentication.** Neither outcome identifies which source or person authorized a mark. The unchanged detector in [journal_bch_readout.py](../../scripts/journal_bch_readout.py) receives the suspect, public owner/profile and pinned models; its `detect_rgb` interface receives no authenticated enrollment record or source reference. It extracts q, routes the instance carrier through q, and compares recovered content quantities with those recomputed from the same suspect. For fixed suspect, owner and configuration, its output is invariant to an external claim that the donor was source A rather than source B: donor identity is not an input. Public reissuance also supplies no private authorization evidence. This is an interface-level identifiability limit, not a measured cryptographic break, proof that every recipient is forgeable, or claim that reference-free attribution is impossible for differently designed signed/keyed systems.

An extra grid cannot remove that information limit. Adding authenticated provenance, an enrollment reference or a signing authority would change the scientific system and task. It would require a separately motivated design rather than retrospective relabelling of E3.

## Why the narrower account is still conditional as an article

The already inspected primary literature occupies the broad claims: FreqMark uses pretrained-feature message alignment and latent-frequency optimization; APT studies alignment under regeneration for localization; SEAL supplies semantic-binding prior art. WAVES and the recent taxonomy already distinguish detector tasks and operating points. Thus observing a separation between payload recovery and acceptance is not itself a new taxonomy or mechanism. MetaSeal also illustrates why recovered-message authentication requires a separate suspect-content interpretation.

These statements use the retained primary-source records, not a fresh exhaustive novelty search: [FreqMark card](../literature/journal-upgrade-20261009/synthesis/papers/freqmark.json), [APT synthesis](../literature/apt-20261013/synthesis/findings.md), [SEAL card](../literature/journal-upgrade-20261009/synthesis/papers/seal.json), [taxonomy card](../literature/journal-upgrade-20261009/two-axis-taxonomy-card.md), and [MetaSeal card](../literature/journal-upgrade-20261009/metaseal-card.md). Their differing tasks do not prove E3 redundant or establish a numerical ranking.

The unresolved article question is whether a consequential effect survives a scientifically comparable external system or a distinct causal explanation. RC1 provides technical component controls and local observations, not an external novelty test. Its crossed-payload construction establishes that the two predicates can contribute separate rejections on actual saved images; it does not establish a new binding principle, human-valid donor semantics, source authentication or broader security. Even a perfect six-pair quality panel would not alone answer the article question. Repairing the three LPIPS misses therefore has lower decision value than finalizing the honest scope now.
