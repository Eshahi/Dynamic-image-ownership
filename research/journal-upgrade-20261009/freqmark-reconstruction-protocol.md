## The single frozen reconstruction

Published ingredients are 512-square images, frozen SD2.1 KL VAE and DINOv2-small, 48 coordinate directions in the first 128 features, Adam learning rate 2.0 for 400 steps in fp32, negative-PSNR weight .05 and LPIPS weight .25. Equation 10 sums clean, latent-noise and pixel-noise message hinges. Section 5.5/Figure 7 supplies latent/pixel noise settings .25/.06. Everything further specified here is an explicit reconstruction default.

1. **VAE and FFT.** Map RGB [0,1] to [-1,1], encode once using posterior mode, detach the raw four-channel latent. Do not apply the diffusion scaling factor. Apply `fft2` over the two spatial axes with `norm='backward'`. Optimize separate zero-initialized real and imaginary fp32 perturbation arrays of the full FFT shape; decode the real part of `ifft2(F + complex(a,b), norm='backward')`. This full-complex/real-output choice contains redundant directions; it is not an assertion about author Hermitian handling. Do not switch to orthonormal FFT, rFFT, real-latent optimization or a different learning rate after results. Decode raw latent directly, map to [0,1] and hard-clamp for the image supplied to loss/extraction.
2. **DINO.** Use the frozen model in evaluation mode and its final-LayerNorm CLS token, first 128 coordinates; directions are coordinate axes 0–47. No L2 normalization, whitening, feature centering beyond the model's own LayerNorm, or sign balancing. Use differentiable bicubic antialiased resize from 512 to 256, center crop 224, then official ImageNet input mean/std. Check the pinned processor config for agreement before execution; any disagreement is a documented prerequisite failure, not an automatic recipe change. Direct tensor and delivered-PNG decoding use the same tensor preprocessing. This is a declared approximation to official image-processing numerics and an unknown author choice.
3. **Objective.** Let `h(x)=mean(relu(1 - message * CLS(x)[:48]))`. Thus **mu=1**, a declared positive margin on raw LayerNorm-coordinate scale, not a recovered author hyperparameter. Use `h(clean) + h(latent_noisy) + h(pixel_noisy) - .05*PSNR(clean,source) + .25*LPIPS(clean,source)`. PSNR is `-10*log10(max(mean((clean-source)^2),1e-12))` on [0,1]. LPIPS is the local frozen AlexNet implementation with inputs mapped to [-1,1]. Adam betas (.9,.999), epsilon 1e-8, no weight decay, no gradient clipping, batch one, all network parameters frozen, gradients retained through their inputs.
4. **Noise routing.** Each step first draws iid Gaussian latent noise with SD .25 in **raw VAE units**, decodes the perturbed latent and clamps its image to [0,1]. Then draw independent pixel Gaussian noise SD .06 in **[0,1] units**, add to the clamped clean decoded image and clamp again. Draw once per branch per step, with fresh draws next step. Only the clean branch receives quality losses. Implement Equation 10 literally. Omit rotation/crop and the ambiguous equal-probability appendix routing; label this omission, which means this is not the complete appendix-augmentation recipe. Their spatial-robustness ablation is outside the present T3-focused feasibility question.
5. **Output.** Finish at exactly update 400; no best-quality/bit-accuracy checkpoint selection, contraction, residual blend, early success stopping or source-dependent budget. Quantize the final clamped tensor by `floor(255*x+.5)` to 8-bit PNG and reload before scoring. Intermediate steps 0, 1, 10, 100 and 400 are diagnostics only. No recipe sensitivity branches are scheduled: unresolved margin/FFT/preprocessing/routing interactions make a tiny branch tournament uninterpretable.

## Interpretation limits

Raw DINO coordinate signs are not established balanced independent random bits on natural images. No whitening calibration is smuggled into this implementation. If a query message is independently uniform, its Hamming agreement with any fixed decoded string has a binomial law over **message randomization**; that fact does not establish a fixed-owner natural-image FPR, robustness, or safety under owner/template search. With two photos, publish no FPR, population interval, security or superiority claim and import no theoretical tail probability as an observed detector result.

These 48 arbitrary bits and E3's 32 content-conditioned q are different tasks and capacities. Do not truncate FreqMark, feed it E3 q, compare 48-bit exact success to checked q as equivalent, or call payload presence content authentication. This detector receives DINO/preprocessing/directions and, for a match score, the candidate message; it needs no clean image, VAE or inversion at detection. It supplies no certificate or binding rule for T4/T5.

The main unresolved fidelity issues are author code/checkpoint identity, numeric mu, feature processing, FFT optimizer coordinates, raw/scaled noise units and appendix routing. A failure of the chosen defaults leaves those live explanations. A positive test only warrants designing a separately budgeted fair development comparison. An automatic failed-baseline score or E3-win row would merely add a weak comparator and is prohibited. Source outcomes, whether favorable or unfavorable, do not change H1/H2 or consume their mechanism count.
