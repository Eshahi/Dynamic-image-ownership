## Why VAE success did not guarantee DDIM survival

The inspected objective applies a score hinge toward 9 and per-bit gap hinges toward 1 to clean and selected VAE round trips. RC2 optimizes clean plus SDXL; RC3 adds the SD1.5 VAE round trip. Worst-branch updates never include stochastic latent preparation, added diffusion noise, denoising steps or post-regeneration semantic binding. Exact success through either deterministic VAE is therefore a surrogate result, not a constraint on the DDIM path used by T3.

Writing x plus a small delta changes a regenerated output locally by approximately the attack Jacobian times delta. A VAE-loss gradient need not align with a DDIM bit-margin gradient. No bound on that Jacobian or on the four vulnerable bit margins has been measured. Consequently the additional 52 dB perturbation bound constrains input distortion but supplies no certified lower bound on post-DDIM margins. The single-bit regressions and near-boundary 6012 failures are consistent with this mismatch. They do not prove the approximation, prove a robustness impossibility, or show that RC3 must fail.
