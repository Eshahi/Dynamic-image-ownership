The existing M2 result is competitive **against v5 in its measured setting**, especially at .2/.4, and has promising photographic fidelity. It is currently unranked against external methods. The minimum useful next comparison uses the same source images, fixed seeds/attacks, separate calibration at the same nominal FPR, matched distortion, explicit payload and detector side information, and source-level paired uncertainty. Show original method settings alongside the common constrained operating point.

Further literature already retained in the project includes initial-noise methods and forgery studies. They remain relevant background, but this focused comparison does not reproduce them or certify an exhaustive novelty search. Recheck the literature before submission; a preprint's existence and reported metrics do not establish peer-reviewed validity.

## Local evidence added on October 10

A newer search also inspected RAIN v1 (September 2026), a learned one-step Gaussian-Shading extractor. Its reported speed excludes VAE encoding and uses two GPUs; its tables do not measure our matched regeneration/binding threats. It is relevant prior art for a separately labelled neural comparator, not validation of image-DCT extraction. [Primary-source card and precise limits](../literature/journal-upgrade-20261009/rain-v1-card.md).

The author-reported table above remains a literature comparison. We subsequently reproduced the official WAM SA-1B MIT checkpoint, retaining its native w=2 and predeclared w=.5 settings. This checkpoint differs from the paper's COCO checkpoint. Both arms satisfy the strict clean quality limits on 12/12 sources; native mean clean PSNR is 38.322 dB, SSIM .9772, LPIPS .0290. Native exact clean message recovery is 12/12 and attenuated recovery 10/12. [Clean/VAE reproduction](wam-results.md).

These are complete planned-denominator counts: unavailable outputs remain unsuccessful attempts. All 216 WAM records are retained, including 15 safety blocks; no operational failures or missing identities occurred. WAM's nonempty-mask bit accuracies are .8844 and .7776 for the native arm, with 30 and 26 readable outputs respectively. Thus high average bit recovery can coexist with poor exact-message recovery; neither metric is a substitute for the other. [Matched T3 results and paired availability](wam-t3/wam-t3-results.md).

This supports a narrow observed payload-recovery advantage for frozen F5 on this development grid. It does not rank whole systems: WAM messages, F5 content-derived q/h, false-positive operating points and authentication predicates differ; no calibrated WAM image-presence rule was fitted. Twelve reused sources are not an independent journal confirmation, and native WAM mask-positive C0 outputs are not automatically ownership false positives. Human admissibility is unmeasured. FreqMark/ZoDiac feasibility gaps and matched-FPR comparison remain open; D2's separate VAE prerequisite failure is preserved in [its results](native-writer-results.md).

Two additional primary-source checks constrain interpretation of the new readout study: [ECCV 2024 decoder smoothing](../literature/journal-upgrade-20261009/certified-smoothing-card.md) is prior art for observation aggregation, and the [October 2026 taxonomy](../literature/journal-upgrade-20261009/two-axis-taxonomy-card.md) separates verification, identification and conditional retention. V1 is neither a new majority-voting principle nor a certified smoothing method.

## Current E3 result and what it changes

The later [supplied-payload E3 assessment](e3-m1a-assessment.md) improves exact semantic q recovery to 36/36 at strength .1 and 35/36 at .2 on the same planned twelve-source DDIM panel. Its one blocked C1 remains in the planned denominator. All twelve selected clean marks satisfy strict quality, with minimum PSNR 40.526 dB, minimum SSIM .9564 and maximum LPIPS .07616. The fixed matched semantic-presence comparison is 29/29 and 28/28 versus v5 27/29 and 18/28; these fixed subsets are distinct from the full planned exact-message panel.

This makes E3 a stronger development candidate than the earlier F5/WAM exact-message rows, but it does not supply the missing matched-FPR external ranking. E3 uses a 32-bit content-dependent semantic target protected by BCH(59,32), an operational instance target, and a writer optimized on the same SD1.5 strengths/seeds reported here. WAM's learned native 32-bit message and its training/access assumptions differ. Coding, writing, readout and selection changed together, so this comparison does not isolate a causal coding or latent-carrier benefit. The SDXL result is VAE reconstruction, not full independent-backbone diffusion.

Public-vector attacks deliver both E3 targets in 20/20 transfer attempts, with zero combined acceptance; perceptual-only also rejects all twenty. Thus delivery has become a stronger diagnostic, while the thesis's incremental two-signature advantage remains unsupported. T5 geometry remains a geometry measurement, not a reference-specific attribution test. All these results are exploratory and reuse twelve sources adaptively.

The historical cached target pairs are not a deployable source-to-mark algorithm. The separately frozen E3-S1 fresh supplier has passed its two-source screen and is undergoing full twelve-source qualification. Its own new pixels, total supply-plus-write cost and failures must be reported before an end-to-end result can replace the supplied-payload entry. A journal article still needs independently reserved data, unseen transformation evidence, matched operating points, scope-appropriate attribution endpoints and a defensible contribution beyond these development counts.


Two inspected security references further bound the contribution. [MetaSeal v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.10766v2) signs recovered caption messages with ECDSA P-256 and embeds a caption/signature QR representation; its illustrated regeneration failure is one example, not a comparable success-rate grid. Authenticating a recovered message still requires a defined binding check to support a claim about the suspect image ([evidence card](../literature/journal-upgrade-20261009/metaseal-card.md)). [LDPC-PRC cryptanalysis v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17310v2) studies weaknesses in particular public code parameters and access assumptions; it neither attacks our BCH construction directly nor grants BCH cryptographic security ([evidence card](../literature/journal-upgrade-20261009/ldpc-prc-cryptanalysis-card.md)). These sources motivate precise ownership and access claims, not additional out-of-scope attack experiments.
