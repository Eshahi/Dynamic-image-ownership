## Decision

F5 r2 is a credible starting point for a paper, but the evidence does not yet support a competitive broad robustness/authentication claim. Its strongest result is high-fidelity marking of existing photographs with improved semantic survival over the retained v5 system on the historical SD1.5 grid. Its main weaknesses are limited regeneration generalization, small-sample binding evidence without component benefit, public payload construction, and absence of a matched external baseline. Address those before scaling experiments or polishing a claim of novelty.

The proposed paper question is: **At a fixed false-positive rate and strict photographic fidelity, can VAE-guided marking improve checked semantic survival across regeneration models, while content verification demonstrably rejects delivered forgeries?** If binding fails, narrow the paper to robustness and the measured authentication limitation. If independent-model survival fails, report the family-specific effect and its ceiling. A rigorous negative or narrower paper remains a valid outcome.

Q1 describes a journal's rank within a category/year, not an acceptance threshold for an individual experiment. No attack percentage guarantees publication. Select the ranking system required by the institution and verify the current category/year at submission; this plan does not certify a current quartile. TIFS is a scope candidate for a strong forensic/security contribution, subject to a later venue-fit decision. [Clarivate JCR guidance](https://clarivate.libguides.com/jcr/basics); [official TIFS scope](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/ieee-transactions-information-forensics-and-security).

## Thresholds and attack ranges for the next study

Separate detector calibration, attack intensity, admissibility and acceptance. A strength of .4 is an img2img parameter, not a 40% successful attack. The following values are prospective proposals, chosen before the next results; preserve all historical M2 settings.

| Item | Proposed primary setting | Secondary/sensitivity |
|---|---|---|
| Detector operating point | Nominal FPR .001 (0.1%), threshold selected using calibration negatives only | .01 and .0001, clearly separated; rare-event uncertainty reported |
| Clean marked quality | PSNR >35 dB, SSIM >.9, LPIPS <.1 on every evaluated image | Continuous distributions, worst cases and failure counts |
| T3 regeneration | VAE-only; strengths .05/.1/.2/.3/.4/.5/.6; seeds 0-2 | SD1.5 continuity, another backbone sharing a VAE, and independently trained VAE/backbone; controlled regeneration after the cheap tests |
| T3 admissibility | LPIPS <=.30 versus marked input **and** blinded human majority preserving entities, actions and scene identity | LPIPS <=.20; report PSNR/SSIM and independent semantic similarity continuously |
| T4 transfer | Patch area 1/64, 1/16, 1/4; alpha .25/.5/1; two fixed positions; K=18 | No-transfer sham and separately labelled residual-transfer diagnostic |
| T4 admissibility | LPIPS <=.20 versus recipient and blinded majority preserving recipient identity | .30 sensitivity; report actual donor delivery before binding |
| T5 collision | One independently selected semantic-nearest distinct instance, K=1 | K=10/100 search budgets, dissimilar controls, same/different owner |
| Attacker access | One claimed owner R=1, no detector feedback Q=0; fixed candidate budget K | R=10/100 roster and feedback-enabled attacks require separate endpoints/calibration |

Use three raters with blinded order for human admissibility. No missing human response counts as a pass. Human judgments remain pending until actually collected. The LPIPS caps are explicit engineering choices, not validated perceptual truths; report coverage and sensitivity rather than treating them as universal semantic thresholds. Do not use the detector's own CLIP to determine semantic eligibility. Equal strength across pipelines is not equal severity: pin checkpoints, effective timesteps, sampler, guidance, prompt policy and resolution, and compare performance versus achieved distortion. The fixed-FPR and quality-performance approach follows [WAVES v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08573v2#S3), restricted here to the three thesis threats.

For every attack report admissibility coverage, joint admissible attack success over **all** attempts, and success conditional on admissibility. T3 success means loss of the declared checked-semantic endpoint; T4 success means false donor attribution; T5 success requires the precisely defined false-instance or false-owner claim. Also report all-source survival and paired comparisons on the common admissible subset. Retain all failed seeds, rejected outputs and denominators.

Report random-seed average and per-source any-attempt attack success separately. With K=3 seeds a source-majority endpoint is not a best-of-three defense. Across seven strengths K=21 per model for an any-attempt summary. For T4/T5, binomial units are independent disjoint donor/recipient or target/candidate clusters, with success defined as any allowed attempt. Reusing one target across many donors does not increase the independent sample size. Full details are in the [threshold design review](threshold-design-review.md).

## Scientific targets and sample-size decisions

| Claim | Proposed future evidence requirement |
|---|---|
| Reliable clean marking | Strict quality on every observed image; clean full-match lower one-sided 95% bound >=.95 |
| Low false-positive detection | Each declared primary negative cell has simultaneous one-sided upper bound <=.001 |
| Cross-model regeneration improvement | Checked source-majority point estimates >=90% at .2 and >=70% at .4; >=10 percentage-point improvement over the matched pixel ablation, with adjusted paired lower difference bound >0 |
| Effective content binding | At least 100 independent **delivered** pairs; >=10-point false-acceptance reduction versus disabled binding on identical signals, with adjusted lower difference bound >0 |
| Low T4/T5 attack success | Simultaneous upper bound <=1% for each of three prespecified attack cells at fixed K |

The T3 90%/70% targets are point-estimate milestones, not claims that the population rate exceeds them with 95% confidence. The 100-pair binding experiment tests a mechanism; it does not certify a rare error rate. No gate compensates for another through a weighted score.

For zero events in n independent units, an individual upper 95% bound is `1 - .05^(1/n)`. A below-1% bound needs 299 zero-error units for one cell, or 408 per cell for three simultaneous Bonferroni-adjusted cells. A below-.1% bound needs 2,995 or 4,093 respectively. Plan 10,000 calibration negatives and 5,000 fresh confirmation groups per target population, applying all three negative conditions. Zero/5,000 suffices for the three-cell .1% bound; any errors require the actual exact bound. Calibration at nominal .001 does not guarantee that confidence bound: choose a conservative development operating point, then report the confirmation result without retuning.

Plan at least 300 independent positive sources per declared domain for future confirmation, with paired power analysis from development effects before freezing the final count. Expand the multiplicity family or narrow the claims if adding domains/endpoints. Use exact paired tests/source-cluster intervals and Holm adjustment for a small prespecified family of primary comparisons. Secondary grids remain descriptive. Sample-size arithmetic is not a substitute for power analysis, diverse source sampling or independence.

The current suspect-image-plus-OwnerID detector has no claimed enrollment reference. Another legitimately marked image belonging to the same owner is a true owner positive, not a false attribution. Either define an enrollment-aware instance endpoint or retain the narrower self-consistency claim. Public OwnerID-derived patterns do not provide cryptographic unforgeability. Signing a record would authenticate the record, not automatically bind its pixels after regeneration; capacity and noisy verification need a separate derivation before implementation.
2. **Act on the completed cheap tests.** The fixed twelve-source SDXL-VAE screen produced F5 checked recovery 10/12 versus v5 12/12, missing the prospective 11/12 screen. Prepare a bounded readable-code robustness amendment before a broad independent-diffusion grid; do not relax the frozen thresholds to rescue the result. Expand that mechanism study using independently selected pairs and component controls. One accepted public recipient-payload construction preserves the authorization limitation. The six-configuration development round (R1-R4 plus RC2/RC3) has now reached matched SD1.5 continuity. Preserve the preliminary successes and failures without changing thresholds. 3. **Isolate the improvement and fix quality tails.** Match carrier ablations on payload, views, matcher, optimization and observed distortion. Compare semantic-only, perceptual-only, combined, and binding-disabled verification without confusing re-embedding with detector ablation. Diagnose quality failures on development data, without inspecting M2 failure images for tuning. Use constrained embedding or explicit abstention; abstentions count as coverage failures, not hidden exclusions. Replicate the selected method on a separately reserved development panel.
4. **Establish external competitiveness.** Reproduce pinned FreqMark, Watermark Anything and ZoDiac baselines where free code/checkpoints allow it; assess implementation/license/dependency feasibility first. Show matched and native quality frontiers, calibrated FPR, T3 curves and relevant T4/T5 endpoints. Compare full detector and embedding time on the same hardware, batch size and warm/cold policy, including CLIP/VAE/inversion, peak memory, training cost and payload. If a baseline cannot be reproduced, report the limitation and make no state-of-the-art ranking from its paper numbers. Add CtrlRegen only if usable vetted assets and resources exist.
5. **Freeze and evaluate fresh data.** Only after development survives, preregister exact models, thresholds, primary contrasts, source-selection rule, seeds, power, negative populations and human protocol. Reserve fresh development/calibration/confirmation sources by IDs and hashes, checking all existing reservations without viewing held-out images. Previously observed M2 data remain historical evidence for the amended method. Prepare the required single independent review and existing-runner rehearsals, then the user's confirmatory review. No new runner or manifest is built by this planning task.
6. **Write the article around the evidence.** Deliver quality/coverage and low-FPR curves, matched ablations, independent-model results, delivered-forgery diagnostics and complete failure accounting. Present the scope decision explicitly. If the proposed mechanism fails, write the measured ceiling and negative result instead of claiming general robustness, legal ownership, or cryptographic security. Align unprotected thesis chapters afterward; proposal and protected claim/source records remain untouched.

Suggested bounded search policy: at most six distinct candidate configurations in a development round, including rejected/crashed candidates; replicate no more than two survivors on the independent development panel. Set GPU/wall-time/VRAM/token caps from the first complete local grid before starting the round. Document a new hypothesis and budget before any additional round. This limits adaptive overfitting without pretending that six trials supplies a statistical correction. Keep all evidence and report the Pareto frontier. The [independent discovery review](discovery-review.md) adds two design constraints.
