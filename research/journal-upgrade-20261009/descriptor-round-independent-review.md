## Non-blocking warnings

1. The terminal FG128 result is a real failure of its frozen engineering guard, but a single symmetric collision does not establish that 128 bits are intrinsically inadequate. Likewise, even zero matches would not validate a population tail rate. Preserve both qualifications in any article.
2. The phrase “capacity observation” must continue to describe a combined intervention. Increased bit count, Gaussian instead of Rademacher projections, fine-only representation, canonical targets and changed tolerance do not isolate a capacity effect. A factorial causal claim would need different evidence.
3. The pre-existing `revised_watermark_v4._sign_projections` docstring states the angle/pi identity for Rademacher projections. That statement is not generally exact. The new protocols explicitly correct this limitation, and no new result relies on the inaccurate docstring; it is therefore not a blocker in this scoped diff. Do not promote it into theoretical justification.

These are minor receipt-label limitations, not missing scientific outcomes. Recorded durations, commits and retained commands suffice for this exploratory scope. ## Scientific allocation decision

**Close the fixed route now. I do not recommend another local image experiment on the evidence presented.** The old descriptor is demonstrably the wrong content check for a transport-only repair on the retained pixels. CD1 trades away the protected texture distinction. BI1 does not resolve the tradeoff. FG128 shows that useful fine information survives, but its chosen finite representation fails the real-source guard before a costlier carrier is justified. Implementing the carrier anyway would test a candidate already disqualified under its prospective decision rule.

This recommendation is an allocation judgment, not an impossibility theorem. A different sketch seed, lowered radius, more bits, selected hyperplanes or a fitted blend would be a rescue of an observed failure on the same consulted panel; none currently supplies an independently supported new mechanism. Enlarging the panel alone would measure this failed candidate more extensively without resolving its declared defect. The attractive continuous cosine separation keeps representation research scientifically open, but it does not by itself nominate a distinct descriptor, affordable transport mechanism and falsifier whose result would warrant further expenditure now.

The scientifically useful deliverable from this round is the bounded separation of writer accumulation, payload transport, descriptor stability and discrimination. Preserve the successful positive observations alongside every failed conjunction. Retain E3 and its original M1a/M2 qualifications. CD1, BI1 and FG128 are related descriptor interventions, not three independently established failed design families, and this review must not trigger the original honest-negative exit by counting names. Further reopening should be driven by a concrete mechanism with new evidence or a consequential transferable comparison, not by the need to obtain a higher headline number.
