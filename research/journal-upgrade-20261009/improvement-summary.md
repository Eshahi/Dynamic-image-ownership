## Decision

Retain frozen F5 r2 as the comparator. None of the six declared configurations advances: R1–R4 fail clean exact-payload eligibility, and the separately versioned RC2/RC3 writer-decoder combinations fail matched diffusion continuity. Do not spend the remaining allowance on undeclared variants or downstream transfer/synthesis tests for failed candidates. Preserve the successful VAE repair as a measured intermediate finding.

The separate [method diagnosis](rc-regeneration-decision.md) explains why the next research question is an actual-DDIM-aware writer objective. That is a new prospective experiment requiring a fixed design and a cheap feasibility test, not a rescue of this completed round. Six related configurations are not three substantially different design families; this is not the project's overall honest-negative exit.

## Revised development plan

1. **Keep the verification claim precise.** Suspect image plus public OwnerID supports a bounded consistency claim. The inherited all-alpha T5 geometry panel has no distinct-owner false-attribution denominator; another legitimate image from the same owner is not a false owner attribution. Define an enrollment-aware instance endpoint separately if that claim is required. Public construction prevents a cryptographic unforgeability claim for the current method.
2. **Derive one actual-DDIM-aware writer experiment.** Fix its objective, stochastic latent preparation, quantization handling, training channels/seeds, global update schedule and local cost cap before generating marks. Start with the smallest gradient/memory feasibility check. Then use the unchanged twelve-source .1/.2 grid as the kill test, retaining all earlier identity obligations and clean/VAE requirements. Disclose transformation and seed reuse as development, not validation. No new candidate is activated by this roadmap.
3. **Require a survivor before expanding attacks.** Only an eligible candidate gets matched T4 delivery controls, explicitly interpretable T5 tests, and fixed-budget public donor synthesis. Preserve failed deliveries and separate readable delivery from rejection. Compare binding components on the same extracted signal. The present six-configuration round has no survivor and no such new downstream result.
4. **Establish external and model generalization evidence.** Keep WAM's checkpoint and native operating point explicit. Add reproducible FreqMark/ZoDiac references as feasible, calibrate each method independently, and compare matched FPR and achieved distortion. SDXL-VAE is now a seen development channel; generalization requires an untouched backbone/transformation and separately reserved images. No full independent-backbone diffusion result is available yet.
5. **Freeze, confirm, then write.** After a scientifically defensible development survivor, use fresh calibration/confirmation sources, power calculations, a blinded human admissibility protocol and the existing approved-run process. The article should report measured gains and ceilings, quality tails, coverage, cost and authorization limits. The protected proposal and claim/source records remain unchanged.

The numerical ranges and prospective acceptance targets remain in [plan.md](plan.md): nominal FPR .001; clean PSNR >35, SSIM >.9 and LPIPS <.1; T3 strengths .05–.6; source-majority checked-survival point targets .90/.70 at .2/.4; and simultaneous T4/T5 upper error bounds <=.01 for properly defined independent attack cells. These are research design choices, not publication guarantees. Twelve reused images cannot certify them.

The [autoresearch assessment](autoresearch-assessment.md) remains applicable: borrow a bounded hypothesis/commit/measure/log loop and frozen evaluation, with multiple hard scientific constraints. No language-model stack or extra orchestration infrastructure is needed. The local GPU completed this entire round; no additional GPU is currently required. A future actual-DDIM backward pass needs a measured feasibility check before requesting hardware.
