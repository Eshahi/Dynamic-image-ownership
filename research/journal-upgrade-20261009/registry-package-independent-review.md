Therefore the reported registry 0/13 versus fingerprint 4/13 at `registry-verification-results.md:15` and `:422` establishes rejection under that tested gate, not successful registry descriptor discrimination when a valid same-owner pointer reaches a confusing near record. Preserve the counts, but explicitly state this qualification whenever treating the comparison as added watermark value. The independent competing-enrollment scenario is the stronger bounded pointer-attribution evidence. No new experiment is needed to report the current failure.

**W2 — Encoder-only VAE comparison remains unresolved.** `unseen-regenerator-protocol.md:5` promises full-state and encoder-tensor comparison. `scripts/registry_step1_transfer.py:239-246` and the saved `sd21_vae_comparison` provide only one full-state fp16 equality Boolean. Unequal full states do not establish unequal encoders, much less independent ancestry. The result's caveat about ancestry is appropriate. Mark encoder sharing as unresolved or supply a small read-only encoder-subset comparison separately; do not upgrade the claim to wholly independent representation transfer. This does not change the fixed pipeline-level selection or failed registry gate.

**W3 — Descriptor-container availability is not D1 searchability.** `registry-verification-results.md:521` reports 78-80 descriptor-bearing records, counting available descriptor containers. D1 searchability is actually 74-76/80 in the preceding per-descriptor scenario table. That table correctly retains abstentions and unknowns. Label the closing sentence as container availability to prevent it being read as the primary D1 searchable registry size; no recalculation is needed.

**W4 — Retain the stated inferential and reproducibility limits.** The post-calibration amendment, 819 blocked pairs, one-production near footage, fixed development cohorts, missing human ratings, partial timings and arbitrary-owner limitation remain warnings, not hidden exclusions. Source-clustered bounds are explicitly hypothetical exchangeability calculations, not demonstrated population guarantees.
