## Decision and falsifiable scope

Freeze the alpha-only **S1 supplier plus E3 image-domain writer** and its source-blind BCH/RC1 detector. Confirm on the remaining admitted COCO300, against a freshly constructed native v5 r3 alpha comparator. The primary question is whether source-level semantic detection under the pinned SD1.5 regeneration channel is higher for S1 at both strengths .1 and .2 on new sources and evaluation seeds 3, 4 and 5. These seeds are absent from the writer's fixed 0/1/2 objective. Strengths, scheduler, model and image domain are shared with development; this is source and noise-seed generalization, not independent-model or unseen-attack generalization.

The package also measures legitimate-mark coverage, strict quality, checked/exact payload survival, finite negative-call behavior, residual/public copy transfer and source-selected same-topic transfer. It does not promise that all of these will succeed. No post-result tuning, supplier replacement, owner extension, extra attack, threshold calibration or sample enlargement is allowed. Negative confirmation remains reportable.

This is a deliberately narrow confirmation, not the journal roadmap's nominal .001 FPR, .4 robustness or simultaneous 1% attack-error target. It cannot establish the proposal's latent method, owner-generic security, reference-specific same-owner attribution, cryptographic authorization, combined-binding advantage or broad journal readiness.

## Endpoints, uncertainty and decision rules

For a combined call define S=semantic.found AND semantic.content_match; C=semantic.read AND semantic.content_match; Q=semantic.read AND decoded_q equals frozen intended q; J=Q AND semantic.content_match; H=instance.read AND decoded_h equals its frozen target; D=Q AND H; F=outcome both_match; A=semantic.found OR instance.found. E3 h is explicitly operational. Missing/unreadable words make Q/H false with unknown bit-error count, never zero bit errors. Recomputed-only S is separately visible. Freeze these definitions and source aggregation in the analysis module.

**Primary paired efficacy.** For each method/source/strength let Y=1 only if its clean mark is admitted with strict quality, exact Q/H and F, and at least two of the three scheduled T3 alpha calls have S. Otherwise Y=0, including failed/missing construction or attack. Compute the paired source risk difference mean(Y_E3-Y_v5) at .1 and .2. No successful-source or jointly available intersection replaces N. Report both per-source paired 2x2 tables, per-seed S cells and attack/mark availability. The prospective superiority claim requires positive differences and one-sided exact McNemar/sign-test p<.05 at **both** strengths. This is an intersection-union claim: rejection at only one strength does not establish the conjunction. Do not select a strength after results. Individual unqualified superiority claims require Holm correction across the two tests.

Report paired source bootstrap 95% intervals for each difference (100,000 complete-source resamples, NumPy PCG64 seed61010), labelled approximate; bootstrap intervals are descriptive and do not replace the exact test. Also report exact binomial 95% intervals for each method's source success proportion under an explicitly hypothetical exchangeable-source model. They quantify a sampling model, not a guaranteed population parameter for this fixed cohort. Dependence across seeds, owners or strengths is retained within each resampled source. Do not pool the six attacks as independent observations. Checked C, exact J, D/F, raw-bit errors and their paired source-majority results are secondary; no confirmatory combined-binding or matched-FPR claim follows.

**Legitimate-mark coverage.** Report N, successful supplier, returned final pixels, admitted marks, each clean metric pass, joint strict quality, exact Q/H/F and all joint intersections, by method. List measured failures. The finite statement “strict quality on all fresh sources” is permitted only at N/N; do not weaken it after a miss. Report source proportion intervals and minimum/maximum/median metrics with absent rows explicit. A statistically positive conditional attack result cannot conceal inadequate clean coverage; the primary Y already penalizes it. Universal, owner-generic or population-wide quality is not inferred even at N/N.

**Negatives.** Keep each clean/T3 owner/arm/strength cell and availability separate. In addition report per-source any-observed-A across the fixed negative query family, separately for clean and T3. For each family report observed positives, completely evaluated source units and sources with unavailable calls; bracket the planned-source error rate by positives/N and (positives+unresolved)/N. A source with any observed positive is already adverse, not double-counted unresolved. Never turn safety blocks into observed positives or observed negatives. A clean all-zero fully available panel at N=300 has a one-sided95% exact upper bound about .00994 for an exchangeable source-level any-query event, not .001. Thousands of repeated owner/seed calls cannot improve this independent-unit bound. The .001 claim would require roughly2,995 independent zero-event units even before additional assumptions/multiplicity; this design intentionally does not pursue it.

**Transfers and binding.** For each T4 condition and T5 stratum separately report A/F over all planned pairs, observed F plus unavailable outcome bounds, exact donor Q/H/D delivery, and F/rejection conditional on observed D. Zero delivered pairs means undefined conditional binding. Distortion is measured against recipient C0 for all saved outputs; predeclare LPIPS<=.2 and <=.3 descriptive proxy subsets, without changing the primary all-attempt denominator or asserting human admissibility. Report retained same-call semantic-only/perceptual-only/combined predicates and their discordant counts; native-mode routing controls are not a pure binding ablation. No combined advantage is promised or claimed from simple equal zeros.

For source-reference content mismatch diagnostics, record the actual attack call's q_now, h_now, q_bound, donor-target-to-current-code distances, semantic.content_match and own-h content result before semantic propagation, alongside source C0 descriptors. Record whether the fixed donor q/h equal the recipient C0-derived codes under the same declared route; h routes must be named, because E3 h is operational rather than an original source hash. These are evaluator-only explanations of content consistency/rejection and exact donor delivery. They are not extra detector inputs or reference-specific same-owner decisions. A donor pair that happens to match recipient content codes stays in the all-attempt denominator with that fact disclosed; it is not replaced.

Thirty disjoint fully observed zero-F pairs yield a one-sided95% upper bound .0950 under a pair-sampling model; even this bound is descriptive because pairs are selected. A smaller T5 selection has a weaker bound. Do not claim1% attack error, population cryptographic security or multiply the sample by four predicate modes. Within each condition compare methods pairwise; cross-condition repeated pairs remain correlated. T4 and T5 sets may overlap and cannot be pooled as independent evidence. These are explicit bounded challenge results, not confirmation of all original T5 interpretations.
