## Why not select a second mechanism now?

A stronger margin, different chip layout, more redundancy or higher attack severity could generate separation; none currently supplies a distinct, evidence-backed explanation with credible novelty. Searching these settings until separation appears would answer a selected optimization question. The predetermined .4 screen already supplied the permitted stress contrast.

A content-compatible versus random/scrambled supplier sounds distinct, but changing q can change the content-agreement target and changes h/admission through the bootstrap. A lost J would then be partly designed into the control. Exact-Q, matched-energy controls could avoid that tautology, but there is no current evidence that their resulting contrast identifies a novel transferable mechanism rather than differing embedding difficulty. Removing the approximately 53-second supplier may be valuable engineering; it first requires a demonstrated equivalent target task, not a presumed free speed gain.

Primary prior art narrows the space. [FreqMark, Sections 4.2–4.5, Equations 3–10 and Section 5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.20824v1) already optimizes latent-frequency perturbations against pretrained feature-direction message margins with quality losses and latent/pixel noisy branches. Its reported experiment uses a different quality regime and attack procedure. Consequently, pretrained feature alignment, hinge objectives and regeneration-oriented noise augmentation alone are not our novelty, and its published numbers do not rank E3.

[APT, Sections 3.2, 4.1–4.3 and 5.1–5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30656v1) aligns dense features to anchors, penalizes weak alignments and uses a noisy latent branch for fully regenerated inpainting localization. Its endpoint differs from exact message recovery. It neither proves E3 redundant nor establishes E3's proposed mechanism; it blocks an unrestricted novelty claim for uniform feature alignment surviving regeneration. Both primary methods were opened for this decision; their existing cards and bibliography entries remain authoritative records.

## Strongest honest article direction

Working question: **When does reliable recovery of a content-conditioned watermark remain insufficient for an ownership decision under regeneration?** E3 is a case study with strong exact semantic recovery, a fragile instance tier and explicitly measured admission costs. The potential contribution is a consequential empirical explanation of where decisions fail, and whether the same separation changes comparisons across relevant methods. Merely naming separate endpoints is insufficient novelty.

Preserve the frozen M2 composite, but show its admission dependence: E3/native-v5 eligibility is 294/159; their composite counts are 281/151 at .1 and 278/129 at .2. E3's exact-Q majority counts are 294/300 and 297/300, while checked J is 287/300 and 284/300. Full H/F majority acceptance is zero after regeneration. Strict clean quality is 294/300. Combined and perceptual-only binding both reject every prescribed delivered T4/T5 pair, so incremental combined-binding value is absent. These distinctions are observations, not a causal decomposition of the whole E3/v5 difference.

Keep alpha-only ownership, image-domain writing, VAE/CLIP extraction, unresolved negative families and absent human admissibility explicit. Do not advertise arbitrary payload capacity, cryptographic authorization, latent insertion, lightweight extraction or general ownership robustness. The original proposal obligations remain separate.

## Minimum work to justify an article rather than a local case report

1. **Valid closest comparison.** Finish the already capped 1,800-second, two-image FM-R48-Eq10-SD2community feasibility exercise and report its result without extending the deadline. It is an equation reconstruction of uncertain native equivalence, not an official baseline or superiority test. Before scaling, resolve enough fidelity to support the actual comparison, or state that competitiveness remains unavailable. Match feasible quality, task, payload meaning, reader information and independently calibrated operating points; also retain native frontiers and total costs. WAM/ZoDiac can answer distinct detection questions, not substitute for a missing closest-method comparison.
2. **Independent scope check.** Freeze methods before a separately reserved domain and genuinely unconsulted regeneration channel. Compare achieved distortion, not equal numeric strength alone. Keep source groups as units and seeds as clustered observations. A tuned-channel-only result stays a narrow case study.
3. **Decision validity.** Prepare blinded semantic/visual assessment and valid-positive binding controls. Compare component decisions on the same delivered T4/T5 attempts. Exact q with content rejection is not a false rejection until independent admissibility is established; missing human judgments remain missing.
4. **Proportionate confirmation.** If a substantive external tradeoff or reproducible consequential failure survives those screens, predeclare the principal claim, source-level effect/precision target, calibration and missingness treatment, then seek the required fresh-confirmation decision. Do not launch a broad benchmark merely to accumulate numbers.

The article continuation gate is an externally meaningful tradeoff or a transferable failure explanation, with valid task and admissibility controls. If neither emerges, retain a rigorous thesis/report and revise journal ambition; do not manufacture a second mechanism or claim a Q1 contribution from sample size.
