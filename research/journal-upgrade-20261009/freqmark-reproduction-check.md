## Reconstructable v1 recipe

From sections 4.2-4.5, 5.5 and A.1: process RGB at 512 square; freeze SD2.1 KL VAE and DINOv2-small. Encode once, FFT the latent, optimize an additive frequency perturbation, inverse FFT and VAE-decode. Decode bits by signs of feature projections. Use the first 128 feature coordinates and 48 coordinate-basis directions; nonnegative projections mean +1.

Use Adam learning rate 2.0 for 400 steps, fp32. Minimize three message hinge losses (clean, latent-noised, pixel-noised), plus .05 times negative PSNR and .25 times LPIPS. Hinge is mean max(0, mu - projection times target sign). Noise standard deviations are .25 latent and .06 pixel. Appendix specifies equal-probability rotation/crop/pixel-noise augmentation, rotations in 90-degree increments, crop scale [.2,1] and aspect ratio [.75,4/3]. Decoder needs DINO/directions and a candidate message, with no original image, VAE or diffusion inversion.

This is payload extraction, not content authentication or cryptographic ownership. Paper's detection inequality differs between prose (>= tau) and Eq. 1 (> tau); declare the implemented convention and empirically calibrate it. Its balanced independent-bit null assumption is not established for arbitrary natural images or multiple searched owners.

## Material ambiguities and their consequences

- **Frequency parameterization:** FFT axes/normalization, complex optimizer representation, perturbation initialization, Hermitian constraint and inverse-FFT real handling are absent. An unconstrained complex inverse cannot be passed directly to a real VAE. Taking its real part discards directions; Hermitian projection or rFFT parameterization changes Adam coordinates. These choices can materially alter learning-rate meaning and convergence.
- **VAE:** exact checkpoint/revision, posterior mean versus sample, encode/decode scaling, pixel normalization and clipping are unspecified. Noise .25 means different effective perturbations under scaled versus raw latents. Declare posterior mode and raw-VAE units as reconstruction choices, not facts about author code.
- **DINO:** coordinate directions are specified; CLS versus pooled patch output, final LayerNorm, feature normalization/whitening, image resize/interpolation and input normalization are not. These determine feature sign/margin and quality cost. A standard normalized CLS output with official preprocessing is a defensible declared choice; a differentiable tensor equivalent is required for optimization.
- **Loss/numerics:** PSNR and hinge formulas are explicit, but numerical mu, PSNR data range/epsilon/reduction, LPIPS backbone/version and tensor range, Adam betas/epsilon, stopping/output selection and gradient clipping are not. Do not invent a PSNR hinge: the paper specifies negative PSNR. The missing message margin prevents claiming a complete exact recipe.
- **Noise/augmentation:** independent redraw timing, noise clipping, augmentation branch routing and composition with the three-loss formula are unclear. Equal-probability three-way augmentation does not uniquely specify whether it replaces the pixel-noise branch, augments every branch, or adds another loss. Also missing: message distribution/seeds, optimizer/noise seeds and dataset selection identities.

These are implementation unknowns rather than proof the idea cannot work. Freeze one transparent reconstruction first; assess a tiny predeclared sensitivity set only for choices that block meaningful extraction. Do not select a reconstruction using held-out data or describe its losses as author's implementation.

## Smallest actionable feasibility sequence

1. Preserve native reproduction as **unavailable** until author code/final details and authoritative VAE identity resolve. For an independent reconstruction, explicitly choose either community-VAE candidate (equivalence unknown) or already local SD1.5 VAE (**modified-backbone**). Neither becomes the unchanged native baseline. Retain 48-bit directions and the published optimizer/loss weights; list every assumed item above in config/comments.
2. After a separate asset decision/receipt, test batch-one fp32 clean encode/decode and finite-gradient optimization on one prospectively selected development photo, measuring clean PNG quality, exact 48-bit extraction, time and peak VRAM. A100 batch-two author timing does not predict laptop time or prove a 40-GB requirement. There is no diffusion trajectory in embedding, so a 12-GB attempt is plausible but unmeasured.
3. If finite gradients/extraction work, use a small development-only panel to identify a declared quality-constrained operating point; keep the published-setting reconstruction as a separate row. Failures remain counted. Strict PSNR >35/SSIM >.9/LPIPS <.1 must be measured locally rather than inferred from reported percentages. No held-out result or threshold tuning is authorized by this memo.
4. Only then share fixed source images, delivered 8-bit PNGs and T3 DDIM identities with E3/WAM; use project T4/T5 pairs. Report native message-presence score at a separately calibrated FPR, exact 48-bit recovery and bit accuracy separately. E3 32-bit semantic recovery is a separate endpoint; an optional 32-bit reconstruction is a declared payload adaptation, not native FreqMark. Disclose owner trials and detector side information.
5. Report quality distributions, source-level paired uncertainty, clean/attacked C0 and wrong-message negatives, embedding/detection wall time and VRAM. T4 donor-bit recovery is not authentication success; FreqMark supplies no certificate/content-binding rule. Import no author TPR/bit percentages into a local ranking. No confirmatory manifest, runner or new lifecycle gate is needed for this feasibility work.

**Conclusion:** proceed only as a named independent reconstruction with unverified VAE fidelity, or a named SD1.5 backbone adaptation. Exact native reproduction remains blocked by absent identified author code, unresolved implementation details/OpenReview access and unverified authoritative checkpoint identity.
