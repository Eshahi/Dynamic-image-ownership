## Question and possible contribution

**H1:** At equal saved-image perturbation energy and comparable strict clean quality, the initializer's chipwise constraints preserve semantic recovery through regeneration better than constraints on the actual 59 decoded symbol sums, because chipwise constraints suppress within-symbol host variation that regeneration otherwise redistributes. This is a falsifiable explanation for a specific initializer, not a claim of content-adaptive carrier placement. `f5_latent_codec.Layout` depends on public owner/profile, band and whitening; q selects signs on that fixed layout.

The alternative explanation is that matching the 59 symbol sums is sufficient, or uses the quality budget more efficiently. The current objective may waste effort in reader-irrelevant directions. A tie rejects a useful demonstrated advantage of chipwise constraints on this panel; an opposite result rejects H1. Neither result proves component redundancy universally.

The comparison is **not novel by construction**: symbol sums are matched-filter sufficient statistics under an additive isotropic channel, and hinge optimization of fixed neural directions is established prior art. FreqMark already optimizes neural message margins; PhotoGuard already uses encoder sensitivity; SEAL already binds semantic information. The potential contribution is a reproducible, quality-controlled failure or benefit of within-symbol shaping under nonlinear regeneration, followed by a useful transferable tradeoff. No new coding, cryptography, content-adaptive allocation or latent-writing invention is introduced here. A positive local contrast alone cannot support the methods headline.
