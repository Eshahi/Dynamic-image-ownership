# Descriptor capacity amendment: E3-FG128

Prospective scientific decision, 2026-10-14. Run one fixed CPU experiment: a 128-bit Gaussian random-hyperplane sketch of the existing native fine-detail vector. The old 32-bit blend-rebalancing protocol is closed. This amendment changes the representation, sketch capacity and content tolerance explicitly; it is not another blend weight, seed retry, or an unchanged-capacity improvement. Commit this specification and its ordinary probe before scoring any FG128 image. This author performed no candidate execution and changes no shared state, protected files or retained outputs.

## Evidence, hypothesis and scope

The retained old-h oracle has .2 dual-content success 6/18. Coarse-only CD1 reaches 18/18 but newly accepts four legacy-rejected synthetic siblings. BI1, committed at `380a6e8b27ed25ed59f7864cc79adeebc6342f5f`, has clean 24/24, SD1.5 VAE 5/6, .2 10/18 with majority 3/6, and .4 7/18; it newly accepts two protected siblings. However, BI1's measured native fine cosine on all 18 .2 positives is .540617--.791331, mean .661413. Its six fixed texture-sibling fine cosines are .015456--.052097. All these normalized fine norms are one to floating-point precision, so the native floor is inactive here. Coarse cosine instead puts siblings closer than many valid regenerations. Existing detail information is therefore not demonstrably absent: it was compressed into 32 bits together with a confounding coarse channel. A single finite Rademacher outcome cannot establish whether non-isotropy, projection sampling, correlated templates or the coarse allocation caused the loss.

The precise hypothesis is: **the already implemented detail features contain enough regeneration-stable instance information that one larger isotropic sketch can retain positive agreement and reject the fixed texture controls.** Remove coarse features entirely; increase to 128 bits; use one reproducible Gaussian stream. This is a combined representation/compression intervention. A success does not identify which component caused it, and a failure does not prove native features or future representations incapable. No new learned feature, source, attack, transform, frequency band, owner secret or enrollment reference enters.

Primary-method check: Plan and Vershynin's [random hyperplane tessellations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.4452v2), Definition 1.1, Remark 1.3 and Theorems 1.2/1.5, inspected here, define the Gaussian sign embedding and relate Hamming disagreement to normalized angular distance. Their uniform guarantee depends on Gaussian mean width and unknown absolute constants; it does not certify 128 bits for these images, finite public pseudorandom matrices, or natural-image false positives. The parent records the paper under `research/literature/dual-carrier-20261014/random-hyperplane-card.json` and `PlanVershynin2013Tessellations` in the bibliography. This is prior art for the mechanism, not evidence that the proposed experiment passed.

128 is a declared fourfold instance-message budget, not a dimension chosen by scanning outcomes. The improvement sought is sufficient separation to justify paying that cost in a subsequent transport test. All results remain exploratory on consulted development data. They do not provide M1a, population FPR, unseen-source generalization or authenticated ownership.

## Thresholds derived before new outcomes

Define B(n,r)=sum(comb(n,k),k=0..r)/2**n. This is the mass of a Hamming ball under an independent uniform n-bit null, not an image-distribution FPR.

The legacy match ball is B(32,6)=1,149,017/2**32=.0002675263676792383. Choose the largest integer r with B(128,r)<=B(32,6). Exactly r=44: its numerator is 87,756,044,132,513,512,515,796,533,961,962,373, giving .0002578918353207025; r=45 gives .0004979984371356306. Therefore FG128 **matches at distance <=44**. This raises tolerated fraction from 6/32=.1875 to 44/128=.34375 while paying four times the bits. It is an explicit content-decision amendment, not evidence of matched empirical FPR or unchanged instance specificity.

For the descriptive far category, preserve the analogous old non-far ball mass B(32,9)=43,081,973/2**32=.010030803503468633. The largest new radius no greater than that mass is 50, B(128,50)=.008335367134848675; B(128,51)=.01336758642798774. Freeze **45..50 uncertain, >=51 mismatch**. This also supplies the q-nearest far criterion below. Neither threshold may be changed after the new outputs are seen.

For ideal independent Gaussian hyperplanes and fixed nonzero vectors with cosine rho, rotational symmetry in their two-dimensional span gives disagreement probability p=acos(rho)/pi. The disagreeing angular wedges occupy fraction p; independent rows then give D~Binomial(128,p). The observed .2 fine cosine range predicts mean distances 26.8--40.7; the sibling range predicts approximately 61.9--63.4. Under that ideal projection ensemble, radius-44 acceptance ranges approximately .765--.9999 for these positive endpoints and .00039--.00097 for sibling endpoints. These are explanatory predictions across hypothetical projection matrices, not repeated-image success probabilities or guarantees for the one frozen public matrix. No binomial confidence interval over dependent source pairs or multiplication with q FPR is justified.

## Conditional transport feasibility, fully budgeted before construction

Only a complete CPU pass permits the following single carrier test. A 128-bit oracle word cannot be placed silently into DV1's one BCH(59,32) bank. The prior two-bank design is superseded for this candidate by **one unchanged q bank plus two orthogonal h banks**, 320 projections each, for 960 total projections and 160 payload bits (32 q +128 h).

Keep native q slots, weights, chip assignment, 320 signs, BCH encoding, threshold and suspect-only decode exactly unchanged. On the physical coefficient support of each native chip k, write its actual weighted unit vector as a_k. Using public domain-separated streams of owner, config, decoded q, chip k and bank index, construct b_k then c_k by modified Gram--Schmidt against a_k and against a_k,b_k respectively. Freeze carrier domain `b'E3-FG128-orthogonal-carrier-v1'`; derive preliminary signs as native MSB-first Rademacher signs on that support. Normalize in the actual DCT coefficient coordinates including native radial weights in a_k. For a degenerate residual, try canonical unit-coordinate vectors in native slot order and take the first with residual norm>1e-10; this fallback depends only on geometry, never images. Apply the same procedure twice to reduce numerical projection residue. Every chip needs at least three physical coefficients. Before any GPU image, require max absolute Gram error<=1e-8 in float64 for all triples and record fallback counts. Failure is a geometry failure, not permission to alter the carrier band.

Split h into four 32-bit words from most significant to least significant and apply the existing shortened BCH(59,32,t=5) encoder independently. There are 236 h coded bits plus q's59. This provides correction of up to five hard errors **in each block**, not arbitrary twenty errors across the 236 bits. Beyond the bounded decoding radius, wrong-codeword decode remains possible and must be scored/content-checked. No expected-word or recomputed-h fallback is allowed.

Enumerate h projections by t=2*k+b, k=0..319,b=0,1, where b selects b_k,c_k. ## Interpretation and next obligations

A CPU pass establishes finite descriptor separation under perfect transport. A GPU pass would establish a costlier finite dual-content mechanism lead. Neither upgrades the old results retrospectively or passes M1a. Before advancing a candidate, run the prescribed twelve-source .1/.2 seeds0..2 M1a comparison, unchanged strict quality and C0/wrong-owner requirements, and the existing T4/T5 assessors against retained v5 and full E3 comparators. The changed tolerance can affect T4/T5 even if real-impostor and texture controls pass. Keep those threats central; no T1/T2 campaign enters.

The verifier sees suspect pixels, public owner/profile, fixed models and recovered words; it gets no enrolled original or private authorization. It can measure approximate public content consistency. An attacker can recompute and reissue those words, and longer h does not prevent this. Hamming-ball arithmetic is neither cryptographic authentication nor a natural-image false-positive calibration. The potentially useful finding is whether explicit compression capacity, followed by an explicitly paid carrier budget, repairs the observed instance bottleneck without discarding the measured specificity requirements. This is an original experimental derivation from inspected local evidence, not a claim that Gaussian sketches, BCH or orthogonal carriers are novel.
