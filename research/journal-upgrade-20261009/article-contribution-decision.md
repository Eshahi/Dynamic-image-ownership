# Article contribution decision

2026-10-13. Strategic decision memo; proposed work only. This memo changes no frozen method, evidence, claim ledger or lifecycle verdict.

## Recommendation

Pursue one prospective methods article: **explain and improve the mechanism that preserves a content-conditioned semantic payload through regeneration at strict photographic quality, using an explicitly separate fragile instance tier**. Start with the simplest E3 construction that works. The scientific contribution must be a controlled, transferable mechanism or a useful quality–recovery–cost improvement over the closest external method. Removing an expensive redundant stage, combining existing components, and beating native v5 are insufficient novelty on their own.

The leading hypothesis is that initialization and content-compatible carrier construction, rather than iterative attack-aware refinement or decoding rescue, account for much of E3's present recovery. This is a hypothesis to falsify, not a result. Determine which part of initialization causes the benefit before choosing the article title. A mechanism that survives a changed regeneration channel would be more valuable than a larger table from the channel used to design it.

Retain the fragile instance tier as a disclosed limitation and separately evaluated function. Do not promise full ownership authentication after regeneration. Do not make the combination of two bindings the headline unless it rejects admissible delivered transfers that each individual binding sometimes accepts, while accepting valid positives.

This route can yield a publishable contribution if its novelty and evidence survive the gates below. A Q1 journal is a submission target, not a guaranteed outcome; ranking depends on index, subject category and year, and acceptance also depends on editorial fit and review. More sources or smaller p values cannot substitute for scientific novelty.

## Smallest useful next phase

Reserve a small fresh development panel by a written metadata-only rule, excluding all validation, test and held-out IDs/hashes. An initial 24-source panel is a screening size, not publication confirmation. Keep the original 12-source results as adaptive exploratory history. No infrastructure expansion is needed.

1. **Simplification screen.** Compare full E3 and its identical initializer plus fixed quality selector, sharing targets, extraction, seeds and attack identities. Retain native v5 as a separate end-to-end comparator. Measure complete supplier-through-final-image time on the same hardware. Include all construction, admission and safety failures. Predeclare a small practical noninferiority margin and a meaningful time-saving threshold before execution; a suggested screening rule is at most one fewer majority-J source at each .1/.2 strength, no additional strict-quality failures, and at least 25% lower median end-to-end construction time. This rule selects a development candidate; it cannot establish statistical noninferiority from 24 sources.
2. **Causal mechanism screen.** Use the selected construction to isolate carrier, target supplier and objective in sequential paired contrasts, not a large factorial. Hold effective payload, owner access, detector information and quality constraints fixed. Report achieved distortion, admission coverage and reader margins. For code comparisons, disclose code rate and occupied carrier dimensions; raw systematic equality is only a diagnostic. Write the mechanism prediction and a practical effect gate before the first run.
3. **Resolve the easy-channel ceiling.** If both arms saturate, use a small prospectively fixed harder T3 panel with an admissibility rule and an attempt budget. Test matched identities across arms; do not select a winning severity after observing relative performance. Separate message errors from semantic-content mismatch. Human admissibility remains missing until assessed; image metrics alone do not turn a destructive attack into a valid threat. Reserve a genuinely unseen model/channel for the later transfer gate.
4. **Binding sanity and value.** First show the detector accepts legitimate same-owner/content positives under the transformations its binding claim allows. Then test delivered T4 and T5 attacks with same-call semantic-only, perceptual-only and combined decisions. Use a fixed attempted-pair budget and preserve failed deliveries. If all individual checks reject everything, the combined mechanism remains unproved; do not enlarge the sample merely to seek a favorable anecdote.

Run these as short, source-clustered local experiments. Stop each branch as soon as its predeclared mechanism prediction fails. Permit at most two clearly distinct mechanism hypotheses in this discovery round; assess the article route before another tuning cycle. This is a resource decision for the prospective article, not a retroactive redefinition of historical milestone gates.

## Gates before a substantial article experiment

**Mechanism gate.** Require a reproducible causal effect under common targets, reader and quality constraints, or an efficiency improvement tied to a defensible explanation. A carrier result that disappears when the supplier or quality budget is controlled kills that causal claim. A simplification with no new explanation may be good engineering, but is not enough for the main article contribution.

**Prior-art and external-baseline gate.** Recheck primary methods and code before claiming novelty. FreqMark is the closest latent/frequency comparison; WAM supplies a neural decoder; ZoDiac supplies inversion. Existing WAM runs are useful feasibility evidence, not calibrated system superiority. Begin with a small matched FreqMark feasibility screen before scaling all baselines. If the claimed mechanism is already supplied by prior art and no meaningful advantage remains under matched conditions, abandon the methods-paper headline rather than relabel it.

**Transfer gate.** Freeze the candidate before one genuinely unseen regeneration model/channel and independently reserved sources. Include both real photographs and existing generated images in the eventual scope, using separately reported domains. Do not label the already-seen SDXL VAE reconstruction as unseen full-model diffusion. A benefit restricted to the writer's tuned SD1.5 channel supports only a narrow claim; it cannot support general regeneration robustness.

**Binding gate.** To claim complementarity, obtain admissible delivered T4/T5 examples with actual component disagreement and preserve valid-positive acceptance. To claim reference-specific attribution, separately define enrollment, same-owner/different-image challenges and what reference the detector receives. Public payload embedding is not cryptographic authorization; content-dependent bits are not freely selectable payload capacity. Unsupported ownership claims are removed, not rescued by an extra signature label.

**Quality and negative-rate gate.** Freeze detector thresholds using development calibration separate from final evaluation. Compare native frontiers and a common feasible quality/payload/negative-rate operating point, with explicit side information and full detector costs. Keep strict-quality failures in the denominator and publish coverage. If a method cannot meet the common constraint, report infeasibility and its native frontier rather than inventing a score.

Choose negative sample size from the claim, not a round large number. With zero events and genuinely independent, fully observed source trials, the one-sided 95% binomial upper bound is `1 - 0.05^(1/n)`; demonstrating an upper bound below 1% requires at least 299 such trials, and below 0.1% requires 2,995. Correlated seeds/owners do not multiply n. Missing safety calls require adverse bounds. These are statistical model-based bounds, not cryptographic security guarantees. A defensible 1% operating-point study may be preferable to an underpowered 0.1% claim.

## Execution order and stop decisions

The order is: fresh simplification screen → isolated mechanism → small closest-baseline feasibility → binding positives/disagreement and frozen-channel transfer → matched external comparison → blinded human admissibility → frozen fresh confirmation and independent claims review → manuscript and journal fit. Human-assessment materials can be prepared earlier, but human judgments must not be fabricated or replaced by metrics.

Do not expand to JPEG, grayscale or crop benchmarks; the declared threats are T3 regeneration, T4 copy-paste and T5 semantic collision. Do not build new launchers, audit engines or collection machinery to support a speculative contribution. Use existing execution tools and concise development provenance.

If the lean method preserves useful recovery and a novel mechanism survives the closest baseline and transfer tests, write the paper around that specific result, including its failure envelope. If only simplification survives, retain it as engineering and reassess publication scope. If two bounded mechanism hypotheses fail or only the tuned channel works, evaluate the contingency study against prior art before spending on a large experiment. If neither route adds new knowledge, document that conclusion and redirect; larger confirmation cannot manufacture novelty.

The immediate decision is therefore **invest in a small fresh causal discovery phase, not manuscript polishing or a large benchmark**. The present evidence is promising enough to justify that phase, but not sufficient to promise a Q1-ready article.
