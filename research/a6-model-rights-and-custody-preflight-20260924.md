## Operational boundary before scientific manifests

Keep all candidate weights in the ignored local asset directory; do not commit, upload or distribute them with a paper artifact. Preserve the source/revision, exact local digest, acquisition URL, card/license link, and the fact that the SD files are a third-party mirror conversion. Do not replace the A5-pinned multi-folder route with the archive or disable its safety components without a reviewed method amendment. A future exact manifest must separately record model-use conditions, permitted dataset use, safety behavior and any release plan. If institutional or publication requirements require publisher-chain certainty, obtain that evidence or an explicit supervisory/institutional decision rather than marking custody `VERIFIED`.

This preflight narrows the **published-terms** question; it does not close A6. The pending user decision on a bounded CLIP/LPIPS numerical probe is separate and does not become authorized by this document. No model was loaded, inferred, fine-tuned or scored here; no dataset, paid service or scientific run was used.
