# C4 pilot evidence criteria

Engineering completeness: one actual fixed source, canonical digest, environment/input/CLIP/SD identities, matched C0 and marked candidate, every update plus final post-update evaluation, safety results and saved PNG/pixel custody, measured gradient/u/resource records. Any zero gradient must be visible and does not establish nonzero perturbation influence. Finite nonzero gradient and rho compliance support only this setting's feasibility.

Failure inventory: retain source/rights/canonical rejection, detached/zero/nonfinite gradients, loader/network/safety errors, OOM, unavailable headroom and timeouts. No retry or excluded seed/source. Timeout may leave only journals and runner failure; explicitly enumerate pending/missing outputs. No quality, q/h drift or blind result was executed in this worker: NOT_RUN. No thresholds, calibrated decision, owner authenticity or final thesis acceptance.

No accepted science before exact user manifest approval and actual run. Package/code review is narrow launch readiness only. A successful one-source pilot does not complete C4, EXP-EMBED/EXP-QUALITY, C10, 6900-image coverage or final detector calibration. Analyze retained local trajectory/PNG evidence independently before any next experiment, and request separate exact approval for further scientific compute.
