# Acceptance criteria

- Exact reviewed clean Git commit, all code/config/input bindings and original32
  development identities; reject held-out access or any hash mismatch.
- All96 planned case rows or explicit failures; same-image q drift is a failure
  to meet deterministic engineering acceptance, not silently a detector negative.
- Record every JPEG/different-content drift and collision without a seed/threshold
  change or replacement image. C2 completeness does not require invented JPEG
  robustness or perfect discrimination.
- Reproducible outputs and failed runs must receive independent evidence review.
  Do not close #16 on synthetic tests, software imports or this design alone.
- No claim of full latent embedding, native2K method success, final FPR/TPR,
  legal ownership, publication clearance or scientific lifecycle acceptance.
