## Decision

The current source-preserving method has **failed its three quality targets on the only measured source**. There is no evidence that merely improving DDIM inversion, buying more GPU memory, or passing more component tests will make it reach the targets. There is also no basis for a universal impossibility claim: better latent choices and different reconstruction architectures remain untested, and other watermark systems report better quality under different protocols.

Prioritize a bounded **frozen-decoder source-latent refinement experiment**, followed by exact inverse/replay of that refined target and a separate latent-to-DCT check. Do not make the GNRI-inspired arm the presumed repair. The main risk is architectural: four necessary properties remain separate—source reconstruction, latent controllability of the image-DCT statistic, public-code recovery, and synchronization after image transforms. Fixing the first alone establishes none of the other three.

## Blocking scientific findings

**F1 — Exact inversion of the unchanged encoded latent cannot repair the codec endpoint.** `src/embedding/proposed.py:211-239` encodes the posterior mode, adds fresh noise and denoises; this is not source inversion. `src/embedding/reconstruction_comparison.py` passes that same encoded latent to both inverse arms, then decodes the roundtrip result. At exact latent reconstruction, its output is the same frozen `D(E(I))` endpoint as VAE-only, which fails PSNR and SSIM here. A finite residual could change pixels accidentally, but is not evidence of a designed source-quality repair. This conclusion is conditional on the same encoding, decoding and image handling; it is not a bound on all decoder latents or replacement VAEs. The code's actual final-alpha handling is explicit and should not be replaced by an assumed alpha=1.

**F2 — The current quality objective conflicts with restoring the source.** `configs/c4-development.json:71-73` uses two iterations, rho=.01, alpha_s=alpha_i=.2, all four loss weights1 and both score margins.1. In `src/embedding/proposed.py:323-332`, source MSE and distance to the poor control have equal weights. Ignoring watermark terms and decoder restrictions, minimizing those two terms yields `(I+Iref)/2`, with source MSE one quarter of control MSE, only26.4168dB here. Thus the regularizer deliberately resists a full repair toward I. This is an objective-design concern, **not a26.4dB mathematical upper bound on the whole constrained watermark objective**. Its watermark terms and feasible set can change the optimum. A new version should justify the purpose/weight of the control term after a good reconstruction anchor exists; do not silently alter the frozen historical experiment.

**F3 — Gradient existence is not the latent-to-blind-DCT bridge.** Unit-norm latent carriers and image-DCT SHAKE templates use different domain-separated constructions. Their relationship is supplied by the differentiable optimization, not by the common key alone. Nonzero total-loss gradients can arise from reconstruction terms; they do not prove that either score has usable gradient, that the two score directions are independent, or that both can rise within the quality budget. The last two source-informed scores remain below the chosen .1 margins. These margins are not calibrated detection thresholds. The missing scientific evidence is a source-quality-constrained improvement of both saved-PNG scores relative to matched C0 and wrong-owner C2, using actual suspect-only recomputation. Source-oracle scores and a good unmarked reconstruction are insufficient.

**F4 — Public key recovery is discontinuous and geometry-sensitive.** `research/method-spec.md:26-58` hashes the entire12-bit q and32-bit pHash. One changed bit makes an unrelated SHA256 template; radius-one enumeration covers13 semantic and429 instance candidates per owner only while source codes remain within those neighborhoods. Saved-pair q drift0/h drift1 is favorable contrary evidence, but both unmarked and marked outputs have the same drift, and pHash minimum margin0 is a warning, not robustness proof. Twelve q bits also cannot make all semantic codes unique for more than4096 images under a fixed owner; instance binding must carry the separation burden. No independence/uniform-code assumption is warranted.

The template input includes **H and W**, and uses a fixed top-left8x8 grid. A size-changing crop/resize creates a different pseudorandom template even if q/h do not change. Same-size crops/resizes can destroy block alignment. No synchronization search is specified. Hence robustness to geometric transforms is structurally unsupported in this profile, independently of reconstruction. This does not prove every cropped image will be rejected: random matches are possible, and the exact frozen attack may restore dimensions. It means the original mark has no preserved template-coordinate guarantee. Any synchronization change must be versioned and reviewed, not hidden in evaluation.

**F5 — Method-wide numerical claims are not ready for acceptance.** `research/claims.csv` and original extraction Table11 fix PSNR>35, SSIM>.9, LPIPS<.1; dataset counts1000 COCO,800+100 DIV2K and5000 DiffusionDB; and8x8 DCT. They do **not** fix numerical detection-rate, AUC, ACC, regeneration survival, or speedup minima. The80% TPR and1% FPR goals in `research/acceptance.md:13` are subsequent technical design choices, not proposal numbers. None has measured blind-detector evidence. For300 independent negatives, zeroFP can resolve the1% upper-bound goal; oneFP cannot (1.571% one-sided95% bound). This is a narrow resolution constraint, not a prediction of accuracy.

The current maximum_side1024 cannot support native2K DIV2K inputs; it records unsupported images. Full dataset coverage cannot be claimed by resizing or discarding them. Full detector timing must include CLIP, pHash,13/429 candidate scoring and owner trials; DCT-only timing is not RQ3/HYP3. The study-level quality success fraction/noninferiority rule remains unassigned in `research/acceptance.md:31`, so do not turn a per-image diagnostic into a completed study verdict.

## Algorithm concerns that are hypotheses, not demonstrated defects

The fixed-point inverse requires a favorable local Jacobian/contraction to converge; the actual UNet profile has no such evidence. Its honest nonconvergence statuses are appropriate. The guided profile is explicitly **GNRI-inspired**, with a sum-L1 residual plus L2 prior and reciprocal-coordinate gradient updates. It is not full vector Newton and not a faithful paper reproduction. A positive prior can prefer a point with nonzero reconstruction residual, and its scaling varies with dimension/timestep: L1 and L2 terms do not have the same dimension dependence. Evaluate actual residual and image endpoints, not only decreasing objective or low NFE. Safeguards and durable rejection records improve evidence integrity, not scientific fidelity.

The refinement component `src/embedding/latent_refinement.py:127-198` directly addresses source MSE through a fixed decoder with an explicit trust ball and optional latent penalty. This is the right discriminating direction. It optimizes no SSIM/LPIPS/key-stability/DCT objective; monotone penalized loss does not guarantee all targets or even monotone source MSE when the penalty trades off. Strict fp32-radius refusal can terminate without a scientific quality conclusion. A finite unsuccessful optimization is not a proof that the decoder's representational manifold excludes a good image. Checkpointing changes memory/recomputation behavior; it is not itself a reconstruction algorithm or an explanation of an approved run's quality failure.

Public OwnerID and publicly computable templates cannot authenticate enrollment or prove legal ownership. A permitted public re-embed under another owner is a separate threat from copy-paste; passing a content-binding test must not be relabeled cryptographic unforgeability. A semantic-only hit cannot uniquely diagnose regeneration.

## Primary-source evidence and contrary evidence

GNRI's SDXL-Turbo comparison reports23.9dB versus26.1 for EditFriendly, with a speed tradeoff (AppendixH2). It is not evidence of35dB in empty-conditioned SD1.5. The local pinned TRDI inspection/cards report captioned SD1.5 ReNoise+TRDI22.67 and GNRI+TRDI22.32 in Table1. Source protocols differ from this single native-grid case; the result is a caution against an assumed solver cure, not a universal upper bound. [GNRI v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.12540v5), [TRDI v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.15389v1).

There is contrary evidence to **blanket unattainability**. VINE reports40.51/.9954/.0029 (Base) and37.34/.9934/.0063 (Robust), in its Tables1-2. It uses a trained architecture with VAE skip connections and a ConvNeXt-B watermark decoder, under its own resolution-scaling/aggregation protocol. That demonstrates reported quality above these numerical thresholds elsewhere, not that our three per-image criteria or blind-DCT architecture are satisfied. Directly adopting its decoder would change the core detector. Its reconstruction design is a useful contingency requiring a concrete compatibility/training/rights/resource review. [VINE v1, sections3.2 and4.2-4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18775v1).

FreqMark supplies a closer warning: its reported DiffusionDB-label VAE31.22/.879 and marked31.20/.854 still miss this proposal's PSNR/SSIM thresholds. Its latent optimization and learned-feature decoding differ from our image-DCT rule. Neither paper's task/source/aggregation can be silently pooled with local measurements. [FreqMark v1, Figure4](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.20824v1). REED-VAE's iterative decoder training is also a different intervention from this fixed-decoder optimization; no performance promise follows from its name.

## Smallest coherent discriminating experiment to prepare

Prepare one exact package on **the existing development source**, retaining historical controls and every failure. This is a recommendation for design, not approval or a ready manifest. Freeze every policy/resource/arm and conditional branch before execution; no adaptive undeclared radius, prompt, seed, threshold, or solver selection.

1. Reuse the retained source/control hashes and verify the unchanged control replay. Include VAE-only as the reconstruction anchor. No need to frame the already disproved VAE-target hypothesis as unknown again.
2. Run one prospectively fixed frozen-decoder refinement policy from E(I), preserving its accepted/rejected trajectory and final latent. Measure **native saved-PNG** source PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS, q/h drift/margins, resource/time and safety. Continuous MSE is not the final endpoint.
3. Invert and replay that refined latent with one fixed-point reference and a predeclared bounded policy. Compare it with direct refined decode using latent residual and saved-PNG metrics. This distinguishes “decoder can represent a better source” from “diffusion can reproduce that target.” The unrefined inverse arm is useful as a parity/control check; GNRI-inspired extra comparison is secondary, not the indispensable first answer.
4. If the prospective package explicitly includes the branch and its entry criteria are met, use the refined inverse state as a new marked/control pair. Measure separate gradients of semantic score, instance score and source distortion; their norms/alignment; final both scores; all suspect-derived q/h candidate scores for enrolled/wrong owner; and native PNG quality. Label these development diagnostics, **not calibrated detection**. Do not feed source keys to verification or improvise a threshold.

The first decisive question is whether refinement closes the17.85-fold MSE gap. If it cannot within its fixed budget, report the remaining gap and termination reason; do not conclude universal impossibility. If it can but inverse/replay loses it, solver/path is the next bottleneck. If reconstruction survives but DCT scores cannot separate under the quality budget, bridge design is the bottleneck. If scores separate only with source keys, recovery is the bottleneck. These outcomes give a concrete reason for the next route choice instead of a sequence of arbitrary solver additions.

Only after local mechanism evidence should a prospectively locked multi-image development sample across texture/domain/resolution strata test generality, and later validation freeze operating points. One source cannot establish any rate, scientific superiority, native2K behavior or held-out conclusion. If a source bypass, trained skip-VAE, alternative decoder or revised synchronization is necessary, prepare a separately reviewed architecture amendment preserving the original claims/failures; do not disguise it as the existing candidate.

## What would change this review

Evidence changing F1's practical priority: a source-refined latent with verified saved-PNG quality, followed by a faithful inverse/replay and no hidden source-image output bypass. Evidence resolving F2: a versioned source-focused objective with a justified fidelity/mark tradeoff measured on development data. Evidence resolving F3/F4: complete suspect-only scoring, retained negative controls, stability/synchronization experiments, then disjoint validation/test calibration with original denominators. Evidence supporting full quality/coverage claims: prespecified study acceptance plus complete native-grid data coverage, failed-run accounting and uncertainty. None is supplied by more ordinary component tests, a paper's average metric, an approval record, or a larger GPU alone.
